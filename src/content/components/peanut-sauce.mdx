<Step id="grind-peanuts">
  Grind the <Qty ref="peanuts"/> to a coarse, oily meal — short of a smooth butter.
</Step>

<Step id="fry-garlic">
  Warm the <Qty ref="peanut-oil"/> in a small pan and fry the <Qty ref="garlic"/> until
  it just turns golden.
</Step>

<Step id="simmer-sauce">
  Add the ground peanuts, <Qty ref="coconut-milk"/>, <Qty ref="water"/>,
  <Qty ref="palm-sugar"/>, <Qty ref="soy-sauce"/> and <Qty ref="chilli"/>. Simmer,
  stirring often so the peanuts do not catch on the bottom of the pan, for
  <Dur step="simmer-sauce"/>, until thick enough to coat the back of a spoon. Take it
  off the heat and stir in the <Qty ref="lime-juice"/> and <Qty ref="salt"/>.
</Step>

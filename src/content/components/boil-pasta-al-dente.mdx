---
title: Pasta, boiled al dente
summary: Dried pasta cooked in heavily salted water and lifted out while it still has bite, with a mugful of its starchy water kept back.
batchLabel: Two servings
ingredients:
  - id: pasta-water
    ingredientRef: water
    form: tap
    amount: 2000
    unit: ml
  - id: pasta-salt
    ingredientRef: fine-sea-salt
    form: fine
    amount: 20
    unit: g
    consumedFraction: 0.1
    consumedFractionNote: "Most of the salt in the pan is drained away with the water; only what the pasta absorbs is eaten."
  - id: spaghetti
    ingredientRef: spaghetti
    form: dried
    amount: 180
    unit: g
steps:
  - { id: boil-water, durationMin: 9, type: active, phase: cook }
  - { id: cook-pasta, durationMin: 9, type: active, phase: cook }
---

<Step id="boil-water">
  Bring the <Qty ref="pasta-water"/> to a rolling boil in the widest pan you have,
  then add the <Qty ref="pasta-salt"/> and let it dissolve.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-pasta">
  Add the <Qty ref="spaghetti"/> and cook for <Dur step="cook-pasta"/>, or two minutes
  short of whatever the packet claims. Before draining, lift out a mugful of the
  cooking water — the starch it carries is what will let the sauce bind. Drain the
  pasta while the centre still resists a bite.
</Step>

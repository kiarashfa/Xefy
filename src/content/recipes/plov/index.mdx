<Step id="brown-lamb">
  Heat the <Qty ref="oil"/> in a heavy pot until shimmering and brown the
  <Qty ref="lamb"/> on all sides over a high heat. This takes <Dur step="brown-lamb"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-onion">
  Add the <Qty ref="onion"/> and cook, stirring often, for <Dur step="cook-onion"/>,
  until it has turned a deep golden-brown.
</Step>

<Step id="build-zirvak">
  Add the <Qty ref="carrot"/> and cook briefly without much stirring, so it keeps
  some structure. Nestle in the <Qty ref="garlic"/> heads, then add the
  <Qty ref="cumin"/>, <Qty ref="chilli"/>, the <Qty ref="salt"/> and the
  <Qty ref="stock-water"/>. Bring to a simmer, cover, and cook gently for
  <Dur step="build-zirvak"/>, until the lamb is tender and the stock is richly
  flavoured. Lift out the garlic heads and set them aside.
</Step>

<Step id="add-rice">
  Rinse the <Qty ref="rice"/> until the water runs clear and spread it evenly over
  the zirvak without stirring it in. Add just enough hot water to cover the rice by
  a fingertip's depth, and boil, uncovered, for <Dur step="add-rice"/>, until the
  water has dropped level with the rice.
</Step>

<Step id="steam-rice">
  Poke several holes down through the rice to the base of the pot, tuck the garlic
  heads back in, cover tightly, and cook over the lowest heat for
  <Dur step="steam-rice"/>, undisturbed, until the rice is tender and every drop of
  stock is gone.
</Step>

<Step id="rest-and-serve">
  Let the pot rest off the heat, still covered, before folding the rice and zirvak
  gently together. Spread onto a platter and set the whole garlic heads on top for
  guests to split apart at the table. This takes <Dur step="rest-and-serve"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="brown-lamb">
  Heat the <Qty ref="oil"/> in a heavy pot until shimmering and brown the
  <Qty ref="lamb"/> on all sides over a high heat. This takes <Dur step="brown-lamb"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-onion">
  Add the <Qty ref="onion"/> and cook, stirring often, for <Dur step="cook-onion"/>,
  until it has turned a deep golden-brown.
</Step>

<Step id="build-zirvak">
  Add the <Qty ref="carrot"/> and cook briefly without much stirring, so it keeps
  some structure. Nestle in the <Qty ref="garlic"/> heads, then add the soaked
  <Qty ref="chickpeas"/>, the <Qty ref="cumin"/>, the <Qty ref="salt"/> and the
  <Qty ref="stock-water"/>. Bring to a simmer, cover, and cook gently for
  <Dur step="build-zirvak"/>, until the lamb and chickpeas are both tender and the
  stock is richly flavoured. Lift out the garlic heads and set them aside.
</Step>

<Step id="add-rice">
  Rinse the <Qty ref="rice"/> until the water runs clear and spread it evenly over
  the zirvak without stirring it in. Add just enough hot water to cover the rice by
  a fingertip's depth, and boil, uncovered, for <Dur step="add-rice"/>, until the
  water has dropped level with the rice.
</Step>

<Step id="steam-rice">
  Scatter the <Qty ref="raisins"/> over the rice, poke several holes down through
  to the base of the pot, tuck the garlic heads back in, cover tightly, and cook
  over the lowest heat for <Dur step="steam-rice"/>, undisturbed, until the rice is
  tender and every drop of stock is gone.
</Step>

<Step id="rest-and-plate">
  Let the pot rest off the heat, still covered, then plate in distinct layers
  rather than mixing the pot together: rice first, spread wide over the platter,
  then the carrot, chickpeas and raisins over that, and the lamb and whole garlic
  heads set on top. This takes <Dur step="rest-and-plate"/>.
</Step>

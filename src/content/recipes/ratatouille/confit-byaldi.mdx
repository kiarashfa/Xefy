<Step id="make-piperade">
  Heat the <Qty ref="oil"/> in a wide, shallow pan over a medium heat. Add the <Qty ref="onion"/>
  and cook it until soft. Add the <Qty ref="pepper"/>, the <Qty ref="garlic"/>, the
  <Qty ref="tomato-piperade"/> and the <Qty ref="thyme"/>, and cook gently for
  <Dur step="make-piperade"/>, until the mixture is thick and jammy.
</Step>

<Step id="slice-rounds">
  Cut the <Qty ref="courgette"/>, the <Qty ref="aubergine"/> and the <Qty ref="tomato-rounds"/> into
  thin rounds of an even thickness. This takes <Dur step="slice-rounds"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="assemble-dish">
  Spread the piperade thinly in a shallow ovenproof dish. Fan the rounds of courgette, aubergine
  and tomato over it in overlapping rows, seasoning them with the <Qty ref="salt"/> and the
  <Qty ref="pepper-ground"/>. Cover with a sheet of baking paper. This takes
  <Dur step="assemble-dish"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="bake-covered">
  Bake at <Temp c={120}/> for <Dur step="bake-covered"/>, until the vegetables are soft right
  through.
</Step>

<Step id="uncover-roast">
  Remove the baking paper and roast at <Temp c={190}/> for <Dur step="uncover-roast"/>, until the
  edges of the rounds are lightly caramelised. Serve it warm.
</Step>

<Step id="cut-vegetables">
  Cut the <Qty ref="aubergine"/> into cubes, the <Qty ref="courgette"/> into thick half-moons, and
  deseed the <Qty ref="pepper"/>. Chop the <Qty ref="onion"/> and skin and chop the
  <Qty ref="tomatoes"/>. This takes <Dur step="cut-vegetables"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="fry-aubergine">
  Heat some of the <Qty ref="oil"/> in a wide pan over a high heat and fry the <Qty ref="aubergine"/>
  in batches for <Dur step="fry-aubergine"/>, until golden on all sides. Drain the batches on
  kitchen paper as they are done.
</Step>

<Step id="fry-courgette">
  Add a little more of the <Qty ref="oil"/> to the pan and fry the <Qty ref="courgette"/> for
  <Dur step="fry-courgette"/>, until it is lightly coloured. Drain it.
</Step>

<Step id="soften-pepper-onion">
  Add the remaining <Qty ref="oil"/> to the pan with the <Qty ref="pepper"/> and the
  <Qty ref="onion"/>, and cook them for <Dur step="soften-pepper-onion"/>, until soft and beginning
  to colour. Add the <Qty ref="garlic"/> for the last minute.
</Step>

<Step id="stew">
  Return the aubergine and courgette to the pan with the <Qty ref="tomatoes"/>, the
  <Qty ref="thyme"/>, the <Qty ref="bay"/>, the <Qty ref="salt"/> and the <Qty ref="pepper-ground"/>.
  Cover and simmer very gently for <Dur step="stew"/>, stirring now and then, until the vegetables
  are tender and the liquid has reduced to a thick sauce.
</Step>

<Step id="finish">
  Take out the bay leaf and stir in the <Qty ref="basil"/>. This takes <Dur step="finish"/>.
</Step>

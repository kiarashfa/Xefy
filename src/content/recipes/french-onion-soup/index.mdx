<Step id="slice-onions">
  Peel the <Qty ref="onions"/> and slice them thinly into rings. This takes <Dur step="slice-onions"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="caramelise-onions">
  Melt the <Qty ref="butter"/> in a heavy, wide pot over a moderate heat. Add the onions and cook
  them slowly for <Dur step="caramelise-onions"/>, stirring often, until they are deep golden brown
  and very soft. Scrape the base of the pot as the onions brown.
</Step>

<Step id="deglaze">
  Pour in the <Qty ref="wine"/> and let it bubble, scraping up the browned bits from the base. This
  takes <Dur step="deglaze"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="simmer-soup">
  Add the <Qty ref="stock"/>, the <Qty ref="thyme"/>, the <Qty ref="bay"/>, the <Qty ref="salt"/> and
  the <Qty ref="pepper"/>. Bring to a simmer and cook gently for <Dur step="simmer-soup"/>, until the
  onions are tender and the soup tastes deep and savoury. Take out the thyme stalks and the bay leaf.
</Step>

<Step id="toast-bread">
  Toast the <Qty ref="baguette"/> rounds in a hot oven until crisp and golden. This takes
  <Dur step="toast-bread"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="grate-cheese">
  Grate the <Qty ref="gruyere"/> finely. This takes <Dur step="grate-cheese"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="gratinate">
  Ladle the soup into ovenproof bowls, float the toasted bread on top and pile the cheese over it.
  Set the bowls under a hot grill for <Dur step="gratinate"/>, until the cheese has melted and
  browned in patches. Serve at once.
</Step>

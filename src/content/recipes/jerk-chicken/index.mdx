<Step id="blend-marinade">
  Blend the <Qty ref="scotch-bonnet"/>, <Qty ref="allspice"/>,
  <Qty ref="thyme"/>, <Qty ref="garlic"/>, <Qty ref="ginger"/>,
  <Qty ref="spring-onion"/>, <Qty ref="soy-sauce"/>,
  <Qty ref="brown-sugar"/>, <Qty ref="lime-juice"/>,
  <Qty ref="vegetable-oil"/>, <Qty ref="nutmeg"/>, <Qty ref="cinnamon"/>,
  <Qty ref="salt"/> and <Qty ref="black-pepper"/> to a smooth paste.
</Step>

<Step id="marinate">
  Rub the paste all over the <Qty ref="chicken"/>, working it under the
  skin where possible, then cover and refrigerate for
  <Dur step="marinate"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="char">
  Sear the chicken over direct high heat on a grill or ridged grill pan for
  <Dur step="char"/>, turning occasionally, until the surface is
  well-blackened in patches on all sides.
</Step>

<Step id="finish">
  Transfer the chicken to a tray and finish in an oven preheated to
  <Temp c={180}/> for <Dur step="finish"/>, until the thickest pieces are
  cooked through and the juices run clear.
</Step>

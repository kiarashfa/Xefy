<Step id="blend-marinade">
  Blend the <Qty ref="scotch-bonnet"/>, <Qty ref="allspice"/>,
  <Qty ref="thyme"/>, <Qty ref="garlic"/>, <Qty ref="ginger"/>,
  <Qty ref="spring-onion"/>, <Qty ref="soy-sauce"/>,
  <Qty ref="brown-sugar"/>, <Qty ref="lime-juice"/>,
  <Qty ref="vegetable-oil"/>, <Qty ref="nutmeg"/>, <Qty ref="cinnamon"/>,
  <Qty ref="salt"/> and <Qty ref="black-pepper"/> to a smooth paste.
</Step>

<Step id="marinate">
  Rub the paste all over the <Qty ref="chicken"/>, working it under the
  skin where possible, then cover and refrigerate for
  <Dur step="marinate"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="build-fire">
  Light a bed of charcoal or split pimento wood and let it burn down to
  steady, smouldering embers, banking them to one side of the grill for
  indirect heat, for <Dur step="build-fire"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="smoke">
  Set the chicken on the side of the grill away from the embers, cover, and
  smoke over the low, indirect heat for <Dur step="smoke"/>, turning
  occasionally, until the skin is deeply darkened and the thickest pieces are
  cooked through.
</Step>

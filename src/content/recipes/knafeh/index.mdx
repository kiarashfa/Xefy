<Step id="soak-cheese">
  Cover the <Qty ref="akkawi"/> with cold water and leave it to soak for
  <Dur step="soak-cheese"/>, changing the water once or twice, until it tastes only
  mildly salty. Drain well and slice thinly.
</Step>

<Step id="make-syrup">
  Meanwhile, combine the <Qty ref="sugar-syrup"/>, <Qty ref="water"/> and
  <Qty ref="lemon-juice"/> in a small pan and bring to the boil, stirring until the
  sugar has dissolved. Simmer for <Dur step="make-syrup"/>, then take off the heat,
  stir in the <Qty ref="rose-water"/> and set aside to cool.
</Step>

<Step id="mix-crust">
  Rub the <Qty ref="butter-crust"/> through the <Qty ref="semolina"/> and
  <Qty ref="sugar-crust"/> until the mixture looks like damp, even sand. This takes
  <Dur step="mix-crust"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="assemble">
  Grease a round baking tray with the <Qty ref="butter-tray"/>. Lay the sliced
  <Qty ref="akkawi"/> over the base in an even layer, then press the semolina crust
  mixture evenly on top, right to the edges, smoothing the surface with the back of a
  spoon. This takes <Dur step="assemble"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="bake">
  Bake at <Temp c={200}/> for <Dur step="bake"/>, until the crust is deep golden and
  the cheese underneath has melted.
</Step>

<Step id="finish">
  Run a knife around the edge and invert the tray onto a serving platter so the golden
  crust is now on top. Pour the cooled syrup evenly over the surface while the knafeh
  is still hot, then scatter over the <Qty ref="pistachio"/>. Cut into wedges once the
  syrup has soaked in, after <Dur step="finish"/>.
</Step>

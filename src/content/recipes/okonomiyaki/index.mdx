<Step id="prepare-toppings">
  Shred the <Qty ref="cabbage"/> very finely and slice the <Qty ref="spring-onion"/> finely.
  Lay the <Qty ref="pork"/> slices out on a board. This takes <Dur step="prepare-toppings"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="mix-batter">
  Whisk the <Qty ref="flour"/>, the <Qty ref="egg"/> and the <Qty ref="salt"/> with the
  strained dashi until the batter is smooth. Fold in the cabbage and the spring onion, so
  that every piece is coated, and do this in the time it takes <Dur step="mix-batter"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-pancakes">
  Heat the <Qty ref="oil"/> in a wide, heavy pan over a medium heat. Spoon in half the
  batter and spread it into a round about two fingers deep. Lay half the <Qty ref="pork"/>
  over the top and cook for <Dur step="cook-pancakes"/>, until the base is firm and golden
  and the edges have set. Turn it with two spatulas, and cook the other side until the pork
  is cooked through and the centre is set. Repeat with the remaining batter and pork.
</Step>

<Step id="finish-okonomiyaki">
  Slide each pancake onto a plate, pork side up. Brush it with the <Qty ref="sauce"/>, drizzle
  the <Qty ref="mayonnaise"/> over in lines, and finish with the <Qty ref="aonori"/> and the
  <Qty ref="pickled-ginger"/>. Serve at once, cut into wedges at the table.
</Step>

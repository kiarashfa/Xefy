<Step id="prepare-layers">
  Shred the <Qty ref="cabbage"/> thinly and pick over the <Qty ref="bean-sprouts"/>. Lay the
  <Qty ref="pork"/> slices out on a board. This takes <Dur step="prepare-layers"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="mix-thin-batter">
  Whisk the <Qty ref="flour"/> with the strained dashi and the <Qty ref="salt"/> until smooth
  and thin, thinner than the mixed batter. This takes <Dur step="mix-thin-batter"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="fry-noodles">
  Heat the <Qty ref="oil"/> in a wide pan over a medium-high heat. Fry the <Qty ref="noodles"/>
  loose for <Dur step="fry-noodles"/>, turning them with chopsticks, and then set them aside
  on a plate.
</Step>

<Step id="build-layers">
  Pour a thin layer of the batter into a clean hot pan and let it set for a moment. Pile
  the <Qty ref="cabbage"/> on top and press it down, then add the <Qty ref="bean-sprouts"/>,
  the <Qty ref="pork"/> and the fried noodles in turn, one layer on another. Crack the
  <Qty ref="egg"/> on top, and cook for <Dur step="build-layers"/>, turning the whole pancake
  once with two spatulas, until the cabbage is tender and the pork is cooked through.
</Step>

<Step id="finish-hiroshima">
  Slide each pancake onto a plate with the egg side up. Brush it with the <Qty ref="sauce"/>
  and finish with the <Qty ref="aonori"/> and the <Qty ref="pickled-ginger"/>. Serve at once,
  with no mayonnaise.
</Step>

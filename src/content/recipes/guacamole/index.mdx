<Step id="grind-paste">
  Grind the <Qty ref="onion"/>, the <Qty ref="chilli"/>, the <Qty ref="coriander-paste"/>
  and the <Qty ref="salt"/> together in a mortar — a basalt molcajete if there is one —
  working for <Dur step="grind-paste"/> until they collapse into a coarse green paste and
  the onion has given up its juice. It should smell of all four at once rather than of
  each in turn.
</Step>

<Step id="crush-avocado">
  Halve the <Qty ref="avocado"/>, lever out the stones and scoop the flesh in on top.
  Crush it into the paste with a fork or the pestle for <Dur step="crush-avocado"/>,
  turning it over rather than beating it, until it is bound but still plainly in pieces.
  Fold through the <Qty ref="lime-juice"/>, scatter the <Qty ref="coriander-finish"/>
  over the top and serve it in the mortar.
</Step>

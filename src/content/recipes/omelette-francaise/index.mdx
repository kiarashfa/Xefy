<Step id="beat">
  Beat the <Qty ref="eggs"/> with the <Qty ref="salt"/> and the <Qty ref="pepper"/> in a
  bowl, for <Dur step="beat"/>, until no streaks of white remain. Stir in the
  <Qty ref="chives"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="heat-pan">
  Set a small non-stick pan over a moderate heat and melt the <Qty ref="butter-pan"/>
  until it foams, without letting it colour. This takes <Dur step="heat-pan"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-omelette">
  Pour in the eggs. Stir them with a fork and shake the pan, for <Dur step="cook-omelette"/>,
  until the eggs are just set but the top is still a little moist.
</Step>

<Step id="roll-omelette">
  Tilt the pan and roll the omelette towards the handle, folding it over itself as it
  goes, so that it lands on a warm plate seam side down. Rub the surface with the
  <Qty ref="butter-finish"/>. This takes <Dur step="roll-omelette"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="mix-dough">
  Pour the <Qty ref="boiling-water"/> into the <Qty ref="flour"/> and stir
  quickly with chopsticks, then add the <Qty ref="cold-water"/> and
  <Qty ref="salt"/> and bring together into a soft dough, taking
  <Dur step="mix-dough"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="rest-dough">
  Cover the dough and rest it for <Dur step="rest-dough"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="roll-and-layer">
  Roll the dough out into a large, thin rectangle. Mix the
  <Qty ref="lamination-oil"/> with the <Qty ref="lamination-flour"/> into a
  paste and spread it evenly over the surface, then scatter with the
  <Qty ref="spring-onion"/>. Roll up tightly from one long edge into a
  log, then coil the log into a flat spiral and roll it out gently into a
  disc, taking <Dur step="roll-and-layer"/> in total.
</Step>

<Step id="rest-again">
  Rest the shaped disc for <Dur step="rest-again"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="fry">
  Heat the <Qty ref="frying-oil"/> in a wide pan over medium heat and fry the
  pancake for <Dur step="fry"/> in total, turning occasionally, until deep
  golden and crisp on both sides. Drain briefly before cutting into wedges.
</Step>

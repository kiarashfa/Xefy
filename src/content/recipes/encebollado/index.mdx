<Step id="pickle-onions">
  Toss the <Qty ref="onion"/> with the <Qty ref="lime-juice"/> and
  <Qty ref="pickle-salt"/> and leave to pickle for
  <Dur step="pickle-onions"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-cassava">
  Meanwhile, simmer the <Qty ref="cassava"/> in the <Qty ref="water"/> for
  <Dur step="cook-cassava"/>, until tender. Do not drain.
</Step>

<Step id="build-broth">
  Add the <Qty ref="garlic"/>, <Qty ref="tomato"/>, <Qty ref="cumin"/>,
  <Qty ref="achiote"/> and <Qty ref="broth-salt"/> to the cassava and its
  liquid and simmer for <Dur step="build-broth"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-fish">
  Add the <Qty ref="tuna"/> and simmer for <Dur step="cook-fish"/>, until
  just cooked through.
</Step>

<Step id="finish">
  Stir in the <Qty ref="coriander"/>, ladle into bowls, and top with the
  pickled onion. This takes about <Dur step="finish"/>.
</Step>

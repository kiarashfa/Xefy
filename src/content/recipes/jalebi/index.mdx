<Step id="mix-batter">
  Whisk the <Qty ref="flour"/>, <Qty ref="cornflour"/> and <Qty ref="yeast"/>
  together, then whisk in the <Qty ref="yoghurt"/> and the <Qty ref="batter-water"/>
  to a smooth, pourable batter, about the thickness of double cream. This takes
  <Dur step="mix-batter"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="ferment-batter">
  Cover the batter and leave it somewhere warm to ferment for
  <Dur step="ferment-batter"/>, until it has loosened further and turned lightly
  bubbly and faintly sour-smelling.
</Step>

<Step id="make-syrup">
  Combine the <Qty ref="caster-sugar"/> and the <Qty ref="syrup-water"/> in a pan
  with the <Qty ref="cardamom"/> and bring to a simmer, stirring until the sugar
  dissolves. Stir in the <Qty ref="saffron"/> and the <Qty ref="lemon-juice"/> and
  simmer gently for <Dur step="make-syrup"/>, until very slightly thickened. Keep
  warm.
</Step>

<Step id="fry-jalebi">
  Heat the <Qty ref="frying-oil"/> in a wide, shallow pan to a moderate heat.
  Transfer the fermented batter to a piping bag or a bottle with a narrow nozzle
  and pipe it into the oil in tight, overlapping coils, working in batches. Fry for
  <Dur step="fry-jalebi"/> in total, turning once, until crisp and golden all over.
  Lift out with a slotted spoon, draining briefly.
</Step>

<Step id="soak-jalebi">
  Slide the hot, freshly fried jalebi straight into the warm syrup and turn them to
  coat for <Dur step="soak-jalebi"/>, until they have soaked through and turned
  glossy. Lift out and serve warm.
</Step>

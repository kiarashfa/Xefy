<Step id="soak-chillies">
  Cover the <Qty ref="ancho"/> with just-boiled water and leave to soften for
  <Dur step="soak-chillies"/>, then drain.
</Step>

<Step id="blend-marinade">
  Blend the softened chillies with the <Qty ref="achiote"/>, <Qty ref="garlic"/>,
  <Qty ref="vinegar"/>, <Qty ref="pineapple-marinade"/>, <Qty ref="cumin"/>,
  <Qty ref="oregano"/>, <Qty ref="pepper"/> and <Qty ref="salt"/> to a smooth,
  brick-red paste.
</Step>

<Step id="marinate">
  Coat the <Qty ref="pork"/> thoroughly in the paste. Cover and marinate,
  refrigerated, for <Dur step="marinate"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="stack">
  Thread the marinated pork tightly onto a vertical skewer, pressing each slice down
  onto the last, building an even, dense cone.
</Step>

<Step id="roast">
  Roast the stacked pork close to a vertical heat source, turning it steadily, for
  <Dur step="roast"/>, until the outer surface is deeply browned and the centre is
  cooked through with no pink remaining.
</Step>

<Step id="sear-pineapple">
  Sear the <Qty ref="pineapple-topping"/> in a hot dry pan for <Dur step="sear-pineapple"/>,
  until caramelised at the edges.
</Step>

<Step id="shave">
  Shave the roasted pork downward off the spit in thin ribbons for <Dur step="shave"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="warm-tortillas">
  Warm the <Qty ref="tortillas"/> briefly over direct heat until soft and lightly
  charred, and double each one up.
</Step>

<Step id="assemble">
  Pile the shaved pork onto the doubled tortillas, top with the seared pineapple,
  <Qty ref="onion"/> and <Qty ref="coriander"/>, and serve with the <Qty ref="lime"/>.
</Step>

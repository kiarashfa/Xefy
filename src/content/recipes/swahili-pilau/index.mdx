<Step id="brown-beef">
  Heat half of the <Qty ref="vegetable-oil"/> in a heavy pot over a high heat and
  brown the <Qty ref="beef"/> on all sides, working in batches if needed, for
  <Dur step="brown-beef"/>. Lift out and set aside.
</Step>

<Step id="fry-aromatics">
  Add the remaining <Qty ref="vegetable-oil"/> to the same pot and fry the
  <Qty ref="onion"/> over a medium heat until deep golden. Stir in the
  <Qty ref="garlic"/>, <Qty ref="ginger"/>, <Qty ref="cumin"/>,
  <Qty ref="cinnamon"/>, <Qty ref="cloves"/>, <Qty ref="cardamom"/>,
  <Qty ref="black-pepper"/> and <Qty ref="bay-leaf"/> and fry for
  <Dur step="fry-aromatics"/>, until deeply fragrant and the oil has taken on the
  colour of the spice. Stir in the <Qty ref="tomato"/> and cook until it has broken
  down into the onions.
</Step>

<Step id="braise-beef">
  Return the browned <Qty ref="beef"/> to the pot along with the <Qty ref="stock"/>
  and the <Qty ref="fine-sea-salt"/>. Bring to a simmer, cover, and cook for
  <Dur step="braise-beef"/>, until the beef is tender and the liquid is
  well-flavoured.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-rice">
  Stir the rinsed <Qty ref="rice"/> into the pot, spreading it in an even layer
  over the beef and liquid. Bring back to the boil, then cover tightly and reduce
  to the lowest possible heat, cooking undisturbed for <Dur step="cook-rice"/>,
  until the liquid is fully absorbed and the rice is tender.
</Step>

<Step id="rest-pilau">
  Take the pot off the heat and leave it covered and undisturbed for
  <Dur step="rest-pilau"/>, then fluff the rice through with a fork, folding the
  beef up from the bottom. Scatter over the <Qty ref="coriander"/> to serve.
</Step>

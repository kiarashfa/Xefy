<Step id="prepare-chicken">
  Pat the <Qty ref="chicken"/> dry inside and out. Work the <Qty ref="butter"/> under the skin
  of the breast and thighs with your fingers, then rub the rest over the outside. Season the
  cavity and the skin with the <Qty ref="salt"/> and the <Qty ref="pepper"/>. Put the
  <Qty ref="garlic"/>, the <Qty ref="lemon"/> and the <Qty ref="thyme"/> inside the cavity.
  This takes <Dur step="prepare-chicken"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="roast">
  Set the chicken on a rack in a roasting tin and roast it at <Temp c={200}/> for
  <Dur step="roast"/>, basting it with the fat in the tin once or twice. It is ready when the
  thickest part of the thigh is cooked through and its juices run clear.
</Step>

<Step id="make-jus">
  While the chicken roasts, pour off most of the fat from the tin, leaving the juices and the
  sediment. Set the tin over a medium heat, pour in the <Qty ref="stock"/>, and scrape up the
  browned bits from the base. Simmer for <Dur step="make-jus"/>, until the jus has
  reduced slightly and tastes concentrated.
</Step>

<Step id="rest">
  Lift the chicken onto a board, cover it loosely with foil, and leave it to rest for
  <Dur step="rest"/>. Pour any juices from the cavity into the jus.
</Step>

<Step id="carve">
  Take off the legs and wings, then the breast on each side of the bone. Carve the breast across
  the grain and arrange it on a warm plate with the legs and wings, and spoon the jus over.
  This takes <Dur step="carve"/>.
</Step>

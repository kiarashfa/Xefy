<Step id="mix-dry">
  Whisk the <Qty ref="flour"/>, <Qty ref="baking-powder"/>, <Qty ref="bicarb"/> and
  <Qty ref="salt"/> together in a wide bowl.
</Step>

<Step id="cut-butter">
  Rub or cut the <Qty ref="butter-cut"/> into the flour for <Dur step="cut-butter"/>,
  until the largest pieces are the size of small peas. Keep the mixture cold throughout.
</Step>

<Step id="fold-dough">
  Pour in the <Qty ref="buttermilk"/> and bring the dough together with a few strokes,
  stopping while it still looks shaggy. Turn it onto a floured surface and pat it into a
  rectangle, then fold it into thirds like a letter. Repeat this fold twice more over
  <Dur step="fold-dough"/>, patting the dough back out each time, before patting it to
  its final thickness.
</Step>

<Step id="cut-rounds">
  Cut rounds straight down without twisting the cutter, which would seal the cut edge and
  blunt the rise. Gather the scraps, pat them back together once, and cut the rest. Set
  the rounds close together on a lined tray, touching at the edges, over
  <Dur step="cut-rounds"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="bake">
  Bake at <Temp c={220}/> for <Dur step="bake"/>, until well risen and golden on top.
  Brush the tops with the <Qty ref="butter-brush"/> as soon as they come out of the oven.
</Step>

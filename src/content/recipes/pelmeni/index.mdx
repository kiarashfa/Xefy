<Step id="mix-dough">
  Combine the <Qty ref="flour-dough"/>, <Qty ref="egg"/>, <Qty ref="water"/> and
  <Qty ref="dough-salt"/> in a bowl and bring together into a firm dough, then turn it
  out and knead for <Dur step="mix-dough"/> until smooth and no longer tacky.
</Step>

<Step id="rest-dough">
  Cover the dough and rest it for <Dur step="rest-dough"/> at room temperature, until it
  has relaxed and no longer springs back when pressed.
</Step>

<Step id="make-filling">
  Meanwhile, combine the <Qty ref="beef"/>, <Qty ref="pork"/>, <Qty ref="onion"/>,
  <Qty ref="black-pepper"/> and <Qty ref="filling-salt"/> in a bowl and mix by hand
  until evenly combined.
</Step>

<Step id="roll-and-fill">
  Roll the rested dough out on a surface dusted with the <Qty ref="flour-bench"/> and
  cut into small rounds about <Len cm={5}/> across. Spoon a little filling into the
  centre of each round, fold in half and pinch the curved edge fully shut into a
  half-moon, then bring the two pointed corners around and pinch them together to
  close the ring into the classic ear shape.
</Step>

<Step id="boil">
  Drop the pelmeni into a large pot of well-salted, boiling water, stirring once so
  they do not settle on the base, and cook for <Dur step="boil"/>, until they float
  and the dough is cooked through.
</Step>

<Step id="serve">
  Lift the pelmeni out with a slotted spoon and serve with the <Qty ref="soured-cream"/>
  and the <Qty ref="butter"/> spooned over.
</Step>

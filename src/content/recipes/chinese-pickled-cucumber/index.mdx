<Step id="smash-cucumber">
  Lay the <Qty ref="cucumber"/> on a board and strike firmly along its length
  with the flat side of a large knife or a rolling pin, until it splits open.
  Cut into rough chunks, taking <Dur step="smash-cucumber"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="salt-cucumber">
  Toss the cucumber with the <Qty ref="salt"/> and leave to stand for
  <Dur step="salt-cucumber"/>, then drain off any liquid that collects.
</Step>

<Step id="dress">
  Toss the drained cucumber with the <Qty ref="garlic"/>, <Qty ref="black-vinegar"/>,
  <Qty ref="soy-sauce"/>, <Qty ref="sugar"/>, <Qty ref="sesame-oil"/> and
  <Qty ref="chilli-oil"/>, taking <Dur step="dress"/>. Finish with the
  <Qty ref="sesame-seed"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-rice">
  Rinse the <Qty ref="rice"/> in several changes of cold water until the water runs almost clear,
  drain it well, and cook it with a measured amount of water in a heavy pan with a tight lid. Leave
  it covered off the heat for the last part of the <Dur step="cook-rice"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="prepare-fish">
  Slice the <Qty ref="tuna"/>, the <Qty ref="bream"/> and the <Qty ref="mackerel"/> thinly across the
  grain, one slice for each piece of rice. Keep them cold on a plate in the fridge. This takes
  <Dur step="prepare-fish"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="dress-rice">
  Warm the <Qty ref="vinegar"/> with the <Qty ref="sugar"/> and the <Qty ref="salt"/> until the
  sugar and salt have dissolved. Spread the hot rice in a wide wooden tray, pour the seasoning over
  it, and fold it through with a rice paddle, fanning it as you go, until it is glossy and cooled
  to the touch. This takes <Dur step="dress-rice"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="shape-nigiri">
  Wet your hands with a little of the seasoning water and take a small portion of the rice in your
  palm. Shape it into an oval with a light touch, then lay a dab of the <Qty ref="wasabi"/> on the
  fish, and press the slice of fish gently onto the rice. This takes <Dur step="shape-nigiri"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="serve-nigiri">
  Arrange the nigiri on a plate in the order of their fish, with the <Qty ref="pickled-ginger"/>
  beside them and the <Qty ref="soy"/> in a small dish for dipping. Serve at once. This takes
  <Dur step="serve-nigiri"/>.
</Step>

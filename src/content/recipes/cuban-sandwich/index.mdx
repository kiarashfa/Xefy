<Step id="marinate-pork">
  Combine the <Qty ref="sour-orange-juice"/>, <Qty ref="garlic"/>,
  <Qty ref="cumin"/>, <Qty ref="oregano"/>, <Qty ref="olive-oil"/>,
  <Qty ref="salt"/> and <Qty ref="black-pepper"/>, rub over the
  <Qty ref="pork"/> and refrigerate for <Dur step="marinate-pork"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="roast-pork">
  Roast the pork with its marinade at <Temp c={160}/> for
  <Dur step="roast-pork"/>, until completely tender and cooked through.
</Step>

<Step id="rest-and-slice">
  Rest the pork, then slice it thinly. This takes about
  <Dur step="rest-and-slice"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="assemble">
  Split the <Qty ref="bread"/> lengthwise and spread the cut sides with the
  <Qty ref="mustard"/>. Layer the <Qty ref="ham"/>, sliced roast pork,
  <Qty ref="cheese"/> and <Qty ref="pickles"/> onto the bottom half and close
  each sandwich. This takes about <Dur step="assemble"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="press">
  Spread the outside of each sandwich with the <Qty ref="butter"/> and cook
  in a sandwich press or under a heavy pan over a medium heat for
  <Dur step="press"/>, until the bread is flattened and deeply golden and the
  cheese has melted.
</Step>

<Step id="boil-plantains">
  Put the <Qty ref="plantain"/> in a pot with the <Qty ref="boiling-water"/>
  and <Qty ref="salt"/> and bring to a boil, then cook for
  <Dur step="boil-plantains"/>, until completely tender. Reserve some of the
  cooking water, then drain.
</Step>

<Step id="saute-onions">
  Heat the <Qty ref="oil"/> in a pan over a medium heat and cook the
  <Qty ref="onion"/> for most of <Dur step="saute-onions"/>, until softened,
  then add the <Qty ref="vinegar"/> and simmer briefly until it reduces
  slightly.
</Step>

<Step id="mash">
  Mash the drained plantains with the <Qty ref="butter"/> and enough of the
  reserved cooking water to make a smooth, soft mash, working for about
  <Dur step="mash"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="top">
  Spread the mash onto plates and spoon the sautéed onions over the top;
  serve at once.
</Step>

<Step id="mix-dough">
  Combine the <Qty ref="flour-dough"/>, <Qty ref="water"/> and <Qty ref="dough-salt"/> in
  a bowl and bring together into a firm dough, then turn it out and knead for
  <Dur step="mix-dough"/> until smooth.
</Step>

<Step id="rest-dough">
  Cover the dough and rest it for <Dur step="rest-dough"/> at room temperature, until it
  has relaxed and no longer springs back when pressed.
</Step>

<Step id="make-filling">
  Meanwhile, combine the <Qty ref="beef"/>, <Qty ref="onion"/>, <Qty ref="garlic"/>,
  <Qty ref="ginger"/>, <Qty ref="spring-onion"/>, <Qty ref="coriander"/>,
  <Qty ref="cumin"/>, <Qty ref="black-pepper"/>, <Qty ref="sesame-oil"/> and
  <Qty ref="filling-salt"/> in a bowl and mix by hand until evenly combined and
  slightly sticky.
</Step>

<Step id="roll-and-fill">
  Roll the rested dough into a log and pinch off small, even pieces, keeping the rest
  covered. Roll each piece with the <Qty ref="flour-bench"/> underneath into a thin
  round about <Len cm={7}/> across, thicker at the centre than at the rim. Spoon
  filling into the middle of each round, then gather the edge up around it in fine
  pleats, twisting them together at the top to seal the pouch shut.
</Step>

<Step id="steam">
  Set the momo into a well-oiled or lined steamer basket, leaving space between each,
  and steam over rapidly boiling water for <Dur step="steam"/>, until the wrappers turn
  glossy and the filling is cooked through.
</Step>

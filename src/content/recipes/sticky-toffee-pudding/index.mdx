<Step id="soak-dates">
  Put the <Qty ref="dates"/> and the <Qty ref="bicarb"/> in a bowl, pour over the
  <Qty ref="water"/> and leave to stand for <Dur step="soak-dates"/>, until softened.
</Step>

<Step id="cream-butter">
  Beat the <Qty ref="sponge-butter"/> and the <Qty ref="sponge-sugar"/> together until
  pale and fluffy.
</Step>

<Step id="make-batter">
  Beat the <Qty ref="egg"/> and the <Qty ref="vanilla"/> into the creamed butter, then
  fold in the <Qty ref="flour"/> along with the soaked dates and their liquid until
  just combined. Pour into a lined baking dish.
</Step>

<Step id="bake">
  Bake at <Temp c={180}/> for <Dur step="bake"/>, until risen and a skewer pushed into
  the centre comes out clean.
</Step>

<Step id="make-sauce">
  Melt the <Qty ref="sauce-butter"/> with the <Qty ref="sauce-sugar"/> in a saucepan
  over a medium heat, then stir in the <Qty ref="sauce-cream"/> and simmer for
  <Dur step="make-sauce"/>, until glossy and thickened enough to coat the back of a
  spoon.
</Step>

<Step id="assemble">
  Cut the warm sponge into portions and pour the hot sauce generously over each before
  serving.
</Step>

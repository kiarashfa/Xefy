<Step id="fry-noodle-garnish">
  Heat the <Qty ref="oil-fry"/> in a small pan and fry a handful of the
  <Qty ref="noodles-fry"/>, uncooked and dry, until golden and crisp, for
  <Dur step="fry-noodle-garnish"/>. Drain and set aside for garnish.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-curry-base">
  Heat the <Qty ref="oil-cook"/> in a large pot and fry the <Qty ref="onion"/>,
  <Qty ref="garlic"/> and <Qty ref="ginger"/> until softened. Stir in the
  <Qty ref="turmeric"/>, <Qty ref="paprika"/> and <Qty ref="dried-chilli"/> and
  cook for <Dur step="cook-curry-base"/>, until fragrant. Add the
  <Qty ref="chicken"/> and turn to coat it in the spiced base.
</Step>

<Step id="simmer-broth">
  Whisk the <Qty ref="gram-flour"/> into the <Qty ref="coconut-milk"/> until
  smooth, then pour this and the <Qty ref="stock"/> into the pot with the
  <Qty ref="fine-sea-salt"/>. Bring to a gentle simmer and cook for
  <Dur step="simmer-broth"/>, stirring occasionally, until the chicken is fully
  tender and the broth has thickened.
</Step>

<Step id="boil-noodles">
  Boil the <Qty ref="noodles-boil"/> in plenty of water for
  <Dur step="boil-noodles"/>, until just tender, then drain.
</Step>

<Step id="assemble">
  Divide the noodles between bowls and ladle over the chicken and broth. Top
  each bowl with the <Qty ref="egg"/>, the fried noodle garnish, the
  <Qty ref="spring-onion"/> and <Qty ref="coriander"/>, and serve with the
  <Qty ref="lime-juice"/> alongside. This takes <Dur step="assemble"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="season">
  Season the <Qty ref="short-rib"/>, <Qty ref="picanha"/> and
  <Qty ref="sausage"/> all over with the <Qty ref="salt"/>. Thread each onto
  its own skewer, or leave the ribs whole to sit directly on the grate.
</Step>

<Step id="light-fire">
  Light a wood or charcoal fire and let it burn for <Dur step="light-fire"/>,
  until it has settled into a steady bed of glowing coals rather than open
  flame.
</Step>

<Step id="grill-ribs">
  Set the ribs over the fire, not too close to the coals, and cook for
  <Dur step="grill-ribs"/>, turning occasionally, until the outside is deeply
  browned and the meat pulls easily from the bone.
</Step>

<Step id="grill-picanha">
  Partway through, set the picanha over the fire fat side down and grill for
  <Dur step="grill-picanha"/>, turning once the fat has rendered and
  browned, until the meat is cooked through to a firm medium.
</Step>

<Step id="grill-sausage">
  Add the sausage to the fire and grill for <Dur step="grill-sausage"/>,
  turning occasionally, until the casing is deeply coloured and it is cooked
  through.
</Step>

<Step id="rest">
  Move everything to a board and rest for <Dur step="rest"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="serve">
  Slice the picanha thinly across the grain, cut the ribs apart along the
  bones and slice the sausage, then serve everything together.
</Step>

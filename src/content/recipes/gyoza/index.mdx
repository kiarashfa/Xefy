<Step id="mix-dough">
  Combine the <Qty ref="flour-dough"/>, <Qty ref="dough-water"/> and
  <Qty ref="dough-salt"/> in a bowl and bring together into a shaggy dough, then turn it
  out and knead for <Dur step="mix-dough"/> until smooth and firm.
</Step>

<Step id="rest-dough">
  Cover the dough and rest it for <Dur step="rest-dough"/> at room temperature, until it
  has relaxed and no longer springs back when pressed.
</Step>

<Step id="make-filling">
  Meanwhile, combine the <Qty ref="pork"/>, <Qty ref="cabbage"/>, <Qty ref="chives"/>,
  <Qty ref="garlic"/>, <Qty ref="ginger"/>, <Qty ref="sesame-oil"/>,
  <Qty ref="pepper"/>, <Qty ref="filling-soy"/> and <Qty ref="filling-salt"/> in a bowl
  and stir hard in one direction until the mixture stiffens and holds together.
</Step>

<Step id="make-dip">
  Stir the <Qty ref="dip-soy"/> and <Qty ref="rice-vinegar"/> together in a small dish.
</Step>

<Step id="roll-and-fill">
  Roll the rested dough into a log and pinch off small, even pieces, keeping the rest
  covered. Roll each piece with the <Qty ref="flour-bench"/> underneath into a thin
  round about <Len cm={7}/> across — thinner than a jiaozi wrapper. Spoon filling into
  the centre, fold in half and pleat the front edge only, leaving the back seam flat, so
  each dumpling sits low with a straight base.
</Step>

<Step id="sear">
  Heat the <Qty ref="frying-oil"/> in a wide, lidded pan over a high heat. Stand the
  dumplings in base down in a tight row, and sear for <Dur step="sear"/> until the bases
  are golden.
</Step>

<Step id="steam-fry">
  Pour in the <Qty ref="steam-water"/>, cover at once and let the dumplings steam-fry
  for <Dur step="steam-fry"/>, until the water has all but disappeared and the wrappers
  have turned glossy.
</Step>

<Step id="crisp">
  Uncover and cook for a further <Dur step="crisp"/>, letting the last of the water
  evaporate and the bases fuse into one crisp sheet. Loosen with a wide spatula and turn
  out in one piece, base up. Serve with the dipping sauce.
</Step>

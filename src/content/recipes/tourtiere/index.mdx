<Step id="make-pastry">
  Rub the <Qty ref="lard"/> and the <Qty ref="pastry-butter"/> into the
  <Qty ref="pastry-flour"/> and the <Qty ref="pastry-salt"/> until the mixture looks
  like coarse crumbs, then mix in the <Qty ref="ice-water"/> just until the dough comes
  together. Divide in two, one piece slightly larger, flatten into discs and wrap.
</Step>

<Step id="chill-pastry">
  Chill the pastry for <Dur step="chill-pastry"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-filling">
  Cook the <Qty ref="pork"/> and the <Qty ref="beef"/> in a wide pan over a medium
  heat, breaking them up, until no longer pink. Add the <Qty ref="onion"/>, the
  <Qty ref="garlic"/> and the <Qty ref="potato"/>, then the <Qty ref="stock"/>, the
  <Qty ref="cinnamon"/>, the <Qty ref="cloves"/>, the <Qty ref="nutmeg"/>, the
  <Qty ref="filling-salt"/> and the <Qty ref="pepper"/>. Simmer for
  <Dur step="cook-filling"/>, until the potato has broken down and the mixture holds
  together.
</Step>

<Step id="cool-filling">
  Spread the filling on a tray and leave to cool for <Dur step="cool-filling"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="assemble-pie">
  Roll out the larger disc of pastry and line a pie dish. Fill with the cooled
  filling, roll out the second disc for the lid, seal the edges and cut a small vent.
  Brush with the <Qty ref="egg-wash"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="bake">
  Bake at <Temp c={190}/> for <Dur step="bake"/>, until the crust is deep golden.
</Step>

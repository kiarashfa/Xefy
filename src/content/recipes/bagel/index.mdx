<Step id="mix">
  Put the <Qty ref="flour"/> and the <Qty ref="salt"/> into a large bowl, add the
  <Qty ref="yeast"/>, and stir them together. Add the <Qty ref="water-dough"/> and mix for
  <Dur step="mix"/>, until no dry flour remains and the dough is shaggy.
</Step>

<Step id="knead">
  Turn the dough onto a lightly floured bench and knead for <Dur step="knead"/>, until it is
  smooth, elastic and springs back when pressed. The dough is stiffer than a loaf dough, so it
  will take a little more effort.
</Step>

<Step id="first-rise">
  Put the dough in an oiled bowl, cover it, and leave it to rise for <Dur step="first-rise"/>,
  until it has doubled in size.
</Step>

<Step id="shape">
  Divide the dough into equal pieces and roll each one into a smooth ball. Roll each ball into
  a rope about <Len cm={25}/> long, then join the ends into a ring and pinch them firmly
  together. Set the rings on a floured cloth and cover them.
</Step>

<Step id="second-rise">
  Leave the rings to prove for <Dur step="second-rise"/>, until a fingertip pressed in leaves
  a dent that springs back slowly.
</Step>

<Step id="heat-oven">
  Heat the oven to <Temp c={230}/> with a shelf in the middle, and line a baking tray with
  baking paper.
</Step>

<Step id="heat-water">
  Bring the <Qty ref="water-boil"/> to the boil in a wide pan. Stir in the <Qty ref="malt"/>
  until it dissolves, then reduce the heat to a steady simmer.
</Step>

<Step id="boil">
  Lower the rings into the simmering water, a few at a time, and boil them for
  <Dur step="boil"/> in total, turning them once. Lift them out with a slotted spoon and let
  them drain.
</Step>

<Step id="top">
  Mix the <Qty ref="sesame"/> and the <Qty ref="poppy"/> on a plate. Press the top of each
  drained ring into the seeds, then set it on the lined tray.
</Step>

<Step id="bake">
  Bake for <Dur step="bake"/>, until deep golden and firm to the touch.
</Step>

<Step id="cool">
  Leave the bagels on a rack for <Dur step="cool"/> before cutting, so the crumb can set.
</Step>

<Step id="make-sauce">
  Warm the <Qty ref="sauce-oil"/> and cook the <Qty ref="garlic"/> briefly
  until fragrant, then add the <Qty ref="tomatoes"/> and <Qty ref="oregano"/>.
  Simmer for the remainder of <Dur step="make-sauce"/>, until slightly
  thickened, and season with half the <Qty ref="salt"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="bread-chicken">
  Season the <Qty ref="chicken"/> with the remaining salt. Dredge each piece
  in the <Qty ref="flour"/>, dip in the <Qty ref="egg"/>, then coat evenly in
  the <Qty ref="breadcrumbs"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="fry">
  Heat the <Qty ref="frying-oil"/> in a wide pan over a medium-high heat and
  fry the cutlets for <Dur step="fry"/>, turning once, until deep golden and
  cooked through. Drain on a rack.
</Step>

<Step id="assemble">
  Spoon a little of the sauce into a baking dish, lay the cutlets on top,
  cover with the remaining sauce, then the <Qty ref="mozzarella"/> and the
  <Qty ref="parmesan"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="bake">
  Bake at <Temp c={200}/> for <Dur step="bake"/>, until the cheese has melted
  and turned golden in patches. Scatter over the <Qty ref="basil"/> before
  serving.
</Step>

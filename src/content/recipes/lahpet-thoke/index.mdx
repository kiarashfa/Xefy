<Step id="fry-garlic">
  Heat the <Qty ref="peanut-oil"/> in a small pan and fry the <Qty ref="garlic"/>
  over a medium heat for <Dur step="fry-garlic"/>, until golden and crisp. Lift out
  with a slotted spoon, reserving the oil.
</Step>

<Step id="fry-beans">
  In the same oil, fry the <Qty ref="dried-broad-beans"/> for <Dur step="fry-beans"/>,
  until crisp and golden, then lift out and drain alongside the garlic.
</Step>

<Step id="prep-vegetables">
  Shred the <Qty ref="romaine-lettuce"/>, cut the <Qty ref="tomato"/> into wedges,
  and slice the <Qty ref="serrano-chilli"/>. This takes
  <Dur step="prep-vegetables"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="toss-salad">
  In a large bowl, combine the drained <Qty ref="fermented-tea-leaves"/>, fried
  garlic, fried beans, <Qty ref="peanut"/>, <Qty ref="dried-shrimp"/> and
  <Qty ref="sesame-seed"/> with the shredded lettuce, tomato and chilli. Add the
  <Qty ref="lime-juice"/>, <Qty ref="fish-sauce"/>, the reserved garlic oil and the
  <Qty ref="fine-sea-salt"/>, and toss everything together for
  <Dur step="toss-salad"/> just before serving.
</Step>

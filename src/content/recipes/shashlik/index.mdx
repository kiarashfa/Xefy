<Step id="cube">
  Trim the <Qty ref="lamb"/> of any excess fat and sinew, and cut it into even cubes.
</Step>

<Step id="marinate">
  Layer the cubed lamb with the <Qty ref="onion"/> in a non-reactive container, then add
  the <Qty ref="vinegar"/>, <Qty ref="oil"/>, <Qty ref="salt"/> and <Qty ref="pepper"/>
  and turn everything together so the meat is well coated. Cover and marinate,
  refrigerated, for <Dur step="marinate"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="thread">
  Lift the lamb from the marinade, shaking off the excess, and thread it tightly onto
  skewers, alternating with pieces of the marinated onion.
</Step>

<Step id="grill">
  Grill the skewers over fierce, direct charcoal heat, turning often, for
  <Dur step="grill"/>, until well charred outside and cooked through with a hint of pink
  remaining at the centre.
</Step>

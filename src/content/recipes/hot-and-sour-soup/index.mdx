<Step id="velvet-pork">
  Combine the <Qty ref="velvet-soy"/> and <Qty ref="velvet-cornflour"/> in a
  bowl and turn the <Qty ref="pork"/> through it, taking
  <Dur step="velvet-pork"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="simmer-base">
  Bring the <Qty ref="stock"/> to a simmer and add the pork, separating the
  strands as they go in. Add the <Qty ref="shiitake"/>, <Qty ref="wood-ear"/>,
  <Qty ref="bamboo-shoots"/> and <Qty ref="tofu"/> and simmer for
  <Dur step="simmer-base"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="season">
  Stir in the <Qty ref="black-vinegar"/>, <Qty ref="sauce-soy"/>,
  <Qty ref="white-pepper"/> and <Qty ref="sugar"/>, and simmer for
  <Dur step="season"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="thicken">
  Stir in the <Qty ref="thickener"/> mixture in stages, stirring constantly,
  until the soup thickens to a light coating consistency, over
  <Dur step="thicken"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="add-egg">
  Reduce the heat to a bare simmer and pour in the <Qty ref="egg"/> in a thin,
  slow stream, stirring gently, over <Dur step="add-egg"/>, so it sets into
  fine ribbons. Finish with the <Qty ref="sesame-oil"/> and
  <Qty ref="spring-onion"/>.
</Step>

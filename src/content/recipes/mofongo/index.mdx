<Step id="fry-pork">
  Heat the <Qty ref="frying-oil"/> in a deep, heavy pot and fry the
  <Qty ref="pork-belly"/> for <Dur step="fry-pork"/>, until deeply browned
  and crisp all over. Lift out with a slotted spoon and drain, leaving the
  oil in the pot.
</Step>

<Step id="fry-plantains">
  Fry the <Qty ref="plantain"/> in the same oil for <Dur step="fry-plantains"/>,
  turning occasionally, until golden outside and completely tender when
  pierced. Lift out and drain.
</Step>

<Step id="mash">
  While still hot, pound the fried plantain in a mortar with the
  <Qty ref="garlic"/>, <Qty ref="olive-oil"/> and <Qty ref="salt"/>, working
  in the crisp pork toward the end so it stays in coarse pieces rather than
  breaking down completely. Moisten with a little of the warm
  <Qty ref="stock"/> as needed to bring it together. This takes about
  <Dur step="mash"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="shape">
  Press the mash into mounds, hollowing the centre of each slightly, and
  serve at once with the remaining stock poured around or alongside. This
  takes about <Dur step="shape"/>.
</Step>

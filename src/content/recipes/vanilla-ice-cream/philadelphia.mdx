<Step id="mix-base">
  Whisk the <Qty ref="cream"/>, the <Qty ref="milk"/>, the <Qty ref="sugar"/> and the
  <Qty ref="vanilla"/> together until the sugar has dissolved, over <Dur step="mix-base"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="chill-base">
  Cover and chill the base for <Dur step="chill-base"/>, until thoroughly cold.
</Step>

<Step id="churn">
  Churn the chilled base in an ice cream machine for <Dur step="churn"/>, until it holds
  a soft, spoonable shape.
</Step>

<Step id="freeze">
  Transfer to a container and freeze for <Dur step="freeze"/>, until firm enough to
  scoop.
</Step>

<Step id="heat-cream">
  Warm the <Qty ref="milk"/> and the <Qty ref="cream"/> together with half of the
  <Qty ref="sugar"/> in a pan over a medium heat until steaming, over <Dur step="heat-cream"/>,
  without letting it boil.
</Step>

<Step id="temper-yolks">
  Whisk the <Qty ref="egg-yolks"/> with the remaining sugar until pale, then whisk in a
  ladleful of the hot cream at a time to bring the yolks up to temperature gradually,
  over <Dur step="temper-yolks"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-custard">
  Pour the tempered yolks back into the pan and cook over a low heat, stirring
  constantly, for <Dur step="cook-custard"/>, until the custard thickens enough to coat
  the back of a spoon. Strain immediately into a clean bowl and stir in the
  <Qty ref="vanilla"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="chill-custard">
  Cover and chill the custard for <Dur step="chill-custard"/>, until thoroughly cold.
</Step>

<Step id="churn">
  Churn the chilled custard in an ice cream machine for <Dur step="churn"/>, until it
  holds a soft, spoonable shape.
</Step>

<Step id="freeze">
  Transfer to a container and freeze for <Dur step="freeze"/>, until firm enough to
  scoop.
</Step>

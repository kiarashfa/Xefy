<Step id="heat-liquid">
  Bring the <Qty ref="milk"/>, <Qty ref="oil"/> and <Qty ref="salt"/> to a
  full boil in a saucepan.
</Step>

<Step id="scald-starch">
  Combine the <Qty ref="starch-sour"/> and <Qty ref="starch-sweet"/> in a
  large bowl. Pour over the hot liquid and stir until it forms a rough,
  sticky, partially cooked dough.
</Step>

<Step id="cool">
  Let the dough cool for <Dur step="cool"/>, until only warm to the touch.
</Step>

<Step id="add-eggs-cheese">
  Knead in the <Qty ref="eggs"/> and the <Qty ref="cheese"/> until the dough
  is smooth, glossy and elastic; it will look loose at first and come
  together as the eggs are absorbed.
</Step>

<Step id="shape">
  Roll the dough into small balls and space them on lined trays.
</Step>

<Step id="bake">
  Bake at <Temp c={180}/> for <Dur step="bake"/>, until puffed and golden,
  with a firm crust over a chewy centre.
</Step>

<Step id="mix">
  Put the <Qty ref="flour-dough"/> and the <Qty ref="salt"/> into a bowl. Add the
  <Qty ref="water"/> a little at a time, and mix for <Dur step="mix"/>, until a soft dough
  forms and no dry flour remains.
</Step>

<Step id="knead">
  Turn the dough onto a lightly floured bench and knead for <Dur step="knead"/>, until it is
  smooth and pliable.
</Step>

<Step id="rest">
  Cover the dough and leave it to rest for <Dur step="rest"/>, so that it relaxes and rolls
  out without shrinking.
</Step>

<Step id="shape">
  Divide the dough into equal pieces and roll each one into a smooth ball. Dust the bench with
  the <Qty ref="flour-bench"/>, and roll each ball into a thin round about <Len cm={18}/>
  across. Keep the rounds covered until they go on the pan.
</Step>

<Step id="heat-pan">
  Heat a heavy pan or tawa over a medium-high heat until it is very hot to the touch.
</Step>

<Step id="cook">
  Lay a round on the hot pan and cook it for <Dur step="cook"/> in total, turning it as soon
  as the underside blisters and browns in spots. Cook the other side the same way. Brush each
  roti with the <Qty ref="ghee"/> as it comes off the pan.
</Step>

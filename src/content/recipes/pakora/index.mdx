<Step id="slice-onion">
  Thinly slice the <Qty ref="onion"/> and set aside for <Dur step="slice-onion"/> to let
  it release a little of its own moisture.
</Step>

<Step id="mix-batter">
  Whisk the <Qty ref="gram-flour"/>, <Qty ref="turmeric"/>, <Qty ref="cumin"/>,
  <Qty ref="dried-chilli"/> and <Qty ref="salt"/> together, then work in the
  <Qty ref="water"/> to a thick, barely pourable batter. Fold in the sliced onion and the
  <Qty ref="coriander"/> for <Dur step="mix-batter"/>, coating the onion in as little
  batter as will hold it together.
</Step>

<Step id="fry">
  Heat the <Qty ref="frying-oil"/> in a deep pan to a steady, gentle bubble. Drop in
  loose, craggy spoonfuls of the onion mixture without crowding the pan and fry for
  <Dur step="fry"/>, turning occasionally, until deep golden-brown and crisp throughout.
  Lift out and drain.
</Step>

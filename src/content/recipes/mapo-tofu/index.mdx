<Step id="blanch-tofu">
  Blanch the <Qty ref="tofu"/> in a pot of gently simmering, lightly salted
  water for <Dur step="blanch-tofu"/>, then drain carefully and set aside.
</Step>

<Step id="brown-beef">
  Heat the <Qty ref="oil"/> in a wok over high heat and fry the
  <Qty ref="beef"/> for <Dur step="brown-beef"/>, breaking it up, until browned
  and starting to crisp at the edges.
</Step>

<Step id="fry-paste">
  Add the <Qty ref="doubanjiang"/>, <Qty ref="fermented-black-beans"/>,
  <Qty ref="garlic"/>, <Qty ref="ginger"/> and <Qty ref="dried-chilli"/> and fry
  for <Dur step="fry-paste"/>, until the oil turns red and fragrant.
</Step>

<Step id="simmer-tofu">
  Pour in the <Qty ref="stock"/> and <Qty ref="soy-sauce"/> and bring to a
  simmer. Slide in the blanched tofu, and simmer gently, shaking the pan
  rather than stirring, for <Dur step="simmer-tofu"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="thicken">
  Stir in the <Qty ref="cornflour"/> mixture in stages, folding gently, until
  the sauce clings to the tofu, about <Dur step="thicken"/>. Finish with the
  <Qty ref="sichuan-pepper"/> and <Qty ref="spring-onion"/>.
</Step>

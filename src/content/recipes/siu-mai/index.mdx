<Step id="make-dough">
  Combine the <Qty ref="flour"/>, <Qty ref="egg"/>, <Qty ref="water"/> and
  <Qty ref="dough-salt"/> in a bowl and bring together into a firm dough, then knead for
  <Dur step="make-dough"/> until smooth.
</Step>

<Step id="rest-dough">
  Cover the dough and rest it for <Dur step="rest-dough"/> at room temperature.
</Step>

<Step id="make-filling">
  Meanwhile, combine the <Qty ref="pork"/>, <Qty ref="shrimp"/>, <Qty ref="mushroom"/>,
  <Qty ref="bamboo-shoots"/>, <Qty ref="spring-onion"/>, <Qty ref="ginger"/>,
  <Qty ref="soy-sauce"/>, <Qty ref="sesame-oil"/>, <Qty ref="sugar"/>,
  <Qty ref="pepper"/>, <Qty ref="filling-salt"/> and <Qty ref="starch"/> in a bowl and stir
  hard in one direction until sticky and cohesive, keeping the shrimp in distinct pieces
  rather than mashing it in with the pork.
</Step>

<Step id="roll-and-fill">
  Roll the rested dough into a log and pinch off small, even pieces, keeping the rest
  covered. Roll each piece into a thin round. Cup a round in one hand, spoon filling into
  the centre, and gently press the wrapper up around the sides with your fingers so it
  pleats loosely into itself, leaving the filling showing at the top. Tap each one flat on
  its base so it stands upright.
</Step>

<Step id="steam">
  Line a steamer basket and set the dumplings in with space between each. Steam over
  rapidly boiling water for <Dur step="steam"/>, until the wrapper is glossy and the
  filling is cooked through and firm to the touch.
</Step>

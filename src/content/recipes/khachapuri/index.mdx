<Step id="mix">
  Put the <Qty ref="flour-dough"/> and the <Qty ref="salt"/> into a large bowl, and stir in the
  <Qty ref="yeast"/>. Add the <Qty ref="soured-cream"/> and the <Qty ref="milk"/>, and mix for
  <Dur step="mix"/>, until no dry flour remains and the dough is shaggy.
</Step>

<Step id="knead">
  Turn the dough onto a lightly floured bench and knead for <Dur step="knead"/>, until it is
  smooth, elastic and springs back when pressed.
</Step>

<Step id="first-rise">
  Put the dough in an oiled bowl, cover it, and leave it to rise for <Dur step="first-rise"/>,
  until it has doubled in size.
</Step>

<Step id="shape">
  Divide the dough into two equal pieces and roll each one into a ball. Dust the bench with the
  <Qty ref="flour-bench"/>, and roll each ball into a round about <Len cm={30}/> across. Mix the
  <Qty ref="imeruli"/> with the <Qty ref="sulguni"/>, and divide it between the two rounds,
  piling it in the middle. Gather the edge of the dough up over the cheese in accordion folds,
  pinch the top shut, and turn the piece over. Roll it gently into a round about
  <Len cm={25}/> across, keeping the cheese inside.
</Step>

<Step id="second-rise">
  Leave the khachapuri to prove for <Dur step="second-rise"/>, until a fingertip pressed in
  leaves a dent that springs back slowly.
</Step>

<Step id="heat-pan">
  Heat the <Qty ref="oil"/> in a heavy frying pan over a medium-high heat.
</Step>

<Step id="fry">
  Lay one khachapuri in the hot pan and fry it for <Dur step="fry"/> in total, turning it once
  when the underside is deep golden and the cheese has melted. Fry the second one the same way.
</Step>

<Step id="cool">
  Leave the khachapuri on a board for <Dur step="cool"/>, then cut each into four.
</Step>

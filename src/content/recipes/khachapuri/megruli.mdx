<Step id="mix">
  Put the <Qty ref="flour-dough"/>, the <Qty ref="sugar"/> and the <Qty ref="salt"/> into a
  large bowl, and stir in the <Qty ref="yeast"/>. Add the <Qty ref="milk"/> and the
  <Qty ref="egg-dough"/>, and mix for <Dur step="mix"/>, until no dry flour remains and the
  dough is shaggy.
</Step>

<Step id="knead">
  Turn the dough onto a lightly floured bench and knead for <Dur step="knead"/>, until it is
  smooth and flexible.
</Step>

<Step id="add-butter">
  Add the <Qty ref="butter-dough"/> a few pieces at a time, letting each one be absorbed before
  the next goes in. Keep kneading for <Dur step="add-butter"/>, until the dough is glossy,
  elastic and comes away from the bowl again.
</Step>

<Step id="first-rise">
  Put the dough in an oiled bowl, cover it, and leave it to rise for <Dur step="first-rise"/>,
  until it has doubled in size.
</Step>

<Step id="make-filling">
  Crumble the <Qty ref="imeruli"/> into a bowl. Mix in the <Qty ref="egg-filling"/> and the
  <Qty ref="butter-filling"/> until smooth, for <Dur step="make-filling"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="shape">
  Divide the dough into two equal pieces and roll each one into a ball. Dust the bench with the
  <Qty ref="flour-bench"/>, and flatten each ball into a round about <Len cm={25}/> across.
  Place half the filling in the middle of each round, gather the edges over it and pinch them
  shut firmly. Flatten each piece evenly into a round about <Len cm={25}/> across, and leave a
  small hole in the top for the steam to escape.
</Step>

<Step id="second-rise">
  Leave the khachapuri to prove for <Dur step="second-rise"/>, until a fingertip pressed in
  leaves a dent that springs back slowly.
</Step>

<Step id="heat-oven">
  Heat the oven to <Temp c={230}/> with a shelf in the lower middle.
</Step>

<Step id="top">
  Mix the <Qty ref="sulguni"/> with the <Qty ref="yolk"/> for <Dur step="top"/>, and spread it
  over each round, leaving the edge bare.
</Step>

<Step id="bake">
  Bake for <Dur step="bake"/>, until the edge is golden and the cheese on top has melted and
  browned in patches.
</Step>

<Step id="dab-butter">
  Put a dollop of the <Qty ref="butter-top"/> on each khachapuri as it comes out of the oven.
</Step>

<Step id="cool">
  Leave the khachapuri on a board for <Dur step="cool"/>, then cut each into four.
</Step>

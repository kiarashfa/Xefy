<Step id="mix">
  Put the <Qty ref="flour-dough"/> and the <Qty ref="salt"/> into a large bowl, and stir in the
  <Qty ref="yeast"/>. Add the <Qty ref="soured-cream"/> and the <Qty ref="milk"/>, and mix for
  <Dur step="mix"/>, until no dry flour remains and the dough is shaggy.
</Step>

<Step id="knead">
  Turn the dough onto a lightly floured bench and knead for <Dur step="knead"/>, until it is
  smooth, elastic and springs back when pressed.
</Step>

<Step id="first-rise">
  Put the dough in an oiled bowl, cover it, and leave it to rise for <Dur step="first-rise"/>,
  until it has doubled in size.
</Step>

<Step id="shape">
  Divide the dough into two equal pieces and roll each one into a ball. Dust the bench with the
  <Qty ref="flour-bench"/>, and roll each ball into an oval about <Len cm={35}/> long. Pinch the
  edges up into a boat with a thick rim, leaving the centre open. Mix the <Qty ref="imeruli"/>
  with the <Qty ref="sulguni"/>, and divide it between the two boats.
</Step>

<Step id="second-rise">
  Leave the boats to prove for <Dur step="second-rise"/>, until a fingertip pressed in leaves a
  dent that springs back slowly.
</Step>

<Step id="heat-oven">
  Heat the oven to <Temp c={230}/> with a shelf in the lower middle.
</Step>

<Step id="bake">
  Bake for <Dur step="bake"/>, until the rim is deep golden and the cheese has softened and
  begun to colour at the edges.
</Step>

<Step id="add-yolk">
  Take the boats out of the oven. Break the raw <Qty ref="yolk"/> into the hot cheese of each
  one, and stir it through the cheese for <Dur step="add-yolk"/> until it is glossy.
</Step>

<Step id="butter-top">
  Dab the <Qty ref="butter"/> over the crust of each boat, then cut each boat into four and serve
  at once.
</Step>

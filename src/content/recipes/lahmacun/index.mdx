<Step id="make-dough">
  Dissolve the <Qty ref="yeast"/> in the <Qty ref="water"/>, then work in the
  <Qty ref="flour"/> until no dry flour remains. Add the <Qty ref="dough-salt"/> and
  <Qty ref="dough-oil"/> and knead until smooth.
</Step>

<Step id="rest-dough">
  Cover the dough and leave it to rest at room temperature for <Dur step="rest-dough"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="heat-oven">
  Meanwhile bring the oven to its absolute maximum, with a stone or steel in place,
  and give it <Dur step="heat-oven"/> to saturate.
</Step>

<Step id="make-topping">
  Combine the <Qty ref="lamb"/>, <Qty ref="tomato"/>, <Qty ref="red-pepper"/>,
  <Qty ref="onion"/>, <Qty ref="garlic"/>, <Qty ref="parsley-topping"/>,
  <Qty ref="paprika"/>, <Qty ref="maras-pepper"/>, <Qty ref="cumin"/>,
  <Qty ref="pepper"/>, <Qty ref="topping-salt"/> and <Qty ref="topping-oil"/> to a
  loose, spreadable paste.
</Step>

<Step id="divide-shape">
  Divide the rested dough into even pieces and roll each out paper-thin into a round.
</Step>

<Step id="spread-topping">
  Spread the topping over each round in a thin, even layer, right to the edge.
</Step>

<Step id="bake">
  Bake on the stone for <Dur step="bake"/>, until the base is crisp and the topping
  has set and coloured at the edges.
</Step>

<Step id="serve">
  Scatter the <Qty ref="parsley-serving"/> over the hot lahmacun, roll each one up and
  serve with the <Qty ref="lemon-juice"/> for squeezing over.
</Step>

<Step id="soak">
  Soak the <Qty ref="beans"/>, <Qty ref="carne-seca"/> and
  <Qty ref="salt-pork"/> separately in plenty of cold water for
  <Dur step="soak"/>, changing the water for the meats at least once. Drain
  and rinse all three before cooking.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-beans">
  Put the beans, carne seca and salt pork into a large pot with the
  <Qty ref="water"/> and the <Qty ref="bay-leaf"/>. Bring to a simmer and
  cook for <Dur step="cook-beans"/>, partially covered, until the beans are
  fully tender and the meats fall apart easily.
</Step>

<Step id="saute-aromatics">
  While the pot simmers, melt the <Qty ref="lard"/> in a small pan and cook
  the <Qty ref="onion"/> and <Qty ref="garlic-beans"/> until soft and
  golden, then stir them into the pot.
</Step>

<Step id="add-sausage">
  Stir the <Qty ref="sausage"/> into the pot and simmer for
  <Dur step="add-sausage"/>, until the sausage is cooked through and the stew
  has thickened to a dark, glossy consistency.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-couve">
  Heat the <Qty ref="greens-oil"/> in a wide pan over a high heat and
  stir-fry the <Qty ref="greens"/> and <Qty ref="garlic-couve"/> for
  <Dur step="cook-couve"/>, just until wilted and still bright green.
</Step>

<Step id="serve">
  Serve the stew with the couve alongside and the <Qty ref="orange"/>
  slices on the side.
</Step>

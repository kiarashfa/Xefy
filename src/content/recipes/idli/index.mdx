<Step id="soak">
  Cover the <Qty ref="rice"/> and, in a separate bowl, the <Qty ref="urad-dal"/> and
  <Qty ref="fenugreek-seed"/> with the <Qty ref="soak-water"/>, dividing it between the two
  bowls. Leave both to soak at room temperature for <Dur step="soak"/>, then drain.
</Step>

<Step id="grind">
  Grind the drained dal and fenugreek first, adding the <Qty ref="grind-water"/> a little at
  a time, until the paste is pale, light and roughly doubled in volume. Grind the drained
  rice separately to a slightly grainy paste, then fold the two together with the
  <Qty ref="salt"/> in a large bowl, leaving plenty of headroom for it to rise.
</Step>

<Step id="ferment">
  Cover the bowl loosely and leave it somewhere warm to ferment for <Dur step="ferment"/>,
  until the batter has visibly risen and smells faintly sour and yeasty.
</Step>

<Step id="steam">
  Brush the idli moulds with the <Qty ref="mould-oil"/> and spoon in the batter without
  stirring it down. Steam over boiling water for <Dur step="steam"/>, until a skewer pushed
  into a cake comes out clean. Rest for a minute before unmoulding.
</Step>

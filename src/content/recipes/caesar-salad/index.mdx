<Step id="croutons">
  Warm the <Qty ref="oil-croutons"/> in a wide pan over a medium heat. Put in the
  <Qty ref="bread"/> with the <Qty ref="salt"/> and turn the pieces until they have
  taken up the oil evenly. Fry for <Dur step="croutons"/>, moving them often, until
  they are deep gold and dry through to the middle rather than only at the edges.
  Tip them out onto paper and leave them to crisp as they cool.
</Step>

<Step id="dressing">
  While the croutons colour, pound the <Qty ref="anchovy"/> and the
  <Qty ref="garlic"/> to a smooth paste in a mortar, or chop them together and work
  them under the flat of a knife. Scrape the paste into a wide bowl and whisk in the
  <Qty ref="egg-yolk"/>, the <Qty ref="lemon"/> and the <Qty ref="worcestershire"/>.
  Add the <Qty ref="oil-dressing"/> a few drops at a time to begin with, whisking
  constantly, then in a thin steady stream once the mixture has thickened and taken
  on a pale gloss. Whisk in the <Qty ref="parmigiano-dressing"/> and the
  <Qty ref="pepper"/>. It takes <Dur step="dressing"/> in all, and the dressing
  should end up thick enough to hold a line drawn through it.
</Step>

<Step id="toss">
  Put the <Qty ref="romaine"/> into the widest bowl available and spoon the dressing
  over it. Turn the leaves through it with both hands for <Dur step="toss"/>, until
  every surface is coated and nothing has pooled at the bottom. Add the croutons,
  turn them through once, and scatter over the <Qty ref="parmigiano-finish"/>. Serve
  straight away.
</Step>

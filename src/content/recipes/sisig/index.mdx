<Step id="boil">
  Combine the <Qty ref="pig-ears"/>, <Qty ref="pork-liver"/>, <Qty ref="water"/> and
  <Qty ref="bay-leaf"/> in a pot, bring to the boil, then lower the heat and simmer for
  <Dur step="boil"/>, until both are fully tender. Drain and pat dry.
</Step>

<Step id="grill">
  Grill the ears over a high heat for <Dur step="grill"/>, turning, until charred at the
  edges and crisp on the outside.
</Step>

<Step id="chop">
  Finely chop the grilled ears and the boiled liver for <Dur step="chop"/>, until the
  pieces are small and even.
</Step>

<Step id="season">
  Heat the <Qty ref="oil"/> in a pan, preferably one that can go straight to the table,
  and toss in the chopped mixture with the <Qty ref="onion"/>, <Qty ref="chilli"/>,
  <Qty ref="vinegar"/>, <Qty ref="calamansi"/>, <Qty ref="salt"/> and <Qty ref="pepper"/>.
  Cook for <Dur step="season"/>, stirring, until piping hot.
</Step>

<Step id="top-egg">
  Crack the <Qty ref="egg"/> over the top while the pan is still hot and stir it through
  for <Dur step="top-egg"/>, letting the residual heat just cook it into the mixture.
</Step>

<Step id="soak-potato">
  Rinse the cut <Qty ref="potato"/> and soak it in cold water for <Dur step="soak-potato"/>,
  which draws out surface starch and helps the fries crisp rather than stick together.
  Drain and pat completely dry before frying.
</Step>

<Step id="heat-oil-low">
  Heat the <Qty ref="frying-oil"/> in a deep, heavy pot to <Temp c={130}/>, allowing
  <Dur step="heat-oil-low"/> for it to come to temperature.
</Step>

<Step id="first-fry">
  Fry the potato in batches at this lower temperature for <Dur step="first-fry"/>,
  until softened through but still pale, then lift out and drain.
</Step>

<Step id="rest-fries">
  Spread the par-fried batons out and let them cool and dry for <Dur step="rest-fries"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="heat-oil-high">
  Raise the oil to <Temp c={190}/>, allowing <Dur step="heat-oil-high"/> to reach the
  higher temperature.
</Step>

<Step id="second-fry">
  Fry the rested batons again, in batches, for <Dur step="second-fry"/>, until deep
  golden and crisp. Drain well and season immediately with the <Qty ref="salt"/> while
  still hot.
</Step>

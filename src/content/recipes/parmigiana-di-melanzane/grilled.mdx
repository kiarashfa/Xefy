# and charred on a hot pan or under a grill, so no flour and no frying oil are needed.

    - stepId: grill-aubergine

    - stepId: grill-aubergine

<Step id="draw-juice">
  Lay the <Qty ref="aubergine"/> slices in a colander in layers, sprinkling each with the
  <Qty ref="salt-aubergine"/>. Leave them to drain for <Dur step="draw-juice"/>, until
  beads of bitter liquid have come to the surface.
</Step>

<Step id="make-sauce">
  Warm the <Qty ref="oil-sauce"/> in a wide pan, add the <Qty ref="passata"/> and the
  <Qty ref="salt-sauce"/>, and simmer gently for <Dur step="make-sauce"/>, until the sauce
  has thickened and lost its raw edge. Tear in the <Qty ref="basil"/> at the end.
</Step>

<Step id="rinse-dry">
  Rinse the aubergine slices under cold water and press them dry in a clean cloth.
</Step>

<Step id="grill-aubergine">
  Brush the slices on both sides with the <Qty ref="oil-grill"/>. Grill them on a very hot
  ridged pan or under a hot grill, turning once, for <Dur step="grill-aubergine"/>, until
  marked and tender right through.
</Step>

<Step id="assemble">
  Spread a spoonful of the sauce in a deep dish. Lay a layer of the grilled aubergine, then
  scatter over some of the <Qty ref="mozzarella"/> and the <Qty ref="parmigiano"/>, and spoon
  over a little sauce. Repeat until the aubergine is used, and finish with the sauce and the
  cheese.
</Step>

<Step id="bake">
  Bake at <Temp c={180}/> for <Dur step="bake"/>, until the sauce bubbles at the edges and the
  cheese has melted and browned in patches.
</Step>

<Step id="rest-parmigiana">
  Leave the dish to rest for <Dur step="rest-parmigiana"/> before cutting, so that the layers
  hold together when it is served.
</Step>

<Step id="combine">
  Whisk the <Qty ref="milk"/>, <Qty ref="sugar"/>, <Qty ref="bicarbonate"/>
  and <Qty ref="salt"/> together in a wide, heavy-based pot until the sugar
  and bicarbonate are fully dissolved.
</Step>

<Step id="reduce">
  Set the pot over a low heat and simmer gently for <Dur step="reduce"/>,
  stirring every few minutes and scraping the base, until the milk has
  reduced by roughly half and turned a pale gold.
</Step>

<Step id="thicken">
  Continue simmering for <Dur step="thicken"/>, whisking constantly now,
  until the mixture has turned a deep caramel-brown and coats the back of a
  spoon thickly. It will bubble and rise as it thickens, so keep the heat low
  and stay at the pan for this stretch, when it is most likely to catch.
</Step>

<Step id="finish">
  Take the pot off the heat and stir in the <Qty ref="vanilla"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="cool">
  Pour into a jar and let it cool fully, uncovered, for <Dur step="cool"/>
  before sealing — it will continue to thicken as it cools.
</Step>

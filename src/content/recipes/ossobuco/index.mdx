<Step id="dust-veal">
  Season the <Qty ref="veal"/> with the <Qty ref="salt"/> and dust each piece lightly with
  the <Qty ref="flour"/>, shaking off the excess. Keep the string on each shank so it
  holds its shape as it cooks.
</Step>

<Step id="chop-vegetables">
  Finely chop the <Qty ref="onion"/>, the <Qty ref="carrot"/> and the <Qty ref="celery"/>.
  Crush the <Qty ref="garlic-braise"/> with the flat of a knife. Keep the
  <Qty ref="garlic-gremolata"/> apart for the gremolata.
</Step>

<Step id="brown-veal">
  Heat the <Qty ref="olive-oil"/> and the <Qty ref="butter-browning"/> in a heavy casserole
  over a moderately high flame until the butter foams. Brown the shanks on their cut faces
  and round sides, turning them with tongs, for <Dur step="brown-veal"/>, until deeply
  coloured. Lift them out onto a plate.
</Step>

<Step id="soffritto">
  Lower the heat, add the vegetables and the crushed garlic, and cook gently for
  <Dur step="soffritto"/>, until soft and sweet but not coloured. Pour in the
  <Qty ref="white-wine"/> and let it bubble, scraping the base of the pan, until it has
  reduced by about half.
</Step>

<Step id="braise">
  Pour in the <Qty ref="stock"/>, add the <Qty ref="bay-leaf"/> and bring to a simmer. Return
  the shanks to the casserole standing upright, with the liquid coming only part way up
  the meat. Cover tightly and braise in an oven at <Temp c={160}/> for
  <Dur step="braise"/>, turning the shanks once, until the meat is tender and comes away
  from the bone.
</Step>

<Step id="gremolata">
  Chop the <Qty ref="lemon"/>, the <Qty ref="parsley"/> and the
  <Qty ref="garlic-gremolata"/> together on the board until fine, and keep the mixture
  cold until it is needed.
</Step>

<Step id="reduce-sauce">
  Lift the shanks out and keep them warm. Skim the fat from the liquid, set the pan over a
  brisk flame and reduce it for <Dur step="reduce-sauce"/>, until it coats the back of a
  spoon. Whisk in the <Qty ref="butter-sauce"/>, season with the <Qty ref="pepper"/>, and
  serve each shank with its sauce, scattered with the gremolata.
</Step>

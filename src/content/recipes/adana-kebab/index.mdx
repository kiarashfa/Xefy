<Step id="knead">
  Combine the <Qty ref="lamb"/>, <Qty ref="onion"/>, <Qty ref="garlic"/>,
  <Qty ref="maras-pepper"/> and <Qty ref="salt"/> in a wide bowl. Knead and squeeze the
  mixture by hand for <Dur step="knead"/>, until it turns sticky and dense and holds
  together in a mass rather than falling apart in loose grains.
</Step>

<Step id="chill">
  Cover the mince and chill it for <Dur step="chill"/>, so the fat firms and the mixture
  is easier to press onto the skewers.
</Step>

<Step id="shape">
  Divide the mince into even portions. With wet hands, mould each portion around a
  broad, flat metal skewer, pressing and smoothing it into a long ribbon so it grips the
  blade and will not slip or spin. Work for <Dur step="shape"/> in total.
</Step>

<Step id="grill">
  Grill the skewers over fierce, direct charcoal heat, turning them often, for
  <Dur step="grill"/>, until the outside is well charred in places and the meat is cooked
  through with no pink remaining at the centre.
</Step>

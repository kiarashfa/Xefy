<Step id="start-grits">
  Bring the <Qty ref="grits-water"/> to the boil in a heavy saucepan. Whisk in the
  <Qty ref="grits"/> in a steady stream, whisking constantly to stop it clumping.
</Step>

<Step id="simmer-grits">
  Reduce the heat to the lowest setting and cook for <Dur step="simmer-grits"/>,
  stirring every few minutes, until the grits are thick, creamy and no longer gritty
  on the tongue.
</Step>

<Step id="prep-shrimp">
  While the grits cook, dice the <Qty ref="sausage"/>, mince the <Qty ref="garlic"/> and
  thinly slice the <Qty ref="spring-onion"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="enrich-grits">
  Off the heat, stir the <Qty ref="butter-grits"/>, <Qty ref="cheddar"/> and
  <Qty ref="salt"/> into the grits until melted and smooth. Cover and keep warm.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-andouille">
  Heat a large skillet over a medium-high heat and cook the sausage for
  <Dur step="cook-andouille"/>, until browned and much of its fat has rendered.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-shrimp">
  Add the <Qty ref="shrimp"/> and garlic to the skillet and cook for
  <Dur step="cook-shrimp"/>, turning once, until the shrimp are pink and just cooked
  through. Add the <Qty ref="sauce-water"/>, <Qty ref="lemon-juice"/>,
  <Qty ref="butter-sauce"/>, <Qty ref="cayenne"/> and <Qty ref="black-pepper"/>, and
  swirl the pan until the butter melts into a light sauce. Spoon the shrimp, sausage
  and sauce over the grits and scatter with the spring onion.
</Step>

<Step id="soak-beans">
  Soak the <Qty ref="black-beans"/> in plenty of cold water for
  <Dur step="soak-beans"/>, then drain.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-beans">
  Simmer the soaked beans in the <Qty ref="water"/> for
  <Dur step="cook-beans"/>, until tender, topping up with water as needed to
  keep them covered. Do not drain.
</Step>

<Step id="sofrito">
  Heat the <Qty ref="olive-oil"/> in a wide pot over a medium heat and cook
  the <Qty ref="onion"/>, <Qty ref="green-pepper"/> and <Qty ref="garlic"/>
  for <Dur step="sofrito"/>, until softened.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-rice">
  Add the cooked beans and their liquid to the sofrito along with the
  <Qty ref="cumin"/>, <Qty ref="oregano"/>, <Qty ref="bay-leaf"/>,
  <Qty ref="salt"/> and <Qty ref="black-pepper"/>, and bring to a boil. Stir
  in the <Qty ref="rice"/>, cover, reduce the heat to its lowest setting and
  cook undisturbed for <Dur step="cook-rice"/>, until the liquid is absorbed
  and the rice is tender.
</Step>

<Step id="rest">
  Take the pot off the heat and leave it covered for <Dur step="rest"/>
  before fluffing with a fork and removing the bay leaf.
</Step>

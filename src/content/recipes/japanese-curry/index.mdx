<Step id="prepare-vegetables">
  Cut the <Qty ref="carrot"/> and the <Qty ref="potato"/> into chunks, slice the
  <Qty ref="onion"/> finely, and grate the <Qty ref="garlic"/> and the <Qty ref="ginger"/>.
  Pat the <Qty ref="pork"/> dry. This takes <Dur step="prepare-vegetables"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-rice">
  Rinse the <Qty ref="rice"/> in several changes of cold water until the water runs almost
  clear. Cook it in a heavy pan with a tight lid, and leave it covered after the boil so
  that it steams for the last part of the <Dur step="cook-rice"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="brown-pork">
  Heat the <Qty ref="oil"/> in a heavy pot over a high heat. Brown the <Qty ref="pork"/> in
  batches for <Dur step="brown-pork"/>, so that the pieces colour rather than steam, and
  lift each batch out onto a plate.
</Step>

<Step id="soften-onion">
  Lower the heat, add the <Qty ref="onion"/>, the <Qty ref="garlic"/> and the
  <Qty ref="ginger"/> to the fat left in the pot, and cook them for
  <Dur step="soften-onion"/>, stirring often, until the onion is soft and deep gold.
</Step>

<Step id="simmer-curry">
  Return the pork to the pot with any juices, add the <Qty ref="carrot"/> and the
  <Qty ref="potato"/>, pour in the <Qty ref="water"/> and bring it to the boil. Skim off the
  scum, cover, and simmer gently for <Dur step="simmer-curry"/>, until the pork and the
  potatoes are tender.
</Step>

<Step id="make-roux">
  While the stew simmers, melt the <Qty ref="butter"/> in a small pan over a low heat. Stir
  in the <Qty ref="roux-flour"/> and the <Qty ref="curry-powder"/> and cook them together
  for <Dur step="make-roux"/>, stirring, until the paste smells toasted and has darkened
  slightly. Take it off the heat.
</Step>

<Step id="thicken-curry">
  Whisk the roux into a ladle of the hot stew until smooth, then stir it back into the pot.
  Add the <Qty ref="ketchup"/>, the <Qty ref="worcestershire"/> and the <Qty ref="salt"/>,
  and simmer for <Dur step="thicken-curry"/> more, until the sauce coats the back of a
  spoon. Serve the curry over the rice.
</Step>

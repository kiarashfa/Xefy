<Step id="soak">
  Cover the <Qty ref="rice"/> and, in a separate bowl, the <Qty ref="urad-dal"/> and
  <Qty ref="fenugreek-seed"/> with the <Qty ref="soak-water"/>, dividing it between the
  two bowls. Leave both to soak at room temperature for <Dur step="soak"/>, then drain.
</Step>

<Step id="grind">
  Grind the drained dal and fenugreek to a smooth, light paste. Grind the drained rice
  separately to a fine paste, using the <Qty ref="grind-water"/> as needed, then combine
  the two with the <Qty ref="batter-salt"/> into a batter with the consistency of thin
  double cream.
</Step>

<Step id="ferment">
  Cover the bowl loosely and leave it somewhere warm to ferment for <Dur step="ferment"/>,
  until risen and faintly sour-smelling. Stir in any remaining <Qty ref="grind-water"/>
  afterwards to bring it to a thin, pourable batter.
</Step>

<Step id="boil-potatoes">
  Put the <Qty ref="potato"/> into the <Qty ref="boil-water"/> and bring to a simmer.
  Cook for <Dur step="boil-potatoes"/>, until completely tender, then drain and roughly
  crush.
</Step>

<Step id="temper">
  Meanwhile, warm the <Qty ref="temper-ghee"/> in a separate pan. Add the
  <Qty ref="mustard-seed"/> and let it pop, then add the <Qty ref="curry-leaves"/>,
  <Qty ref="turmeric"/> and <Qty ref="chilli"/> and fry briefly before stirring in the
  <Qty ref="onion"/>. Cook for <Dur step="temper"/>, until the onion has softened.
</Step>

<Step id="make-chutney">
  Blend the softened <Qty ref="dried-chilli"/>, the <Qty ref="garlic"/>, the
  <Qty ref="tamarind"/> and the <Qty ref="chutney-salt"/> with the
  <Qty ref="chutney-water"/> to a smooth, thick paste. Warm the <Qty ref="chutney-oil"/>
  in a small pan and fry the paste for most of <Dur step="make-chutney"/>, until it
  darkens slightly and smells cooked rather than raw.
</Step>

<Step id="combine-filling">
  Fold the crushed potato and the <Qty ref="filling-salt"/> into the tempered onion and
  cook for <Dur step="combine-filling"/>, mashing lightly so the mixture holds together
  loosely.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-dosas">
  Heat a flat griddle and wipe it with a little of the <Qty ref="cook-ghee"/>. Pour on a
  ladleful of batter and spread it outward from the centre in one continuous spiral into
  a thin, even round. Drizzle a little more of the <Qty ref="cook-ghee"/> around the edge
  and cook for most of <Dur step="cook-dosas"/>, until the underside is golden and
  lacy and the top has dried out — dosa is cooked on one side only. Spread the red
  chutney thinly over the cooked surface, spoon a line of potato filling across the
  centre, and fold the dosa over it. Repeat with the remaining batter, chutney and
  filling.
</Step>

<Step id="mix">
  Put the <Qty ref="flour"/>, the <Qty ref="salt"/> and the <Qty ref="yeast"/> into a large
  bowl and stir them together. Add the <Qty ref="water"/> and the <Qty ref="oil-dough"/>, and
  mix for <Dur step="mix"/>, until no dry flour remains and the dough is shaggy.
</Step>

<Step id="knead">
  Turn the dough onto a lightly floured bench and knead for <Dur step="knead"/>, until it is
  smooth, elastic and springs back when pressed.
</Step>

<Step id="first-rise">
  Put the dough in an oiled bowl, cover it, and leave it to rise for <Dur step="first-rise"/>,
  until it has doubled in size.
</Step>

<Step id="shape">
  Pour the <Qty ref="oil-tin"/> into a large shallow tin and spread it over the base and sides.
  Lift the dough into the tin and press it out with your fingers until it fills the tin and
  lies evenly across the base.
</Step>

<Step id="second-rise">
  Leave the shaped dough to prove for <Dur step="second-rise"/>, until a fingertip pressed in
  leaves a dent that springs back slowly.
</Step>

<Step id="heat-oven">
  Heat the oven to <Temp c={230}/> with a shelf in the lower middle.
</Step>

<Step id="top">
  Press your fingers deep into the dough all over, making dimples that will hold the oil. Pour
  the <Qty ref="oil-top"/> over the surface, letting it collect in the dimples, and scatter the
  <Qty ref="flaky-salt"/> over the top.
</Step>

<Step id="bake">
  Bake for <Dur step="bake"/>, until golden on top and crisp on the base, and the base sounds
  hollow when tapped.
</Step>

<Step id="cool">
  Leave the focaccia in the tin for <Dur step="cool"/>, then lift it out and cut it into
  squares.
</Step>

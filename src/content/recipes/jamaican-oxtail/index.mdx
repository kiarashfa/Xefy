<Step id="season">
  Season the <Qty ref="oxtail"/> with the <Qty ref="salt"/> and
  <Qty ref="black-pepper"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="marinate">
  Cover and refrigerate for <Dur step="marinate"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="brown-sugar-step">
  Heat the <Qty ref="vegetable-oil"/> in a heavy pot over a medium-high heat
  and stir in the <Qty ref="brown-sugar"/>, cooking for
  <Dur step="brown-sugar-step"/> until it darkens and turns syrupy.
</Step>

<Step id="sear">
  Add the oxtail to the pot in batches and brown on all sides for
  <Dur step="sear"/> in total, turning to coat each piece in the caramelised
  sugar.
</Step>

<Step id="aromatics">
  Return all the oxtail to the pot with the <Qty ref="onion"/>,
  <Qty ref="garlic"/>, <Qty ref="ginger"/>, <Qty ref="scotch-bonnet"/>,
  <Qty ref="allspice"/>, <Qty ref="thyme"/> and <Qty ref="spring-onion"/>,
  and cook for <Dur step="aromatics"/>, stirring, until fragrant.
</Step>

<Step id="braise">
  Pour in the <Qty ref="soy-sauce"/> and <Qty ref="stock"/>, add the
  drained <Qty ref="broad-beans"/>, and bring to a simmer. Cover and cook at a
  bare simmer for <Dur step="braise"/>, until the meat is tender enough to
  fall from the bone.
</Step>

<Step id="reduce">
  Stir in the <Qty ref="carrot"/> and simmer uncovered for
  <Dur step="reduce"/>, until the carrot is tender and the sauce has thickened
  to coat the back of a spoon. Discard the whole scotch bonnet before serving.
</Step>

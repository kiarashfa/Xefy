<Step id="brown-beef">
  Heat the <Qty ref="oil"/> in a heavy pot and brown the <Qty ref="beef"/> over
  a high heat, breaking it into small pieces, for <Dur step="brown-beef"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="saute-aromatics">
  Add the <Qty ref="onion"/> and <Qty ref="garlic"/> and cook for
  <Dur step="saute-aromatics"/>, until the onion has softened and turned
  translucent.
</Step>

<Step id="bloom-spices">
  Stir in the <Qty ref="ancho"/>, <Qty ref="cumin"/> and <Qty ref="paprika"/>
  and cook for <Dur step="bloom-spices"/>, until the spices smell toasted.
</Step>

<Step id="simmer">
  Add the <Qty ref="kidney-beans"/>, <Qty ref="tomato"/>, <Qty ref="bouillon"/>,
  <Qty ref="water"/> and <Qty ref="salt"/>. Bring to a full rolling boil for a
  few minutes, then reduce to a simmer and cook uncovered for
  <Dur step="simmer"/>, stirring occasionally and topping up with water if it
  gets too thick, until the beans are fully tender.
</Step>

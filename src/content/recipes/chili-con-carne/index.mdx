<Step id="toast-chillies">
  Toast the <Qty ref="ancho"/> and <Qty ref="guajillo"/> in a dry pan over a
  medium heat for <Dur step="toast-chillies"/>, pressing them flat and turning
  often, until they are fragrant and slightly blistered.
</Step>

<Step id="soak-chillies">
  Cover the toasted chillies with the <Qty ref="water"/>, brought to a boil,
  and leave them to soften for <Dur step="soak-chillies"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="blend-chillies">
  Blend the soaked chillies with their soaking liquid, the <Qty ref="garlic"/>
  and the <Qty ref="cumin"/> into a smooth, thick paste.
</Step>

<Step id="brown-beef">
  Heat the <Qty ref="oil"/> in a heavy pot and brown the <Qty ref="beef"/> hard
  over a high heat, breaking it into small pieces, for
  <Dur step="brown-beef"/>. Add the <Qty ref="onion"/> and cook until it has
  softened.
</Step>

<Step id="simmer">
  Stir in the chilli paste, the <Qty ref="bouillon"/>, the <Qty ref="beer"/>
  and the <Qty ref="salt"/>, bring to a boil, then reduce to a bare simmer and
  cook uncovered for <Dur step="simmer"/>, stirring occasionally, until the
  fat has separated to the surface and the sauce has thickened enough to coat
  the back of a spoon.
</Step>

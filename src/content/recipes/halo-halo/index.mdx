<Step id="sweeten-beans">
  Drain the soaked <Qty ref="kidney-beans"/>, cover with the <Qty ref="bean-water"/> and
  simmer for most of <Dur step="sweeten-beans"/>, until tender. Drain again, return to
  the pan with the <Qty ref="sugar-beans"/> and a splash of water, and simmer for the
  remaining few minutes until syrupy.
</Step>

<Step id="make-ube-halaya">
  Meanwhile, cover the <Qty ref="ube"/> with the <Qty ref="ube-water"/> and simmer for
  most of <Dur step="make-ube-halaya"/>, until fork-tender. Drain, mash while hot with
  the <Qty ref="sugar-ube"/> and <Qty ref="condensed-ube"/>, and continue cooking
  briefly over a low heat, stirring, until thick and glossy.
</Step>

<Step id="make-banana-syrup">
  Simmer the <Qty ref="banana"/> with the <Qty ref="sugar-banana"/> and
  <Qty ref="banana-water"/> for <Dur step="make-banana-syrup"/>, until the banana has
  softened and the liquid has thickened into a light syrup.
</Step>

<Step id="assemble">
  Divide the sweetened beans, banana syrup and ube halaya between glasses. Mound shaved
  ice on top and pour over the <Qty ref="evaporated-milk"/> and
  <Qty ref="condensed-topping"/>. Stir together at the table before eating.
</Step>

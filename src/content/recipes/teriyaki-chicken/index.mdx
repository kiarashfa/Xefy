<Step id="prep-chicken">
  Pat the <Qty ref="chicken"/> dry with kitchen paper and make a few shallow cuts across the
  thickest part of each fillet so that it cooks evenly. This takes <Dur step="prep-chicken"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="make-sauce">
  Put the <Qty ref="sake"/>, the <Qty ref="mirin"/> and the <Qty ref="sugar"/> into a small pan
  and bring them to the boil, then let the alcohol cook off for a moment. Add the
  <Qty ref="soy"/> and keep it at a simmer. This takes <Dur step="make-sauce"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-chicken">
  Heat the <Qty ref="oil"/> in a wide frying pan over a medium heat. Lay in the chicken and fry
  it for <Dur step="cook-chicken"/>, turning it every few minutes, until it is golden on both
  sides and cooked through at the centre.
</Step>

<Step id="glaze-chicken">
  Pour off any fat from the pan and add the sauce. Let it bubble and reduce around the chicken for
  <Dur step="glaze-chicken"/>, turning the fillets so that each one is coated, until the sauce is
  thick and glossy and clings to the meat.
</Step>

<Step id="rest-chicken">
  Lift the chicken onto a board and leave it for <Dur step="rest-chicken"/>. Slice it across the
  grain, arrange the slices on the plate and spoon over any sauce left in the pan. Finish with the
  <Qty ref="spring-onion"/>.
</Step>

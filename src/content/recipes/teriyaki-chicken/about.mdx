Teriyaki is a cooking method before it is a dish. Nippon.com defines it as pan-broiling or
frying food with a glaze so as to give it a gloss, and gives the mirin in the glaze as the
source of that gloss <Cite ref="nippon-teriyaki"/>. Just One Cookbook reads the word as *teri*,
luster, and *yaki*, grilled, and says that teriyaki first appeared as a cooking method in
Japan in the 1600s, a date the article states without a source <Cite ref="jtc-teriyaki"/>.

The word now covers both the cooked food and the sauce. Just One Cookbook notes that outside
Japan the food cooked teriyaki-style and the sauce itself are both called teriyaki
<Cite ref="jtc-teriyaki"/>. That is why the same name appears on a bottle of glaze and on a
plate of chicken, and this recipe makes the sauce from scratch and uses it for the glaze.

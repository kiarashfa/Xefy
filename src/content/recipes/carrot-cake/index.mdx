<Step id="mix-dry">
  Whisk together the <Qty ref="flour"/>, the <Qty ref="baking-powder"/>, the
  <Qty ref="bicarb"/>, the <Qty ref="cinnamon"/>, the <Qty ref="nutmeg"/> and the
  <Qty ref="salt"/>. This takes <Dur step="mix-dry"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="heat-oven">
  Meanwhile heat the oven to <Temp c={175}/>, giving it <Dur step="heat-oven"/> to settle,
  and butter and line two round cake tins.
</Step>

<Step id="whisk-wet">
  Whisk the <Qty ref="sugar"/> and the <Qty ref="brown-sugar"/> into the <Qty ref="oil"/>,
  then whisk in the <Qty ref="eggs"/> and the <Qty ref="vanilla"/>, over
  <Dur step="whisk-wet"/>, until smooth and glossy.
</Step>

<Step id="combine-batter">
  Fold the dry ingredients into the wet ingredients, then fold through the
  <Qty ref="carrot"/> and the <Qty ref="pecans-batter"/>, using <Dur step="combine-batter"/>
  and stopping as soon as no dry flour is visible.
</Step>

<Step id="bake">
  Divide the batter between the tins and bake for <Dur step="bake"/>, until a skewer pushed
  into the centre of each comes out clean and the tops spring back when pressed lightly.
</Step>

<Step id="cool">
  Turn the layers out onto a rack and leave them to cool completely, for
  <Dur step="cool"/>, before frosting.
</Step>

<Step id="make-frosting">
  Meanwhile beat the <Qty ref="cream-cheese"/> with the <Qty ref="frosting-butter"/> until
  smooth, then beat in the <Qty ref="icing-sugar"/> and the <Qty ref="frosting-vanilla"/>
  over <Dur step="make-frosting"/>, stopping as soon as the frosting is thick and holds its
  shape.
</Step>

<Step id="frost">
  Set one cooled layer on a stand and spread a third of the frosting over the top. Set the
  second layer on top and cover the whole cake with the remaining frosting, using
  <Dur step="frost"/> to keep the sides even. Scatter the <Qty ref="pecans-garnish"/> over
  the top to finish.
</Step>

notes:
  techniqueRationale:
    - stepId: simmer-broth
      text: The chicken is simmered gently rather than boiled, so that the fat and collagen emulsify into a clean, pale broth. A hard boil breaks the fat up into a cloudy broth that tastes greasier.
    - stepId: make-tare
      text: The shoyu tare is kept separate and is put into each bowl before the broth goes in. The broth is the same for every bowl, so the seasoning, not the stock, sets the flavour of each one.
  commonPitfalls:
    - stepId: strain-broth
      text: Pressing the bones and skin in the sieve pushes cloudiness and bitter fat into the broth. The solids are left to drain on their own.

<Step id="simmer-broth">
  Put the <Qty ref="chicken"/>, the <Qty ref="water"/>, the <Qty ref="ginger"/> and the
  <Qty ref="garlic"/> into a large pot and bring it up to a bare simmer. Skim off the scum,
  then lower the heat so that the surface only trembles, and leave it for
  <Dur step="simmer-broth"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="braise-chashu">
  Put the <Qty ref="pork"/> into a pan with the <Qty ref="soy-braise"/>, the
  <Qty ref="mirin-braise"/> and the <Qty ref="sake"/>, and add enough water to cover it
  halfway. Cover and simmer gently for <Dur step="braise-chashu"/>, turning once, until the
  pork is tender. Leave it to cool in its liquid.
</Step>

<Step id="boil-eggs">
  Lower the <Qty ref="egg"/> into boiling water and cook them for <Dur step="boil-eggs"/>,
  then cool them under cold water and peel them.
</Step>

<Step id="marinate-eggs">
  Put the peeled eggs into a bag with a little of the chashu braising liquid and leave them
  for <Dur step="marinate-eggs"/>, so that they take on colour and seasoning.
</Step>

<Step id="make-tare">
  Put the <Qty ref="soy-tare"/> and the <Qty ref="mirin-tare"/> into a small pan and warm them
  through for <Dur step="make-tare"/>. Divide the tare between two bowls.
</Step>

<Step id="strain-broth">
  Strain the broth through a fine sieve into a clean pan, letting it drain without pressing,
  and keep it hot at a low simmer. This takes <Dur step="strain-broth"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-noodles">
  Cook the <Qty ref="noodles"/> in a large pan of boiling water for <Dur step="cook-noodles"/>,
  until they are just tender, then drain them well.
</Step>

<Step id="assemble-bowls">
  Pour the hot broth over the tare in each bowl and stir to combine. Add the drained noodles,
  then the sliced chashu, the halved marinated eggs, the <Qty ref="menma"/>, the
  <Qty ref="spring-onion"/> and the <Qty ref="nori"/>, and serve at once.
</Step>

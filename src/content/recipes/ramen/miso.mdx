<Step id="simmer-broth">
  Put the <Qty ref="chicken"/>, the <Qty ref="water"/> and the <Qty ref="ginger"/> into a large
  pot and bring it up to a bare simmer. Skim off the scum, then lower the heat so that the
  surface only trembles, and leave it for <Dur step="simmer-broth"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="braise-chashu">
  Put the <Qty ref="pork"/> into a pan with the <Qty ref="soy"/>, the <Qty ref="mirin-braise"/>
  and the <Qty ref="sake-braise"/>, and add enough water to cover it halfway. Cover and simmer
  gently for <Dur step="braise-chashu"/>, turning once, until the pork is tender. Leave it to
  cool in its liquid.
</Step>

<Step id="boil-eggs">
  Lower the <Qty ref="egg"/> into boiling water and cook them for <Dur step="boil-eggs"/>, then
  cool them under cold water and peel them.
</Step>

<Step id="marinate-eggs">
  Put the peeled eggs into a bag with a little of the chashu braising liquid and leave them for
  <Dur step="marinate-eggs"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="fry-garlic">
  Warm the <Qty ref="sesame-oil"/> in a small pan over a low heat, add the <Qty ref="garlic"/> and
  fry it gently for <Dur step="fry-garlic"/>, until it is pale gold and smells sweet. Do not let it
  colour.
</Step>

<Step id="make-tare">
  Take the pan off the heat. Put the <Qty ref="miso"/> into a ladle, dip the ladle into the hot
  broth and work the paste apart until it has gone. Stir it into the garlic oil with the
  <Qty ref="mirin-tare"/> and the <Qty ref="sake-tare"/>, and divide it between two bowls.
  This takes <Dur step="make-tare"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="strain-broth">
  Strain the broth through a fine sieve into a clean pan, letting it drain without pressing, and
  keep it hot at a low simmer. This takes <Dur step="strain-broth"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-noodles">
  Cook the <Qty ref="noodles"/> in a large pan of boiling water for <Dur step="cook-noodles"/>,
  until just tender, then drain them well.
</Step>

<Step id="assemble-bowls">
  Ladle the hot broth over the tare in each bowl and stir until smooth. Add the drained noodles,
  then the sliced chashu, the halved marinated eggs, the <Qty ref="menma"/>, the
  <Qty ref="spring-onion"/> and the <Qty ref="nori"/>, and serve at once.
</Step>

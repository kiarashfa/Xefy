<Step id="simmer-broth">
  Put the <Qty ref="chicken"/>, the <Qty ref="water"/> and the <Qty ref="ginger"/> into a large
  pot and bring it up to a bare simmer. Skim off the scum, then lower the heat so that the
  surface only trembles, and leave it for <Dur step="simmer-broth"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="braise-chashu">
  Put the <Qty ref="pork"/> into a pan with the <Qty ref="soy"/>, the <Qty ref="mirin"/> and the
  <Qty ref="sake-braise"/>, and add enough water to cover it halfway. Cover and simmer gently for
  <Dur step="braise-chashu"/>, turning once, until the pork is tender. Leave it to cool in its
  liquid.
</Step>

<Step id="boil-eggs">
  Lower the <Qty ref="egg"/> into boiling water and cook them for <Dur step="boil-eggs"/>, then
  cool them under cold water and peel them.
</Step>

<Step id="marinate-eggs">
  Put the peeled eggs into a bag with a little of the chashu braising liquid and leave them for
  <Dur step="marinate-eggs"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="make-tare">
  Warm the <Qty ref="salt"/> and the <Qty ref="sake-tare"/> together in a small pan until the
  salt has dissolved, for <Dur step="make-tare"/>. Divide the tare between two bowls.
</Step>

<Step id="strain-broth">
  Strain the broth through a fine sieve into a clean pan, letting it drain without pressing, and
  keep it hot at a low simmer. This takes <Dur step="strain-broth"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-noodles">
  Cook the <Qty ref="noodles"/> in a large pan of boiling water for <Dur step="cook-noodles"/>,
  until just tender, then drain them well.
</Step>

<Step id="assemble-bowls">
  Pour the hot broth over the tare in each bowl and stir. Add the drained noodles, then the sliced
  chashu, the halved marinated eggs, the <Qty ref="menma"/>, the <Qty ref="spring-onion"/> and the
  <Qty ref="nori"/>, and serve at once.
</Step>

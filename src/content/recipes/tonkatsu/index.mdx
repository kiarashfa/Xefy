<Step id="prep-pork">
  Season the <Qty ref="pork"/> on both sides with the <Qty ref="salt"/> and the
  <Qty ref="pepper"/>. Trim any sinew from the edges and make a few cuts through the fat
  so that the chops do not curl in the oil. Lay each chop between sheets of cling film and
  beat it lightly to an even thickness. This takes <Dur step="prep-pork"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="shred-cabbage">
  Shred the <Qty ref="cabbage"/> very finely and leave it in a bowl of cold water for
  <Dur step="shred-cabbage"/>, so that it crisps. Drain it well and pat it dry before it
  goes on the plate.
</Step>

<Step id="heat-oil">
  Heat the <Qty ref="oil"/> in a deep, heavy pan to <Temp c={170}/>, checking it with a
  thermometer. It takes <Dur step="heat-oil"/> to come up to temperature and then holds
  steady on a moderate flame.
</Step>

<Step id="coat-pork">
  Set out three shallow dishes with the <Qty ref="flour"/>, the <Qty ref="egg"/> and the
  <Qty ref="panko"/>. Dust each cutlet in flour and shake off the excess, dip it in the egg,
  then press it into the panko on both sides so the crumb covers it evenly. This takes
  <Dur step="coat-pork"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="fry-cutlets">
  Lower the cutlets into the oil, which should stay at <Temp c={170}/>, and fry them for
  <Dur step="fry-cutlets"/>, turning once, until the crumb is deep gold and the pork is
  cooked through at its centre.
</Step>

<Step id="rest-cutlets">
  Lift the cutlets out and drain them on a wire rack for <Dur step="rest-cutlets"/>. Do not
  stack them, because the crust softens where they touch.
</Step>

<Step id="serve-tonkatsu">
  Slice each cutlet across into strips and fan them on the plate beside the cabbage. Serve
  the <Qty ref="tonkatsu-sauce"/> alongside in a small dish, to be spooned over.
</Step>

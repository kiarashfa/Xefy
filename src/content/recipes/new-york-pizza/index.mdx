<Step id="mix-dough">
  Dissolve the <Qty ref="yeast"/> in the <Qty ref="water"/>, then work in the
  <Qty ref="flour-dough"/> and the <Qty ref="oil-dough"/> until a shaggy dough
  forms. Add the <Qty ref="salt"/> and mix until no dry flour remains.
</Step>

<Step id="knead">
  Knead for <Dur step="knead"/>, until smooth and elastic.
</Step>

<Step id="cold-ferment">
  Cover and refrigerate for <Dur step="cold-ferment"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="divide">
  Turn the dough out and divide it into two equal pieces, shaping each into a
  tight ball.
</Step>

<Step id="rest-dough">
  Let the balls come back to room temperature over <Dur step="rest-dough"/>,
  covered, until soft and relaxed.
</Step>

<Step id="heat-oven">
  Meanwhile heat the oven as high as it will go, with a stone or steel in
  place, for <Dur step="heat-oven"/> to saturate fully.
</Step>

<Step id="stretch">
  Using the <Qty ref="flour-bench"/> as needed, stretch each ball by hand from
  the centre outward into a wide, thin round, leaving a slightly thicker rim.
</Step>

<Step id="top">
  Crush the <Qty ref="tomatoes"/> and spread a thin layer over each round,
  scatter with the <Qty ref="oregano"/>, cover with the <Qty ref="mozzarella"/>
  and finish with the <Qty ref="oil-finish"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="bake">
  Bake at <Temp c={260}/> for <Dur step="bake"/>, until the crust is well
  browned with charred spots and the cheese is fully melted and beginning to
  colour.
</Step>

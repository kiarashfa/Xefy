<Step id="scramble-egg">
  Beat the <Qty ref="egg"/> with the <Qty ref="egg-salt"/>. Heat the
  <Qty ref="egg-oil"/> in a wok over high heat and pour in the egg, stirring
  briefly for <Dur step="scramble-egg"/> to large, soft curds, just set. Lift
  out and set aside.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-tomato">
  Heat the <Qty ref="tomato-oil"/> in the same wok and add the
  <Qty ref="tomato"/>. Cook for <Dur step="cook-tomato"/>, pressing gently,
  until the wedges soften and slump into their own juice.
</Step>

<Step id="combine">
  Stir in the <Qty ref="sugar"/> and <Qty ref="sauce-salt"/>, then return the
  scrambled egg to the wok and fold through gently for <Dur step="combine"/>,
  until just combined. Finish with the <Qty ref="spring-onion"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="prep">
  Score the fat cap of the <Qty ref="picanha"/> in a shallow crosshatch,
  without cutting into the meat beneath it, and curve the piece into a loose
  crescent. Season all over with the <Qty ref="salt"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="light-fire">
  Light a wood or charcoal fire and let it burn for <Dur step="light-fire"/>,
  until it has settled into a steady bed of glowing coals.
</Step>

<Step id="sear-fat">
  Set the picanha fat side down over the fire and cook for
  <Dur step="sear-fat"/>, until the fat has rendered and turned deeply
  browned and crisp.
</Step>

<Step id="finish-grill">
  Turn the picanha and continue grilling for <Dur step="finish-grill"/>,
  turning as needed, until it reaches a firm medium throughout.
</Step>

<Step id="rest">
  Move the picanha to a board and rest for <Dur step="rest"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="slice">
  Slice thinly across the grain and serve at once.
</Step>

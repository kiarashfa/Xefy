<Step id="scrub-mussels">
  Scrub the <Qty ref="mussels"/> under cold running water, pull away the beards, and tap any
  that are open against the board. Discard any that stay open or are cracked. This takes
  <Dur step="scrub-mussels"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="soften-shallot">
  Melt the <Qty ref="butter-shallot"/> in a large, deep pan with a lid over a medium heat. Add the
  <Qty ref="shallot"/> and cook for <Dur step="soften-shallot"/>, until soft and translucent
  but not coloured.
</Step>

<Step id="steam-mussels">
  Pour in the <Qty ref="wine"/>, bring it to a boil, and add the mussels. Cover and steam over a
  high heat for <Dur step="steam-mussels"/>, shaking the pan now and then, until the shells have
  opened. Lift the mussels out into a warm bowl as they open.
</Step>

<Step id="reduce-liquor">
  Pour the liquor from the pan through a fine sieve to remove any grit, and return it to the pan.
  Boil it hard for <Dur step="reduce-liquor"/>, until it has reduced by about half. Take the pan
  off the heat and whisk in the <Qty ref="butter-finish"/>. Stir in the <Qty ref="parsley"/> and
  the <Qty ref="pepper"/>, return the mussels, and toss them in the sauce before serving.
</Step>

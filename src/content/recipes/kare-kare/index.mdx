<Step id="simmer-oxtail">
  Combine the <Qty ref="oxtail"/>, <Qty ref="water"/> and <Qty ref="onion"/> in a large
  pot, bring to the boil, skim, then lower the heat and simmer for
  <Dur step="simmer-oxtail"/>, until the meat is fork-tender. Lift out the oxtail and
  reserve the broth.
</Step>

<Step id="make-sauce">
  Meanwhile, heat the <Qty ref="oil-sauce"/> in a separate pan and cook the
  <Qty ref="garlic-broth"/> until fragrant. Stir in the <Qty ref="achiote"/> and cook
  briefly to colour the oil, then add the <Qty ref="peanut"/> and <Qty ref="rice"/> and
  enough of the reserved broth to loosen into a thick, pourable sauce, stirring for
  <Dur step="make-sauce"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="combine">
  Return the oxtail to the pot with the sauce, adding more reserved broth as needed, and
  simmer gently for <Dur step="combine"/>, until the sauce coats the meat thickly.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-vegetables">
  Add the <Qty ref="aubergine"/> and <Qty ref="long-beans"/> and simmer for
  <Dur step="cook-vegetables"/>, then stir in the <Qty ref="bok-choy"/> for the final
  minute, just until it wilts.
</Step>

<Step id="bagoong">
  Separately, heat the <Qty ref="oil-bagoong"/> and cook the <Qty ref="garlic-bagoong"/>
  until golden. Add the <Qty ref="shrimp-paste"/> and cook for <Dur step="bagoong"/>,
  stirring, until fragrant. Serve alongside the stew as a condiment.
</Step>

<Step id="render-bacon">
  Cook the <Qty ref="bacon"/> in a wide, dry pan over a medium heat for
  <Dur step="render-bacon"/>, until crisp, then lift out and set aside,
  leaving the fat in the pan.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-aromatics">
  Add the <Qty ref="butter"/> to the pan and cook the <Qty ref="onion"/> and
  <Qty ref="garlic"/> for <Dur step="cook-aromatics"/>, until soft and
  golden.
</Step>

<Step id="toast-flour">
  Return the bacon to the pan and stir in the <Qty ref="cassava-flour"/>,
  <Qty ref="salt"/> and <Qty ref="pepper"/>. Toast, stirring constantly, for
  <Dur step="toast-flour"/>, until the flour is evenly golden and smells
  toasted rather than raw.
</Step>

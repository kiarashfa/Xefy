<Step id="make-pastry">
  Rub the <Qty ref="lard"/> and the <Qty ref="pastry-butter"/> into the
  <Qty ref="pastry-flour"/> and the <Qty ref="pastry-salt"/> until the mixture looks
  like coarse crumbs, then mix in the <Qty ref="ice-water"/> just until the dough
  comes together.
</Step>

<Step id="chill-pastry">
  Chill the pastry for <Dur step="chill-pastry"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="line-tins">
  Roll the pastry thin and cut rounds to line the cups of a muffin tin, pressing them
  in gently.
</Step>

<Step id="make-filling">
  Whisk the <Qty ref="filling-butter"/>, the <Qty ref="brown-sugar"/>, the
  <Qty ref="corn-syrup"/>, the <Qty ref="egg"/> and the <Qty ref="vanilla"/> together
  until smooth.
</Step>

<Step id="fill-tarts">
  Scatter a few <Qty ref="raisins"/> into each pastry shell and pour over the filling,
  leaving a small gap below the rim.
</Step>

<Step id="bake">
  Bake at <Temp c={200}/> for <Dur step="bake"/>, until the pastry is golden and the
  filling has puffed at the edges but still wobbles gently in the centre.
</Step>

<Step id="cool">
  Cool in the tin for <Dur step="cool"/> before lifting out.
</Step>

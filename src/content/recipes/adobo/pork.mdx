<Step id="brown">
  Heat the <Qty ref="oil"/> in a wide, heavy pot over a medium-high heat and brown the
  <Qty ref="pork"/> on all sides, in batches if needed, for <Dur step="brown"/>. Lift out
  and set aside.
</Step>

<Step id="aromatics">
  Add the <Qty ref="garlic"/> and <Qty ref="onion"/> to the same pot and cook for
  <Dur step="aromatics"/>, stirring, until softened and just turning gold.
</Step>

<Step id="add-liquid">
  Return the pork to the pot and pour in the <Qty ref="soy-sauce"/>,
  <Qty ref="vinegar"/>, <Qty ref="water"/>, <Qty ref="bay-leaf"/> and <Qty ref="pepper"/>.
  Do not stir; let the pot come up to a simmer undisturbed for <Dur step="add-liquid"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="simmer-covered">
  Cover and simmer for <Dur step="simmer-covered"/>, until the pork is fork-tender.
</Step>

<Step id="reduce">
  Uncover and simmer for a further <Dur step="reduce"/>, turning the pork occasionally,
  until the sauce has reduced and thickened enough to coat the pieces and the fat has
  begun to render into it.
</Step>

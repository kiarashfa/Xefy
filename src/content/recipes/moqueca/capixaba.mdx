<Step id="marinate">
  Toss the <Qty ref="fish"/> with the <Qty ref="lime-juice"/>,
  <Qty ref="fish-salt"/> and <Qty ref="garlic"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="rest-fish">
  Set the fish aside to cure for <Dur step="rest-fish"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="build-base">
  While the fish rests, warm the <Qty ref="olive-oil"/> in a clay pot or wide
  shallow pan and stir in the <Qty ref="achiote"/> until it colours the oil
  deep red. Add the <Qty ref="onion"/> and <Qty ref="tomato"/> and cook for
  <Dur step="build-base"/>, until softened.
</Step>

<Step id="simmer">
  Lay the marinated fish over the vegetables, pour in the <Qty ref="water"/>
  and season with the <Qty ref="finishing-salt"/>. Simmer gently, without
  stirring, for <Dur step="simmer"/>, until the fish is just cooked through.
</Step>

<Step id="finish">
  Scatter over the <Qty ref="coriander"/> and serve at once, in the pot it
  cooked in.
</Step>

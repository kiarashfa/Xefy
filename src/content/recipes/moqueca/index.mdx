<Step id="marinate">
  Toss the <Qty ref="fish"/> with the <Qty ref="lime-juice"/>,
  <Qty ref="fish-salt"/> and <Qty ref="garlic"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="rest-fish">
  Set the fish aside to cure for <Dur step="rest-fish"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="build-base">
  While the fish rests, layer the <Qty ref="onion"/>, <Qty ref="tomato"/>,
  <Qty ref="red-pepper"/> and <Qty ref="chilli"/> in a wide, shallow pan and
  pour over the <Qty ref="palm-oil"/>. Cook over a medium heat for
  <Dur step="build-base"/>, until the vegetables have softened at the edges.
</Step>

<Step id="simmer">
  Lay the marinated fish over the vegetables, pour in the
  <Qty ref="coconut-milk"/> and season with the <Qty ref="finishing-salt"/>.
  Simmer gently, without stirring, for <Dur step="simmer"/>, until the fish
  is just cooked through and the sauce has taken on the oil's deep colour.
</Step>

<Step id="finish">
  Scatter over the <Qty ref="coriander"/> and serve at once.
</Step>

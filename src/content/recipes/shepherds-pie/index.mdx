<Step id="boil-potatoes">
  Cover the <Qty ref="potato"/> with cold salted water, bring to the boil and cook for
  <Dur step="boil-potatoes"/>, until completely tender.
</Step>

<Step id="brown-lamb">
  Brown the <Qty ref="lamb"/> hard in a dry pan over a high heat for
  <Dur step="brown-lamb"/>, breaking it up as it cooks, then set aside.
</Step>

<Step id="soften-vegetables">
  Melt the <Qty ref="saute-butter"/> in the same pan and cook the <Qty ref="onion"/>,
  the <Qty ref="carrot"/> and the <Qty ref="celery"/> for <Dur step="soften-vegetables"/>,
  until softened, then stir in the <Qty ref="garlic"/> and the <Qty ref="flour"/> and
  cook briefly.
</Step>

<Step id="simmer-filling">
  Return the lamb to the pan with the <Qty ref="thyme"/>, the <Qty ref="tomato-puree"/>,
  the <Qty ref="worcestershire"/>, the <Qty ref="stock"/>, the <Qty ref="salt-filling"/>
  and the <Qty ref="pepper-filling"/>. Simmer for <Dur step="simmer-filling"/>, until
  thickened and no longer watery, then spread into a baking dish and leave to cool
  slightly.
</Step>

<Step id="mash-potatoes">
  Drain the potatoes and mash with the <Qty ref="mash-butter"/>, the
  <Qty ref="mash-milk"/>, the <Qty ref="salt-mash"/> and the <Qty ref="pepper-mash"/>
  until smooth.
</Step>

<Step id="assemble">
  Spread the mash evenly over the filling, right to the edges of the dish, and rough up
  the surface with a fork.
</Step>

<Step id="bake">
  Bake at <Temp c={200}/> for <Dur step="bake"/>, until the top is golden and the
  filling bubbles up at the edges.
</Step>

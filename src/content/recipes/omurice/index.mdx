<Step id="prep-filling">
  Season the <Qty ref="chicken"/> with the <Qty ref="salt"/>, and have the
  <Qty ref="onion"/> finely chopped beside it. This takes <Dur step="prep-filling"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-rice">
  Rinse the <Qty ref="rice"/> in several changes of cold water until the water runs almost
  clear. Cook it in a heavy pan with a tight lid, and leave it covered after the boil for
  the last part of the <Dur step="cook-rice"/>. Spread it on a tray to cool a little.
</Step>

<Step id="beat-eggs">
  Beat the <Qty ref="egg"/> with the <Qty ref="pepper"/> in a bowl until the whites and yolks
  are just combined. Do not whisk it to a foam. This takes <Dur step="beat-eggs"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="fry-rice">
  Heat the <Qty ref="oil"/> in a wide pan over a medium-high heat. Fry the chicken until it
  has lost its pink, then add the onion and cook until it is soft. Tip in the cooked rice,
  add the <Qty ref="ketchup-rice"/> and the <Qty ref="butter"/>, and stir for
  <Dur step="fry-rice"/>, until every grain is coated and glossy. Tip it out onto a plate.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-omelettes">
  Divide the beaten egg into two equal parts. Heat a small non-stick pan over a moderate
  heat with a little butter. Pour in one part and stir with chopsticks until it is half set,
  then tip the pan and let it settle into an oval. Spoon half of the fried rice down the
  centre, fold the omelette over it, and roll it onto a plate. Repeat for the second
  omelette. This takes <Dur step="cook-omelettes"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="plate-omurice">
  Spread the <Qty ref="ketchup-top"/> over the top of each omelette in a line, and serve at
  once while the egg is still soft.
</Step>

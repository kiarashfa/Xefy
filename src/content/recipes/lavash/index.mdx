<Step id="mix-dough">
  Stir the <Qty ref="yeast"/> into the warm <Qty ref="water"/> and leave it for a few
  minutes until foamy. Combine the <Qty ref="flour"/> and <Qty ref="fine-sea-salt"/>
  in a large bowl, pour in the yeasted water, and bring it together into a rough
  dough. This takes <Dur step="mix-dough"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="knead">
  Turn the dough out and knead for <Dur step="knead"/>, until smooth, elastic and no
  longer sticky.
</Step>

<Step id="prove">
  Cover the dough and leave it to prove somewhere warm for <Dur step="prove"/>, until
  roughly doubled.
</Step>

<Step id="divide-and-rest">
  Knock the dough back and divide it into equal pieces, shaping each into a ball.
  Cover and let them rest for <Dur step="divide-and-rest"/>, so the gluten relaxes
  before rolling.
</Step>

<Step id="roll-and-bake">
  Heat a baking stone or steel on the floor of the oven to its highest setting.
  Roll each ball out as thin as possible, close to translucent, and lift it
  carefully onto the hot stone. Bake until puffed and lightly spotted with brown,
  then remove immediately. Repeat with the remaining dough, spending
  <Dur step="roll-and-bake"/> in total, and stack the sheets to cool under a cloth.
</Step>

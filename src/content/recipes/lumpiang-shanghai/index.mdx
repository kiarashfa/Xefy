<Step id="mix-filling">
  Combine the <Qty ref="pork"/>, <Qty ref="carrot"/>, <Qty ref="onion"/>,
  <Qty ref="garlic"/>, <Qty ref="spring-onion"/>, <Qty ref="soy-sauce"/>,
  <Qty ref="salt"/> and <Qty ref="pepper"/> in a bowl and mix for
  <Dur step="mix-filling"/>, until evenly combined.
</Step>

<Step id="roll">
  Lay out the <Qty ref="wrappers"/> and spoon a line of filling along one edge of each.
  Roll tightly into a thin cylinder, sealing the final edge with the
  <Qty ref="egg-white"/>. Repeat for <Dur step="roll"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="fry">
  Heat the <Qty ref="frying-oil"/> to a steady deep-frying temperature and fry the rolls
  in batches for <Dur step="fry"/>, turning, until golden and crisp all over. Drain on
  paper towels before serving.
</Step>

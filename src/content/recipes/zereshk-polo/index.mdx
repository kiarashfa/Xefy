<Step id="braise-chicken">
  Season the <Qty ref="chicken"/> with the <Qty ref="turmeric"/>,
  <Qty ref="black-pepper"/> and <Qty ref="salt-chicken"/>. Melt the
  <Qty ref="butter-chicken"/> in a wide pan over a medium heat, brown the chicken on
  both sides, then add the <Qty ref="onion"/> and the <Qty ref="lemon-juice"/>. Cover
  and cook over a low heat for <Dur step="braise-chicken"/>, until the chicken is
  fully tender and the onion has collapsed into the sauce.
</Step>

<Step id="rinse-rice">
  Rinse the <Qty ref="rice"/> in several changes of cold water, swirling and
  draining each time, for <Dur step="rinse-rice"/>, until the water runs clear.
</Step>

<Step id="soak-rice">
  Cover the rinsed rice with the <Qty ref="boil-water"/> and the
  <Qty ref="salt-rice"/> and leave it to soak for <Dur step="soak-rice"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="bloom-saffron">
  Grind the <Qty ref="saffron"/> and stir it into the hot <Qty ref="saffron-water"/>,
  setting it aside to steep for <Dur step="bloom-saffron"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="glaze-barberries">
  Melt the <Qty ref="butter-barberries"/> in a small pan, add the
  <Qty ref="barberries"/> and the <Qty ref="caster-sugar"/>, and cook over a moderate
  heat, stirring constantly, for <Dur step="glaze-barberries"/>, until glossy and just
  softened. Take off the heat immediately.
</Step>

<Step id="parboil-rice">
  Bring the rice in its soaking water to the boil and cook for
  <Dur step="parboil-rice"/>, until the grains have softened at the edge but are
  still firm at the core. Drain in a fine sieve.
</Step>

<Step id="steam-rice">
  Melt the <Qty ref="butter-rice"/> in the base of a heavy, non-stick pot. Mound the
  drained rice over it, drizzle with the bloomed <Qty ref="saffron-water"/>, cover
  the lid with a clean cloth and steam over a low heat for <Dur step="steam-rice"/>,
  until the rice is tender and separate.
</Step>

<Step id="assemble">
  Spoon the rice onto a platter, folding through most of the glazed
  <Qty ref="barberries"/>. Arrange the braised chicken alongside, spoon over its
  sauce, and scatter the remaining barberries over the top. This takes
  <Dur step="assemble"/>.
</Step>

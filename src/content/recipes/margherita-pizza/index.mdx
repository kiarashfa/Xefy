<Step id="mix-dough">
  Dissolve the <Qty ref="yeast"/> in the <Qty ref="water"/>, then work in the
  <Qty ref="flour-dough"/> a handful at a time until no dry flour remains. Rest the
  shaggy dough for <Dur step="mix-dough"/>, then add the <Qty ref="salt"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="knead">
  Knead for <Dur step="knead"/>. The dough starts slack and tacky and finishes smooth
  and slightly resistant; it is ready when a pulled corner stretches thin enough to
  see light through before it tears.
</Step>

<Step id="bulk-ferment">
  Cover and leave at room temperature for <Dur step="bulk-ferment"/>, until roughly
  doubled and domed.
</Step>

<Step id="divide">
  Turn the dough out and divide it into two equal pieces. Shape each into a tight
  ball by dragging it against the bench until the surface is under tension.
</Step>

<Step id="prove-balls">
  Cover the balls and prove for <Dur step="prove-balls"/>. They should spread and
  soften but still hold their shape when nudged.
</Step>

<Step id="heat-oven">
  Meanwhile bring the oven to its absolute maximum, with a stone or steel in place,
  and give it <Dur step="heat-oven"/> to saturate. A true Neapolitan oven runs at
  <Temp c={450}/>; a domestic one will not, which is what the home-oven version of
  this recipe is for.
</Step>

<Step id="shape">
  Working with the <Qty ref="flour-bench"/>, press each ball out from the centre with
  your fingertips, leaving the outer rim untouched, then stretch it over your knuckles
  to a <Len cm={28}/> round. Leave it thicker at the edge.
</Step>

<Step id="top">
  Crush the <Qty ref="tomatoes"/> by hand, season them with nothing, and spread a thin
  film to within a finger's width of the rim. Scatter the <Qty ref="mozzarella"/> over,
  add most of the <Qty ref="basil"/>, and thread the <Qty ref="olive-oil"/> across in a
  spiral.
</Step>

<Step id="bake">
  Launch onto the stone and bake for <Dur step="bake"/>, turning once, until the rim
  has blistered and charred in spots and the centre is set. Finish with the remaining
  basil.
</Step>

    - stepId: cold-ferment
      text: A domestic oven cannot supply the colour a wood fire gives in ninety seconds, so the flavour has to come from somewhere else. A full day in the fridge is where.

    - stepId: heat-oven

<Step id="mix-dough">
  Dissolve the <Qty ref="yeast"/> in the <Qty ref="water"/>, then work in the
  <Qty ref="flour-dough"/> a handful at a time until no dry flour remains. Rest the
  shaggy dough for <Dur step="mix-dough"/>, then add the <Qty ref="salt"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="knead">
  Knead for <Dur step="knead"/>. Bread flour takes longer to come together than 00 and
  fights back more; work it until the surface is smooth and a pulled corner stretches
  thin without tearing.
</Step>

<Step id="cold-ferment">
  Cover and refrigerate at <Temp c={4}/> for <Dur step="cold-ferment"/>. The dough will
  rise slowly and smell faintly of beer when it is ready.
</Step>

<Step id="divide">
  Turn the dough out and divide it into two equal pieces. Shape each into a tight ball
  by dragging it against the bench until the surface is under tension.
</Step>

<Step id="prove-balls">
  Cover and leave at room temperature for <Dur step="prove-balls"/>, until the chill
  has gone out of the dough and it has softened and spread.
</Step>

<Step id="heat-oven">
  Meanwhile set the oven to its maximum with a stone or steel on the upper shelf, and
  give it <Dur step="heat-oven"/>. Most domestic ovens reach <Temp c={250}/>; use the
  grill element for the last stretch if yours has one.
</Step>

<Step id="shape">
  Working with the <Qty ref="flour-bench"/>, press each ball out from the centre,
  leaving the rim untouched, and stretch it to a <Len cm={26}/> round — slightly
  smaller than the Neapolitan, which helps the base set before the top dries.
</Step>

<Step id="top">
  Crush the <Qty ref="tomatoes"/> by hand and spread a thin film to within a finger's
  width of the rim. Scatter the <Qty ref="mozzarella"/> over, add most of the
  <Qty ref="basil"/>, and thread the <Qty ref="olive-oil"/> across.
</Step>

<Step id="bake">
  Bake for <Dur step="bake"/> on the stone, switching to the grill for the final
  minute so the top colours before the base overcooks. Finish with the remaining basil.
</Step>

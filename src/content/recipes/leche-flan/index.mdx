  techniqueRationale:
    - stepId: steam
      text: Steaming rather than baking in a water bath keeps the custard at a gentler, more even temperature throughout, which is what gives leche flan its dense, silky set without the small air bubbles a hotter oven bake can leave behind.
  commonPitfalls:
    - stepId: mix-custard

<Step id="make-caramel">
  Melt the <Qty ref="sugar"/> in a dry pan over a medium heat, swirling rather than
  stirring, for <Dur step="make-caramel"/>, until deep amber. Pour immediately into
  the base of the mould or moulds, tilting to coat, and set aside to harden.
</Step>

<Step id="mix-custard">
  Gently whisk the <Qty ref="egg-yolk"/> with the <Qty ref="condensed-milk"/>,
  <Qty ref="evaporated-milk"/> and <Qty ref="vanilla"/> for <Dur step="mix-custard"/>,
  until just combined, then strain through a fine sieve into the caramel-lined mould.
</Step>

<Step id="steam">
  Cover the mould with foil and steam over gently simmering water for
  <Dur step="steam"/>, until the custard is set but still faintly wobbly at the centre.
</Step>

<Step id="chill">
  Cool, then refrigerate for <Dur step="chill"/>. Run a knife around the edge and invert
  onto a plate to unmould, letting the caramel run down over the custard.
</Step>

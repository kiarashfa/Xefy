<Step id="slice-onion">
  Halve the <Qty ref="onion"/> from root to tip and slice each half into thin half-moons.
  This takes <Dur step="slice-onion"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-rice">
  Rinse the <Qty ref="rice"/> in several changes of cold water until the water runs almost
  clear. Cook it in a heavy pan with a tight lid, and leave it covered after the boil for
  the last part of the <Dur step="cook-rice"/>. Keep it warm.
</Step>

<Step id="cut-chicken">
  Cut the <Qty ref="chicken"/> into bite-sized pieces, and cut the
  <Qty ref="spring-onion"/> into short lengths. This takes <Dur step="cut-chicken"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="beat-eggs">
  Beat the <Qty ref="egg"/> lightly in a jug with a fork, without making a foam. Divide it
  into two batches. This takes <Dur step="beat-eggs"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="simmer-chicken">
  Pour the strained dashi into a wide, shallow pan with the <Qty ref="soy"/>, the
  <Qty ref="mirin"/> and the <Qty ref="sugar"/>, and bring it to the boil. Add the
  <Qty ref="onion"/> and the <Qty ref="chicken"/>, and simmer for <Dur step="simmer-chicken"/>,
  skimming, until the chicken is cooked through and the onion is soft.
</Step>

<Step id="add-eggs">
  Scatter the <Qty ref="spring-onion"/> over the pan and pour in the first batch of beaten
  egg, moving it gently from the edge to the centre. Cover and cook for a few seconds, then
  pour in the second batch and cover again for <Dur step="add-eggs"/>, until the surface has
  just set and still looks soft. Take the pan off the heat. Divide the rice between two deep
  bowls, slide the chicken, onion and egg over the top, and serve at once.
</Step>

  aheadInstructions: The whole point of the dish is advance preparation — the brisket must be dry-cured for about a week before it is smoked, and this cannot be rushed. Once smoked, it keeps well and is meant to be steamed fresh just before serving.
  freezable: true
  freezeInstructions: Freeze the smoked, unsteamed brisket, wrapped tightly, for up to two months; thaw fully in the refrigerator before steaming.
  reheatInstructions: Re-steam sliced meat gently over simmering water until hot through and tender again; a microwave dries it out badly.
notes:
  techniqueRationale:
    - stepId: cure-brisket
      text: A week of dry curing gives the salt and sugar time to penetrate the full thickness of a whole brisket, not just its surface — a shorter cure seasons the outside and leaves the centre bland.
  commonPitfalls:
    - stepId: steam-brisket
      text: Skipping the steam and eating the brisket straight from the smoker leaves it too firm to slice cleanly and too salty to eat in quantity. The steam is what finishes the cooking and drives out enough salt to make it a sandwich meat rather than a cured joint.

<Step id="mix-cure">
  Combine the <Qty ref="cure-salt"/>, the <Qty ref="cure-sugar"/>, the
  <Qty ref="cure-garlic"/>, the <Qty ref="cure-bay"/>, the <Qty ref="pepper-cure"/> and
  the <Qty ref="coriander-cure"/>, and rub the mixture all over the <Qty ref="brisket"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="cure-brisket">
  Seal the brisket in a bag or covered container and refrigerate for
  <Dur step="cure-brisket"/>, turning it over once a day.
</Step>

<Step id="rinse-brisket">
  Rinse the cure off the brisket under cold water and pat completely dry.
</Step>

<Step id="coat-crust">
  Press the <Qty ref="pepper-crust"/>, the <Qty ref="coriander-crust"/>, the
  <Qty ref="mustard-seed"/> and the <Qty ref="paprika"/> firmly over the whole
  surface of the brisket.
</Step>

<Step id="smoke-brisket">
  Cold-smoke the brisket over hardwood at a low temperature of around
  <Temp c={100}/> for <Dur step="smoke-brisket"/>, until deeply coloured and smoky
  throughout.
</Step>

<Step id="steam-brisket">
  Steam the brisket over simmering water for <Dur step="steam-brisket"/>, until fully
  tender and easily pierced with a fork.
</Step>

<Step id="rest">
  Rest for <Dur step="rest"/> before slicing.
</Step>

<Step id="slice-and-serve">
  Slice thinly against the grain and pile onto the <Qty ref="rye-bread"/> with the
  <Qty ref="mustard"/>.
</Step>

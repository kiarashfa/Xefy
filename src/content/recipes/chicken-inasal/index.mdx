<Step id="marinate">
  Combine the <Qty ref="calamansi"/>, <Qty ref="vinegar"/>, <Qty ref="lemongrass"/>,
  <Qty ref="garlic"/>, <Qty ref="pepper"/> and <Qty ref="salt"/> in a bowl and turn the
  <Qty ref="chicken"/> through it, coating every piece, for <Dur step="marinate"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="rest-marinade">
  Cover and marinate in the refrigerator for <Dur step="rest-marinade"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="make-baste">
  Meanwhile, warm the <Qty ref="oil"/> with the <Qty ref="achiote"/> over a low heat for
  <Dur step="make-baste"/>, until the oil turns deep orange, then strain out the seeds.
</Step>

<Step id="grill">
  Lift the chicken from the marinade and grill over hot coals for
  <Dur step="grill"/>, turning and basting repeatedly with the annatto oil, until the
  skin is deeply coloured and the chicken is cooked through.
</Step>

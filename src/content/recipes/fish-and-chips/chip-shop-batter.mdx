<Step id="soak-peas">
  Cover the <Qty ref="peas"/> with the <Qty ref="pea-soak-water"/> and leave to soak for
  <Dur step="soak-peas"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="cut-chips">
  Peel the <Qty ref="chip-potato"/> and cut into thick batons.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-peas">
  Drain the soaked peas, cover with the <Qty ref="pea-cook-water"/>, add the
  <Qty ref="pea-bicarb"/> and the <Qty ref="salt-peas"/>, and simmer for
  <Dur step="cook-peas"/>, until completely soft, then mash roughly with a fork or
  potato masher, leaving some texture.
</Step>

<Step id="par-fry-chips">
  Heat the <Qty ref="frying-oil"/> to a moderate temperature of about <Temp c={130}/>
  and fry the chips in batches for <Dur step="par-fry-chips"/>, until soft all the way
  through but still pale. Lift out and drain.
</Step>

<Step id="rest-chips">
  Leave the chips to cool and dry out on a wire rack for <Dur step="rest-chips"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="make-batter">
  Whisk the <Qty ref="batter-flour"/>, the <Qty ref="batter-cornflour"/> and the
  <Qty ref="salt-batter"/> together, then whisk in the <Qty ref="batter-water"/> until
  just combined into a thin, smooth batter.
</Step>

<Step id="crisp-chips">
  Raise the oil to <Temp c={190}/> and fry the chips a second time, in batches, for
  <Dur step="crisp-chips"/>, until deep golden and crisp. Drain and season with the
  <Qty ref="salt-serve"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="fry-fish">
  Pat the <Qty ref="fish"/> dry, dip each fillet in the batter to coat completely, and
  lower into the <Temp c={190}/> oil. Fry for <Dur step="fry-fish"/>, until the batter is
  pale gold and crisp and the fish is opaque all the way through.
</Step>

<Step id="serve">
  Serve the fish and chips at once with the mashed peas and the <Qty ref="vinegar"/>
  alongside.
</Step>

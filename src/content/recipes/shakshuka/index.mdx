notes:
  techniqueRationale:
    - stepId: reduce
      text: The sauce has to be finished before the eggs go in. Once they are set into it the pan cannot be cooked any further without ruining them, so a thin sauce at that point stays thin.
    - stepId: bloom-spices
      text: The spices go into the hot oil rather than into the tomatoes. Their flavour compounds are fat-soluble and barely move in water, which is why a spoonful stirred into the sauce at the end tastes dusty.
  commonPitfalls:
    - stepId: poach-eggs
      text: Too much heat under the pan sets the whites into rubber before the yolks have warmed. The eggs go into wells pushed down to the base of the pan, with a lid on and the flame as low as it goes.

<Step id="soften">
  Warm the <Qty ref="olive-oil"/> in a wide, shallow pan over a low flame and add the
  <Qty ref="onion"/> and the <Qty ref="red-pepper"/>. Cook them for <Dur step="soften"/>,
  turning them over occasionally, until both have collapsed to softness and the pepper
  has begun to slump against the pan. They should take almost no colour.
</Step>

<Step id="bloom-spices">
  Add the <Qty ref="garlic"/>, the <Qty ref="caraway"/>, the <Qty ref="cumin"/>, the
  <Qty ref="paprika"/> and the <Qty ref="chilli"/>, and stir them through the hot oil for
  <Dur step="bloom-spices"/>, until the paprika has darkened a shade and the pan smells
  of spice rather than of raw powder.
</Step>

<Step id="reduce">
  Crush the <Qty ref="tomatoes"/> in your hand as they go into the pan, add the
  <Qty ref="salt"/> and the <Qty ref="black-pepper"/>, and simmer for
  <Dur step="reduce"/>. It is done when the sauce has darkened to brick, the oil has
  separated out around the edge, and a spoon dragged across the base leaves a channel
  that fills in slowly.
</Step>

<Step id="poach-eggs">
  Push a well down to the base of the pan for each egg with the back of a spoon and slide
  the <Qty ref="eggs"/> in one at a time. Season the tops, put a lid on and cook over the
  lowest heat you can hold for <Dur step="poach-eggs"/>, until the whites have turned
  opaque and firm and the yolks still shift when the pan is nudged.
</Step>

<Step id="settle">
  Take the pan off the heat and leave it to stand for <Dur step="settle"/>; the whites
  finish setting on residual heat while the yolks do not. Tear the <Qty ref="coriander"/>
  over the top and bring the pan to the table.
</Step>

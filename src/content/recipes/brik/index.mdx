<Step id="boil-potato">
  Boil the <Qty ref="potato"/> in salted water for <Dur step="boil-potato"/>, until tender
  all the way through, then drain and mash roughly.
</Step>

<Step id="make-filling">
  Combine the mashed potato with the <Qty ref="tuna"/>, <Qty ref="parsley"/>,
  <Qty ref="spring-onion"/>, <Qty ref="salt"/> and <Qty ref="pepper"/>, breaking the tuna
  up as you mix so the filling holds together without clumping.
</Step>

<Step id="heat-oil">
  Pour the <Qty ref="frying-oil"/> into a wide pan to a depth that will cover a folded
  brik and heat it for <Dur step="heat-oil"/>, until a corner of pastry dropped in sizzles
  and colours within a few seconds.
</Step>

<Step id="assemble-and-fry">
  Lay a sheet of <Qty ref="malsouka"/> flat and spoon a portion of the filling onto one
  half, leaving a border clear. Make a small well in the filling and crack one
  <Qty ref="egg"/> into it whole, keeping the yolk intact. Fold the pastry over into a
  half-moon or a triangle, pressing the border to seal, and slide it straight into the hot
  oil. Fry for <Dur step="assemble-and-fry"/> in total across the batch, turning once,
  until the pastry is blistered and deep gold and the egg white has just set — lift each
  one out the moment it is coloured, so the yolk inside is still soft and runs when the
  brik is cut. Drain briefly and serve at once.
</Step>

<Step id="make-adobo">
  Mash the <Qty ref="garlic"/> with the <Qty ref="oregano"/>,
  <Qty ref="cider-vinegar"/>, <Qty ref="olive-oil"/>, <Qty ref="salt"/> and
  <Qty ref="black-pepper"/> into a coarse paste.
</Step>

<Step id="cut-and-pack">
  Cut deep pockets all over the <Qty ref="pork"/>, avoiding the skin, and
  pack the adobo into each one, then rub any remaining paste over the
  surface.
</Step>

<Step id="marinate">
  Cover and refrigerate for <Dur step="marinate"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="slow-roast">
  Set the pork skin-side up in a roasting tray and roast at <Temp c={150}/>
  for <Dur step="slow-roast"/>, until the meat is fully tender.
</Step>

<Step id="crisp">
  Raise the oven to <Temp c={220}/> and roast for a further
  <Dur step="crisp"/>, until the skin is deeply browned and crackling.
</Step>

<Step id="rest">
  Rest the pork loosely covered for <Dur step="rest"/> before carving or
  pulling it apart.
</Step>

<Step id="mix-marinade">
  Stir the <Qty ref="coconut-milk"/>, <Qty ref="fish-sauce"/>, <Qty ref="palm-sugar"/>,
  <Qty ref="garlic"/>, <Qty ref="coriander"/> and <Qty ref="pepper"/> together in a bowl
  until the sugar has mostly dissolved.
</Step>

<Step id="marinate">
  Add the <Qty ref="pork"/> to the marinade, turning to coat every slice, and leave it to
  marinate, covered and refrigerated, for <Dur step="marinate"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="thread">
  Thread the marinated slices onto skewers, weaving each slice back and forth so it sits
  in a compact ripple rather than a flat sheet.
</Step>

<Step id="grill">
  Grill the skewers over hot charcoal for <Dur step="grill"/>, turning often, until the
  edges are lightly charred and sticky and the pork is cooked through.
</Step>

<Step id="slice-potatoes">
  Peel the <Qty ref="potatoes"/> and slice them into rounds about <Len cm={0.3}/> thick. Keep the slices
  in cold water until you need them, then drain and dry them. This takes
  <Dur step="slice-potatoes"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="rub-dish">
  Rub a shallow ovenproof dish all over with the cut side of the <Qty ref="garlic"/>, then butter
  it with the <Qty ref="butter"/>. This takes <Dur step="rub-dish"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="layer">
  Arrange the potato slices in overlapping layers in the dish, seasoning each layer with a little of
  the <Qty ref="salt"/> and the <Qty ref="pepper"/>. Pour the <Qty ref="cream"/> over, so it just
  reaches the top layer, and grate the <Qty ref="nutmeg"/> over the surface. This takes
  <Dur step="layer"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="bake">
  Bake at <Temp c={170}/> for <Dur step="bake"/>, until the top is golden and the cream has thickened,
  and a knife slides easily through the potatoes.
</Step>

<Step id="rest">
  Leave the gratin to rest for <Dur step="rest"/> before it is cut, so the cream sets around the
  potatoes.
</Step>

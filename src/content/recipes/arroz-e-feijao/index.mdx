<Step id="soak-beans">
  Soak the <Qty ref="beans"/> in plenty of cold water for
  <Dur step="soak-beans"/>, then drain.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-beans">
  Warm the <Qty ref="oil-beans"/> in a pot and cook the
  <Qty ref="onion-beans"/> and <Qty ref="garlic-beans"/> until soft. Add the
  drained beans, the <Qty ref="bay-leaf"/> and the <Qty ref="bean-water"/>.
  Bring to a simmer and cook for <Dur step="cook-beans"/>, partially covered,
  until the beans are fully tender and the liquid has thickened slightly.
  Stir in the <Qty ref="salt-beans"/> once tender.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-rice">
  While the beans simmer, warm the <Qty ref="oil-rice"/> in a separate pot
  and cook the <Qty ref="onion-rice"/> and <Qty ref="garlic-rice"/> until
  soft. Add the <Qty ref="rice"/> and stir for a minute to coat the grains
  in the oil, then pour in the <Qty ref="rice-water"/> and the
  <Qty ref="salt-rice"/>. Bring to a boil, then cover and cook over the
  lowest heat for <Dur step="cook-rice"/>, until the water is fully absorbed
  and the rice is tender. Rest, covered and off the heat, for a few minutes,
  then fluff with a fork.
</Step>

<Step id="knead">
  Squeeze the grated <Qty ref="onion"/> firmly to remove excess liquid, then combine the
  pulp with the <Qty ref="lamb"/>, <Qty ref="beef"/>, <Qty ref="salt"/>,
  <Qty ref="pepper"/> and <Qty ref="bicarb"/> in a wide bowl. Knead and pound the mixture
  by hand for <Dur step="knead"/>, until it turns dense, sticky and uniform.
</Step>

<Step id="chill">
  Cover the mince and chill it for <Dur step="chill"/>, so it firms up and is easier to
  mould onto the skewers.
</Step>

<Step id="shape">
  Divide the mince into even portions. With wet hands, mould each portion around a
  broad, flat metal skewer, pressing and smoothing it into a long, even ribbon that
  grips the blade. Work for <Dur step="shape"/> in total.
</Step>

<Step id="grill">
  Grill the skewers over fierce, direct charcoal heat, turning them often, for
  <Dur step="grill"/>, until evenly charred outside and cooked through with no pink
  remaining at the centre.
</Step>

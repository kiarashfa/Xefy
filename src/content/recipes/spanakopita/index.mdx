<Step id="wilt-spinach">
  Heat the <Qty ref="cook-oil"/> in a wide pan and cook the <Qty ref="spring-onion"/> for
  a minute, then add the <Qty ref="spinach"/> in batches and wilt it down over
  <Dur step="wilt-spinach"/>, until fully collapsed and no longer releasing liquid. Tip
  into a sieve and press out as much water as possible, then chop roughly once cool
  enough to handle.
</Step>

<Step id="make-filling">
  Combine the wilted spinach mixture with the <Qty ref="feta"/>, <Qty ref="egg"/>,
  <Qty ref="dill"/>, <Qty ref="salt"/> and <Qty ref="pepper"/> in a bowl and mix until
  evenly combined.
</Step>

<Step id="layer-base">
  Brush a baking tray with a little of the <Qty ref="brushing-oil"/> and layer in half
  the sheets of <Qty ref="filo"/>, brushing each sheet with oil before adding the next
  and letting the edges overhang the sides of the tray.
</Step>

<Step id="fill-and-top">
  Spread the spinach filling evenly over the base layer. Layer the remaining filo on top
  in the same way, brushing each sheet with oil, then fold the overhanging edges back
  over the top and brush again. Score the top layers into portions with a sharp knife,
  cutting through the pastry but not into the filling.
</Step>

<Step id="bake">
  Bake for <Dur step="bake"/>, until deeply golden and crisp all the way through. Cut
  along the scored lines to serve.
</Step>

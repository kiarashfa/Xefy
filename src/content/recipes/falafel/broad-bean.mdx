<Step id="soak">
  Cover the <Qty ref="broad-bean"/> with the <Qty ref="soak-water"/> and leave to soak
  for <Dur step="soak"/>. The beans should roughly double in size and still be raw and
  firm, not softened.
</Step>

<Step id="peel">
  Rub the soaked beans between your hands in their soaking water to loosen the skins,
  then skim off and discard the skins as they float free. Drain the peeled beans,
  working for <Dur step="peel"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="grind">
  Pulse the peeled beans in a food processor with the <Qty ref="spring-onion"/>,
  <Qty ref="garlic"/>, <Qty ref="parsley"/>, <Qty ref="dill"/>, <Qty ref="coriander"/>,
  <Qty ref="pepper"/> and <Qty ref="salt"/> for <Dur step="grind"/>, stopping while the
  mixture is still coarse and slightly grainy rather than smooth.
</Step>

<Step id="rest">
  Stir in the <Qty ref="baking-soda"/> and rest the mixture for <Dur step="rest"/> before
  shaping.
</Step>

<Step id="coat-and-fry">
  Heat the <Qty ref="frying-oil"/> in a deep pan to a steady, gentle bubble. Shape the
  mixture into small flattened patties, pressing both sides into the
  <Qty ref="sesame-coating"/> to coat, and lower them in without crowding the pan. Fry
  for <Dur step="coat-and-fry"/>, turning once, until deeply browned and firm to the
  touch. Lift out and drain.
</Step>

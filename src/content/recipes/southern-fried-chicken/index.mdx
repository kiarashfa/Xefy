<Step id="brine">
  Combine the <Qty ref="buttermilk"/> and <Qty ref="brine-hot-sauce"/> in a large bowl
  or dish, submerge the <Qty ref="chicken"/> in it, cover, and refrigerate for
  <Dur step="brine"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="prep-dredge">
  Whisk the <Qty ref="flour"/>, <Qty ref="cayenne"/>, <Qty ref="paprika"/>,
  <Qty ref="salt"/> and <Qty ref="black-pepper"/> together in a wide shallow dish.
</Step>

<Step id="dredge-chicken">
  Lift each piece of chicken from the buttermilk, letting the excess drip off, and
  press it into the seasoned flour, turning to coat evenly on all sides. Set the
  dredged pieces on a rack.
</Step>

<Step id="heat-oil">
  Heat the <Qty ref="oil"/> in a heavy skillet over a medium heat until it reaches
  <Temp c={165}/>.
</Step>

<Step id="fry-chicken">
  Fry the chicken in batches for <Dur step="fry-chicken"/>, turning occasionally and
  adjusting the heat to hold the oil near <Temp c={165}/>, until the crust is deep
  golden and the meat is cooked through to the bone.
</Step>

<Step id="rest">
  Drain the fried chicken on a wire rack and let it rest for <Dur step="rest"/> before
  serving, so the crust can set.
</Step>

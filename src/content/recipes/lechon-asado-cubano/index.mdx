<Step id="blend-mojo">
  Blend the <Qty ref="sour-orange-juice"/>, <Qty ref="lime-juice"/>,
  <Qty ref="garlic"/>, <Qty ref="oregano"/>, <Qty ref="cumin"/>,
  <Qty ref="olive-oil"/>, <Qty ref="salt"/> and <Qty ref="black-pepper"/>
  into the mojo.
</Step>

<Step id="marinate">
  Pierce the <Qty ref="pork"/> all over and place it in a deep dish with the
  mojo and the <Qty ref="bay-leaf"/>, turning to coat completely. Cover and
  refrigerate for <Dur step="marinate"/>, turning occasionally.
</Step>

<Step id="slow-roast">
  Scatter the <Qty ref="onion"/> in a roasting tray, set the pork on top
  skin-side up with its marinade poured around it, and roast at
  <Temp c={150}/> for <Dur step="slow-roast"/>, basting occasionally, until
  the meat is fully tender.
</Step>

<Step id="crisp">
  Raise the oven to <Temp c={220}/> and roast for a further
  <Dur step="crisp"/>, until the skin is deeply browned and crackling.
</Step>

<Step id="rest">
  Rest the pork loosely covered for <Dur step="rest"/> before carving or
  pulling it apart.
</Step>

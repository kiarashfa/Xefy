<Step id="make-dough">
  Combine the <Qty ref="flour"/> and <Qty ref="dough-salt"/> in a bowl. Add
  the melted <Qty ref="dough-lard"/> and the <Qty ref="dough-water"/>, mixing
  until a smooth, firm dough comes together.
</Step>

<Step id="rest-dough">
  Wrap the dough and let it rest for <Dur step="rest-dough"/> at room
  temperature.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-filling">
  While the dough rests, melt the <Qty ref="cook-lard"/> in a wide pan and
  cook the <Qty ref="onion"/> and <Qty ref="red-pepper"/> over a medium heat
  until soft. Add the <Qty ref="beef"/>, <Qty ref="paprika"/> and
  <Qty ref="cumin"/>, season with the <Qty ref="filling-salt"/> and
  <Qty ref="pepper"/>, and cook for <Dur step="cook-filling"/>, stirring
  often, until the beef is cooked through and the mixture is dry rather than
  saucy, with no free liquid left in the pan.
</Step>

<Step id="cool-filling">
  Spread the filling on a tray and cool completely, for
  <Dur step="cool-filling"/>, before using.
</Step>

<Step id="finish-filling">
  Stir the <Qty ref="egg"/> and <Qty ref="olives"/> through the cooled
  filling.
</Step>

<Step id="shape">
  Roll the dough thin and cut into rounds. Spoon filling onto one half of
  each round, fold over and press the edges together, then crimp the sealed
  edge by folding it over on itself in a repeating fold along its length.
</Step>

<Step id="bake">
  Bake at <Temp c={200}/> for <Dur step="bake"/>, until deep golden and
  crisp.
</Step>

<Step id="boil-beans">
  Drain the soaked <Qty ref="beans"/>, cover with the <Qty ref="water"/> and simmer for
  <Dur step="boil-beans"/>, until completely tender and starting to break down.
</Step>

<Step id="boil-eggs">
  Meanwhile, boil the <Qty ref="egg"/> for <Dur step="boil-eggs"/>, then cool, peel and
  quarter.
</Step>

<Step id="mash">
  Drain most of the cooking liquid, reserving a little, and mash the beans with the
  <Qty ref="garlic"/>, <Qty ref="cumin"/> and <Qty ref="salt"/> for <Dur step="mash"/>,
  loosening with the reserved liquid to a thick, coarse texture.
</Step>

<Step id="dress">
  Stir in the <Qty ref="lemon-juice"/> and <Qty ref="oil-mash"/>. Spoon into bowls and
  top with the <Qty ref="tomato"/>, <Qty ref="parsley"/>, quartered egg and the
  <Qty ref="oil-finish"/>, working for <Dur step="dress"/>.
</Step>

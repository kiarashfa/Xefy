<Step id="blanch-bitterleaf">
  Boil the <Qty ref="bitterleaf"/> in plenty of water, draining and covering with
  fresh water two more times, for <Dur step="blanch-bitterleaf"/> in total, until
  much of its natural bitterness has gone. Drain well and set aside.
</Step>

<Step id="brown-beef">
  Brown the <Qty ref="beef"/> in a dry, heavy pot over a high heat for
  <Dur step="brown-beef"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="simmer-beef">
  Add the <Qty ref="water"/> and the <Qty ref="fine-sea-salt"/> to the pot,
  bring to a simmer, cover, and cook for <Dur step="simmer-beef"/>, until the
  beef is tender.
</Step>

<Step id="build-sauce">
  Stir the <Qty ref="onion"/>, <Qty ref="garlic"/>, <Qty ref="ginger"/>,
  <Qty ref="palm-oil"/>, <Qty ref="bouillon-powder"/> and <Qty ref="nutmeg"/>
  into the pot. Stir in the ground <Qty ref="peanut"/> and cook, stirring
  often, for <Dur step="build-sauce"/>, until the sauce has thickened.
</Step>

<Step id="finish">
  Add the blanched bitterleaf, the <Qty ref="shrimp"/> and the
  <Qty ref="dried-shrimp"/> to the pot. Simmer for <Dur step="finish"/>, until
  the shrimp are cooked through and the stew has come together into a thick,
  dark sauce.
</Step>

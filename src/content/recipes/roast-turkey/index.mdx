<Step id="dry-brine">
  Pat the <Qty ref="turkey"/> completely dry and season it all over,
  including inside the cavity, with the <Qty ref="salt"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="chill">
  Set the bird uncovered on a rack set in a tray and refrigerate for
  <Dur step="chill"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="make-butter">
  Mash the <Qty ref="butter"/> with the <Qty ref="garlic"/>, the
  <Qty ref="thyme"/> and the <Qty ref="pepper"/> until evenly combined.
</Step>

<Step id="butter-bird">
  Loosen the skin over the breast and legs and work most of the herb butter
  underneath it directly against the meat, then rub whatever remains over the
  outside of the skin.
</Step>

<Step id="roast-covered">
  Tent the bird loosely with foil and roast at <Temp c={160}/> for
  <Dur step="roast-covered"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="roast-uncovered">
  Remove the foil and continue roasting for <Dur step="roast-uncovered"/> at
  <Temp c={220}/>, until the skin is deeply golden and a thermometer in the
  thickest part of the thigh, away from the bone, reads <Temp c={74}/>.
</Step>

<Step id="rest">
  Transfer the bird to a board and rest, loosely tented, for
  <Dur step="rest"/> before carving.
</Step>

<Step id="carve">
  Carve the breast and legs away from the carcass and slice.
</Step>

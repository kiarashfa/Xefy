<Step id="spatchcock">
  Turn the <Qty ref="turkey"/> breast side down and cut along both sides of
  the backbone to remove it. Turn the bird over and press down firmly on the
  breastbone until it cracks and the bird lies flat.
</Step>

<Step id="dry-brine">
  Pat the bird completely dry and season it all over, including under the
  legs, with the <Qty ref="salt"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="chill">
  Set the bird uncovered, skin side up, on a rack set in a tray and
  refrigerate for <Dur step="chill"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="make-butter">
  Mash the <Qty ref="butter"/> with the <Qty ref="garlic"/>, the
  <Qty ref="thyme"/> and the <Qty ref="pepper"/> until evenly combined.
</Step>

<Step id="butter-bird">
  Loosen the skin over the breast and legs and work most of the herb butter
  underneath it directly against the meat, then rub whatever remains over the
  outside of the skin.
</Step>

<Step id="roast">
  Roast flat, skin side up, at <Temp c={220}/> for <Dur step="roast"/>, until
  the skin is deeply golden and a thermometer in the thickest part of the
  thigh, away from the bone, reads <Temp c={74}/>.
</Step>

<Step id="rest">
  Transfer to a board and rest for <Dur step="rest"/> before carving.
</Step>

<Step id="carve">
  Carve the breast and legs away from the carcass and slice.
</Step>

notes:
  techniqueRationale:
    - stepId: sear-chicken
      text: Searing the marinated chicken hot and fast, separately from the sauce, gives it the charred edges the dish is named for; simmering it directly in the sauce from raw would steam it instead.
  commonPitfalls:
    - stepId: combine
      text: Adding the cream over high heat can cause it to split against the acidity of the tomatoes. It goes in once the sauce has come down to a gentle simmer, off any hard boil.

<Step id="marinate-chicken">
  Combine the <Qty ref="chicken"/> with the <Qty ref="yoghurt"/>, the
  <Qty ref="lemon-juice"/>, the <Qty ref="garlic-marinade"/>, the
  <Qty ref="ginger-marinade"/>, the <Qty ref="chilli-marinade"/>, the
  <Qty ref="turmeric"/>, the <Qty ref="cumin-marinade"/> and the
  <Qty ref="salt-marinade"/>. Cover and refrigerate for <Dur step="marinate-chicken"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-onion">
  Melt the <Qty ref="ghee-sauce"/> in a wide pan and cook the <Qty ref="onion"/> over a
  medium heat for <Dur step="cook-onion"/>, stirring occasionally, until soft and
  turning golden.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-spices">
  Stir in the <Qty ref="garlic-sauce"/>, the <Qty ref="ginger-sauce"/>, the
  <Qty ref="chilli-sauce"/>, the <Qty ref="cumin-sauce"/>, the
  <Qty ref="coriander-seed"/>, the <Qty ref="cinnamon"/>, the <Qty ref="cardamom"/> and
  the <Qty ref="cloves"/>, and cook for <Dur step="cook-spices"/>, until fragrant.
</Step>

<Step id="simmer-sauce">
  Add the <Qty ref="tinned-tomato"/>, the <Qty ref="tomato-puree"/> and the
  <Qty ref="salt-sauce"/>, breaking the tomatoes up, and simmer for
  <Dur step="simmer-sauce"/>, until thickened and no longer watery.
</Step>

<Step id="sear-chicken">
  Lift the chicken from its marinade, shaking off the excess, and sear in the
  <Qty ref="ghee-sear"/> in a very hot pan for <Dur step="sear-chicken"/>, turning
  occasionally, until charred at the edges and cooked through.
</Step>

<Step id="combine">
  Blend the tomato sauce until smooth, return it to the pan and stir in the
  <Qty ref="cream"/>. Add the seared chicken and simmer gently for
  <Dur step="combine"/>, until the chicken is coated and heated through. Scatter over
  the <Qty ref="coriander-leaf"/> to serve.
</Step>

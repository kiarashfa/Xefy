<Step id="boil-potatoes">
  Put the <Qty ref="potato"/> into a pan of cold water and bring it to the boil. Cook
  until a knife slides into the centre with no resistance, for
  <Dur step="boil-potatoes"/>, then drain.
</Step>

<Step id="heat-water">
  While the potatoes cook, bring the <Qty ref="water"/> to the boil in a wide pan, and
  add the <Qty ref="salt-water"/> once it is rolling.
</Step>

<Step id="peel-mash">
  Peel the potatoes while they are still too hot to hold comfortably. Pass the flesh
  through a ricer onto a clean board, spread it out so the steam can leave, and let it
  cool until it is warm rather than hot.
</Step>

<Step id="make-dough">
  Scatter the <Qty ref="flour-dough"/> and the <Qty ref="salt-dough"/> over the potato, make a
  well in the middle and drop in the <Qty ref="egg"/>. Bring it together with the
  fingertips, working only until no dry flour remains and the dough is soft and just
  slightly sticky.
</Step>

<Step id="shape">
  Dust the board with the <Qty ref="flour-dusting"/>. Divide the dough into pieces and roll
  each into a rope about <Len cm={2}/> thick. Cut the ropes into pieces about
  <Len cm={2}/> long, and press each one with the back of a fork so it takes a ridge
  on one side. Set them on a floured tray as you go, for <Dur step="shape"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-gnocchi">
  Lower the gnocchi into the boiling water in batches, stirring once so they do not
  catch. Lift them out with a slotted spoon as soon as they rise to the surface, which
  takes <Dur step="cook-gnocchi"/>, and drain them well.
</Step>

<Step id="sage-butter">
  While the gnocchi cook, melt the <Qty ref="butter"/> in a wide pan over a moderate
  flame with the <Qty ref="sage"/> leaves. Let it foam and turn a hazelnut brown, and
  take the pan off the heat before it darkens further, for <Dur step="sage-butter"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="combine">
  Tip the drained gnocchi into the sage butter and toss gently for
  <Dur step="combine"/>, so each one is coated. Serve at once, with the
  <Qty ref="parmigiano"/> over the top.
</Step>

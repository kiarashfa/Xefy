Gnocchi di patate are dumplings of cooked potato, flour and egg. They are a
different dish from the gnocchi alla romana, which are made from semolina, set in
a tray and cut into rounds rather than boiled as small pillows. The potato is
cooked in its skin and pushed through a ricer while it is still hot, so that the
flesh stays light; the flour is worked in only as far as it needs to hold the dough.

Potato gnocchi are particularly popular in the Veneto <Cite ref="wiki-gnocchi"/> <Cite ref="dissapore-gnocchi"/>.

In Verona, Dissapore pairs the potato gnocchi with pastissada, the horse-meat
stew typical of the city <Cite ref="dissapore-gnocchi"/>. The same survey describes
the Papà del Gnoco, a traditional Carnival mask worn during the parade in the city
<Cite ref="dissapore-gnocchi"/>.

The Veneto is not all potato. The survey's entry for Recoaro recommends gnocchi
made from fioreta, a semi-liquid ricotta worked with flour, which is a different
dough altogether <Cite ref="dissapore-gnocchi"/>.

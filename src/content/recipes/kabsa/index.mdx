<Step id="brown-chicken">
  Melt half of the <Qty ref="ghee"/> in a heavy pot over a high heat and brown the
  <Qty ref="chicken"/> on all sides, working in batches if needed so it colours
  rather than steams. This takes <Dur step="brown-chicken"/>. Lift out and set
  aside.
</Step>

<Step id="build-base">
  Add the remaining ghee to the pot with the <Qty ref="onion-base"/> and cook until
  soft, then add the <Qty ref="garlic"/>, <Qty ref="cardamom"/>, <Qty ref="cloves"/>,
  <Qty ref="cinnamon"/>, <Qty ref="bay-leaf"/>, <Qty ref="black-pepper"/>,
  <Qty ref="cumin"/>, <Qty ref="paprika"/> and the <Qty ref="salt"/>. Cook briefly
  until fragrant, then stir in the <Qty ref="tomato"/>, breaking it down into the
  onion. Spend <Dur step="build-base"/> building the base.
</Step>

<Step id="simmer-chicken">
  Return the browned chicken to the pot, pour over the <Qty ref="stock-water"/> and
  bring to a simmer. Cover and cook gently for <Dur step="simmer-chicken"/>, until
  the chicken is cooked through and tender. Lift the chicken out and keep it warm;
  strain and reserve the spiced stock.
</Step>

<Step id="fry-garnish">
  While the chicken simmers, fry the <Qty ref="onion-garnish"/> in a little oil until
  deep golden, then set aside. Toast the <Qty ref="almonds"/> in the same pan until
  golden. This takes <Dur step="fry-garnish"/> in total.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-rice">
  Rinse the <Qty ref="rice"/> and add it to the reserved stock in the pot, along with
  the <Qty ref="raisins"/>. Bring back to a simmer, then cover tightly and cook over
  the lowest heat for <Dur step="cook-rice"/>, undisturbed, until the stock is fully
  absorbed and the rice is tender.
</Step>

<Step id="rest-and-serve">
  Let the rice rest off the heat, still covered, before fluffing it with a fork.
  Spread it on a platter, set the chicken on top, and scatter over the fried onion
  and toasted almonds. This takes <Dur step="rest-and-serve"/>.
</Step>

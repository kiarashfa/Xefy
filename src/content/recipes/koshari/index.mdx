  techniqueRationale:
    - stepId: cook-rice
      text: Toasting the vermicelli in oil before the rice and water go in is what gives it its brown colour and nutty flavour, rather than the pale, soft strands that boiling it raw alongside the rice would produce.
  commonPitfalls:
    - stepId: fry-onions
      text: Onions pulled from the pan the moment they look done keep cooking in their own residual heat and turn from golden to burnt within moments. Lifting them slightly before they reach the target colour and draining them at once avoids this.

<Step id="cook-chickpeas">
  Drain the soaked <Qty ref="chickpeas"/>, cover with the <Qty ref="chickpea-water"/>
  and simmer for <Dur step="cook-chickpeas"/>, until tender. Drain and set aside.
</Step>

<Step id="fry-onions">
  Meanwhile, heat the <Qty ref="oil-onions"/> in a wide pan and fry the <Qty ref="onion"/>
  for <Dur step="fry-onions"/>, stirring occasionally, until deep golden and crisp.
  Lift out with a slotted spoon and drain on paper towels.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-rice">
  Heat the <Qty ref="oil-rice"/> in a pot and toast the <Qty ref="vermicelli"/> until
  golden. Add the <Qty ref="rice"/> and <Qty ref="rice-water"/>, cover and cook for
  <Dur step="cook-rice"/>, until the liquid is absorbed and the rice is tender.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-lentils">
  In a separate pot, cover the <Qty ref="lentils"/> with the <Qty ref="lentil-water"/>
  and simmer for <Dur step="cook-lentils"/>, until tender but still holding their
  shape. Drain any excess liquid.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-macaroni">
  Cook the <Qty ref="macaroni"/> in the boiling <Qty ref="macaroni-water"/> for
  <Dur step="cook-macaroni"/>, until just tender, then drain.
</Step>

<Step id="make-sauce">
  Meanwhile, heat the <Qty ref="oil-sauce"/> and cook the <Qty ref="garlic-sauce"/>
  briefly, then stir in the <Qty ref="tomato-puree"/>, <Qty ref="sauce-water"/>,
  <Qty ref="vinegar-sauce"/>, <Qty ref="cumin-sauce"/> and <Qty ref="salt-sauce"/>.
  Simmer for <Dur step="make-sauce"/>, until slightly thickened.
</Step>

<Step id="make-dakka">
  Combine the <Qty ref="oil-dakka"/>, <Qty ref="garlic-dakka"/>,
  <Qty ref="vinegar-dakka"/>, <Qty ref="chilli-flakes"/>, <Qty ref="cumin-dakka"/> and
  <Qty ref="salt-dakka"/> for <Dur step="make-dakka"/>, to make a sharp, garlicky
  dressing.
</Step>

<Step id="assemble">
  Layer the rice, lentils, macaroni and chickpeas in bowls, top with the fried onion,
  and serve with the tomato sauce and dakka alongside for spooning over, working for
  <Dur step="assemble"/>.
</Step>

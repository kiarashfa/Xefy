<Step id="prepare-ingredients">
  Slice the <Qty ref="pork"/> thinly, cut the <Qty ref="cabbage"/> into bite-sized pieces, and
  slice the <Qty ref="onion"/> and the <Qty ref="carrot"/>. Lay everything out beside the stove.
  This takes <Dur step="prepare-ingredients"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="loosen-noodles">
  Separate the <Qty ref="noodles"/> by hand so that the strands do not clump in the pan. This takes
  <Dur step="loosen-noodles"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="fry-pork">
  Heat the <Qty ref="oil"/> in a large wok or frying pan over a high heat until it shimmers. Add the
  <Qty ref="pork"/> and stir-fry it for <Dur step="fry-pork"/>, until the fat has rendered and the
  slices are just cooked, and season with the <Qty ref="salt"/> and the <Qty ref="pepper"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="stir-fry-vegetables">
  Add the <Qty ref="cabbage"/>, the <Qty ref="onion"/> and the <Qty ref="carrot"/> to the pan and
  stir-fry them hard for <Dur step="stir-fry-vegetables"/>, moving them constantly, until they are
  just tender and a little charred at the edges. Add the <Qty ref="bean-sprouts"/> for the last
  moment of the cooking.
</Step>

<Step id="add-noodles">
  Add the <Qty ref="noodles"/> and toss them with the vegetables and pork for
  <Dur step="add-noodles"/>, separating the strands with chopsticks, until they are hot through
  and lightly crisp in places.
</Step>

<Step id="finish-yakisoba">
  Pour the <Qty ref="sauce"/> over the noodles and toss for <Dur step="finish-yakisoba"/>, until
  every strand is coated. Divide between two plates and finish with the <Qty ref="aonori"/> and the
  <Qty ref="pickled-ginger"/>.
</Step>

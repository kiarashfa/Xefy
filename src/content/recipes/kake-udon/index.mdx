<Step id="slice-spring-onion">
  Slice the <Qty ref="spring-onion"/> finely and grate the <Qty ref="ginger"/>. This takes
  <Dur step="slice-spring-onion"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="heat-udon">
  Bring a large pan of water to the boil. Lower in the <Qty ref="udon"/> and heat them
  through for <Dur step="heat-udon"/>, loosening them with chopsticks. Lift them out and
  drain them well, then divide them between two deep bowls.
</Step>

<Step id="simmer-tsuyu">
  Pour the strained dashi into a small pan with the <Qty ref="soy"/> and the
  <Qty ref="mirin"/>. Bring it to the boil, then simmer for <Dur step="simmer-tsuyu"/> to
  cook off the alcohol in the mirin. This is the tsuyu. Dilute it with the
  <Qty ref="water"/>, one part tsuyu to two parts water, and heat the broth until it is
  steaming but not boiling.
</Step>

<Step id="assemble-bowls">
  Pour the hot broth over the noodles in each bowl. Top each with a little of the
  <Qty ref="spring-onion"/> and a small mound of the grated <Qty ref="ginger"/>, and serve
  at once.
</Step>

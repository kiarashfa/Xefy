<Step id="boil-water">
  Bring the <Qty ref="pasta-water"/> to a rolling boil in a wide pan, then add
  the <Qty ref="pasta-salt"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="make-sauce">
  Meanwhile melt the <Qty ref="butter"/> in a wide pan over a low heat and
  stir in the <Qty ref="cream"/>. Keep it warm without letting it boil.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-pasta">
  Add the <Qty ref="fettuccine"/> to the boiling water and cook for
  <Dur step="cook-pasta"/>, or two minutes short of whatever the packet
  claims. Before draining, lift out a mugful of the cooking water, then drain
  the pasta while the centre still resists a bite.
</Step>

<Step id="combine">
  Add the pasta to the butter and cream, take the pan off the heat, and toss
  in the <Qty ref="parmesan"/> a little at a time, loosening with splashes of
  the reserved pasta water until the sauce coats every strand. Finish with the
  <Qty ref="pepper"/> and the <Qty ref="parsley"/>.
</Step>

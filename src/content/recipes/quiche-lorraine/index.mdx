<Step id="make-pastry">
  Rub the <Qty ref="butter"/> into the <Qty ref="flour"/> with your fingertips until the
  mixture looks like fine breadcrumbs. Add the <Qty ref="salt"/>, the <Qty ref="yolk"/> and the
  <Qty ref="water"/>, and bring it together with a knife into a smooth disc. Do not knead it.
  This takes <Dur step="make-pastry"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="chill-pastry">
  Wrap the pastry and chill it for <Dur step="chill-pastry"/>, until firm enough to roll.
</Step>

<Step id="line-tin">
  Roll the pastry to a round a little larger than a <Len cm={24}/> flan tin, about as thick as a
  coin. Lift it into the tin, press it into the corners and trim the edges. Prick the base and
  chill it again if it has softened. This takes <Dur step="line-tin"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="blind-bake">
  Line the pastry with baking paper, fill it with baking beans, and bake at <Temp c={190}/> for
  <Dur step="blind-bake"/>. Lift out the paper and beans and bake for a few minutes more, until the
  base is dry and pale gold.
</Step>

<Step id="render-lardons">
  While the case bakes, put the <Qty ref="lardons"/> in a dry frying pan over a medium heat and fry
  them for <Dur step="render-lardons"/>, until the fat has run and the edges are crisp. Drain them
  on kitchen paper.
</Step>

<Step id="whisk-custard">
  While the lardons cook, beat the <Qty ref="eggs"/> in a bowl, then whisk in the
  <Qty ref="cream"/> and the <Qty ref="pepper"/>. This takes <Dur step="whisk-custard"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="fill-quiche">
  Scatter the lardons over the base of the case. Pour the custard over them, through a sieve if
  you like. This takes <Dur step="fill-quiche"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="bake-quiche">
  Bake at <Temp c={170}/> for <Dur step="bake-quiche"/>, until the filling is just set at the
  edges and still trembles very slightly in the centre.
</Step>

<Step id="rest">
  Leave the quiche to rest in the tin for <Dur step="rest"/>, which lets the custard finish
  setting, then lift it out and cut it into slices.
</Step>

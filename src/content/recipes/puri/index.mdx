<Step id="mix">
  Put the <Qty ref="flour-dough"/> and the <Qty ref="salt"/> into a bowl and rub in the
  <Qty ref="ghee"/>. Add the <Qty ref="water"/> a little at a time, and mix for
  <Dur step="mix"/>, until a firm dough forms and no dry flour remains.
</Step>

<Step id="knead">
  Turn the dough onto a lightly floured bench and knead for <Dur step="knead"/>, until it is
  smooth and firm and no longer sticks to the hands.
</Step>

<Step id="rest">
  Cover the dough and leave it to rest for <Dur step="rest"/>, so that it relaxes and rolls
  out without shrinking.
</Step>

<Step id="shape">
  Divide the dough into equal pieces and roll each one into a smooth ball. Dust the bench with
  the <Qty ref="flour-bench"/>, and roll each ball into a thin round about <Len cm={10}/>
  across. Keep the rounds covered until they go into the oil.
</Step>

<Step id="heat-oil">
  Heat the <Qty ref="frying-oil"/> in a deep, heavy pan to <Temp c={180}/>. A pinch of dough
  dropped into the oil should sizzle and rise at once.
</Step>

<Step id="fry">
  Slide one round into the oil and press it under with a slotted spoon, so that the steam
  inside it expands. It will balloon within a few seconds. Turn it and fry until golden on
  both sides, then lift it onto kitchen paper. Fry one at a time, or as many as fit without
  crowding the pan, for <Dur step="fry"/> in total.
</Step>

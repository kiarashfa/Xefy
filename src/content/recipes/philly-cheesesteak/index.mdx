<Step id="cook-onion">
  Heat half the <Qty ref="oil"/> in a flat pan or griddle over a medium heat
  and cook the <Qty ref="onion"/> for <Dur step="cook-onion"/>, stirring
  occasionally, until soft and lightly browned. Push it to one side of the
  griddle.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-steak">
  Add the remaining <Qty ref="oil"/> and lay the <Qty ref="ribeye"/> across
  the hot surface. Season with the <Qty ref="salt"/> and <Qty ref="pepper"/>
  and cook for <Dur step="cook-steak"/>, chopping and turning it with two
  spatulas as it browns until it is in loose, shredded pieces mixed through
  the onion.
</Step>

<Step id="melt-cheese">
  Gather the meat and onion into a mound and lay the <Qty ref="provolone"/>
  over the top. Let it stand for <Dur step="melt-cheese"/> until fully melted,
  then fold it through.
</Step>

<Step id="toast-roll">
  Meanwhile split the <Qty ref="roll"/> and toast the cut faces on the
  griddle for <Dur step="toast-roll"/>, until golden.
</Step>

<Step id="assemble">
  Pile the meat, onion and cheese into the toasted roll.
</Step>

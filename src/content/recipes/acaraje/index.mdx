<Step id="soak">
  Cover the <Qty ref="black-eyed-peas"/> with the <Qty ref="soak-water"/> and leave to
  soak for <Dur step="soak"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="peel-and-grind">
  Rub the soaked peas between your hands in their soaking water to loosen the skins,
  then skim off and discard the skins as they float free. Drain the peas and grind them
  with the <Qty ref="onion"/> and the <Qty ref="dried-shrimp"/> to a smooth, thick paste,
  working for <Dur step="peel-and-grind"/>. Season with the <Qty ref="salt"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="whip">
  Beat the paste hard with a wooden spoon or in a stand mixer for <Dur step="whip"/>,
  until it lightens noticeably in colour and holds a soft peak.
</Step>

<Step id="fry">
  Heat the <Qty ref="frying-oil"/> in a deep pan to a steady, gentle bubble. Shape
  spoonfuls of the paste into ovals with two spoons and lower them in. Fry for
  <Dur step="fry"/>, turning once, until deep orange-brown and cracked on the surface.
  Lift out and drain.
</Step>

<Step id="soak-beans">
  Soak the <Qty ref="kidney-beans"/> in plenty of cold water for
  <Dur step="soak-beans"/>, then drain.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-beans">
  Simmer the soaked beans in the <Qty ref="water"/> for
  <Dur step="cook-beans"/>, until tender, topping up with water as needed to
  keep them covered. Do not drain.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-rice">
  Add the <Qty ref="coconut-milk"/>, <Qty ref="garlic"/>,
  <Qty ref="spring-onion"/>, <Qty ref="thyme"/>, <Qty ref="allspice"/>,
  <Qty ref="scotch-bonnet"/>, <Qty ref="salt"/> and <Qty ref="black-pepper"/>
  to the beans and bring to a boil. Stir in the <Qty ref="rice"/>, cover,
  reduce the heat to its lowest setting and cook undisturbed for
  <Dur step="cook-rice"/>, until the liquid is absorbed and the rice is
  tender. Remove the scotch bonnet and spring onion.
</Step>

<Step id="rest">
  Take the pot off the heat and leave it covered for <Dur step="rest"/>
  before fluffing the rice with a fork.
</Step>

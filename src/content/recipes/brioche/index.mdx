<Step id="mix">
  Put the <Qty ref="flour"/>, <Qty ref="sugar"/>, <Qty ref="salt"/> and <Qty ref="yeast"/>
  into the bowl of a mixer fitted with a dough hook. Add the <Qty ref="milk"/> and the
  <Qty ref="egg-dough"/>, and mix on low speed until the dough comes together into a soft,
  sticky mass.
</Step>

<Step id="knead">
  Knead on low speed for <Dur step="knead"/>, until the dough is smooth and pulls away
  from the sides of the bowl in a sheet, though it will still stick to your fingers.
</Step>

<Step id="add-butter">
  Add the <Qty ref="butter"/> a few pieces at a time, letting each one be absorbed before
  the next goes in. Keep kneading for <Dur step="add-butter"/>, until the dough is glossy,
  elastic and comes away from the bowl again.
</Step>

<Step id="first-rise">
  Cover the bowl and leave the dough at room temperature for <Dur step="first-rise"/>,
  until it has doubled in size. Knock it back gently to release the air.
</Step>

<Step id="chill">
  Refrigerate the dough for <Dur step="chill"/>, until the butter has firmed and the
  dough can be lifted without sticking.
</Step>

<Step id="shape">
  Divide the dough into two pieces, the larger about three times the size of the smaller.
  Shape the larger into a smooth ball and press it into the bottom of a fluted tin. Make
  a deep dent in its centre with a floured finger, shape the smaller piece into a ball,
  and press it into the dent so that it forms the head. Brush the top with the
  <Qty ref="egg-wash"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="second-rise">
  Cover the tin loosely and leave the brioche to rise for <Dur step="second-rise"/>, until
  the dough reaches the rim of the tin and feels airy when touched.
</Step>

<Step id="heat-oven">
  Heat the oven to <Temp c={180}/> with a shelf in the lower middle while the brioche
  rises.
</Step>

<Step id="bake">
  Bake for <Dur step="bake"/>, until deep golden and a skewer pushed into the centre comes
  out clean. If the top colours before the middle is set, lay a sheet of foil over it.
</Step>

<Step id="cool">
  Turn the brioche out of the tin and leave it on a rack for <Dur step="cool"/> before
  slicing.
</Step>

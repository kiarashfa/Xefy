<Step id="make-pastry">
  Rub the <Qty ref="butter-pastry"/> into the <Qty ref="flour"/> with your fingertips until
  the mixture looks like fine breadcrumbs. Add the <Qty ref="salt"/>, the <Qty ref="yolk"/>
  and the <Qty ref="water"/>, and bring it together with a knife into a smooth disc. Do not
  knead it. This takes <Dur step="make-pastry"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="chill-pastry">
  Wrap the pastry and chill it for <Dur step="chill-pastry"/>, until firm enough to roll.
</Step>

<Step id="caramel">
  Put the <Qty ref="sugar"/> into a heavy, dry ovenproof pan of about <Len cm={22}/> across over a
  medium heat. Let it melt without stirring, then swirl until it is a deep amber. Take it off
  the heat and stir in the <Qty ref="butter-caramel"/>. This takes <Dur step="caramel"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="pack-apples">
  Stand the <Qty ref="apples"/> upright in the caramel, packed tightly in a circle with the
  rounded skin side down. Fill the centre with the pieces that do not fit.
  This takes <Dur step="pack-apples"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-apples">
  Return the pan to a medium heat and cook for <Dur step="cook-apples"/>, until the apples
  are tender right through and the juices have thickened to a glaze.
</Step>

<Step id="roll-pastry">
  While the apples cook, roll the pastry to a round a little larger than the pan, about
  as thick as a coin. Lift it onto the apples, tucking the edges down inside the rim.
  This takes <Dur step="roll-pastry"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="bake">
  Bake at <Temp c={190}/> for <Dur step="bake"/>, until the pastry is deep golden and crisp.
</Step>

<Step id="invert">
  Leave the tart to settle for a few minutes. Set a serving plate over the pan, hold them
  together, and turn them over in one firm movement. Lift off the pan and replace any apples
  that have moved. The whole process takes <Dur step="invert"/>.
</Step>

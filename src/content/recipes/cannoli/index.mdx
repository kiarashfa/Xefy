<Step id="make-dough">
  Rub the <Qty ref="lard"/> into the <Qty ref="flour"/> with the <Qty ref="sugar"/>, the
  <Qty ref="cocoa"/>, the <Qty ref="cinnamon"/> and the <Qty ref="salt"/>. Work in the
  <Qty ref="egg-dough"/>, the <Qty ref="marsala"/> and the <Qty ref="orange"/> until the
  dough comes together, then knead it until smooth and firm.
</Step>

<Step id="rest-dough">
  Wrap the dough and rest it for <Dur step="rest-dough"/>, so that it relaxes enough to roll
  thin without shrinking back.
</Step>

<Step id="drain-ricotta">
  Set the <Qty ref="ricotta"/> in a sieve over a bowl in the fridge for
  <Dur step="drain-ricotta"/>, so that the whey runs away and the filling holds its shape.
</Step>

<Step id="roll-shells">
  Roll the dough out thin and cut it into rounds or rectangles. Wrap each piece around a
  cannoli tube or a clean wooden dowel, brush the overlap with the <Qty ref="egg-seal"/>
  and press it closed. Work for <Dur step="roll-shells"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="fry-shells">
  Heat the <Qty ref="frying-oil"/> to <Temp c={180}/>. Lower the shells in a few at a time
  and fry for <Dur step="fry-shells"/>, until they are blistered and deep gold. Lift them
  out, drain them on kitchen paper, and slide the tubes out while the shells are still warm.
</Step>

<Step id="fill-cannoli">
  Beat the drained <Qty ref="ricotta"/> with the <Qty ref="icing-sugar"/> until smooth, then
  fold in the <Qty ref="chocolate"/>. Pipe or spoon the filling into each cold shell from
  both ends, roll the ends in the <Qty ref="pistachio"/>. Filling the shells takes
  <Dur step="fill-cannoli"/>, and they are served at once.
</Step>

<Step id="cube">
  Trim the <Qty ref="pork"/> of any excess fat and cut it into even cubes.
</Step>

<Step id="marinate">
  Whisk the <Qty ref="oil"/>, <Qty ref="lemon-juice"/>, <Qty ref="garlic"/>,
  <Qty ref="oregano"/>, <Qty ref="salt"/> and <Qty ref="pepper"/> together, add the pork
  and turn to coat every piece. Cover and marinate, refrigerated, for
  <Dur step="marinate"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="thread">
  Lift the pork from the marinade, shaking off the excess, and thread it tightly onto
  skewers.
</Step>

<Step id="grill">
  Grill the skewers over direct charcoal heat, turning often, for <Dur step="grill"/>,
  until well charred outside and cooked through with no pink remaining at the centre.
</Step>

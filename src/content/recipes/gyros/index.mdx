<Step id="slice-meat">
  Trim the <Qty ref="pork"/> of excess fat and slice it thinly against the grain.
</Step>

<Step id="marinate">
  Whisk the <Qty ref="oil-marinade"/>, <Qty ref="lemon-marinade"/>,
  <Qty ref="garlic-marinade"/>, <Qty ref="oregano"/>, <Qty ref="paprika"/>,
  <Qty ref="pepper"/> and <Qty ref="salt-marinade"/> together, add the sliced pork and
  turn to coat every piece. Cover and marinate, refrigerated, for
  <Dur step="marinate"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="stack">
  Thread the marinated pork tightly onto a vertical skewer, pressing each slice down
  onto the last, building an even, dense cylinder with no gaps.
</Step>

<Step id="roast">
  Roast the stacked pork close to a vertical heat source, turning it steadily, for
  <Dur step="roast"/>, until the outer surface is deeply browned and crisp and the
  centre is cooked through with no pink remaining.
</Step>

<Step id="make-tzatziki">
  Grate the <Qty ref="cucumber"/> and squeeze it dry in a clean cloth. Stir it into the
  <Qty ref="yoghurt"/> with the <Qty ref="dill"/>, <Qty ref="garlic-tzatziki"/>,
  <Qty ref="lemon-tzatziki"/>, <Qty ref="oil-tzatziki"/> and <Qty ref="salt-tzatziki"/>.
  Cover and refrigerate until needed.
</Step>

<Step id="shave">
  Shave the roasted pork downward off the spit in thin ribbons for <Dur step="shave"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="warm-bread">
  Warm the <Qty ref="pita"/> briefly over direct heat until soft and lightly charred in
  spots.
</Step>

<Step id="assemble">
  Fill the warm pita with the shaved pork, <Qty ref="tomato"/>, <Qty ref="onion"/> and a
  spoonful of the tzatziki.
</Step>

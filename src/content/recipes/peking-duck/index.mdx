  techniqueRationale:
    - stepId: scald-skin

    - stepId: air-dry

<Step id="scald-skin">
  Set the <Qty ref="duck"/> on a rack over a tray and pour the
  <Qty ref="boiling-water"/> slowly over the skin, working over the whole
  surface, for <Dur step="scald-skin"/>, until the skin visibly tightens. Pat
  completely dry.
</Step>

<Step id="season-duck">
  Rub the <Qty ref="salt"/> and <Qty ref="five-spice"/> inside the cavity only,
  taking <Dur step="season-duck"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="glaze-duck">
  Warm the <Qty ref="maltose"/>, <Qty ref="black-vinegar"/> and
  <Qty ref="glaze-water"/> together until the maltose dissolves, then brush
  evenly over the outside of the duck, taking <Dur step="glaze-duck"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="air-dry">
  Hang or rest the duck uncovered on a rack in a cold, airy spot, ideally in
  front of a fan, for <Dur step="air-dry"/>, until the skin feels completely
  dry and papery to the touch.
</Step>

<Step id="roast">
  Roast the duck breast-side up on a rack over a tray at <Temp c={180}/> for
  <Dur step="roast"/> in total, turning once partway through, until the skin is
  deep mahogany and crackling-crisp and the juices run clear.
</Step>

<Step id="make-pancake-dough">
  Meanwhile, pour the <Qty ref="pancake-water"/> into the
  <Qty ref="pancake-flour"/> and stir into a shaggy dough, then knead briefly
  for <Dur step="make-pancake-dough"/> until smooth. Cover and rest.
</Step>

<Step id="roll-and-cook-pancakes">
  Roll the dough into a log and divide into small, even pieces. Brush one side
  of each with the <Qty ref="pancake-oil"/> and press two together, oiled sides
  in, then roll each pair out thin as one round. Cook each round in a dry pan
  over medium heat for <Dur step="roll-and-cook-pancakes"/> in total, until
  small bubbles appear and the surface sets, then peel the two layers apart
  while still warm and stack them, covered, until needed.
</Step>

<Step id="rest-duck">
  Rest the duck for <Dur step="rest-duck"/> before carving.
</Step>

<Step id="carve">
  Carve the skin and breast meat into thin slices, taking
  <Dur step="carve"/>. Serve with the warm pancakes, <Qty ref="spring-onion"/>,
  <Qty ref="cucumber"/> and <Qty ref="hoisin"/>, for wrapping at the table.
</Step>

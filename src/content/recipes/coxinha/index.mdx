<Step id="poach-chicken">
  Dissolve the <Qty ref="bouillon"/> in the <Qty ref="poach-water"/> in a
  saucepan and bring to a simmer with the <Qty ref="onion"/> and
  <Qty ref="garlic"/>. Add the <Qty ref="chicken"/> and poach for
  <Dur step="poach-chicken"/>, until cooked through. Lift out the chicken,
  strain and reserve the broth.
</Step>

<Step id="make-filling">
  Shred the poached chicken finely and mix with the <Qty ref="cream-cheese"/>,
  <Qty ref="filling-salt"/> and <Qty ref="pepper"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="make-dough">
  Melt the <Qty ref="dough-butter"/> in the reserved broth and bring to a
  boil. Tip in the <Qty ref="dough-flour"/> and <Qty ref="dough-salt"/> all
  at once and stir vigorously over the heat for <Dur step="make-dough"/>,
  until the mixture forms a smooth, heavy dough that pulls cleanly away from
  the sides of the pan.
</Step>

<Step id="rest-dough">
  Turn the dough out and let it cool for <Dur step="rest-dough"/>, until cool
  enough to handle.
</Step>

<Step id="shape">
  Take portions of the dough and flatten each into a disc in your palm. Place
  a spoonful of the chicken filling in the centre, gather the dough around
  it and shape into a teardrop, smoothing the surface as you go, for
  <Dur step="shape"/> in total.
</Step>

<Step id="crumb">
  Dip each shaped coxinha in the <Qty ref="egg-wash"/>, then roll in the
  <Qty ref="breadcrumbs"/> until fully coated.
</Step>

<Step id="fry">
  Heat the <Qty ref="frying-oil"/> and fry the coxinhas in batches for
  <Dur step="fry"/>, until deep golden and crisp. Drain briefly before
  serving.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-sausages">
  Set the <Qty ref="sausages"/> in a frying pan with the <Qty ref="oil-sausages"/> over a
  medium heat and cook for <Dur step="cook-sausages"/>, turning regularly, until browned
  on all sides and cooked through.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-bacon">
  In a second pan, fry the <Qty ref="bacon"/> in the <Qty ref="oil-bacon"/> for
  <Dur step="cook-bacon"/>, turning once, until browned at the edges.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-pudding">
  In the same pan once the bacon is out, fry the <Qty ref="black-pudding"/> in the
  <Qty ref="oil-pudding"/> for <Dur step="cook-pudding"/>, turning once, until crisp at
  the edges and heated through.
</Step>

<Step id="grill-tomato-mushroom">
  Season the <Qty ref="tomato"/> with the <Qty ref="salt"/> and half the <Qty ref="pepper"/>
  and grill cut side up, and cook the <Qty ref="mushroom"/> in the <Qty ref="butter-mushroom"/>
  in a small pan, both for <Dur step="grill-tomato-mushroom"/>, until the tomato has softened
  and the mushroom has released its liquid and browned.
</Step>

<Step id="warm-beans">
  Heat the <Qty ref="beans"/> in a small saucepan over a low heat for
  <Dur step="warm-beans"/>, stirring occasionally, until hot through.
</Step>

<Step id="fry-bread">
  Fry the <Qty ref="bread"/> in the <Qty ref="butter-bread"/> in a clean pan for
  <Dur step="fry-bread"/> a side, until golden and crisp.
</Step>

<Step id="fry-egg">
  At the same time, fry the <Qty ref="egg"/> in the <Qty ref="oil-egg"/> in a separate pan
  for <Dur step="fry-egg"/>, until the white is fully set and the yolk is still soft.
  Season with the rest of the <Qty ref="pepper"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="plate">
  Arrange everything on a warmed plate together and serve at once.
</Step>

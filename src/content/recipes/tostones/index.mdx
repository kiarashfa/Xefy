<Step id="first-fry">
  Heat the <Qty ref="frying-oil"/> in a deep, heavy pan and fry the
  <Qty ref="plantain"/> for <Dur step="first-fry"/>, until pale gold and just
  tender but not browned. Lift out and drain, leaving the oil in the pan.
</Step>

<Step id="flatten">
  Press each piece flat with a tostonera, the base of a glass, or the flat
  side of a knife, to about half its original thickness. This takes about
  <Dur step="flatten"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="soak">
  Stir the <Qty ref="garlic"/> and <Qty ref="soak-salt"/> into the
  <Qty ref="soaking-water"/>, then dip each flattened piece briefly in the
  water and lift straight back out. This takes about <Dur step="soak"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="second-fry">
  Return the plantain to the hot oil and fry for <Dur step="second-fry"/>,
  until deeply golden and crisp at the edges. Drain and season with the
  <Qty ref="finishing-salt"/> while still hot.
</Step>

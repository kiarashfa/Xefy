<Step id="render-bacon">
  Render the <Qty ref="bacon"/> in a heavy pot over a medium heat for
  <Dur step="render-bacon"/>, until crisp. Lift out with a slotted spoon,
  leaving the fat behind.
</Step>

<Step id="soften-veg">
  Add the <Qty ref="onion"/> and <Qty ref="celery"/> to the fat and cook for
  <Dur step="soften-veg"/>, until softened.
</Step>

<Step id="make-roux">
  Stir in the <Qty ref="butter"/> and, once melted, the <Qty ref="flour"/>,
  cooking for <Dur step="make-roux"/> to remove the raw taste.
</Step>

<Step id="simmer-potatoes">
  Whisk in the <Qty ref="water"/> until smooth, add the <Qty ref="potato"/>
  and the <Qty ref="bay"/>, and simmer for <Dur step="simmer-potatoes"/>,
  until the potato is tender.
</Step>

<Step id="add-clams-dairy">
  Stir in the <Qty ref="clams"/>, the <Qty ref="milk"/> and the
  <Qty ref="cream"/>, and warm through gently for
  <Dur step="add-clams-dairy"/> without letting it boil. Season with the
  <Qty ref="pepper"/> and the <Qty ref="salt"/>, and finish with the reserved
  bacon scattered over each bowl.
</Step>

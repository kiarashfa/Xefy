<Step id="slice-onion">
  Halve the <Qty ref="onion"/> from root to tip and slice each half into thin half-moons.
  This takes <Dur step="slice-onion"/>, and the slices go into the pan in a few minutes'
  time, so keep them covered until then.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-rice">
  Rinse the <Qty ref="rice"/> in several changes of cold water until the water runs almost
  clear, then drain it well. Cook it in a heavy pan with a tight lid, and leave it covered
  for the last part of the time, for <Dur step="cook-rice"/> in all. Keep it warm until the
  beef is ready.
</Step>

<Step id="simmer-onion">
  Pour the strained dashi into a wide pan with the <Qty ref="soy"/>, the <Qty ref="mirin"/>
  and the <Qty ref="sugar"/>, and bring it to the boil. Add the <Qty ref="onion"/>, turn the
  heat down and simmer for <Dur step="simmer-onion"/>, until the onion is soft and
  translucent.
</Step>

<Step id="add-beef">
  Lay the <Qty ref="beef"/> over the onion one slice at a time, so that each piece reaches
  the broth separately. Skim off the grey scum that rises, and cook for
  <Dur step="add-beef"/>, only until the beef has lost its pink and the broth has thickened
  slightly around it.
</Step>

<Step id="serve">
  Divide the rice between two deep bowls. Spoon the beef and onion over the top along with
  some of the sauce, and add the <Qty ref="pickled-ginger"/> to one side. Serve at once.
</Step>

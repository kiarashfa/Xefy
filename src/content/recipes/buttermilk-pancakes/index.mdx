<Step id="mix-dry">
  Whisk the <Qty ref="flour"/>, <Qty ref="baking-powder"/>, <Qty ref="bicarb"/>,
  <Qty ref="salt"/> and <Qty ref="sugar"/> together in a large bowl.
</Step>

<Step id="mix-batter">
  Beat the <Qty ref="egg"/> into the <Qty ref="buttermilk"/> along with the
  <Qty ref="butter"/>, then pour this into the dry ingredients and stir over
  <Dur step="mix-batter"/>, mixing only until no dry streaks remain; the batter should
  still look a little lumpy.
</Step>

<Step id="rest-batter">
  Leave the batter to stand for <Dur step="rest-batter"/> before cooking.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-pancakes">
  Ladle small rounds of batter onto a hot, lightly buttered griddle. Cook until bubbles
  rise steadily to the surface and the edges look set, then flip and cook the second
  side until golden. Repeat with the remaining batter, spending <Dur step="cook-pancakes"/>
  in total, keeping cooked pancakes warm as they come off the griddle.
</Step>

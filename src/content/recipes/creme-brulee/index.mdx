<Step id="heat-cream">
  Warm the <Qty ref="cream"/> with the <Qty ref="vanilla"/> in a small pan over a moderate
  heat for <Dur step="heat-cream"/>, until it is steaming at the edges but not boiling.
</Step>

<Step id="whisk-yolks">
  Whisk the <Qty ref="yolks"/> with the <Qty ref="sugar-custard"/> for
  <Dur step="whisk-yolks"/>, until the mixture is pale and thick enough to fall in a ribbon.
</Step>

<Step id="combine">
  Pour the warm cream onto the yolks in a thin stream, whisking all the time, for
  <Dur step="combine"/>. Strain the custard into a jug and divide it between the ramekins.
</Step>

<Step id="bake">
  Set the ramekins in a deep tin, pour in hot water to come halfway up their sides, and
  bake at <Temp c={150}/> for <Dur step="bake"/>, until the edges are set and the centre
  still trembles when the tin is shaken gently.
</Step>

<Step id="chill">
  Lift the ramekins out of the water, cool them, and refrigerate them for
  <Dur step="chill"/> until fully set.
</Step>

<Step id="caramelise">
  Dust the top of each custard with the <Qty ref="sugar-top"/> in an even layer. Caramelise
  it with a kitchen blowtorch, or under a very hot grill, for <Dur step="caramelise"/>,
  until the sugar is a glassy, dark amber sheet. Serve within a few minutes.
</Step>

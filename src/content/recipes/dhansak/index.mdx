<Step id="fry-onion-garnish">
  Heat a little of the <Qty ref="ghee"/> in a pan and fry the <Qty ref="onion-garnish"/>
  over a medium heat for <Dur step="fry-onion-garnish"/>, until deep golden-brown and
  crisp. Lift out and set aside for garnish.
</Step>

<Step id="toast-spices">
  Toast the <Qty ref="cumin"/>, <Qty ref="cinnamon"/>, <Qty ref="cloves"/>,
  <Qty ref="cardamom"/> and <Qty ref="fenugreek-seed"/> in a dry pan for
  <Dur step="toast-spices"/>, until fragrant, then grind to a coarse powder.
</Step>

<Step id="simmer-stew">
  Combine the <Qty ref="lamb"/>, <Qty ref="toor-dal"/>, <Qty ref="red-lentils"/>,
  <Qty ref="pumpkin"/>, <Qty ref="potato"/>, <Qty ref="tomato"/>,
  <Qty ref="onion-stew"/>, <Qty ref="garlic"/>, <Qty ref="ginger"/>,
  <Qty ref="turmeric"/>, <Qty ref="dried-chilli"/>, the <Qty ref="water"/> and the
  <Qty ref="fine-sea-salt"/> in a large, heavy pot. Bring to the boil, then reduce to
  a bare simmer and cook, partly covered, for <Dur step="simmer-stew"/>, until the
  lamb is completely tender and the lentils have broken down.
</Step>

<Step id="blend-stew">
  Lift the lamb pieces out of the pot and set aside. Blend the remaining dal,
  vegetables and cooking liquid until smooth, working for <Dur step="blend-stew"/>,
  then return the lamb to the pot.
</Step>

<Step id="finish-stew">
  Stir the ground spice mix from the pan into the pot along with the
  <Qty ref="coriander-seed"/>, the <Qty ref="tamarind"/> and the
  <Qty ref="brown-sugar"/>, along with the remaining <Qty ref="ghee"/>. Simmer for <Dur step="finish-stew"/>, until the stew has
  thickened to the consistency of a loose porridge and tastes clearly sweet and
  sour. Scatter over the fried <Qty ref="onion-garnish"/> and the
  <Qty ref="coriander"/> to serve.
</Step>

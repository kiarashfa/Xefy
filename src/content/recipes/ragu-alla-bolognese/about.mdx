Ragù alla bolognese is a slow sauce of beef and pancetta, cooked with soffritto,
white wine, milk and tomato. It is made to be served on tagliatelle, and not on
spaghetti, which is not its traditional partner <Cite ref="wiki-bolognese"/>
<Cite ref="gazzetta-ragu"/>. The Gazzetta del Gusto lists tagliatelle first among
the pastas it is served with, ahead of tortelloni and balanzoni
<Cite ref="gazzetta-ragu"/>.

The official recipe was deposited with the Bologna Chamber of Commerce by the
city's delegation of the Accademia Italiana della Cucina on 17 October 1982
<Cite ref="gazzetta-ragu"/> <Cite ref="wiki-bolognese"/>. The Accademia revised the
text on 20 April 2023, when coarsely ground beef replaced the beef cut called
cartella and broth could be meat or vegetable <Cite ref="gazzetta-ragu"/>
<Cite ref="wiki-bolognese"/>. The recipe here follows the 2023 text.

The sauce's liquid base is white wine, milk and a little tomato, and the tomato
is added once the wine has evaporated <Cite ref="gazzetta-ragu"/> <Cite ref="wiki-bolognese"/>.
In the 2023 text the milk goes in halfway through the cooking and reduces away, which
is the order the recipe here follows <Cite ref="gazzetta-ragu"/>.

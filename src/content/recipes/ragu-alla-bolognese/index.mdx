<Step id="render-pancetta">
  Put the <Qty ref="pancetta"/> and the <Qty ref="olive-oil"/> into a heavy casserole over a
  moderate flame. Let the pancetta render for <Dur step="render-pancetta"/>, until its fat
  has run and the pieces are crisp at the edges.
</Step>

<Step id="soffritto">
  Add the <Qty ref="onion"/>, the <Qty ref="carrot"/> and the <Qty ref="celery"/>, and cook
  gently for <Dur step="soffritto"/>, until soft and sweet without any colour.
</Step>

<Step id="brown-beef">
  Raise the heat slightly, add the <Qty ref="beef"/> and break it up with a wooden spoon.
  Brown it thoroughly for <Dur step="brown-beef"/>, until no pink remains and the meat has
  taken on colour.
</Step>

<Step id="simmer-ragu">
  Pour in the <Qty ref="white-wine"/> and let it cook away completely. Stir in the
  <Qty ref="passata"/> and the <Qty ref="tomato-puree"/>, then the <Qty ref="stock"/>.
  Reduce the heat to the lowest that keeps a bare bubble, and cook with the lid ajar for
  <Dur step="simmer-ragu"/>, stirring now and then. Halfway through, add the
  <Qty ref="milk"/> and let it reduce away. Season with salt and the
  <Qty ref="pepper"/> near the end, when the sauce is thick and the fat has separated.
</Step>

<Step id="combine">
  Drain the pasta while it still has a little bite, and toss it through the ragù over a low
  flame with a splash of the reserved cooking water, so the sauce coats every strand. Serve
  at once.
</Step>

<Step id="boil-peas">
  Rinse the <Qty ref="split-peas"/> and simmer in plenty of water for
  <Dur step="boil-peas"/>, until completely soft, then drain well.
</Step>

<Step id="make-dough">
  Combine the <Qty ref="flour"/>, <Qty ref="baking-powder"/> and
  <Qty ref="salt-dough"/> in a large bowl, then work in the <Qty ref="water"/>
  and knead for <Dur step="make-dough"/>, until the dough is smooth and
  elastic.
</Step>

<Step id="rest-dough">
  Cover the dough and leave it to rest at room temperature for
  <Dur step="rest-dough"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="grind-filling">
  Grind the drained split peas with the <Qty ref="garlic"/>,
  <Qty ref="cumin"/> and <Qty ref="salt-filling"/> to a dry, fine paste,
  working in batches if needed; this takes about <Dur step="grind-filling"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="stuff-and-roll">
  Divide the dough into as many equal pieces as roti are being made. Flatten
  each piece into a cup, place a portion of the split pea filling in the
  centre, then draw the edges up and pinch them closed around it. Flatten each
  stuffed ball with the palm and roll it out gently into a thin, even round,
  taking care not to tear the dough or force filling out at the edges. This
  takes about <Dur step="stuff-and-roll"/> in total.
</Step>

<Step id="cook">
  Cook each round on a dry, very hot griddle for about a minute a side, until
  set and lightly spotted, then brush both sides with the <Qty ref="oil"/> and
  press gently around the edges with a folded cloth to encourage it to puff.
  Stack the cooked roti under a clean cloth to keep them soft while the rest
  cook, in total about <Dur step="cook"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="render-guanciale">
  While the water heats, put the <Qty ref="guanciale"/> into a cold, dry pan and set it
  over a low flame. Let it render for <Dur step="render-guanciale"/>, until the fat has
  run clear and the edges have gone glassy and crisp. Take the pan off the heat and
  leave the fat in it.
</Step>

<Step id="mix-eggs">
  Beat the <Qty ref="egg-yolks"/> and the <Qty ref="whole-egg"/> together with the
  <Qty ref="pecorino-sauce"/> and most of the <Qty ref="pepper"/> until the mixture is
  thick and pale and holds a ribbon.
</Step>

<Step id="combine">
  Tip the drained pasta into the pan with the guanciale and toss it through the warm
  fat. Wait half a minute for the pan to drop below <Temp c={70}/>, then add the egg
  mixture along with a splash of the reserved cooking water, tossing hard and
  continuously for <Dur step="combine"/>. It will look loose at first and then turn
  glossy. Serve at once, with the <Qty ref="pecorino-serve"/> and the rest of the
  pepper over the top.
</Step>

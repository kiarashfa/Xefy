<Step id="mix">
  Put the <Qty ref="flour"/> and the <Qty ref="salt"/> into a large bowl, add the
  <Qty ref="yeast"/>, and stir them together. Add the <Qty ref="water-dough"/> and the
  <Qty ref="butter"/>, and mix for <Dur step="mix"/>, until no dry flour remains and the dough
  is shaggy.
</Step>

<Step id="knead">
  Turn the dough onto a lightly floured bench and knead for <Dur step="knead"/>, until it is
  smooth, elastic and springs back when pressed.
</Step>

<Step id="first-rise">
  Put the dough in an oiled bowl, cover it, and leave it to rise for <Dur step="first-rise"/>,
  until it has doubled in size.
</Step>

<Step id="shape">
  Divide the dough into equal pieces and roll each one into a smooth ball. Roll each ball into a
  rope about <Len cm={60}/> long, leaving the middle a little thicker than the ends. Form each
  rope into a U, cross the two ends over each other, and press them onto the thick part of the
  U. Set the pretzels on a lined tray.
</Step>

<Step id="second-rise">
  Leave the pretzels to prove for <Dur step="second-rise"/>, until a fingertip pressed in
  leaves a dent that springs back slowly.
</Step>

<Step id="make-bath">
  Wear rubber gloves and safety goggles. Pour the <Qty ref="water-bath"/> into a glass,
  stainless steel or other non-aluminium bowl. Stir in the <Qty ref="lye"/> slowly, a little at
  a time, with a non-aluminium spoon, for <Dur step="make-bath"/>, until it has dissolved. Keep
  the bath away from aluminium foil and aluminium tins, which it attacks.
</Step>

<Step id="heat-oven">
  Heat the oven to <Temp c={200}/> with a shelf in the middle.
</Step>

<Step id="chill">
  Put the shaped pretzels in the fridge for <Dur step="chill"/>, until they have firmed enough to
  hold their shape in the bath.
</Step>

<Step id="dip">
  Lower each pretzel into the lye bath, one at a time, and dip it for <Dur step="dip"/> in
  total, turning it once. Lift each one out with a slotted spoon and let it drain back onto the
  lined tray.
</Step>

<Step id="top">
  Sprinkle the drained pretzels with the <Qty ref="pretzel-salt"/>, and slash the thick part of
  each knot once so that it opens in the oven.
</Step>

<Step id="bake">
  Bake for <Dur step="bake"/>, until deep mahogany brown and firm to the touch.
</Step>

<Step id="cool">
  Leave the pretzels on a rack for <Dur step="cool"/> before eating.
</Step>

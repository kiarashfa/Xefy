<Step id="mix-dough">
  Combine the <Qty ref="flour-dough"/>, <Qty ref="egg"/>, <Qty ref="water"/> and
  <Qty ref="dough-salt"/> in a bowl and bring together into a firm dough, then turn it
  out and knead for <Dur step="mix-dough"/> until smooth and no longer tacky.
</Step>

<Step id="rest-dough">
  Cover the dough and rest it for <Dur step="rest-dough"/> at room temperature, until it
  has relaxed and no longer springs back when pressed.
</Step>

<Step id="make-filling">
  Meanwhile, boil the <Qty ref="potato"/> in salted water until tender, then drain and
  mash while still hot. Melt the <Qty ref="onion-butter"/> and cook the
  <Qty ref="onion"/> gently until soft and golden. Combine the mashed potato, fried
  onion, <Qty ref="twarog"/>, <Qty ref="black-pepper"/> and <Qty ref="filling-salt"/>,
  mixing until even, then leave to cool for <Dur step="make-filling"/> in total.
</Step>

<Step id="roll-and-fill">
  Roll the rested dough out thinly on a surface dusted with the
  <Qty ref="flour-bench"/> and cut into rounds about <Len cm={8}/> across. Spoon
  filling into the centre of each round, fold in half and crimp the damp edge fully
  shut into a sealed half-moon.
</Step>

<Step id="boil">
  Drop the pierogi into a large pot of well-salted, boiling water and cook for
  <Dur step="boil"/>, until they float and the dough is cooked through. Lift out with
  a slotted spoon.
</Step>

<Step id="pan-fry">
  Melt the <Qty ref="serving-butter"/> in a wide pan over a medium-high heat and fry
  the boiled pierogi for <Dur step="pan-fry"/>, turning once, until golden on both
  flat sides.
</Step>

<Step id="serve">
  Serve at once with the <Qty ref="soured-cream"/> spooned over.
</Step>

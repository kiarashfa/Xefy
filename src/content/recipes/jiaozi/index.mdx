<Step id="mix-dough">
  Combine the <Qty ref="flour-dough"/>, <Qty ref="water"/> and <Qty ref="dough-salt"/> in
  a bowl and bring together into a shaggy dough, then turn it out and knead for
  <Dur step="mix-dough"/> until smooth and firm.
</Step>

<Step id="rest-dough">
  Cover the dough and rest it for <Dur step="rest-dough"/> at room temperature, until it
  has relaxed and no longer springs back when pressed.
</Step>

<Step id="make-filling">
  Meanwhile, combine the <Qty ref="pork"/>, <Qty ref="cabbage"/>, <Qty ref="ginger"/>,
  <Qty ref="spring-onion"/>, <Qty ref="soy-sauce"/>, <Qty ref="sesame-oil"/>,
  <Qty ref="pepper"/> and <Qty ref="filling-salt"/> in a bowl and stir hard in one
  direction until the mixture stiffens and holds together.
</Step>

<Step id="roll-and-fill">
  Roll the rested dough into a log and pinch off small, even pieces, keeping the rest
  covered. Roll each piece with the <Qty ref="flour-bench"/> underneath into a thin
  round about <Len cm={8}/> across, thicker at the centre than at the rim. Spoon
  filling into the middle of each round and pleat the edge closed into a sealed
  half-moon, standing it upright on its base.
</Step>

<Step id="boil">
  Drop the dumplings into a large pot of rapidly boiling water, working in batches so
  the pot is not crowded. Once the water returns to the boil, add a splash of cold
  water to check it, and repeat as the water threatens to boil over. Cook for
  <Dur step="boil"/> in total, until the dumplings float and the wrappers look
  taut and slightly translucent.
</Step>

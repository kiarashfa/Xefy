<Step id="prepare-vegetables">
  Peel the <Qty ref="shrimp"/> and devein them, leaving the tails on. Cut the
  <Qty ref="sweet-potato"/>, the <Qty ref="aubergine"/> and the <Qty ref="green-pepper"/> as
  the ingredient lines describe, and pat everything dry. This takes
  <Dur step="prepare-vegetables"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="heat-oil">
  Heat the <Qty ref="oil"/> in a deep, heavy pan to a steady moderate heat, checking it with a
  thermometer. It takes <Dur step="heat-oil"/> to come up to temperature.
</Step>

<Step id="simmer-tentsuyu">
  Pour the strained dashi into a small pan with the <Qty ref="soy"/> and the <Qty ref="mirin"/>.
  Bring it to the boil, then simmer for <Dur step="simmer-tentsuyu"/> to cook off the alcohol in the
  mirin. This is the tentsuyu. Divide it between two small bowls, and add a spoonful of the
  <Qty ref="daikon"/> and of the <Qty ref="ginger"/> to each.
</Step>

<Step id="make-batter">
  Put the <Qty ref="ice-water"/> into a bowl with the <Qty ref="egg"/> and beat them together
  lightly. Tip in the <Qty ref="flour"/> all at once and mix with chopsticks for only a few
  seconds, leaving lumps. This takes <Dur step="make-batter"/>, and the batter is used straight away.
</Step>

<Step id="fry-tempura">
  Dip each piece of <Qty ref="shrimp"/> and vegetable into the batter, let the excess drip off,
  and lower it into the oil. Fry in small batches for <Dur step="fry-tempura"/>, turning once, until
  the batter is pale gold and crisp, and the shrimp are just cooked through.
</Step>

<Step id="drain-tempura">
  Lift the pieces out onto a wire rack and leave them to drain for <Dur step="drain-tempura"/>
  before they are served, so the coating stays crisp. Serve at once with the tentsuyu.
</Step>

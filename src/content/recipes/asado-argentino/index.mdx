<Step id="light-fire">
  Light a wood or charcoal fire at one side of the grill and let it burn down
  for <Dur step="light-fire"/>, until it has stopped flaming and the coals
  are covered in grey ash. Rake a portion of the coals under the cooking
  grate to give a steady, moderate heat rather than a fierce one.
</Step>

<Step id="season">
  Season the <Qty ref="short-ribs"/> and <Qty ref="chorizo"/> all over with
  the <Qty ref="salt"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="grill-chorizo">
  Set the chorizo over the fire once the ribs are underway and grill for
  <Dur step="grill-chorizo"/>, turning occasionally, until the casing is
  deeply coloured and the sausage is cooked through. Move it to a board and
  serve it first, sliced, while the ribs continue.
</Step>

<Step id="grill-ribs">
  Set the short ribs bone side down over the fire and cook for
  <Dur step="grill-ribs"/>, without turning more than once or twice, until
  the fat has rendered, the surface is deeply browned and the meat between
  the bones is tender enough to pull away easily.
</Step>

<Step id="rest">
  Move the ribs to a board and rest for <Dur step="rest"/> before cutting.
</Step>

<Step id="serve">
  Cut the rack into individual ribs along the bones and serve at once.
</Step>

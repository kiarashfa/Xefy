<Step id="poach-fish">
  Gently poach the <Qty ref="catfish"/> in the <Qty ref="water"/> at a bare
  simmer for <Dur step="poach-fish"/>, until it flakes easily. Lift the fish out,
  flake it into large pieces discarding any bones, and reserve the poaching
  liquid.
</Step>

<Step id="soak-noodles">
  Cover the <Qty ref="rice-noodles"/> with warm water and leave to soak for
  <Dur step="soak-noodles"/>, until pliable.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-aromatics">
  Heat the <Qty ref="vegetable-oil"/> in a large pot and fry the
  <Qty ref="onion"/>, <Qty ref="garlic"/> and <Qty ref="ginger"/> until softened.
  Add the <Qty ref="lemongrass"/>, <Qty ref="turmeric"/>, <Qty ref="dried-chilli"/>
  and <Qty ref="dried-shrimp"/> and cook for <Dur step="cook-aromatics"/>, until
  fragrant.
</Step>

<Step id="build-broth">
  Pour in the reserved poaching liquid along with the <Qty ref="yellow-split-peas"/>.
  Simmer for most of <Dur step="build-broth"/>, until the peas have fully broken
  down. Whisk the <Qty ref="gram-flour"/> with a little of the hot broth to a
  smooth paste, stir it back in, then return the flaked catfish to the pot with
  the <Qty ref="fish-sauce"/> and <Qty ref="fine-sea-salt"/> and simmer gently for
  the remaining time, until thickened to the consistency of a loose gravy.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-noodles">
  Drop the soaked noodles into a pot of boiling water and cook for
  <Dur step="cook-noodles"/>, until just tender, then drain.
</Step>

<Step id="assemble">
  Divide the noodles between bowls, ladle the hot broth over the top, and finish
  each bowl with the <Qty ref="egg"/>, a squeeze of the <Qty ref="lime-juice"/> and
  the <Qty ref="coriander"/>. This takes <Dur step="assemble"/>.
</Step>

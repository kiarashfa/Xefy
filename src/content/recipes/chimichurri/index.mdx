<Step id="combine">
  Combine the <Qty ref="parsley"/>, <Qty ref="oregano"/>, <Qty ref="garlic"/>
  and <Qty ref="chilli-flakes"/> in a bowl. Stir in the <Qty ref="vinegar"/>
  and <Qty ref="water"/>, then whisk in the <Qty ref="oil"/> along with the
  <Qty ref="salt"/> and <Qty ref="pepper"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="rest">
  Let the sauce stand, covered, at room temperature for <Dur step="rest"/>
  before serving, so the garlic and herbs mellow into the vinegar and oil.
</Step>

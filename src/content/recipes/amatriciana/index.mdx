<Step id="render-guanciale">
  Put the <Qty ref="guanciale"/> into a cold, dry pan and set it over a low flame.
  Let it render for <Dur step="render-guanciale"/>, until the fat has run clear and
  the edges have gone glassy and crisp. Lift the batons out with a slotted spoon and
  leave the fat in the pan.
</Step>

<Step id="build-sauce">
  Add the <Qty ref="chilli"/> to the fat and let it colour for a moment, then pour in
  the <Qty ref="white-wine"/> and let it bubble until it has almost gone. Add the
  <Qty ref="tomatoes"/> and crush them against the side of the pan. Return the guanciale
  to the pan with the <Qty ref="pecorino-sauce"/> and simmer gently for
  <Dur step="build-sauce"/>, until the sauce has thickened and the fat sits on its
  surface in beads.
</Step>

<Step id="combine">
  Tip the drained pasta into the pan with the sauce and a splash of the reserved
  cooking water. Toss hard over a moderate heat for <Dur step="combine"/>, until each
  strand is coated and the sauce clings rather than pools. Serve at once, with the
  <Qty ref="pecorino-serve"/> and the <Qty ref="pepper"/> over the top.
</Step>

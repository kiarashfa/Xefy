<Step id="mix">
  Put the <Qty ref="flour"/>, the <Qty ref="sugar"/>, the <Qty ref="salt"/> and the
  <Qty ref="yeast"/> into a large bowl. Add the <Qty ref="yoghurt"/>, the <Qty ref="water"/>
  and the <Qty ref="ghee-dough"/>, and mix for <Dur step="mix"/>, until no dry flour remains
  and the dough is shaggy.
</Step>

<Step id="knead">
  Turn the dough onto a lightly floured bench and knead for <Dur step="knead"/>, until it is
  smooth, elastic and springs back when pressed.
</Step>

<Step id="first-rise">
  Put the dough in an oiled bowl, cover it, and leave it to rise for <Dur step="first-rise"/>,
  until it has doubled in size.
</Step>

<Step id="shape">
  Divide the dough into equal pieces and roll each one into a smooth ball. Flatten each ball
  and roll it into a teardrop about <Len cm={25}/> long, dusting the bench as needed. Set the
  naan on a floured cloth and cover them.
</Step>

<Step id="second-rise">
  Leave the shaped dough to prove for <Dur step="second-rise"/>, until a fingertip pressed in
  leaves a dent that springs back slowly.
</Step>

<Step id="heat-tandoor">
  Heat the tandoor until its walls are very hot, up to <Temp c={480}/>. Let it heat for
  <Dur step="heat-tandoor"/> before the first naan goes in.
</Step>

<Step id="bake">
  Press each naan onto the inner wall of the tandoor with a damp hand, so that it sticks.
  Bake for <Dur step="bake"/> in total, until it is blistered and browned in patches and
  lifts away from the wall.
</Step>

<Step id="brush">
  Brush each naan with the <Qty ref="ghee-brush"/> as it comes out of the tandoor, and scatter
  the <Qty ref="nigella"/> over it.
</Step>

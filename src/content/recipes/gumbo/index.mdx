<Step id="prep-veg">
  Dice the <Qty ref="onion"/>, <Qty ref="celery"/> and <Qty ref="green-pepper"/>, mince
  the <Qty ref="garlic"/>, and slice the <Qty ref="sausage"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="brown-meats">
  Heat half the <Qty ref="oil"/> in a large, heavy pot over a medium-high heat and
  brown the <Qty ref="chicken"/> and sausage for <Dur step="brown-meats"/>. Lift both
  out and set aside.
</Step>

<Step id="make-roux">
  Add the remaining oil to the pot along with the <Qty ref="flour"/> and set over a
  medium-low heat. Cook, stirring constantly, for <Dur step="make-roux"/>, until the
  roux turns a deep, dark mahogany, close to the colour of melted chocolate.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-trinity">
  Add the onion, celery and green pepper to the roux and cook for
  <Dur step="cook-trinity"/>, stirring often, until softened, adding the garlic in the
  last minute.
</Step>

<Step id="simmer-gumbo">
  Whisk in the <Qty ref="water"/> and add the <Qty ref="bay"/>. Return the chicken and
  sausage to the pot, bring to a simmer, and cook, partially covered, for
  <Dur step="simmer-gumbo"/>, until the chicken is tender and the roux has folded
  fully into the liquid.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-rice">
  While the gumbo simmers, combine the <Qty ref="rice"/> and <Qty ref="rice-water"/> in
  a covered saucepan, bring to the boil, then reduce to the lowest heat and cook for
  <Dur step="cook-rice"/>, until the water is absorbed. Leave covered off the heat
  until serving.
</Step>

<Step id="finish">
  Take the pot off the heat and discard the bay leaves. Stir in the
  <Qty ref="cayenne"/>, <Qty ref="black-pepper"/>, <Qty ref="salt"/> and
  <Qty ref="file-powder"/>. Ladle over the rice to serve.
</Step>

<Step id="prep-veg">
  Dice the <Qty ref="onion"/>, <Qty ref="celery"/> and <Qty ref="green-pepper"/>, mince
  the <Qty ref="garlic"/>, and slice the <Qty ref="okra"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="make-roux">
  Combine the <Qty ref="oil"/> and <Qty ref="flour"/> in a large, heavy pot and set
  over a medium-low heat. Cook, stirring constantly, for <Dur step="make-roux"/>, until
  the roux turns a deep, dark mahogany, close to the colour of melted chocolate.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-trinity-okra">
  Add the onion, celery, green pepper and okra to the roux and cook for
  <Dur step="cook-trinity-okra"/>, stirring often, until softened, adding the garlic in
  the last minute.
</Step>

<Step id="simmer-gumbo">
  Whisk in the <Qty ref="water"/> and add the <Qty ref="bay"/>. Bring to a simmer and
  cook, partially covered, for <Dur step="simmer-gumbo"/>, until the roux has folded
  fully into the liquid and the okra is tender.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-rice">
  While the gumbo simmers, combine the <Qty ref="rice"/> and <Qty ref="rice-water"/> in
  a covered saucepan, bring to the boil, then reduce to the lowest heat and cook for
  <Dur step="cook-rice"/>, until the water is absorbed. Leave covered off the heat
  until serving.
</Step>

<Step id="add-seafood">
  Discard the bay leaves. Bring the gumbo back to a simmer and add the
  <Qty ref="shrimp"/> and <Qty ref="crab"/>, cooking until the shrimp turn pink. Stir
  in the <Qty ref="oyster"/> and cook for one more minute, then take off the heat and
  season with the <Qty ref="cayenne"/>, <Qty ref="black-pepper"/> and
  <Qty ref="salt"/>. Ladle over the rice to serve.
</Step>

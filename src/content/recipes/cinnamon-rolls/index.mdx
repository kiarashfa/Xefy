<Step id="make-dough">
  Stir the <Qty ref="yeast"/> and the <Qty ref="sugar-dough"/> into the warm
  <Qty ref="milk"/> and leave until foamy. Beat in the <Qty ref="egg"/> and the
  <Qty ref="butter-dough"/>, then add the <Qty ref="flour"/> and the <Qty ref="salt"/>
  and bring together into a shaggy dough over <Dur step="make-dough"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="knead">
  Knead the dough on a lightly floured surface for <Dur step="knead"/>, until smooth
  and elastic.
</Step>

<Step id="first-prove">
  Put the dough in an oiled bowl, cover, and leave it to rise somewhere warm for
  <Dur step="first-prove"/>, until doubled in size.
</Step>

<Step id="mix-filling">
  While the dough proves, beat the <Qty ref="butter-filling"/> with the
  <Qty ref="brown-sugar"/> and the <Qty ref="cinnamon"/> into a spreadable paste, which
  takes <Dur step="mix-filling"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="roll-and-fill">
  Turn the risen dough out and roll it into a large rectangle. Spread the filling
  evenly over it, leaving one long edge bare, spending <Dur step="roll-and-fill"/> on
  an even layer right to the other three edges. Roll the dough up tightly toward the
  bare edge, finishing with the seam underneath.
</Step>

<Step id="cut-and-arrange">
  Slide a length of thread under the log, cross the ends over the top and pull to slice
  through cleanly, working along the log to cut even rolls over
  <Dur step="cut-and-arrange"/>. Arrange the rolls cut-side up in a buttered baking
  dish, leaving a little space between them to expand.
</Step>

<Step id="second-prove">
  Cover the dish and leave the rolls to prove again for <Dur step="second-prove"/>,
  until visibly puffed and touching one another.
</Step>

<Step id="bake">
  Bake at <Temp c={180}/> for <Dur step="bake"/>, until golden on top and cooked
  through in the centre.
</Step>

<Step id="frost">
  While the rolls bake, beat the <Qty ref="cream-cheese"/> with the
  <Qty ref="butter-icing"/>, the <Qty ref="icing-sugar"/> and the <Qty ref="vanilla"/>
  until smooth, over <Dur step="frost"/>. Spread it over the rolls while they are still
  warm, so it melts slightly into the top.
</Step>

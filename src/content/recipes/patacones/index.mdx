<Step id="make-hogao">
  Heat the <Qty ref="hogao-oil"/> in a small pan over a low heat and cook
  the <Qty ref="onion"/> and <Qty ref="garlic"/> until soft, then add the
  <Qty ref="tomato"/>, <Qty ref="cumin"/> and <Qty ref="hogao-salt"/> and
  cook gently for <Dur step="make-hogao"/>, until the tomato has completely
  broken down into a thick sauce.
</Step>

<Step id="first-fry">
  Meanwhile, heat the <Qty ref="frying-oil"/> in a deep, heavy pan and fry
  the <Qty ref="plantain"/> for <Dur step="first-fry"/>, until pale gold and
  just tender but not browned. Lift out and drain, leaving the oil in the
  pan.
</Step>

<Step id="flatten">
  Press each piece flat with a tostonera, the base of a glass, or the flat
  side of a knife, into a wide disc. This takes about <Dur step="flatten"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="second-fry">
  Return the plantain to the hot oil and fry for <Dur step="second-fry"/>,
  until deeply golden and crisp at the edges. Drain, season with the
  <Qty ref="finishing-salt"/>, and serve with the hogao spooned over or
  alongside.
</Step>

<Step id="make-dough">
  Combine the <Qty ref="flour"/>, <Qty ref="pastry-salt"/> and <Qty ref="pastry-sugar"/>
  in a large bowl. Add the <Qty ref="butter"/> and rub or cut it into the flour until the
  largest pieces are the size of small peas. Sprinkle in the <Qty ref="ice-water"/> a
  little at a time, turning the mixture with a fork until it holds together when pressed,
  then divide it into two discs. The whole of <Dur step="make-dough"/> should leave streaks
  of butter still visible rather than a smooth paste.
</Step>

<Step id="chill-dough">
  Wrap both discs and refrigerate them for <Dur step="chill-dough"/>, until firm but not
  hard.
</Step>

<Step id="make-filling">
  Meanwhile toss the <Qty ref="apples"/> with the <Qty ref="filling-sugar"/>, the
  <Qty ref="brown-sugar"/>, the <Qty ref="cinnamon"/>, the <Qty ref="nutmeg"/>, the
  <Qty ref="filling-lemon-juice"/>, the <Qty ref="cornflour"/> and the
  <Qty ref="filling-salt"/> in a large bowl, and leave it to stand while the dough
  finishes chilling.
</Step>

<Step id="roll-bottom">
  Roll one disc of dough into a round wide enough to line a pie dish with an overhang all
  round, using <Dur step="roll-bottom"/>. Ease it into the dish without stretching it, which
  only pulls the pastry back as it bakes.
</Step>

<Step id="fill-pie">
  Spoon the apple filling into the lined dish, piling it slightly domed in the centre, and
  scatter the <Qty ref="dotting-butter"/> over the top. This takes <Dur step="fill-pie"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="top-pie">
  Roll the second disc and lay it over the filling. Trim the edges, fold them under and
  crimp the rim to seal, then cut several slits across the top to let steam escape. Brush
  the crust with the <Qty ref="egg-wash"/> beaten with the <Qty ref="egg-wash-milk"/>, using
  the whole of <Dur step="top-pie"/> to work carefully round the rim without tearing it.
</Step>

<Step id="chill-shaped">
  Rest the assembled pie in the fridge for <Dur step="chill-shaped"/> so the crust firms
  back up before it meets the oven.
</Step>

<Step id="bake">
  Bake at <Temp c={220}/> for twenty minutes, then lower the oven to <Temp c={190}/> and
  continue for the remainder of <Dur step="bake"/>, until the crust is deep golden and the
  filling bubbles up through the vents.
</Step>

<Step id="cool">
  Leave the pie to cool completely, for <Dur step="cool"/>, before cutting. The filling
  keeps setting as it cools, and a warm pie will not hold a slice.
</Step>

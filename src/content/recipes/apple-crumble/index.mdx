<Step id="toss-apples">
  Toss the <Qty ref="apple"/> with the <Qty ref="filling-sugar"/>, the <Qty ref="cinnamon"/>
  and the <Qty ref="lemon-juice"/> and tip into a baking dish.
</Step>

<Step id="rub-topping">
  Rub the <Qty ref="butter"/> into the <Qty ref="flour"/> and the <Qty ref="salt"/> with
  the fingertips until the mixture looks like coarse crumbs, then stir in the
  <Qty ref="topping-sugar"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="assemble">
  Scatter the topping evenly over the apples, right to the edges of the dish, without
  pressing it down.
</Step>

<Step id="bake">
  Bake at <Temp c={190}/> for <Dur step="bake"/>, until the top is deep golden and the
  filling bubbles up around the edges.
</Step>

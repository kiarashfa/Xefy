<Step id="butter-bread">
  Spread the <Qty ref="butter"/> evenly over one side of each slice of the
  <Qty ref="bread"/>, all the way to the edges.
</Step>

<Step id="assemble">
  Lay the <Qty ref="cheese"/> between the slices, buttered sides facing
  outward.
</Step>

<Step id="cook">
  Set the sandwich in a cold pan over a low heat and cook for
  <Dur step="cook"/>, turning once, until both faces are deep golden brown
  and the cheese has melted completely through to the middle.
</Step>

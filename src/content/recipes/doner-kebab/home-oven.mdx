<Step id="mix-meat">
  Combine the <Qty ref="beef-mince"/>, <Qty ref="lamb-mince"/>, <Qty ref="garlic"/>,
  <Qty ref="paprika"/>, <Qty ref="oregano"/>, <Qty ref="pepper"/>, <Qty ref="salt"/>
  and the <Qty ref="onion-mix"/>. Work the mixture with your hands until it turns
  sticky and slightly emulsified — this is what holds it together once sliced.
</Step>

<Step id="press-loaf">
  Pack the mixture firmly into a loaf tin lined with baking paper, pressing out every
  air pocket and smoothing the top.
</Step>

<Step id="bake">
  Cover the tin tightly with foil and bake for <Dur step="bake"/>, until the centre is
  cooked through with no pink remaining.
</Step>

<Step id="rest">
  Leave the loaf to rest in its tin for <Dur step="rest"/> — slicing it hot breaks it
  apart.
</Step>

<Step id="slice">
  Turn the loaf out and slice it very thinly.
</Step>

<Step id="crisp">
  Sear the slices in a dry, very hot pan for <Dur step="crisp"/>, until the edges
  crisp and char in patches.
</Step>

<Step id="warm-bread">
  Warm the <Qty ref="lavash"/> briefly over direct heat until soft and pliable.
</Step>

<Step id="assemble">
  Fill the warmed lavash with the crisped meat, <Qty ref="tomato"/>,
  <Qty ref="onion-garnish"/> and <Qty ref="lettuce"/>, and roll it closed.
</Step>

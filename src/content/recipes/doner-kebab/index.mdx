<Step id="slice-meat">
  Trim the <Qty ref="lamb"/> and <Qty ref="beef"/> of any sinew and slice both very
  thinly against the grain.
</Step>

<Step id="marinate">
  Combine the <Qty ref="yoghurt"/>, <Qty ref="oil"/>, <Qty ref="garlic"/>,
  <Qty ref="paprika"/>, <Qty ref="oregano"/>, <Qty ref="pepper"/> and <Qty ref="salt"/>,
  add the sliced meat and turn to coat every piece. Cover and marinate, refrigerated,
  for <Dur step="marinate"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="stack">
  Thread the marinated slices tightly onto a vertical skewer, alternating lamb and
  beef and pressing each layer down onto the last, building an even, dense cylinder
  with no gaps.
</Step>

<Step id="roast">
  Roast the stacked meat close to a vertical heat source, turning it steadily, for
  <Dur step="roast"/>, until the outer surface is deeply browned and crisp and the
  centre is cooked through with no pink remaining.
</Step>

<Step id="shave">
  Shave the roasted meat downward off the spit in thin ribbons for <Dur step="shave"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="warm-bread">
  Warm the <Qty ref="lavash"/> briefly over direct heat until soft and pliable.
</Step>

<Step id="assemble">
  Fill the warmed lavash with the shaved meat, <Qty ref="tomato"/>, <Qty ref="onion"/>
  and <Qty ref="lettuce"/>, and roll it closed.
</Step>

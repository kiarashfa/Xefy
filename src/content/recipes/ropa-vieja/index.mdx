<Step id="braise-beef">
  Put the <Qty ref="beef"/> in a pot with the <Qty ref="stock"/> and
  <Qty ref="bay-leaf"/>, bring to a simmer, then cover and cook at a bare
  simmer for <Dur step="braise-beef"/>, until fully tender.
</Step>

<Step id="shred">
  Lift the beef out, reserving the braising liquid, and pull it apart into
  fine strands with two forks for <Dur step="shred"/>. Discard the bay leaf.
</Step>

<Step id="sofrito">
  Meanwhile, heat the <Qty ref="olive-oil"/> in a wide pan and cook the
  <Qty ref="onion"/>, <Qty ref="green-pepper"/>, <Qty ref="red-pepper"/> and
  <Qty ref="garlic"/> for <Dur step="sofrito"/>, until softened.
</Step>

<Step id="simmer">
  Stir the <Qty ref="tinned-tomato"/>, <Qty ref="cumin"/>,
  <Qty ref="oregano"/>, <Qty ref="salt"/> and <Qty ref="black-pepper"/> into
  the sofrito, then add the shredded beef with enough of the reserved
  braising liquid to keep it moist. Simmer for <Dur step="simmer"/>, then stir
  in the <Qty ref="olives"/> and <Qty ref="capers"/> just before serving.
</Step>

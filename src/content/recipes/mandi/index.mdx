<Step id="marinate">
  Combine the <Qty ref="lamb"/> with the <Qty ref="yoghurt"/>, <Qty ref="garlic"/>,
  <Qty ref="ginger"/>, <Qty ref="turmeric"/>, <Qty ref="cumin"/>,
  <Qty ref="black-pepper"/>, <Qty ref="paprika"/> and the <Qty ref="marinade-salt"/>,
  turning the pieces to coat them evenly. This takes <Dur step="marinate"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="rest-marinade">
  Cover and refrigerate the lamb for <Dur step="rest-marinade"/>, so the yoghurt and
  spices work into the meat.
</Step>

<Step id="build-rice-base">
  Melt the <Qty ref="ghee"/> in a heavy pot with a tight-fitting lid and cook the
  <Qty ref="onion"/> until soft. Add the <Qty ref="cardamom"/>, <Qty ref="cloves"/>,
  <Qty ref="cinnamon"/>, <Qty ref="bay-leaf"/>, the <Qty ref="rice-salt"/> and the
  <Qty ref="stock-water"/>, and bring to a simmer. Rinse the <Qty ref="rice"/> and
  stir it in. This takes <Dur step="build-rice-base"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="suspend-and-cook">
  Set a wire rack or trivet inside the pot, just above the level of the rice, and
  arrange the marinated lamb on it in a single layer. Cover the pot tightly and
  cook over the lowest possible heat for <Dur step="suspend-and-cook"/>, undisturbed,
  until the lamb is completely tender and the rice below has absorbed the stock and
  the meat's dripping juices.
</Step>

<Step id="smoke">
  Lift out the rack of lamb and set it aside, covered. Nestle a small heatproof
  bowl into the rice, put a piece of very hot lit charcoal into it, and pour a
  little ghee over the coal so it smokes at once. Cover the pot immediately and
  let the smoke steep through the rice for <Dur step="smoke"/>, then lift out the
  bowl and any spent coal.
</Step>

<Step id="rest-and-serve">
  Fluff the smoked rice, spread it over a platter, and set the lamb on top. This
  takes <Dur step="rest-and-serve"/>.
</Step>

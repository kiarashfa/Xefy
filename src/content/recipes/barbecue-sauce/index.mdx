<Step id="combine">
  Combine the <Qty ref="ketchup"/>, <Qty ref="molasses"/>,
  <Qty ref="vinegar"/>, <Qty ref="brown-sugar"/>, <Qty ref="worcestershire"/>,
  <Qty ref="mustard-powder"/>, <Qty ref="paprika"/>, <Qty ref="pepper"/> and
  <Qty ref="salt"/> in a heavy saucepan and whisk until smooth.
</Step>

<Step id="simmer">
  Bring the sauce to a bare simmer and cook, stirring often, for
  <Dur step="simmer"/>, until it has thickened enough to hold a trail when a
  spoon is drawn through it.
</Step>

<Step id="cool">
  Take the sauce off the heat and let it cool for <Dur step="cool"/> before
  bottling or serving; it will thicken further as it cools.
</Step>

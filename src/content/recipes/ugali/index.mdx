<Step id="boil-water">
  Bring the <Qty ref="water"/> and <Qty ref="salt"/> to a rolling boil in a heavy pot.
  This takes about <Dur step="boil-water"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="add-meal">
  Reduce the heat slightly and add the <Qty ref="maize-meal"/> in a thin, steady stream,
  stirring constantly with a wooden spoon to stop lumps forming. This takes
  <Dur step="add-meal"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="simmer">
  Cover and simmer over a low heat for <Dur step="simmer"/>, stirring every so often to
  stop the bottom catching, until the mixture has thickened into a dense mass.
</Step>

<Step id="work">
  Uncover and beat and fold the dough hard against the side of the pot for
  <Dur step="work"/>, over the heat the whole time, until it is very stiff, smooth and
  turns over as one piece with no loose porridge left at the bottom. Turn out onto a
  plate as a single mound and serve hot.
</Step>

<Step id="make-yaji">
  Combine the ground <Qty ref="peanuts"/>, <Qty ref="paprika"/>, <Qty ref="chilli"/>,
  <Qty ref="garlic"/>, <Qty ref="ginger"/>, <Qty ref="bouillon"/> and <Qty ref="salt"/>
  in a bowl and mix into an even, dry spice rub. Work for <Dur step="make-yaji"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="coat">
  Brush the sliced <Qty ref="beef"/> all over with the <Qty ref="oil"/>, then coat it
  thickly and evenly in the yaji, pressing the spice onto the meat. Thread the coated
  slices onto skewers, working for <Dur step="coat"/> in total.
</Step>

<Step id="rest">
  Leave the skewers to rest for <Dur step="rest"/> so the rub sets against the oiled
  meat.
</Step>

<Step id="grill">
  Grill the skewers over fierce, direct charcoal heat for <Dur step="grill"/>, turning
  often, until the crust is dry and lightly charred and the beef is cooked through.
  Serve with the sliced <Qty ref="onion"/> and <Qty ref="tomato"/> alongside.
</Step>

<Step id="prep-wings">
  Pat the <Qty ref="wings"/> completely dry with paper towels and season all over with
  the <Qty ref="salt"/> and <Qty ref="black-pepper"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="heat-oil">
  Heat the <Qty ref="oil"/> in a deep, heavy pot or a deep-fat fryer to
  <Temp c={190}/>.
</Step>

<Step id="fry-wings">
  Fry the wings in batches for <Dur step="fry-wings"/>, turning occasionally, until deep
  golden and cooked through. Lift out onto a wire rack to drain.
</Step>

<Step id="make-sauce">
  While the wings fry, melt the <Qty ref="butter"/> with the <Qty ref="hot-sauce"/> in a
  small saucepan over a low heat, stirring until smooth.
</Step>

<Step id="toss">
  Tip the hot wings into a large bowl, pour over the sauce, and toss to coat evenly.
  Serve at once.
</Step>

<Step id="mix-dough">
  Put the <Qty ref="flour"/>, <Qty ref="sugar"/>, <Qty ref="salt"/> and <Qty ref="yeast"/>
  into a bowl. Add the <Qty ref="water"/>, the <Qty ref="milk"/> and the
  <Qty ref="butter-dough"/>, and mix until it comes together. Knead for
  <Dur step="mix-dough"/>, until smooth and elastic.
</Step>

<Step id="rest-dough">
  Shape the dough into a square, wrap it, and chill it for <Dur step="rest-dough"/>, so
  that it firms and the gluten relaxes.
</Step>

<Step id="beat-butter">
  Beat the <Qty ref="butter-laminate"/> between two sheets of baking paper with a rolling
  pin, shaping it into a flat square about two thirds the size of the dough. Keep it cold
  but pliable. The butter and the dough must have the same firmness.
</Step>

<Step id="first-turn">
  Roll the dough into a rectangle, lay the butter in the centre, and fold the sides over
  it so that it is fully enclosed, sealing the edges. Roll the parcel into a long
  rectangle and fold it in thirds, like a letter. This takes <Dur step="first-turn"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="rest-first">
  Wrap the dough and chill it for <Dur step="rest-first"/>, so the layers relax and the
  butter firms again.
</Step>

<Step id="second-turn">
  Turn the dough so that a folded edge faces you, roll it into a long rectangle, and fold
  it in thirds again.
</Step>

<Step id="rest-second">
  Wrap the dough and chill it for <Dur step="rest-second"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="third-turn">
  Give the dough a third turn in the same way: roll it out, fold it in thirds, and wrap it.
</Step>

<Step id="chill-laminated">
  Chill the laminated dough for <Dur step="chill-laminated"/>, until it is firm enough to
  cut cleanly.
</Step>

<Step id="shape">
  Roll the dough into a long rectangle of even thickness, trim the edges straight, and cut
  it into long triangles. Roll each triangle up from the wide end, then curve the two ends
  into a crescent. Lay the croissants on a lined tray with the tip underneath, well apart.
</Step>

<Step id="proof">
  Leave the shaped croissants somewhere warm but not hot for <Dur step="proof"/>, until they
  are puffed and soft and clearly larger, with the layers still visible on the surface.
</Step>

<Step id="heat-oven">
  Heat the oven to <Temp c={200}/> with a shelf in the middle while the croissants proof.
</Step>

<Step id="glaze">
  Brush the croissants lightly with the <Qty ref="egg"/>, keeping it off the cut edges,
  because glaze that runs down the sides seals the layers and stops them rising.
</Step>

<Step id="bake">
  Bake for <Dur step="bake"/>, until deep golden and crisp, turning the tray halfway
  through if your oven heats unevenly.
</Step>

<Step id="cool">
  Leave the croissants on the tray for <Dur step="cool"/>, so the layers set before they
  are eaten.
</Step>

<Step id="fry-onions">
  Heat a third of the <Qty ref="ghee"/> in a wide pan and fry the
  <Qty ref="onion-gravy"/> and <Qty ref="onion-layer"/> together over a medium heat,
  stirring often, for <Dur step="fry-onions"/>, until deep golden-brown. Lift out and
  drain, keeping the two portions separate.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-meat">
  Return the fried <Qty ref="onion-gravy"/> to the pan with the <Qty ref="lamb"/>,
  <Qty ref="yoghurt"/>, <Qty ref="ginger"/>, <Qty ref="garlic"/>,
  <Qty ref="turmeric"/>, <Qty ref="chilli"/>, <Qty ref="lemon-juice"/>, the
  <Qty ref="gravy-salt"/> and the <Qty ref="cook-water"/>. Cover and simmer,
  stirring occasionally, for <Dur step="cook-meat"/>, until the lamb is tender and
  the gravy has thickened around it.
</Step>

<Step id="parboil-rice">
  Rinse the <Qty ref="rice"/> until the water runs clear, then bring the
  <Qty ref="boil-water"/> to the boil with the <Qty ref="rice-salt"/>, the
  <Qty ref="cardamom"/>, <Qty ref="cloves"/>, <Qty ref="cinnamon"/>,
  <Qty ref="bay-leaf"/> and <Qty ref="shahi-jeera"/>. Add the rinsed rice and boil
  for <Dur step="parboil-rice"/>, until the grains are still firm at the core, then
  drain.
</Step>

<Step id="layer-and-seal">
  Spread the cooked lamb and its gravy across the base of a heavy, wide pot. Mound
  the drained rice on top, then scatter over the fried <Qty ref="onion-layer"/>,
  the <Qty ref="coriander"/> and the <Qty ref="mint"/>. Bloom the
  <Qty ref="saffron"/> in the warm <Qty ref="saffron-water"/> and drizzle it over the
  rice along with the remaining <Qty ref="ghee"/>. Seal the lid tightly, sealing
  the rim with a strip of dough or a double layer of foil if it does not fit
  snugly. This takes <Dur step="layer-and-seal"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="dum-finish">
  Set the sealed pot over the lowest possible heat for <Dur step="dum-finish"/>.
  Since the meat is already cooked, this dum only has to finish the rice and let
  the two layers trade steam and aroma.
</Step>

<Step id="rest-and-serve">
  Break the seal away from the heat and let the biryani rest briefly before
  turning it out, working from the bottom up so the rice, meat and crisped onion
  come together in each portion. This takes <Dur step="rest-and-serve"/>.
</Step>

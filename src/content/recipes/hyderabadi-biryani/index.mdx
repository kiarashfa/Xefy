  techniqueRationale:
    - stepId: dum-cook
      text: In kacchi biryani the meat goes into the pot raw. It cooks entirely inside the sealed pot, in the steam given off by the rice and its own juices, rather than in a pan beforehand — the technique that separates kacchi from a pakki biryani made with pre-cooked meat.
  commonPitfalls:
    - stepId: parboil-rice

<Step id="fry-onions">
  Heat half of the <Qty ref="ghee"/> in a wide pan and fry the <Qty ref="onion-marinade"/>
  and <Qty ref="onion-layer"/> together over a medium heat, stirring often, for
  <Dur step="fry-onions"/>, until deep golden-brown. Lift out and drain, keeping the
  two portions separate.
</Step>

<Step id="mix-marinade">
  Combine the <Qty ref="lamb"/>, <Qty ref="yoghurt"/>, <Qty ref="ginger"/>,
  <Qty ref="garlic"/>, <Qty ref="turmeric"/>, <Qty ref="chilli"/>,
  <Qty ref="lemon-juice"/>, the <Qty ref="marinade-salt"/> and the fried
  <Qty ref="onion-marinade"/> in a large bowl, working the marinade into the meat
  by hand for <Dur step="mix-marinade"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="marinate">
  Cover the bowl and refrigerate for <Dur step="marinate"/>, so the yoghurt and
  spices work into the meat before it goes anywhere near heat.
</Step>

<Step id="parboil-rice">
  Rinse the <Qty ref="rice"/> until the water runs clear, then bring the
  <Qty ref="boil-water"/> to the boil with the <Qty ref="rice-salt"/>, the
  <Qty ref="cardamom"/>, <Qty ref="cloves"/>, <Qty ref="cinnamon"/>,
  <Qty ref="bay-leaf"/> and <Qty ref="shahi-jeera"/>. Add the rinsed rice and boil
  for <Dur step="parboil-rice"/>, until the grains are still firm at the core, then
  drain.
</Step>

<Step id="layer-and-seal">
  Spread the marinated lamb in an even layer across the base of a heavy, wide pot.
  Mound the drained rice on top, then scatter over the fried <Qty ref="onion-layer"/>,
  the <Qty ref="coriander"/> and the <Qty ref="mint"/>. Bloom the <Qty ref="saffron"/>
  in the warm <Qty ref="saffron-water"/> and drizzle it over the rice along with the
  remaining <Qty ref="ghee"/>. Seal the lid tightly, sealing the rim with a strip of
  dough or a double layer of foil if it does not fit snugly. This takes
  <Dur step="layer-and-seal"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="dum-cook">
  Set the sealed pot over the lowest possible heat, ideally on top of a heat
  diffuser, and leave it completely undisturbed for <Dur step="dum-cook"/>. The
  meat cooks through entirely in the steam trapped under the seal.
</Step>

<Step id="rest-and-serve">
  Break the seal away from the heat and let the biryani rest briefly before
  turning it out, working from the bottom up so the rice, meat and crisped onion
  come together in each portion. This takes <Dur step="rest-and-serve"/>.
</Step>

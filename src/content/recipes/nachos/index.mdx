<Step id="layer">
  Spread half the <Qty ref="chips"/> over a baking tray, scatter over half
  the <Qty ref="cheddar"/>, then repeat with the remaining chips and cheddar
  so every layer of chips carries its own cheese.
</Step>

<Step id="bake">
  Bake at <Temp c={200}/> for <Dur step="bake"/>, until the cheese has
  melted completely and started to bubble at the edges. Scatter the
  <Qty ref="jalapeno"/> over the top as soon as the tray comes out and serve
  immediately.
</Step>

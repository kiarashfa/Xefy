<Step id="cream">
  Beat the <Qty ref="butter"/> with the <Qty ref="sugar"/> for <Dur step="cream"/>, until
  pale and fluffy.
</Step>

<Step id="heat-oven">
  Meanwhile heat the oven to <Temp c={175}/>, giving it <Dur step="heat-oven"/> to settle,
  and line a square baking tin with paper, leaving an overhang on two sides.
</Step>

<Step id="beat-in-eggs">
  Beat in the <Qty ref="eggs"/> and the <Qty ref="vanilla"/> a little at a time over
  <Dur step="beat-in-eggs"/>, letting each addition disappear before the next goes in.
</Step>

<Step id="alternate-dry-milk">
  Sift the <Qty ref="flour"/>, the <Qty ref="cocoa"/>, the <Qty ref="baking-powder"/> and the
  <Qty ref="salt"/> together. Fold them into the batter in two additions, alternating with
  the <Qty ref="milk"/>, over <Dur step="alternate-dry-milk"/>, finishing on the dry
  ingredients and stopping as soon as the batter is smooth.
</Step>

<Step id="bake">
  Pour into the lined tin and bake for <Dur step="bake"/>, until a skewer pushed into the
  centre comes out clean and the top springs back when pressed lightly.
</Step>

<Step id="cool-in-tin">
  Leave the tray to cool for <Dur step="cool-in-tin"/> before lifting out and cutting into
  squares.
</Step>

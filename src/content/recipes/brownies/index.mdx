<Step id="melt-chocolate">
  Melt the <Qty ref="chocolate"/> with the <Qty ref="butter"/> together over a low heat or
  in short bursts in a microwave, stirring until smooth. This takes <Dur step="melt-chocolate"/>;
  let it cool slightly while the oven heats.
</Step>

<Step id="heat-oven">
  Heat the oven to <Temp c={175}/>, giving it <Dur step="heat-oven"/> to settle, and line a
  square baking tin with paper, leaving an overhang on two sides to lift the brownies out
  later.
</Step>

<Step id="beat-sugar-eggs">
  Whisk the <Qty ref="sugar"/> into the <Qty ref="eggs"/> and the <Qty ref="vanilla"/> for
  the whole of <Dur step="beat-sugar-eggs"/>, until the mixture is pale, thick and holds a
  ribbon briefly when the whisk is lifted.
</Step>

<Step id="combine">
  Fold the cooled chocolate mixture into the egg mixture over <Dur step="combine"/>, mixing
  just until the streaks disappear.
</Step>

<Step id="fold-dry">
  Sift the <Qty ref="flour"/>, the <Qty ref="cocoa"/> and the <Qty ref="salt"/> over the
  batter and fold them through with <Dur step="fold-dry"/>, stopping the moment no dry
  patches remain — overworking it here is what turns a fudgy brownie cakey.
</Step>

<Step id="bake">
  Pour into the lined tin and bake for <Dur step="bake"/>, until the top has set to a thin,
  crackled crust and a skewer pushed into the centre comes out with a little wet batter
  still clinging to it.
</Step>

<Step id="cool-in-tin">
  Leave the tray to cool completely, for <Dur step="cool-in-tin"/>, before lifting out and
  cutting into squares. Cutting warm brownies tears the crumb rather than slicing it.
</Step>

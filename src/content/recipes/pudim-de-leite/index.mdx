<Step id="make-caramel">
  Melt the <Qty ref="caramel-sugar"/> with the <Qty ref="caramel-water"/> in
  a saucepan over a medium heat, swirling occasionally, until it turns a deep
  amber. Immediately pour it into a metal ring mould, tilting to coat the
  base and lower sides, then set aside to harden.
</Step>

<Step id="blend-custard">
  Blend the <Qty ref="condensed-milk"/>, <Qty ref="milk"/> and
  <Qty ref="eggs"/> together until smooth, then pour over the set caramel in
  the mould.
</Step>

<Step id="bake">
  Set the mould in a deep tray and pour in hot water to come halfway up its
  sides. Bake at <Temp c={180}/> for <Dur step="bake"/>, until the custard is
  set at the edges but still holds a gentle wobble at the centre.
</Step>

<Step id="chill">
  Lift the mould from the water bath, cool to room temperature, then
  refrigerate for <Dur step="chill"/>, until completely cold and firm.
</Step>

<Step id="unmould">
  Run a knife around the edge, invert a serving plate over the mould and turn
  out in one motion, letting the liquid caramel run down over the custard.
</Step>

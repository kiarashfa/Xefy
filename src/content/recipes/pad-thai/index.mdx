<Step id="soak-noodles">
  Cover the <Qty ref="noodles"/> with plenty of cold water and leave them for
  <Dur step="soak-noodles"/>, until they are white, pliable and limp but still firm
  enough to snap. Drain them and leave them to drip dry.
</Step>

<Step id="make-sauce">
  Break the <Qty ref="tamarind"/> into the <Qty ref="tamarind-water"/>, hot from the
  kettle, and work it apart with your fingers until it has dissolved into a thick brown
  liquid; push it through a sieve and throw away the fibre. Set it over a low heat with
  the <Qty ref="palm-sugar"/> and the <Qty ref="fish-sauce"/> and simmer for
  <Dur step="make-sauce"/>, until it thickens to a loose syrup that coats a spoon. It
  should read sour first, then sweet, then salt.
</Step>

<Step id="prep-everything">
  Everything from here happens faster than it can be prepared, so prepare it all now and
  set it beside the stove in the order it goes in: the <Qty ref="garlic"/>, the
  <Qty ref="radish"/>, the <Qty ref="shrimp"/>, the <Qty ref="tofu"/>, the beaten
  <Qty ref="eggs"/>, the <Qty ref="sprouts-wok"/> and the <Qty ref="chives-wok"/>. Take
  <Dur step="prep-everything"/> over it.
</Step>

<Step id="fry-aromatics">
  Heat a wok until a drop of water skitters and vanishes. Add the <Qty ref="oil"/>, then
  the tofu, and fry without moving it much until the faces are gold. Add the garlic, the
  preserved radish and the dried shrimp and stir for <Dur step="fry-aromatics"/>, until
  the garlic smells sweet and the shrimp have crisped.
</Step>

<Step id="fry-noodles">
  Add the drained noodles and pour the sauce down the side of the wok. Toss and lift for
  <Dur step="fry-noodles"/>, keeping the heat high, until the noodles have drunk all the
  liquid, turned a deep russet and started to catch in places. They should be tender with
  a little resistance left at the centre.
</Step>

<Step id="add-egg">
  Push the noodles to one side of the wok, pour the egg into the space and leave it alone
  for a moment to set at the edges. Break it up, then fold it through the noodles over
  <Dur step="add-egg"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="finish">
  Throw in the <Qty ref="sprouts-wok"/>, the <Qty ref="chives-wok"/> and the
  <Qty ref="peanuts-wok"/> and toss for <Dur step="finish"/> — no longer, or the sprouts
  go limp. Turn out onto two plates and serve at once with the <Qty ref="sprouts-raw"/>,
  the <Qty ref="chives-serve"/>, the <Qty ref="peanuts-serve"/>, the <Qty ref="chilli"/>
  and the <Qty ref="lime"/> alongside.
</Step>

<Step id="velvet-chicken">
  Combine the <Qty ref="velvet-soy"/>, <Qty ref="velvet-wine"/> and
  <Qty ref="velvet-cornflour"/> in a bowl and turn the <Qty ref="chicken"/>
  through it.
</Step>

<Step id="rest-chicken">
  Leave the chicken to marinate for <Dur step="rest-chicken"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="mix-sauce">
  Meanwhile, whisk together the <Qty ref="sauce-soy"/>, <Qty ref="black-vinegar"/>,
  <Qty ref="sauce-sugar"/>, <Qty ref="sauce-cornflour"/> and
  <Qty ref="sauce-water"/> until smooth.
</Step>

<Step id="sear-chicken">
  Heat the <Qty ref="wok-oil"/> in a wok over high heat and sear the chicken for
  <Dur step="sear-chicken"/>, until coloured on the outside, then lift out and set
  aside.
</Step>

<Step id="fry-aromatics">
  Lower the heat slightly and add the <Qty ref="dried-chilli"/> and
  <Qty ref="sichuan-pepper"/>, frying for <Dur step="fry-aromatics"/> until
  fragrant and just beginning to darken. Add the <Qty ref="garlic"/>,
  <Qty ref="ginger"/> and <Qty ref="spring-onion"/> and stir briefly.
</Step>

<Step id="combine">
  Return the chicken to the wok, pour in the sauce mixture and toss over high
  heat for <Dur step="combine"/>, until thickened and glossy and the chicken is
  cooked through. Off the heat, stir through the <Qty ref="peanut"/>.
</Step>

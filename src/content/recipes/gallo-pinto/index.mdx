<Step id="soak-beans">
  Soak the <Qty ref="black-beans"/> in plenty of cold water for
  <Dur step="soak-beans"/>, then drain.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-beans">
  Simmer the soaked beans in the <Qty ref="water"/> for
  <Dur step="cook-beans"/>, until tender. Drain, reserving some of the
  cooking liquid.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-rice">
  Meanwhile, cook the <Qty ref="rice"/> according to the packet
  instructions, then set aside. This takes about <Dur step="cook-rice"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="saute-vegetables">
  Heat the <Qty ref="oil"/> in a large pan over a medium heat and cook the
  <Qty ref="onion"/>, <Qty ref="red-pepper"/> and <Qty ref="garlic"/> for
  <Dur step="saute-vegetables"/>, until softened.
</Step>

<Step id="combine-and-fry">
  Add the cooked rice, the drained beans and the
  <Qty ref="worcestershire-sauce"/> to the pan and fry over a fairly high
  heat, stirring and turning often, for <Dur step="combine-and-fry"/>, until
  the grains are speckled brown and lightly crisp in places, adding a
  splash of the reserved bean liquid if the pan looks dry.
</Step>

<Step id="finish">
  Stir in the <Qty ref="coriander"/> and <Qty ref="salt"/> and serve at
  once. This takes about <Dur step="finish"/>.
</Step>

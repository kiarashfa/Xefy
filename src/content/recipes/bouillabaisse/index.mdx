<Step id="prepare-fish">
  Scale and clean the <Qty ref="rockfish"/>, keeping the heads and bones. Cut the flesh into thick
  pieces and set them aside on a plate in the fridge. Chop the heads and bones into pieces the size
  of a fist. This takes <Dur step="prepare-fish"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="soften-base">
  Heat the <Qty ref="oil-broth"/> in a large, deep pot over a medium heat. Add the <Qty ref="onion"/>,
  the <Qty ref="leek"/>, the <Qty ref="fennel"/> and the <Qty ref="garlic-broth"/>, and cook for
  <Dur step="soften-base"/>, until soft but not coloured.
</Step>

<Step id="simmer-broth">
  Add the chopped fish heads and bones, the <Qty ref="tomatoes"/>, the <Qty ref="saffron-broth"/>,
  the <Qty ref="bay"/>, the <Qty ref="thyme"/> and the <Qty ref="water"/>. Season with the
  <Qty ref="salt"/>, bring to a boil and simmer hard for <Dur step="simmer-broth"/>, until the broth
  has a good flavour. Strain it into a clean pot, pressing the solids to extract their juices, and
  return it to a simmer.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-potatoes">
  Add the <Qty ref="potatoes"/> to the strained broth and simmer gently for
  <Dur step="cook-potatoes"/>, until they are tender right through.
</Step>

<Step id="make-rouille">
  Crush the <Qty ref="garlic-rouille"/> to a paste with a pinch of the <Qty ref="salt"/>. Work in the
  <Qty ref="yolk"/>, then whisk in the <Qty ref="oil-rouille"/> a little at a time, as for mayonnaise,
  until it is thick. Steep the <Qty ref="saffron-rouille"/> in a spoonful of the hot broth and whisk
  it in with the <Qty ref="cayenne"/>. This takes <Dur step="make-rouille"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="toast-bread">
  Toast the <Qty ref="baguette"/> slices in a hot oven or under a grill until crisp and golden on both
  sides. This takes <Dur step="toast-bread"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="poach-fish">
  Lower the <Qty ref="rockfish"/> into the simmering broth and cook for
  <Dur step="poach-fish"/>, until the flesh is just opaque and flakes easily.
</Step>

<Step id="serve">
  Lift the fish and potatoes onto a warm platter. Lay the toasted bread in warm bowls, ladle the
  broth over it, and serve the fish with the rouille on the side. This takes <Dur step="serve"/>.
</Step>

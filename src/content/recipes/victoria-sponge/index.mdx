<Step id="cream-butter">
  Beat the <Qty ref="butter"/> and the <Qty ref="sugar-batter"/> together until pale,
  light and fluffy.
</Step>

<Step id="beat-in-eggs">
  Beat in the <Qty ref="egg"/> a little at a time, along with the <Qty ref="vanilla"/>,
  beating well between additions.
</Step>

<Step id="fold-flour">
  Sift the <Qty ref="flour"/> and the <Qty ref="baking-powder"/> over the mixture and
  fold in gently with a large metal spoon until just combined.
</Step>

<Step id="divide-and-bake">
  Divide the batter evenly between two lined sandwich tins and bake at <Temp c={180}/>
  for <Dur step="divide-and-bake"/>, until well risen, golden, and springing back when
  pressed lightly in the centre.
</Step>

<Step id="cool">
  Turn out onto a wire rack and leave to cool completely, for <Dur step="cool"/>,
  before filling.
</Step>

<Step id="fill">
  Spread the <Qty ref="jam"/> over the flat side of one sponge, set the second sponge
  on top, and dust with the <Qty ref="sugar-dust"/>.
</Step>

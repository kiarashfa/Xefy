<Step id="cream">
  Beat the <Qty ref="butter"/> and <Qty ref="sugar"/> together until pale and
  fluffy.
</Step>

<Step id="add-yolks">
  Beat in the <Qty ref="egg-yolks"/> and <Qty ref="vanilla"/> until fully
  combined.
</Step>

<Step id="add-dry">
  Sift the <Qty ref="cornflour"/>, <Qty ref="flour"/>, <Qty ref="baking-powder"/>,
  <Qty ref="bicarbonate"/> and <Qty ref="salt"/> together, then fold into the
  butter mixture just until a soft dough comes together. Avoid overworking it.
</Step>

<Step id="chill">
  Wrap the dough and chill it for <Dur step="chill"/>, until firm enough to
  roll.
</Step>

<Step id="shape">
  Roll the dough between two sheets of baking paper to the thickness of a
  coin and cut into small rounds, gathering and re-rolling the scraps. Set
  the rounds on lined trays, spaced well apart, since they spread very little
  and are cut close together to keep an even thickness.
</Step>

<Step id="bake">
  Bake at <Temp c={170}/> for <Dur step="bake"/>, until the tops look dry
  and set rather than coloured. The rounds will still feel soft in the
  centre; they firm up entirely as they cool.
</Step>

<Step id="cool">
  Slide the rounds onto a rack and cool completely, for <Dur step="cool"/>,
  before filling — a warm cookie melts the dulce de leche straight through it.
</Step>

<Step id="assemble">
  Spread half the rounds generously with the <Qty ref="dulce-de-leche"/> and
  sandwich with the remaining rounds, pressing gently so the filling meets
  the edge all the way round. Roll the exposed rim of filling in the
  <Qty ref="coconut"/> to coat it.
</Step>

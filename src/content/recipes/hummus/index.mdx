<Step id="cook-chickpeas">
  Drain the soaked <Qty ref="chickpeas"/> and put them in a pan with the
  <Qty ref="bicarb"/> and the <Qty ref="cooking-water"/>. Bring to the boil, skim off
  the foam, then simmer for <Dur step="cook-chickpeas"/>, until a chickpea crushes to
  nothing between finger and thumb. They should be well past the point of merely
  cooked.
</Step>

<Step id="steep-garlic">
  Meanwhile crush the <Qty ref="garlic"/> and leave it in the <Qty ref="lemon-juice"/>
  for <Dur step="steep-garlic"/>. The acid takes the raw heat out of the garlic, which
  would otherwise dominate everything.
</Step>

<Step id="blend">
  Drain the chickpeas, keeping the water, and tip them straight into a blender while
  still steaming. Add the <Qty ref="tahini"/>, the strained lemon and garlic, the
  <Qty ref="cumin"/> and the <Qty ref="salt"/>, and blend for <Dur step="blend"/>,
  loosening with the cooking water until it turns pale and moves like whipped cream.
  It should be looser than you think — it sets as it cools.
</Step>

<Step id="rest">
  Leave for <Dur step="rest"/> before serving, so the garlic and cumin settle into it.
  Spread into a shallow bowl, work a hollow into the middle with the back of a spoon,
  and pour the olive oil into it.
</Step>

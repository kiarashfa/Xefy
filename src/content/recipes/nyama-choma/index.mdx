<Step id="season-goat">
  Rub the <Qty ref="goat"/> all over with the <Qty ref="garlic"/>,
  <Qty ref="lemon-juice"/>, <Qty ref="vegetable-oil"/>, <Qty ref="fine-sea-salt"/>
  and <Qty ref="black-pepper"/>, working the seasoning into the meat and between
  the joints. This takes <Dur step="season-goat"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="rest-goat">
  Leave the goat to sit at room temperature for <Dur step="rest-goat"/>, so the
  seasoning reaches the meat before it hits the fire.
</Step>

<Step id="grill-goat">
  Build a charcoal fire and let it burn down to a steady, even bed of hot coals.
  Grill the goat over indirect heat, turning regularly, for
  <Dur step="grill-goat"/>, until the outside is well charred and a skewer pushed
  into the thickest piece meets no resistance.
</Step>

<Step id="rest-and-chop">
  Rest the goat off the heat for <Dur step="rest-and-chop"/>, then chop it into
  smaller pieces directly on the board it is served from.
</Step>

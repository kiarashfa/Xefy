<Step id="boil-water">
  Bring the <Qty ref="pasta-water"/> to a rolling boil in a large pot, then add the
  <Qty ref="macaroni-salt"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-pasta">
  Add the <Qty ref="macaroni"/> and cook for <Dur step="cook-pasta"/>, two minutes short
  of whatever the packet suggests, so it still has a firm bite. Drain well.
</Step>

<Step id="make-roux">
  While the pasta cooks, melt the <Qty ref="butter"/> in a separate pan and whisk in
  the <Qty ref="flour"/>, cooking over a low heat for <Dur step="make-roux"/> until
  smooth and no longer smelling raw.
</Step>

<Step id="make-sauce">
  Whisk the <Qty ref="milk"/> into the roux gradually, working out any lumps, and cook
  over a medium heat for <Dur step="make-sauce"/>, until thickened enough to coat the
  back of a spoon. Off the heat, stir in the <Qty ref="mustard-powder"/>, the
  <Qty ref="cheddar-sauce"/>, the <Qty ref="salt-sauce"/> and the <Qty ref="pepper"/>
  until the cheese has melted into a smooth sauce.
</Step>

<Step id="combine-and-top">
  Stir the drained macaroni through the cheese sauce and turn it into a buttered baking
  dish. Scatter the <Qty ref="cheddar-topping"/> evenly over the top, over
  <Dur step="combine-and-top"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="bake">
  Bake at <Temp c={200}/> for <Dur step="bake"/>, until the top is golden and bubbling
  at the edges.
</Step>

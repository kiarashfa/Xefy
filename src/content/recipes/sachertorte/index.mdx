<Step id="melt-chocolate">
  Melt the <Qty ref="cake-chocolate"/> gently over a pan of simmering water and set
  aside to cool slightly.
</Step>

<Step id="cream-butter">
  Beat the <Qty ref="cake-butter"/> and the <Qty ref="cake-icing-sugar"/> together
  until pale, then beat in the <Qty ref="egg-yolk"/> and the melted chocolate.
</Step>

<Step id="whisk-meringue">
  Whisk the <Qty ref="egg-white"/> to soft peaks, then whisk in the
  <Qty ref="meringue-sugar"/> a little at a time until glossy and firm.
</Step>

<Step id="fold-together">
  Fold a third of the meringue into the chocolate mixture to loosen it, then fold in
  the rest along with the <Qty ref="flour"/>, keeping as much air in as possible. Pour
  into a lined tin.
</Step>

<Step id="bake">
  Bake at <Temp c={170}/> for <Dur step="bake"/>, until a skewer comes out with a few
  moist crumbs.
</Step>

<Step id="cool">
  Cool in the tin for <Dur step="cool"/>, then turn out onto a rack.
</Step>

<Step id="glaze-jam">
  Warm the <Qty ref="apricot-jam"/>, strain it, and brush it in a thin, even layer over
  the top and sides of the cooled cake.
</Step>

<Step id="make-icing">
  Heat the <Qty ref="glaze-sugar"/> and the <Qty ref="glaze-water"/> together until
  the sugar dissolves and the syrup thickens slightly, then take off the heat and stir
  in the <Qty ref="glaze-chocolate"/> until completely smooth. Cook for
  <Dur step="make-icing"/> in total.
</Step>

<Step id="pour-icing">
  Set the cake on a rack over a tray and pour the warm icing over in one motion,
  tilting to coat the top and sides completely.
</Step>

<Step id="set">
  Leave undisturbed at room temperature for <Dur step="set"/>, until the icing has set
  to a firm, glossy shell.
</Step>

<Step id="rub-butter">
  Rub the <Qty ref="butter"/> into the <Qty ref="flour"/> and the <Qty ref="salt"/> with the
  fingertips until the mixture looks like fine breadcrumbs, then stir in the
  <Qty ref="sugar"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="mix-dough">
  Make a well in the centre and add the <Qty ref="milk"/> and the <Qty ref="egg-dough"/>.
  Mix with a table knife and then bring together briefly by hand into a soft, slightly
  sticky dough.
</Step>

<Step id="cut-scones">
  Turn the dough out onto a floured surface and pat gently to a thickness of about
  <Len cm={2}/>. Cut into rounds and set them on a lined tray, spaced well apart.
</Step>

<Step id="glaze">
  Brush the tops with the <Qty ref="egg-glaze"/>, taking care not to let it run down the
  cut sides, which would stop them rising evenly.
</Step>

<Step id="bake">
  Bake at <Temp c={220}/> for <Dur step="bake"/>, until well risen and golden on top.
  Cool on a wire rack and split to serve with the <Qty ref="clotted-cream"/> and the
  <Qty ref="jam"/>.
</Step>

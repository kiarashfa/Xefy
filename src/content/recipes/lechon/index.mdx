<Step id="season-cavity">
  Rub the inside of the cavity with the <Qty ref="salt-cavity"/> and
  <Qty ref="pepper"/>, then pack in the <Qty ref="lemongrass"/>, <Qty ref="garlic"/>,
  <Qty ref="onion"/> and <Qty ref="bay-leaf"/> for <Dur step="season-cavity"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="truss">
  Close the cavity by sewing or skewering it shut, then mount the <Qty ref="pig"/> on a
  spit, securing it firmly along its length so it turns evenly, for <Dur step="truss"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="dry-skin">
  Rub the skin all over with the <Qty ref="salt-skin"/> and leave the pig uncovered and
  refrigerated for <Dur step="dry-skin"/>, letting the surface dry out completely.
</Step>

<Step id="roast">
  Roast over a steady bed of live coals, turning constantly, for <Dur step="roast"/>,
  keeping the pig a consistent distance from the heat so it colours gradually and cooks
  through to the bone without the skin scorching.
</Step>

<Step id="rest">
  Lift off the heat and rest for <Dur step="rest"/> before carving.
</Step>

<Step id="carve">
  Carve for <Dur step="carve"/>, separating the crackled skin from the meat in sheets
  and chopping both into serving pieces.
</Step>

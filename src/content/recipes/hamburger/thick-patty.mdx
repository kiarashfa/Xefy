<Step id="shape">
  Shape the <Qty ref="beef"/> into a single patty a little wider than the
  <Qty ref="bun"/>, pressing a shallow dimple into the centre so it cooks flat
  rather than doming.
</Step>

<Step id="sear">
  Season with the <Qty ref="salt"/> and sear over a medium-high heat for
  <Dur step="sear"/>, turning once, laying the <Qty ref="cheese"/> over the
  patty after the flip so it melts as the second side finishes.
</Step>

<Step id="rest">
  Rest the patty for <Dur step="rest"/> before building the burger.
</Step>

<Step id="toast-bun">
  Meanwhile toast the cut faces of the <Qty ref="bun"/> for
  <Dur step="toast-bun"/>, until golden.
</Step>

<Step id="assemble">
  Build the burger on the toasted bun with the <Qty ref="lettuce"/>, the
  <Qty ref="tomato"/>, the <Qty ref="onion"/> and the <Qty ref="ketchup"/>.
</Step>

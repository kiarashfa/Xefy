<Step id="heat-griddle">
  Heat a flat griddle or heavy pan over a high heat for
  <Dur step="heat-griddle"/>, until a drop of water skitters and evaporates
  on contact.
</Step>

<Step id="shape">
  Meanwhile divide the <Qty ref="beef"/> into two loose, equal balls without
  compacting them.
</Step>

<Step id="smash">
  Set the balls on the griddle and immediately press each flat with a heavy
  spatula, holding the pressure for a few seconds before letting go. Season
  with the <Qty ref="salt"/>, cook for <Dur step="smash"/>, then flip once,
  laying the <Qty ref="cheese"/> over the patties as soon as they turn so it
  melts before the second side is done.
</Step>

<Step id="toast-bun">
  While the patties cook, toast the cut faces of the <Qty ref="bun"/> on the
  edge of the griddle for <Dur step="toast-bun"/>, until golden.
</Step>

<Step id="assemble">
  Stack both patties on the toasted bun with the <Qty ref="onion"/>, the
  <Qty ref="pickles"/> and the <Qty ref="ketchup"/>.
</Step>

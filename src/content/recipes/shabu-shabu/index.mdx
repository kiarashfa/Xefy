<Step id="steep-kombu-broth">
  Wipe the <Qty ref="kombu"/> over with a dry cloth, leaving the white bloom on the surface, and put
  it into a pot with the <Qty ref="broth-water"/>. Leave it to steep for
  <Dur step="steep-kombu-broth"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="heat-broth">
  Bring the pot up slowly over a low flame, and lift the <Qty ref="kombu"/> out as soon as the first
  fine bubbles form on the sides. Keep the broth just below a simmer. Kombu held at the boil turns the
  broth slippery and faintly bitter, and the broth is the only cooking liquid the beef meets. This
  takes <Dur step="heat-broth"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="make-ponzu">
  Combine the <Qty ref="soy-ponzu"/> with the <Qty ref="lemon"/> and the <Qty ref="mirin"/>,
  then scatter in the <Qty ref="katsuobushi"/>. Leave it to steep for <Dur step="make-ponzu"/>, then
  strain it through a fine sieve into a small jug. Grate the <Qty ref="daikon"/> and set it out
  beside the ponzu.
</Step>

<Step id="make-goma">
  Work the <Qty ref="sesame-paste"/> in a bowl with the <Qty ref="goma-water"/> until it is smooth and
  loose. Stir in the <Qty ref="soy-goma"/>, the <Qty ref="vinegar"/> and the
  <Qty ref="sugar"/>, and keep stirring until the sugar has dissolved. This takes
  <Dur step="make-goma"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="prep-vegetables">
  Cut the <Qty ref="tofu"/> into cubes and the <Qty ref="napa"/> into large pieces. Separate the
  <Qty ref="enoki"/> into small clumps and slice the <Qty ref="spring-onion"/> thinly on the diagonal.
  Lay the <Qty ref="beef"/> out on a plate in single slices, so that each one can be lifted without
  tearing. This takes <Dur step="prep-vegetables"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-at-table">
  Set the pot of broth on a portable burner at the table and bring it back to just below a simmer.
  Each diner takes a slice of the <Qty ref="beef"/> with chopsticks and swishes it through the broth
  for a few seconds, until it just changes colour, then dips it in the ponzu or the sesame sauce and
  eats it at once. The <Qty ref="tofu"/>, the <Qty ref="napa"/> and the <Qty ref="enoki"/> are cooked
  in the same broth and left in longer, and the <Qty ref="spring-onion"/> goes in last. This takes
  <Dur step="cook-at-table"/>.
</Step>

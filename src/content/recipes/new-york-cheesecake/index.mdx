<Step id="make-crust">
  Stir the <Qty ref="crumbs"/> into the <Qty ref="crust-butter"/> and the
  <Qty ref="crust-sugar"/> until every crumb is coated, then press the mixture firmly into
  the base of a springform tin. This takes <Dur step="make-crust"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="heat-oven">
  Meanwhile heat the oven to <Temp c={170}/>, giving it <Dur step="heat-oven"/> to settle,
  and wrap the outside of the tin in foil to keep water from the later bath seeping in.
</Step>

<Step id="par-bake-crust">
  Bake the crust for <Dur step="par-bake-crust"/>, until just set, then leave it to cool
  while the filling is made. Lower the oven to <Temp c={150}/>.
</Step>

<Step id="beat-cream-cheese">
  Beat the <Qty ref="cream-cheese"/> until perfectly smooth, then beat in the
  <Qty ref="filling-sugar"/> and the <Qty ref="cornflour"/>, over <Dur step="beat-cream-cheese"/>,
  scraping down the bowl so no lumps of cheese are left at the base.
</Step>

<Step id="add-eggs">
  Beat in the <Qty ref="eggs"/> one at a time on the lowest speed, over <Dur step="add-eggs"/>,
  mixing each just until it disappears before adding the next.
</Step>

<Step id="finish-batter">
  Stir in the <Qty ref="cream"/>, the <Qty ref="lemon-juice"/> and the <Qty ref="vanilla"/>
  by hand, using <Dur step="finish-batter"/>, until just combined.
</Step>

<Step id="bake">
  Pour the batter over the crust, set the tin in a larger roasting pan and pour boiling
  water into the pan to come partway up the sides of the foil. Bake for <Dur step="bake"/>,
  until the edges are set and the centre still holds a slight wobble.
</Step>

<Step id="rest-in-oven">
  Turn off the oven and leave the cheesecake inside with the door ajar for
  <Dur step="rest-in-oven"/>, so it finishes setting gradually rather than meeting cold air
  all at once.
</Step>

<Step id="chill">
  Lift the cheesecake out of the water bath, remove the foil and refrigerate it, uncovered
  at first, for <Dur step="chill"/> or overnight, before unmoulding and slicing.
</Step>

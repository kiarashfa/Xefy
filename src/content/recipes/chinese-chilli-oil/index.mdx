<Step id="toast-spices">
  Combine the <Qty ref="sichuan-pepper"/>, <Qty ref="star-anise"/>,
  <Qty ref="cinnamon"/> and <Qty ref="bay-leaf"/> in a dry pan over a low heat and
  toast for <Dur step="toast-spices"/>, until fragrant.
</Step>

<Step id="infuse-oil">
  Add the <Qty ref="base-oil"/>, <Qty ref="garlic"/>, <Qty ref="ginger"/> and
  <Qty ref="spring-onion"/> to the pan and warm gently over a low heat for
  <Dur step="infuse-oil"/>, until the aromatics turn golden and smell toasted.
  Strain, discarding the solids, and return the oil to the pan.
</Step>

<Step id="pour-and-steep">
  Put the <Qty ref="chilli-flakes"/>, <Qty ref="sesame-seed"/> and
  <Qty ref="salt"/> in a heatproof bowl. Reheat the strained oil until just
  beginning to smoke, then pour it over the chilli in two or three additions,
  stirring between each, over <Dur step="pour-and-steep"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="rest">
  Leave the oil to cool and steep for at least <Dur step="rest"/> before
  bottling, undisturbed, so the sediment can begin to settle.
</Step>

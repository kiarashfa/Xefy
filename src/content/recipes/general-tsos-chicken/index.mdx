<Step id="mix-sauce">
  Combine the <Qty ref="soy-sauce"/>, <Qty ref="vinegar"/>, <Qty ref="sugar"/>
  and <Qty ref="sauce-water"/> with the <Qty ref="cornflour-sauce"/>, stirring
  until the cornflour is fully dissolved.
</Step>

<Step id="dredge">
  Toss the <Qty ref="chicken"/> in the <Qty ref="egg"/>, then coat each piece
  evenly in the <Qty ref="cornflour-dredge"/>, shaking off the excess.
</Step>

<Step id="fry">
  Heat the <Qty ref="frying-oil"/> until shimmering and fry the chicken in
  batches for <Dur step="fry"/>, until deeply golden and cooked through.
  Drain on a rack.
</Step>

<Step id="glaze">
  Pour off all but a thin film of the frying oil and stir-fry the
  <Qty ref="garlic"/>, <Qty ref="ginger"/> and <Qty ref="chilli"/> briefly
  until fragrant. Pour in the sauce mixture and cook for
  <Dur step="glaze"/>, stirring, until thickened and glossy. Return the fried
  chicken to the pan and toss to coat, then finish with the
  <Qty ref="sesame-oil"/> and the <Qty ref="spring-onion"/>.
</Step>

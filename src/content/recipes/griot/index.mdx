<Step id="epis">
  Blend the <Qty ref="garlic"/>, <Qty ref="scotch-bonnet"/>,
  <Qty ref="green-pepper"/>, <Qty ref="onion"/>, <Qty ref="spring-onion"/>,
  <Qty ref="parsley"/> and <Qty ref="thyme"/> to a coarse paste; this is the
  epis.
</Step>

<Step id="marinate">
  Toss the <Qty ref="pork"/> with the epis, the <Qty ref="sour-orange-juice"/>,
  the <Qty ref="lime-juice"/> and the <Qty ref="salt"/>, then cover and
  refrigerate for <Dur step="marinate"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="simmer">
  Tip the pork and its marinade into a pot with the <Qty ref="water"/> and the
  <Qty ref="black-pepper"/>, bring to a boil, then reduce to a simmer and cook
  uncovered for <Dur step="simmer"/>, until the pork is tender and the liquid
  has almost entirely reduced away.
</Step>

<Step id="fry">
  Heat the <Qty ref="vegetable-oil"/> in a wide pan over a high heat and fry
  the pork cubes in batches for <Dur step="fry"/>, turning occasionally,
  until deeply browned and crisp on the outside.
</Step>

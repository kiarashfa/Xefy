<Step id="cook-rice">
  Rinse the <Qty ref="rice"/> until the water runs clear, then bring it to the boil
  with the <Qty ref="rice-water"/>. Cover, reduce the heat to a bare simmer and cook
  undisturbed, then rest off the heat with the lid on. This takes
  <Dur step="cook-rice"/> in total.
</Step>

<Step id="cut-fish">
  Cut the <Qty ref="fish"/> into small, even cubes. This takes
  <Dur step="cut-fish"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="crush-macadamia">
  Crush the <Qty ref="macadamia"/> coarsely with the flat of a knife or a mortar and
  pestle, then stir in the <Qty ref="salt"/>. This takes <Dur step="crush-macadamia"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="toss-poke">
  Combine the fish, the <Qty ref="soy-sauce"/>, <Qty ref="sesame-oil"/>,
  <Qty ref="onion"/>, <Qty ref="spring-onion"/>, <Qty ref="sesame-seed"/> and the
  crushed macadamia in a bowl and toss gently to coat every piece. Leave to sit for
  <Dur step="toss-poke"/> so the seasoning draws into the fish.
</Step>

<Step id="plate">
  Divide the rice between plates, spoon the poke and its juices over the top, and
  scatter with the <Qty ref="nori"/>. This takes <Dur step="plate"/>; serve at once.
</Step>

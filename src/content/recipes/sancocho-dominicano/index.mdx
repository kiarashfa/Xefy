<Step id="marinate-meats">
  Toss the <Qty ref="beef"/>, <Qty ref="chicken"/> and <Qty ref="pork"/> with
  the <Qty ref="sour-orange-juice"/>, <Qty ref="garlic-marinade"/> and
  <Qty ref="oregano"/>, and leave to marinate for <Dur step="marinate-meats"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="brown-meats">
  Brown the marinated meats in a large pot over a high heat, in batches if
  needed, for <Dur step="brown-meats"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="simmer-broth">
  Return all the meat to the pot with the <Qty ref="onion"/>,
  <Qty ref="green-pepper"/>, <Qty ref="garlic-broth"/>, <Qty ref="water"/>
  and <Qty ref="salt"/>. Bring to a simmer and cook for
  <Dur step="simmer-broth"/>, skimming occasionally, until the meats are
  tender.
</Step>

<Step id="add-hard-vegetables">
  Add the <Qty ref="yam"/>, <Qty ref="cassava"/>, <Qty ref="potato"/>,
  <Qty ref="pumpkin"/>, <Qty ref="corn"/> and <Qty ref="plantain-green"/>.
  This takes about <Dur step="add-hard-vegetables"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="simmer-vegetables">
  Simmer for <Dur step="simmer-vegetables"/>, until the root vegetables are
  tender.
</Step>

<Step id="add-ripe-plantain">
  Add the <Qty ref="plantain-ripe"/>. This takes about
  <Dur step="add-ripe-plantain"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="simmer-plantain">
  Simmer for a further <Dur step="simmer-plantain"/>, until the ripe
  plantain is tender but still holding its shape.
</Step>

<Step id="finish">
  Stir in the <Qty ref="coriander"/> and <Qty ref="black-pepper"/>, adjust
  the salt, and serve. This takes about <Dur step="finish"/>.
</Step>

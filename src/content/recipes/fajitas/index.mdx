<Step id="marinate">
  Combine the <Qty ref="lime-juice"/>, <Qty ref="garlic"/>,
  <Qty ref="oregano"/>, <Qty ref="cumin"/>, the <Qty ref="oil-marinade"/>, the
  <Qty ref="salt-marinade"/> and the <Qty ref="pepper"/>. Coat the
  <Qty ref="skirt"/> in the mixture and leave it to marinate, covered and
  refrigerated, for <Dur step="marinate"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="sear-steak">
  Lift the steak from the marinade, pat it dry, and sear it in a very hot,
  dry pan or on a hot grill for <Dur step="sear-steak"/>, turning once, until
  it is well charred outside and still pink within.
</Step>

<Step id="rest-steak">
  Rest the steak on a board for <Dur step="rest-steak"/> before slicing.
</Step>

<Step id="saute-vegetables">
  While the steak rests, heat the <Qty ref="oil-vegetables"/> in the same pan
  and cook the <Qty ref="onion"/>, <Qty ref="red-pepper"/> and
  <Qty ref="green-pepper"/> with the <Qty ref="salt-vegetables"/> for
  <Dur step="saute-vegetables"/>, until softened and lightly charred at the
  edges.
</Step>

<Step id="warm-tortillas">
  At the same time, warm the <Qty ref="tortillas"/> in a dry pan or directly
  over a flame for <Dur step="warm-tortillas"/>, until soft and lightly
  spotted, then wrap them to keep warm.
</Step>

<Step id="slice-and-serve">
  Slice the steak thinly across the grain and serve it at once alongside the
  peppers, onion and warm tortillas, for diners to assemble themselves.
</Step>

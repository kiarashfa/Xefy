<Step id="mix">
  Put the <Qty ref="flour"/>, the <Qty ref="sugar-dough"/>, the <Qty ref="salt"/> and the
  <Qty ref="yeast"/> into the bowl of a mixer fitted with a dough hook. Add the
  <Qty ref="milk"/> and the <Qty ref="egg"/>, and mix on low speed for <Dur step="mix"/>, until
  the dough comes together into a soft, sticky mass.
</Step>

<Step id="knead">
  Knead on low speed for <Dur step="knead"/>, until the dough is smooth and pulls away from the
  sides of the bowl in a sheet, though it will still stick to your fingers.
</Step>

<Step id="add-butter">
  Add the <Qty ref="butter-dough"/> a few pieces at a time, letting each one be absorbed before
  the next goes in. Keep kneading for <Dur step="add-butter"/>, until the dough is glossy,
  elastic and comes away from the bowl again.
</Step>

<Step id="first-rise">
  Cover the bowl and leave the dough at room temperature for <Dur step="first-rise"/>, until it
  has doubled in size. Knock it back gently to release the air.
</Step>

<Step id="chill">
  Refrigerate the dough for <Dur step="chill"/>, until the butter has firmed and the dough can
  be lifted without sticking.
</Step>

<Step id="make-filling">
  Melt the <Qty ref="chocolate"/> with the <Qty ref="butter-filling"/> over a gentle heat, then
  stir in the <Qty ref="sugar-filling"/> and the <Qty ref="cocoa"/> until smooth. Leave it to
  cool and thicken for <Dur step="make-filling"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="shape">
  Roll the chilled dough on a floured bench into a rectangle about <Len cm={30}/> wide. Spread
  the filling over it, leaving a narrow border at the far edge. Roll the dough up tightly from
  the near edge, then cut the roll in half lengthwise, turn the cut sides up, and twist the two
  halves over each other. Lay the twist in a buttered loaf tin.
</Step>

<Step id="second-rise">
  Cover the tin loosely and leave the babka to rise for <Dur step="second-rise"/>, until the
  dough reaches the rim of the tin and feels airy when touched.
</Step>

<Step id="heat-oven">
  Heat the oven to <Temp c={180}/> with a shelf in the lower middle.
</Step>

<Step id="bake">
  Bake for <Dur step="bake"/>, until deep golden and a skewer pushed into the centre comes out
  clean. If the top colours before the middle is set, lay a sheet of foil over it.
</Step>

<Step id="make-syrup">
  Heat the <Qty ref="sugar-syrup"/> and the <Qty ref="water"/> in a small pan, stirring until the
  sugar has dissolved, then let the syrup boil for <Dur step="make-syrup"/>. Brush it over the
  babka as soon as it comes out of the oven.
</Step>

<Step id="cool">
  Leave the babka in the tin for <Dur step="cool"/>, then turn it out onto a rack before slicing.
</Step>

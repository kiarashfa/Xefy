<Step id="make-dough">
  Combine the <Qty ref="dough-flour"/> and the <Qty ref="dough-salt"/>, then work in the
  <Qty ref="dough-oil"/>, the <Qty ref="dough-egg"/>, the <Qty ref="dough-water"/> and
  the <Qty ref="dough-vinegar"/> and knead until smooth, soft and elastic.
</Step>

<Step id="rest-dough">
  Shape into a ball, brush with a little oil, cover and rest at room temperature for
  <Dur step="rest-dough"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="prepare-filling">
  Toss the <Qty ref="apple"/> with the <Qty ref="filling-sugar"/>, the
  <Qty ref="cinnamon"/>, the <Qty ref="raisins"/> and the <Qty ref="lemon-juice"/>.
  Fry the <Qty ref="breadcrumbs"/> in the <Qty ref="butter-crumbs"/> until golden and
  set aside.
</Step>

<Step id="stretch-dough">
  On a large floured cloth, stretch the dough from the centre outward with the backs
  of the hands until it is paper-thin and almost translucent, trimming away the
  thicker edge.
</Step>

<Step id="fill-and-roll">
  Scatter the buttered breadcrumbs over two-thirds of the dough, top with the apple
  mixture, and use the cloth to roll the strudel up over the filling into a tight log.
  Transfer to a lined tray, seam side down, and brush all over with the
  <Qty ref="butter-brushing"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="bake">
  Bake at <Temp c={190}/> for <Dur step="bake"/>, until deep golden and crisp. Dust
  with the <Qty ref="icing-sugar"/> once cooled slightly.
</Step>

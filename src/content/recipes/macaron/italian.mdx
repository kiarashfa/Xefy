<Step id="sift">
  Sift the <Qty ref="almonds"/> and the <Qty ref="icing-sugar"/> together twice, into a
  bowl, discarding any coarse pieces left in the sieve. This takes <Dur step="sift"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="syrup">
  Put the <Qty ref="sugar"/> and the <Qty ref="water"/> into a small, heavy pan over a
  medium heat without stirring. Boil until the syrup reaches <Temp c={118}/>, which takes
  <Dur step="syrup"/>. Take it off the heat at once.
</Step>

<Step id="whip-whites">
  While the syrup cooks, whisk the <Qty ref="whites"/> in a clean, dry bowl to soft peaks,
  for <Dur step="whip-whites"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="pour-syrup">
  With the whisk running on a medium speed, pour the hot syrup into the whites in a thin,
  steady stream, aiming it at the side of the bowl. Keep whisking until the meringue is
  stiff and glossy and has cooled to lukewarm, for <Dur step="pour-syrup"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="macaronage">
  Tip the almond and sugar mixture onto the meringue and fold them together with a
  spatula, pressing the batter against the side of the bowl, for <Dur step="macaronage"/>.
  The batter is ready when it falls from the spatula in a slow, unbroken ribbon.
</Step>

<Step id="pipe">
  Spoon the batter into a piping bag fitted with a plain round nozzle and pipe rounds
  of an even size onto baking paper, lifting straight up at the end of each one. Tap the
  tray firmly on the bench to release any air bubbles. This takes <Dur step="pipe"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="rest-shells">
  Leave the piped shells uncovered at room temperature for <Dur step="rest-shells"/>, until
  a dry skin forms and the surface no longer sticks to a fingertip.
</Step>

<Step id="ganache">
  Warm the <Qty ref="cream"/> in a small pan until it is just steaming, and pour it over the
  <Qty ref="chocolate"/>. Leave it for a minute, then stir until smooth and glossy. Chill
  until thick enough to pipe. This takes <Dur step="ganache"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="bake">
  Bake the shells at <Temp c={150}/> for <Dur step="bake"/>, until they lift cleanly from the
  paper and the feet are firm when touched.
</Step>

<Step id="cool-shells">
  Leave the shells on the tray for <Dur step="cool-shells"/>, until cold, before lifting
  them off.
</Step>

<Step id="assemble">
  Pipe a little of the ganache onto the flat side of half the shells and sandwich each with
  another shell of matching size. Set them aside to firm for a while before serving.
  Assembling takes <Dur step="assemble"/>.
</Step>

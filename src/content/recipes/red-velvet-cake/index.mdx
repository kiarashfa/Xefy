<Step id="heat-oven">
  Heat the oven to <Temp c={175}/> and butter and line two cake tins, allowing
  <Dur step="heat-oven"/> for the oven to come to temperature.
</Step>

<Step id="mix-dry">
  Whisk the <Qty ref="flour"/>, <Qty ref="cocoa"/>, <Qty ref="bicarb"/> and
  <Qty ref="salt"/> together in a bowl.
</Step>

<Step id="mix-batter">
  Whisk the <Qty ref="sugar"/> with the <Qty ref="oil"/> and the <Qty ref="eggs"/>
  until smooth. Whisk in the <Qty ref="buttermilk"/>, the <Qty ref="vanilla"/>, the
  <Qty ref="vinegar"/> and the <Qty ref="food-colouring"/>, then fold in the dry
  ingredients over <Dur step="mix-batter"/>, mixing only until smooth. Divide the
  batter between the prepared tins straight away.
</Step>

<Step id="bake">
  Bake at <Temp c={175}/> for <Dur step="bake"/>, until a skewer pushed into the centre
  of each sponge comes out clean.
</Step>

<Step id="cool">
  Cool the sponges in their tins briefly, then turn them out onto a rack and leave to
  cool completely, for <Dur step="cool"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="make-frosting">
  While the sponges cool, beat the <Qty ref="cream-cheese"/> with the
  <Qty ref="butter-icing"/>, the <Qty ref="icing-sugar"/> and the <Qty ref="vanilla-icing"/>
  until smooth and holding soft peaks, over <Dur step="make-frosting"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="frost">
  Spread a layer of frosting over the top of one cooled sponge, set the second sponge
  on top, and cover the top and sides with the remaining frosting, spending
  <Dur step="frost"/> to leave an even coat.
</Step>

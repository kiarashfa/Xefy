<Step id="make-dressing">
  While the chorizo grills, combine the <Qty ref="parsley"/>, <Qty ref="garlic"/>,
  <Qty ref="oregano"/> and <Qty ref="chilli-flakes"/> in a bowl, then stir in
  the <Qty ref="vinegar"/>, <Qty ref="oil"/> and <Qty ref="salt"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="grill-chorizo">
  Grill the <Qty ref="chorizo"/> split side down first, then turn and cook
  for <Dur step="grill-chorizo"/> in total, until the casing is well coloured
  and the sausages are cooked through and opened out flat.
</Step>

<Step id="assemble">
  Warm the <Qty ref="roll"/> briefly on the grill, cut side down. Lay a
  split chorizo inside each roll and spoon over the dressing.
</Step>

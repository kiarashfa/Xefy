<Step id="knead">
  Combine the <Qty ref="lamb"/>, <Qty ref="onion"/>, <Qty ref="ginger"/>,
  <Qty ref="garlic"/>, <Qty ref="chilli"/>, <Qty ref="cumin"/>,
  <Qty ref="chilli-powder"/>, <Qty ref="paprika"/>, <Qty ref="coriander"/>,
  <Qty ref="mint"/> and <Qty ref="salt"/> in a wide bowl. Knead the mixture by hand for
  <Dur step="knead"/>, until it turns dense and sticky and holds together as one mass.
</Step>

<Step id="chill">
  Cover the mince and chill it for <Dur step="chill"/>, so it firms up and is easier to
  mould onto the skewers.
</Step>

<Step id="shape">
  Divide the mince into even portions. With wet hands, mould each portion around a
  skewer, pressing and smoothing it into a long, even cylinder that grips the metal.
  Work for <Dur step="shape"/> in total.
</Step>

<Step id="grill">
  Grill the skewers over fierce, direct heat, turning them often, for
  <Dur step="grill"/>, until well charred outside and cooked through with no pink
  remaining at the centre.
</Step>

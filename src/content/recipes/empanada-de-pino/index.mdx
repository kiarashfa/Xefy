notes:
  techniqueRationale:
    - stepId: cook-filling
      text: Chilean pino is built on far more onion than meat, cooked down slowly until it collapses into the mince rather than staying distinct. That long, gentle cooking is what gives the filling its characteristic sweetness and body, not the beef alone.
  commonPitfalls:
    - stepId: shape
      text: The egg wedge, olive and raisins go in last, on top of the cooled meat, so they keep their own texture rather than being stirred through and crushed. Mixing them into the pino loses the point of including them at all.

<Step id="make-dough">
  Combine the <Qty ref="flour"/> and <Qty ref="dough-salt"/> in a bowl. Add
  the <Qty ref="dough-butter"/>, <Qty ref="dough-egg"/> and the
  <Qty ref="dough-water"/>, mixing until a smooth, pliable dough comes
  together.
</Step>

<Step id="rest-dough">
  Wrap the dough and rest it for <Dur step="rest-dough"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-filling">
  While the dough rests, melt the <Qty ref="cook-butter"/> in a wide pan and
  cook the <Qty ref="onion"/> over a low heat, stirring often, until fully
  soft and collapsing rather than crisp. Add the <Qty ref="beef"/>,
  <Qty ref="cumin"/>, <Qty ref="paprika"/> and <Qty ref="chilli-flakes"/> and
  cook until the meat is no longer pink. Stir the <Qty ref="flour-thickener"/>
  through, then add the <Qty ref="braising-water"/> and the
  <Qty ref="filling-salt"/> and <Qty ref="pepper"/>. Simmer for
  <Dur step="cook-filling"/>, until thickened and glossy rather than wet.
</Step>

<Step id="cool-filling">
  Spread the filling on a tray and cool completely, for
  <Dur step="cool-filling"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="shape">
  Roll the dough and cut into large rounds. Spoon the cooled pino onto one
  half of each round, then top with the <Qty ref="raisins"/>, a wedge of the
  <Qty ref="boiled-egg"/> and a whole <Qty ref="olives"/>. Fold the dough
  over, press the edges firmly together and fold the sealed edge over on
  itself along its length. Brush with the <Qty ref="egg-wash"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="bake">
  Bake at <Temp c={200}/> for <Dur step="bake"/>, until deep golden.
</Step>

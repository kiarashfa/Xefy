<Step id="blanch-pork">
  Blanch the <Qty ref="pork"/> in a pot of boiling water for
  <Dur step="blanch-pork"/>, then drain and rinse.
</Step>

<Step id="caramelise-sugar">
  Heat the <Qty ref="oil"/> in a heavy pot over a low heat and add the
  <Qty ref="sugar"/>. Cook gently, swirling, for <Dur step="caramelise-sugar"/>,
  until melted and a deep amber colour.
</Step>

<Step id="sear-pork">
  Add the blanched pork to the pot and turn it through the caramel until every
  side is coated and lightly coloured, about <Dur step="sear-pork"/>. Add the
  <Qty ref="ginger"/> and <Qty ref="spring-onion"/> and stir briefly.
</Step>

<Step id="braise">
  Pour in the <Qty ref="shaoxing-wine"/>, <Qty ref="light-soy"/>,
  <Qty ref="dark-soy"/> and <Qty ref="water"/>, and add the
  <Qty ref="star-anise"/> and <Qty ref="cinnamon"/>. Bring to a bare simmer,
  cover with the lid slightly ajar, and braise for <Dur step="braise"/>, until
  the pork is completely tender.
</Step>

<Step id="reduce">
  Uncover fully and raise the heat, reducing the sauce for
  <Dur step="reduce"/>, until thick and glossy enough to coat the pork. Discard
  the spices and finish with a little extra sliced spring onion.
</Step>

<Step id="make-dough">
  Combine the <Qty ref="masa-harina"/> and <Qty ref="salt"/>, then work in
  the <Qty ref="water"/> and the <Qty ref="cheese"/> to form a soft, slightly
  sticky dough. This takes about <Dur step="make-dough"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="rest-dough">
  Cover the dough and leave it to rest for <Dur step="rest-dough"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="shape">
  Divide the dough into as many equal portions as arepas are being made and
  pat each into a flat, even round. This takes about <Dur step="shape"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="cook">
  Melt the <Qty ref="butter"/> on a griddle or in a wide pan over a medium
  heat and cook the arepas for <Dur step="cook"/> in total, turning once or
  twice, until deeply golden and cooked through to the centre.
</Step>

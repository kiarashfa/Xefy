<Step id="blend-corn">
  Blend the <Qty ref="corn"/> to a coarse, wet purée. This takes about
  <Dur step="blend-corn"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="make-dough">
  Stir the <Qty ref="masa-harina"/>, <Qty ref="sugar"/>, <Qty ref="salt"/>
  and <Qty ref="cheese"/> into the corn purée to make a soft, thick batter.
  This takes about <Dur step="make-dough"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="shape">
  Divide the batter into as many equal portions as arepas are being made and
  pat each into a flat, even round. This takes about <Dur step="shape"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="cook">
  Melt the <Qty ref="butter"/> on a griddle or in a wide pan over a
  medium-low heat and cook the arepas for <Dur step="cook"/> in total,
  turning once or twice, until golden and set through.
</Step>

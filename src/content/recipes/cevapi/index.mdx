<Step id="mix-meat">
  Combine the <Qty ref="beef-mince"/>, <Qty ref="lamb-mince"/>, <Qty ref="garlic"/>,
  <Qty ref="fine-sea-salt"/>, <Qty ref="black-pepper"/>, <Qty ref="bicarbonate-of-soda"/>
  and the <Qty ref="water"/> in a large bowl. Mix by hand for <Dur step="mix-meat"/>,
  until sticky and well combined.
</Step>

<Step id="rest-meat">
  Cover and refrigerate the mixture for <Dur step="rest-meat"/>, so it firms up and
  the bicarbonate of soda has time to work.
</Step>

<Step id="shape-cevapi">
  With cold, wet hands, roll the mixture into short, finger-thick lengths, working
  for <Dur step="shape-cevapi"/> and keeping the mixture cold throughout.
</Step>

<Step id="grill-cevapi">
  Grill the ćevapi over high, direct heat, turning regularly, for
  <Dur step="grill-cevapi"/>, until well charred outside and cooked through.
</Step>

<Step id="assemble">
  Split open the warm <Qty ref="pita-bread"/> and tuck the grilled ćevapi inside,
  scattering over the diced <Qty ref="onion"/> and spooning over the
  <Qty ref="clotted-cream"/>. This takes <Dur step="assemble"/>.
</Step>

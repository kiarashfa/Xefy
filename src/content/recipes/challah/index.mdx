<Step id="mix">
  Put the <Qty ref="flour"/>, the <Qty ref="salt"/> and the <Qty ref="yeast"/> into a large
  bowl and stir them together. Add the <Qty ref="water"/>, the <Qty ref="honey"/>, the
  <Qty ref="oil"/> and the <Qty ref="egg-dough"/>, and mix for <Dur step="mix"/>, until no dry
  flour remains and the dough is shaggy.
</Step>

<Step id="knead">
  Turn the dough onto a lightly floured bench and knead for <Dur step="knead"/>, until it is
  smooth, elastic and springs back when pressed.
</Step>

<Step id="first-rise">
  Put the dough in an oiled bowl, cover it, and leave it to rise for <Dur step="first-rise"/>,
  until it has doubled in size.
</Step>

<Step id="shape">
  Divide the dough into three equal pieces and roll each one into a rope about <Len cm={40}/>
  long. Lay the three ropes side by side and pinch them together at one end. Bring the right
  rope over the middle one, then the left rope over the middle one, and repeat until you reach
  the other end. Pinch the ends together and tuck them under the loaf.
</Step>

<Step id="second-rise">
  Set the braid on a lined tray, cover it, and leave it to prove for <Dur step="second-rise"/>,
  until a fingertip pressed in leaves a dent that springs back slowly.
</Step>

<Step id="heat-oven">
  Heat the oven to <Temp c={180}/> with a shelf in the middle.
</Step>

<Step id="glaze">
  Brush the braid lightly with the <Qty ref="egg-glaze"/>, and scatter the <Qty ref="poppy"/>
  over the top.
</Step>

<Step id="bake">
  Bake for <Dur step="bake"/>, until deep golden and the base sounds hollow when tapped. If the
  top colours before the middle is cooked, lay a sheet of foil over it.
</Step>

<Step id="cool">
  Lift the loaf onto a rack and leave it for <Dur step="cool"/> before slicing.
</Step>

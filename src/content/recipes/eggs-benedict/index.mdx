<Step id="melt-butter">
  Melt the <Qty ref="butter"/> gently and skim off the pale, milky solids that rise to
  the top, leaving the clear golden fat. This takes <Dur step="melt-butter"/> and keeps
  the finished sauce from tasting of raw milk solids.
</Step>

<Step id="make-hollandaise">
  Whisk the <Qty ref="egg-yolks"/> with the <Qty ref="lemon-juice"/> in a heatproof bowl
  set over a pan of barely simmering water, without letting the bowl touch the water.
  Whisk constantly for <Dur step="make-hollandaise"/>, until the mixture is thick, pale
  and doubled in volume, then take the bowl off the heat and trickle in the warm
  clarified butter, whisking constantly, until fully emulsified. Season with the
  <Qty ref="cayenne"/> and the <Qty ref="salt"/> and keep warm.
</Step>

<Step id="warm-ham">
  Warm the <Qty ref="ham"/> in a dry pan over a medium heat for <Dur step="warm-ham"/>,
  turning once, until heated through and lightly coloured at the edges.
</Step>

<Step id="toast-muffins">
  Toast the <Qty ref="muffins"/>, cut sides up, until golden, over <Dur step="toast-muffins"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="poach-eggs">
  Bring a wide pan of water to a bare simmer and add the <Qty ref="poaching-vinegar"/>.
  Crack the <Qty ref="poaching-eggs"/> one at a time into a cup and slide each gently
  into the water, holding the white close to the yolk with a spoon for the first few
  seconds. Poach for <Dur step="poach-eggs"/>, until the white is set and the yolk still
  soft, then lift out with a slotted spoon onto kitchen paper.
</Step>

<Step id="assemble">
  Top each toasted muffin half with a piece of the warm ham, then a poached egg, and
  spoon the hollandaise generously over the top. Serve immediately, over
  <Dur step="assemble"/>, while the sauce is still warm and the yolk still runs.
</Step>

<Step id="cream-butter">
  Beat the <Qty ref="butter"/> and the <Qty ref="sugar"/> together until smooth and
  pale, without beating in excess air.
</Step>

<Step id="bring-together">
  Add the <Qty ref="flour"/> and the <Qty ref="salt"/> and work them in with a wooden
  spoon and then by hand, just until the mixture comes together into a smooth,
  short dough.
</Step>

<Step id="press-and-score">
  Press the dough evenly into a lined tin, prick all over with a fork, and score into
  fingers without cutting all the way through.
</Step>

<Step id="chill">
  Chill for <Dur step="chill"/>, to firm the butter before it meets the oven.
</Step>

<Step id="bake">
  Bake at <Temp c={150}/> for <Dur step="bake"/>, until pale gold and firm at the
  edges but still soft in the centre.
</Step>

<Step id="cool">
  Recut along the scored lines while still warm, then leave in the tin to cool
  completely for <Dur step="cool"/> before turning out.
</Step>

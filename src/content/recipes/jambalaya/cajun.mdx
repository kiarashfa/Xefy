<Step id="prep-veg">
  Dice the <Qty ref="onion"/>, <Qty ref="celery"/> and <Qty ref="green-pepper"/>, mince
  the <Qty ref="garlic"/>, slice the <Qty ref="sausage"/>, and dice the
  <Qty ref="tasso"/> and <Qty ref="chicken"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="brown-meats">
  Heat the <Qty ref="oil"/> in a large, heavy pot over a medium-high heat and brown the
  sausage, tasso and chicken hard for <Dur step="brown-meats"/>, until a deep brown
  fond builds up on the bottom of the pot. Lift the meats out and set aside.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-trinity">
  Add the onion, celery and green pepper to the pot and cook for
  <Dur step="cook-trinity"/>, scraping up the fond as they release their liquid, and
  adding the garlic in the last minute.
</Step>

<Step id="add-rice">
  Stir in the <Qty ref="rice"/>, <Qty ref="bay"/>, <Qty ref="cayenne"/>,
  <Qty ref="black-pepper"/> and <Qty ref="salt"/>, and return the meats to the pot.
</Step>

<Step id="simmer-jambalaya">
  Pour in the <Qty ref="water"/>, bring to the boil, then cover and reduce to the
  lowest heat. Cook, undisturbed, for <Dur step="simmer-jambalaya"/>, until the rice is
  tender and the liquid is absorbed. Discard the bay leaves before serving.
</Step>

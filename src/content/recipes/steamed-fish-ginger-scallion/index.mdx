<Step id="season-fish">
  Pat the <Qty ref="fish"/> dry and rub the <Qty ref="salt"/> over the skin and
  inside the cavity. Stuff the cavity with the <Qty ref="ginger-cavity"/> and
  <Qty ref="spring-onion-cavity"/>, taking <Dur step="season-fish"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="steam-fish">
  Set the fish on a heatproof plate and steam over rapidly boiling water for
  <Dur step="steam-fish"/>, until the flesh just flakes at the thickest part.
</Step>

<Step id="make-sauce">
  Meanwhile, warm the <Qty ref="soy-sauce"/> and <Qty ref="sugar"/> together
  gently until the sugar dissolves, taking <Dur step="make-sauce"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="finish">
  Lift the fish from the steamer, discarding the liquid that has collected on
  the plate, and pour over the warm soy mixture. Scatter with the
  <Qty ref="ginger-garnish"/> and <Qty ref="spring-onion-garnish"/>. Heat the
  <Qty ref="oil"/> in a small pan until smoking, then pour it directly over the
  aromatics, taking <Dur step="finish"/>, so it sizzles as it lands.
</Step>

<Step id="heat-skillet">
  Put a well-seasoned cast-iron skillet into the oven and heat it to <Temp c={220}/>,
  letting it heat through for <Dur step="heat-skillet"/> while the batter is mixed.
</Step>

<Step id="mix-dry">
  Whisk the <Qty ref="cornmeal"/>, <Qty ref="flour"/>, <Qty ref="baking-powder"/>,
  <Qty ref="bicarb"/> and <Qty ref="salt"/> together in a large bowl.
</Step>

<Step id="mix-batter">
  Beat the <Qty ref="egg"/> into the <Qty ref="buttermilk"/>, then stir this and the
  <Qty ref="butter"/> into the dry ingredients over <Dur step="mix-batter"/>, mixing only
  until no dry streaks remain.
</Step>

<Step id="bake">
  Carefully remove the hot skillet from the oven, add a little more butter and swirl it
  to coat the base and sides, then pour in the batter — it should sizzle on contact.
  Return the skillet to the oven and bake for <Dur step="bake"/>, until the top is
  golden and a skewer pushed into the centre comes out clean. Turn the cornbread out
  onto a board before cutting, so the crisp base stays crisp rather than steaming
  against the hot pan.
</Step>

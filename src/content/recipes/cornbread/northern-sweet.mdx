<Step id="heat-oven">
  Heat the oven to <Temp c={190}/> and butter a square baking tin, allowing
  <Dur step="heat-oven"/> for the oven to come to temperature.
</Step>

<Step id="mix-dry">
  Whisk the <Qty ref="cornmeal"/>, <Qty ref="flour"/>, <Qty ref="baking-powder"/>,
  <Qty ref="salt"/> and <Qty ref="sugar"/> together in a large bowl.
</Step>

<Step id="mix-batter">
  Beat the <Qty ref="egg"/> into the <Qty ref="milk"/>, then stir this and the
  <Qty ref="butter"/> into the dry ingredients over <Dur step="mix-batter"/>, mixing
  only until smooth and no dry streaks remain.
</Step>

<Step id="bake">
  Pour the batter into the prepared tin and bake for <Dur step="bake"/>, until golden
  and a skewer pushed into the centre comes out clean. Cool in the tin a little before
  cutting into squares.
</Step>

    - stepId: soak-bulgur

    - stepId: cut-herbs

<Step id="dice-tomatoes">
  Cut the <Qty ref="tomatoes"/> into small, even dice, working over a bowl so that none
  of the juice is lost. Take <Dur step="dice-tomatoes"/> and keep the pieces small —
  they should be a texture in the salad rather than an obstacle. Turn the
  <Qty ref="salt"/> through them.
</Step>

<Step id="soak-bulgur">
  Stir the <Qty ref="bulgur"/> and the <Qty ref="lemon-soak"/> into the tomatoes and
  leave them for <Dur step="soak-bulgur"/>. The grain swells on the juice alone and
  should finish tender with a distinct bite; if it is still hard, give it longer rather
  than adding water.
</Step>

<Step id="cut-herbs">
  Meanwhile, gather the <Qty ref="parsley"/>, dried thoroughly, into a tight bundle and
  cut across it with the sharpest knife in the house, then turn the pile and cut again,
  working for
  <Dur step="cut-herbs"/> until it is fine but not minced. Do the same with the
  <Qty ref="mint"/>, which bruises faster and goes in last. Slice the
  <Qty ref="spring-onions"/> thinly, greens and all.
</Step>

<Step id="dress">
  Combine the herbs and spring onions with the soaked bulgur and tomatoes, pour over the
  <Qty ref="olive-oil"/> and the <Qty ref="lemon-dress"/>, add the
  <Qty ref="black-pepper"/> and turn it all over with your hands for
  <Dur step="dress"/> — lifting rather than stirring, so the leaves stay separate. Serve
  immediately.
</Step>

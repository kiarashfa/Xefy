<Step id="soak-wakame">
  While the kombu steeps, cover the <Qty ref="wakame"/> with cold water and leave it for
  <Dur step="soak-wakame"/>. It will swell to many times its dry size. Drain it and cut
  any long fronds into short lengths.
</Step>

<Step id="warm-tofu">
  Bring the strained dashi back to just under a simmer, then slide in the
  <Qty ref="tofu"/> and the drained wakame and give them <Dur step="warm-tofu"/> to heat
  through. The surface should barely tremble; silken tofu breaks up if the pan is stirred
  or allowed to boil.
</Step>

<Step id="dissolve-miso">
  Take the pan off the heat. Put the <Qty ref="miso"/> into a ladle, dip the ladle into
  the hot stock and work the paste apart against the metal with chopsticks until it has
  gone completely, then tip it back into the pan and turn it through gently over
  <Dur step="dissolve-miso"/>. Scatter the <Qty ref="spring-onions"/> over and serve at
  once, without letting it near the heat again.
</Step>

<Step id="sear">
  Score the <Qty ref="frankfurter"/> lightly along one side and cook it in a
  dry pan over a medium heat for <Dur step="sear"/>, turning often, until the
  casing is browned and blistered on all sides.
</Step>

<Step id="steam-bun">
  Meanwhile, split the <Qty ref="bun"/> without cutting all the way through
  and warm it over the same pan or in a low oven for <Dur step="steam-bun"/>,
  until soft and heated through.
</Step>

<Step id="assemble">
  Set the frankfurter into the bun and top with the <Qty ref="onion"/>, the
  <Qty ref="relish"/> and, if using, the <Qty ref="ketchup"/>.
</Step>

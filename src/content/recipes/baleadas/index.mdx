<Step id="soak-beans">
  Soak the <Qty ref="kidney-beans"/> in plenty of cold water for
  <Dur step="soak-beans"/>, then drain.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-beans">
  Simmer the soaked beans in the <Qty ref="water"/> for
  <Dur step="cook-beans"/>, until completely soft.
</Step>

<Step id="refry-beans">
  Drain the beans, reserving a little of the cooking liquid, and mash them
  with the <Qty ref="oil"/> and <Qty ref="salt"/> in a pan over a medium
  heat, adding a splash of the reserved liquid as needed, until they form a
  thick, smooth paste. This takes about <Dur step="refry-beans"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="warm-tortillas">
  Warm the <Qty ref="tortilla"/> on a dry, hot griddle or pan for
  <Dur step="warm-tortillas"/>, until soft and lightly speckled.
</Step>

<Step id="assemble">
  Spread the mashed beans over half of each warm tortilla, scatter with the
  <Qty ref="cheese"/> and spoon over the <Qty ref="crema"/>, then fold in
  half. This takes about <Dur step="assemble"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="marinate">
  Combine the <Qty ref="hoisin"/>, <Qty ref="soy-sauce"/>, <Qty ref="dark-soy"/>,
  <Qty ref="shaoxing-wine"/>, <Qty ref="sugar"/>, <Qty ref="garlic"/>,
  <Qty ref="five-spice"/> and <Qty ref="sesame-oil"/> in a bowl and turn the
  <Qty ref="pork"/> through it, coating every side.
</Step>

<Step id="rest-marinade">
  Cover and marinate in the refrigerator for <Dur step="rest-marinade"/>, turning the
  strips over once partway through.
</Step>

<Step id="roast">
  Lift the pork from the marinade, letting the excess drip off, and set the strips on a
  rack over a lined tray. Roast at <Temp c={200}/> for <Dur step="roast"/>, turning
  once, until the edges begin to char and the surface sets.
</Step>

<Step id="baste">
  Brush the strips with the reserved marinade and the <Qty ref="baste-maltose"/>,
  return to the oven and continue roasting for <Dur step="baste"/>, turning and
  basting again halfway through, until the pork is cooked through and deeply
  coloured.
</Step>

<Step id="glaze">
  Warm the <Qty ref="glaze-maltose"/> with the <Qty ref="glaze-water"/> until loose
  enough to brush, and paint it over the hot pork. Rest for <Dur step="glaze"/> before
  slicing.
</Step>

<Step id="soak">
  Cover the <Qty ref="urad-dal"/> with the <Qty ref="soak-water"/> and leave to soak for
  <Dur step="soak"/>, then drain thoroughly.
</Step>

<Step id="grind">
  Grind the drained dal with as little added water as possible into a thick, smooth
  paste, then beat it hard by hand for a share of <Dur step="grind"/> until it lightens
  and holds its shape on a spoon. Fold in the <Qty ref="ginger"/>,
  <Qty ref="curry-leaves"/>, <Qty ref="chilli"/>, <Qty ref="pepper"/> and
  <Qty ref="salt"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="shape-and-fry">
  Heat the <Qty ref="frying-oil"/> in a deep pan to a steady, gentle bubble. With wet
  hands, shape portions of the batter into flattened discs and punch a hole through the
  centre of each to make a ring, then slide them into the oil. Fry for
  <Dur step="shape-and-fry"/>, turning occasionally, until deep golden-brown and crisp
  all over. Lift out and drain.
</Step>

<Step id="prep-vegetables">
  Cut the <Qty ref="tofu"/> into thick cubes and the <Qty ref="napa"/> into wide pieces, keeping
  the stalks and the leaves apart. Separate the <Qty ref="enoki"/> into small clumps and cut the
  <Qty ref="spring-onion"/> on the diagonal. Soak the <Qty ref="shiitake"/> in warm water until
  soft, then trim the stalks and score the caps. Arrange everything on one large platter with the
  <Qty ref="beef"/>, and keep it cool until the pan is ready. This takes
  <Dur step="prep-vegetables"/>, and it runs while the kombu steeps.
</Step>

<Step id="beat-egg">
  Crack the <Qty ref="egg"/> into a bowl and beat it lightly with chopsticks, just enough to break
  up the yolk and white. Divide it between one small bowl for each diner, to be set at each place.
  The egg is raw and is not cooked: it is the dipping sauce for the beef. This takes
  <Dur step="beat-egg"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="make-warishita">
  Pour the strained dashi into a wide, shallow pan with the <Qty ref="soy"/>, the <Qty ref="mirin"/>,
  the <Qty ref="sake"/> and the <Qty ref="sugar"/>. Bring it to a simmer, stirring until the sugar has
  dissolved. This is the warishita, the sweet soy sauce that the beef and vegetables are cooked in.
  It takes <Dur step="make-warishita"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-sukiyaki">
  Set the pan over a medium flame and rub it with the <Qty ref="beef-fat"/>. Add a few slices of the
  <Qty ref="beef"/> and let them colour lightly on one side. Then add the <Qty ref="napa"/>,
  the <Qty ref="shiitake"/> and the <Qty ref="tofu"/>, with the <Qty ref="enoki"/> arranged around them. Simmer for <Dur step="cook-sukiyaki"/>, adding more beef and the
  <Qty ref="spring-onion"/> in the last few minutes, and topping up the sauce with a little dashi
  as it reduces.
</Step>

<Step id="serve-sukiyaki">
  Bring the pan to the table, or serve from it at once, with the bowls of <Qty ref="egg"/> at each
  place. Lift each piece of beef out with chopsticks and dip it in the raw egg before eating it,
  with the vegetables and tofu taken from the pan as they are ready. This takes
  <Dur step="serve-sukiyaki"/>.
</Step>

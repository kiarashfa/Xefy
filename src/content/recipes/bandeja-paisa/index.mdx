<Step id="soak-beans">
  Soak the <Qty ref="kidney-beans"/> in plenty of cold water for
  <Dur step="soak-beans"/>, then drain.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-beans">
  Simmer the soaked beans in the <Qty ref="bean-water"/> with the
  <Qty ref="onion"/>, <Qty ref="garlic"/>, <Qty ref="tomato"/> and half the
  <Qty ref="salt"/> for <Dur step="cook-beans"/>, until completely soft and
  the liquid has thickened.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-rice">
  Meanwhile, cook the <Qty ref="rice"/> according to the packet
  instructions. This takes about <Dur step="cook-rice"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="make-arepas">
  Combine the <Qty ref="masa-harina"/> with the remaining
  <Qty ref="salt"/> and the <Qty ref="arepa-water"/> to form a soft dough,
  divide into four and pat into flat rounds, then cook on a dry, hot
  griddle, turning once or twice, until golden and cooked through. This
  takes about <Dur step="make-arepas"/> in total.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-chicharron">
  Fry the <Qty ref="pork-belly"/> in a dry pan over a medium heat for
  <Dur step="cook-chicharron"/>, until deeply browned and crisp.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-beef">
  Season the <Qty ref="beef-mince"/> and cook it in a hot pan, breaking it
  up or shaping it into thin patties as preferred, for
  <Dur step="cook-beef"/>, until browned through.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-chorizo">
  Cook the <Qty ref="chorizo"/> in a pan over a medium heat for
  <Dur step="cook-chorizo"/>, turning occasionally, until cooked through and
  browned.
</Step>

<Step id="fry-plantain">
  Heat half the <Qty ref="oil"/> in a pan and fry the <Qty ref="plantain"/>
  for <Dur step="fry-plantain"/>, turning once, until golden and caramelised
  at the edges.
</Step>

<Step id="fry-eggs">
  Heat the remaining <Qty ref="oil"/> and fry the <Qty ref="egg"/> for
  <Dur step="fry-eggs"/>, until the whites are set and the yolks still soft.
</Step>

<Step id="plate">
  Divide the beans, rice, beef, chicharrón, chorizo, fried eggs, plantain,
  <Qty ref="avocado"/> and arepas between large plates or trays. This takes
  about <Dur step="plate"/>.
</Step>

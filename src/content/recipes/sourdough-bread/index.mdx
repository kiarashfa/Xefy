<Step id="feed-starter">
  The evening or morning before baking, feed the starter with fresh flour and water and
  leave it at room temperature for <Dur step="feed-starter"/>, until it has visibly
  doubled and looks domed and bubbled across the top.
</Step>

<Step id="autolyse">
  Stir the <Qty ref="flour"/> and the <Qty ref="water"/> together into a shaggy, floury
  dough, with no dry patches left. Cover and leave it to rest for <Dur step="autolyse"/>,
  which lets the flour hydrate fully before any mixing begins.
</Step>

<Step id="mix-dough">
  Add the <Qty ref="starter"/> and the <Qty ref="salt"/> to the rested dough and work
  them in by hand, pinching and folding the dough over itself for
  <Dur step="mix-dough"/>, until both are fully incorporated and the dough feels smooth
  enough to hold together.
</Step>

<Step id="bulk-ferment">
  Cover the dough and leave it to rise at room temperature for <Dur step="bulk-ferment"/>,
  until it looks noticeably fuller and slightly domed.
</Step>

<Step id="fold-dough">
  During the bulk rise, reach into the bowl, stretch one side of the dough up and fold
  it over onto itself, turn the bowl a quarter turn and repeat around all sides. Do this
  a few times at evenly spaced intervals across the rise, spending
  <Dur step="fold-dough"/> in total; each round of folding tightens the dough's
  structure without ever needing to knead it.
</Step>

<Step id="shape">
  Turn the dough out onto a lightly floured surface and shape it into a tight round by
  dragging it against the surface with cupped hands, over <Dur step="shape"/>. Set it
  seam-side up in a floured proving basket.
</Step>

<Step id="cold-retard">
  Cover the basket and refrigerate the shaped dough for <Dur step="cold-retard"/>,
  ideally overnight, so it finishes proving slowly in the cold.
</Step>

<Step id="bake">
  Heat a covered heavy pot in the oven to <Temp c={240}/>. Turn the cold dough out onto
  a sheet of baking paper, score the top with a sharp blade, and lower it into the hot
  pot using the paper as a sling. Bake covered for most of <Dur step="bake"/>, then
  uncover for the final stretch to let the crust brown and turn deep golden.
</Step>

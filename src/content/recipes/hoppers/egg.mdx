<Step id="mix-batter">
  Whisk the <Qty ref="rice-flour"/>, <Qty ref="sugar"/>, <Qty ref="yeast"/> and
  <Qty ref="salt"/> together, then whisk in the <Qty ref="coconut-milk"/> and the
  <Qty ref="water"/> for <Dur step="mix-batter"/>, until completely smooth and about as
  thin as pouring cream.
</Step>

<Step id="ferment">
  Cover the bowl and leave it somewhere warm to ferment for <Dur step="ferment"/>, until
  bubbled and risen.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-base">
  Heat a small curved hopper pan over a high heat and wipe it with a little of the
  <Qty ref="pan-oil"/>. Ladle in a portion of batter and immediately swirl and tilt the
  pan so the batter climbs partway up the sides, leaving a thin lacy edge and a pool in
  the middle. Cook uncovered for a share of <Dur step="cook-base"/>, until the rim has
  set and started to crisp at the edges.
</Step>

<Step id="add-egg">
  Crack the <Qty ref="egg"/> into the pool at the centre, keeping the yolk whole. Cover
  the pan and cook for a share of <Dur step="add-egg"/>, until the white has just set
  and the yolk is still soft. Loosen with a thin spatula and lift out whole. Repeat with
  the remaining batter and egg, re-oiling the pan between hoppers.
</Step>

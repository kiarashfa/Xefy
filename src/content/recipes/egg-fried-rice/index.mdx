<Step id="scramble-egg">
  Heat half the <Qty ref="oil"/> in a wok over high heat and pour in the
  <Qty ref="egg"/>, stirring briefly for <Dur step="scramble-egg"/> to large,
  soft curds. Lift out and set aside.
</Step>

<Step id="fry-rice">
  Heat the remaining oil in the wok and add the <Qty ref="rice"/>, breaking up
  any clumps with the back of a spatula. Fry over high heat for
  <Dur step="fry-rice"/>, tossing constantly, until every grain is hot and
  separate.
</Step>

<Step id="combine">
  Return the egg to the wok with the <Qty ref="spring-onion"/>,
  <Qty ref="soy-sauce"/>, <Qty ref="salt"/> and <Qty ref="white-pepper"/>. Toss
  over high heat for <Dur step="combine"/>, until evenly combined.
</Step>

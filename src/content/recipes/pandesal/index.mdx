<Step id="bloom-yeast">
  Stir the <Qty ref="yeast"/> and <Qty ref="sugar-bloom"/> into the warm
  <Qty ref="milk"/> for <Dur step="bloom-yeast"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="rest-yeast">
  Leave the mixture for <Dur step="rest-yeast"/>, until foamy on top.
</Step>

<Step id="mix-dough">
  Combine the <Qty ref="flour-dough"/>, <Qty ref="salt"/>, <Qty ref="sugar-dough"/>,
  <Qty ref="egg"/> and <Qty ref="butter"/> with the yeast mixture and bring together
  into a dough. Turn out and knead for <Dur step="mix-dough"/>, until smooth and
  elastic.
</Step>

<Step id="first-prove">
  Cover and leave to rise in a warm place for <Dur step="first-prove"/>, until doubled
  in size.
</Step>

<Step id="shape">
  Turn the dough out onto the <Qty ref="flour-bench"/>, divide into even pieces and
  shape into ovals. Roll each piece in the <Qty ref="breadcrumbs"/> to coat, working for
  <Dur step="shape"/>, and arrange on lined trays.
</Step>

<Step id="second-prove">
  Cover loosely and leave to rise again for <Dur step="second-prove"/>, until visibly
  puffed.
</Step>

<Step id="bake">
  Bake at <Temp c={190}/> for <Dur step="bake"/>, until pale gold and firm.
</Step>

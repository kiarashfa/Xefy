<Step id="season">
  Combine the <Qty ref="salt-rub"/>, <Qty ref="pepper-rub"/>, the
  <Qty ref="brown-sugar-rub"/>, <Qty ref="paprika"/>,
  <Qty ref="mustard-powder"/> and <Qty ref="celery-salt"/>, then coat the
  <Qty ref="pork"/> evenly all over.
</Step>

<Step id="smoke">
  Smoke the shoulder at a steady <Temp c={110}/> for <Dur step="smoke"/>,
  until a probe pushed into the thickest part meets no resistance and the
  internal temperature reads close to <Temp c={95}/>.
</Step>

<Step id="rest">
  Wrap the shoulder loosely and rest it for <Dur step="rest"/>, until it is
  cool enough to handle without burning your hands.
</Step>

<Step id="shred">
  Pull the meat apart into shreds by hand or with two forks, discarding any
  large pieces of fat and the bone, for <Dur step="shred"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="sauce">
  Whisk together the <Qty ref="vinegar"/>, the <Qty ref="brown-sugar-sauce"/>,
  the <Qty ref="salt-sauce"/>, the <Qty ref="pepper-sauce"/> and the
  <Qty ref="chilli-flakes"/>, then toss it through the warm pork until it is
  evenly moistened.
</Step>

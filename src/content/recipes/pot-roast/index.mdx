  techniqueRationale:
    - stepId: sear
      text: A hard sear before the liquid goes in builds a deep, browned crust on the meat that the braise itself cannot produce — a roast lowered straight into liquid never develops the same flavour.
  commonPitfalls:
    - stepId: add-vegetables

<Step id="dredge">
  Season the <Qty ref="beef"/> with half the <Qty ref="salt"/> and the
  <Qty ref="pepper"/>, then dust it all over with the <Qty ref="flour"/>,
  patting off the excess.
</Step>

<Step id="sear">
  Heat the <Qty ref="oil"/> in a heavy pot over a high heat and sear the beef
  for <Dur step="sear"/>, turning to brown every side. Remove and set aside.
</Step>

<Step id="soften-veg">
  Lower the heat and cook the <Qty ref="onion"/>, the <Qty ref="carrot-braise"/>,
  the <Qty ref="celery"/> and the <Qty ref="garlic"/> for
  <Dur step="soften-veg"/>, stirring, until softened. Stir in the
  <Qty ref="tomato-puree"/> and cook briefly.
</Step>

<Step id="braise-first">
  Dissolve the <Qty ref="bouillon"/> in the <Qty ref="water"/> and pour it
  over the vegetables. Return the beef to the pot along with the
  <Qty ref="thyme"/> and the <Qty ref="bay"/>, bring to a bare simmer, cover
  and transfer to a low oven at <Temp c={150}/> for
  <Dur step="braise-first"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="add-vegetables">
  Tuck the <Qty ref="potato"/> and the <Qty ref="carrot-chunks"/> into the
  liquid around the beef, seasoning with the remaining salt.
</Step>

<Step id="braise-second">
  Cover and return to the oven for a further <Dur step="braise-second"/>,
  until the beef is fork-tender and the vegetables are cooked through.
</Step>

<Step id="rest">
  Lift the beef onto a board and rest for <Dur step="rest"/>, loosely tented,
  before carving.
</Step>

<Step id="carve">
  Slice the beef against the grain and serve with the vegetables and the
  braising liquid spooned over as a gravy.
</Step>

<Step id="soak-beans">
  Soak the <Qty ref="black-beans"/> in plenty of cold water for
  <Dur step="soak-beans"/>, then drain.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-beans">
  Simmer the soaked beans in the <Qty ref="bean-water"/> for
  <Dur step="cook-beans"/>, until completely soft.
</Step>

<Step id="mash-beans">
  Drain the beans and mash them with the <Qty ref="bean-salt"/> in a pan over
  a medium heat for <Dur step="mash-beans"/>, until they form a thick paste.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-pork">
  Meanwhile, cook the <Qty ref="pork-mince"/> and <Qty ref="onion"/> in a
  separate pan over a medium heat for <Dur step="cook-pork"/>, breaking the
  meat up finely, until browned and cooked through.
</Step>

<Step id="make-dough">
  Combine the <Qty ref="masa-harina"/> and <Qty ref="dough-salt"/>, then work
  in the <Qty ref="dough-water"/> to form a soft, smooth dough. This takes
  about <Dur step="make-dough"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="rest-dough">
  Cover the dough and leave it to rest for <Dur step="rest-dough"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="shape-and-fill">
  Divide the dough into as many equal balls as pupusas are being made.
  Flatten each into a cup, spoon in a little of the mashed beans, cooked
  pork and <Qty ref="cheese"/>, then draw the edges up and pinch closed
  around the filling. Flatten each filled ball gently between the palms
  into a thick disc, taking care not to let the filling break through. This
  takes about <Dur step="shape-and-fill"/> in total.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-pupusas">
  Cook the discs on a lightly oiled griddle with the <Qty ref="oil"/> over a
  moderate heat for <Dur step="cook-pupusas"/> in total, turning once or
  twice, until both sides are browned in spots and the dough is cooked
  through.
</Step>

<Step id="warm-stock">
  Bring the <Qty ref="stock"/> to a bare simmer in a separate pan and keep it there. Ladle
  off a cupful, crumble the <Qty ref="saffron"/> into it and leave it to steep.
</Step>

<Step id="soften-onion">
  Melt the <Qty ref="butter-toast"/> in a wide, heavy pan over a low flame. Add the
  <Qty ref="onion"/> and cook gently for <Dur step="soften-onion"/>, until soft and
  translucent but not coloured.
</Step>

<Step id="toast-rice">
  Tip in the <Qty ref="rice"/> and stir it over a moderate flame until every grain is
  glossy and the edges have gone translucent, for <Dur step="toast-rice"/>. Pour in the
  <Qty ref="white-wine"/> and let it cook away, stirring.
</Step>

<Step id="add-stock">
  Add the hot stock a ladleful at a time, stirring, and wait for each addition to be
  taken up before the next goes in. Stir in the saffron liquor with the last of the
  stock. Cook for <Dur step="add-stock"/>, until the rice is just tender with a little
  bite at the centre and the mixture runs loosely across the pan.
</Step>

<Step id="finish-risotto">
  Take the pan off the heat. Beat in the <Qty ref="butter-finish"/> and the
  <Qty ref="parmigiano"/> with the <Qty ref="salt"/> until the rice is glossy, for
  <Dur step="finish-risotto"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="rest-risotto">
  Cover the pan and leave the risotto to rest for <Dur step="rest-risotto"/>, so that it
  loosens to a slow wave, then serve at once.
</Step>

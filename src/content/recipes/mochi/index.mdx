<Step id="mix-dough">
  Whisk the <Qty ref="flour"/>, the <Qty ref="sugar"/> and the <Qty ref="water"/> in a heatproof bowl
  until there are no lumps. The mixture should be thin and smooth. This takes <Dur step="mix-dough"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="steam-dough">
  Set the bowl in a steamer over boiling water, cover, and steam for <Dur step="steam-dough"/>, stirring
  once halfway through, until the dough is translucent and firm enough to hold its shape.
</Step>

<Step id="dust-board">
  Dust a clean board with the <Qty ref="starch-board"/>, so that the hot dough will not stick. This
  takes <Dur step="dust-board"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="knead-dough">
  Turn the hot dough out onto the dusted board and knead it for <Dur step="knead-dough"/>, using the
  <Qty ref="starch-dough"/> on your hands and the surface, until the dough is smooth and elastic.
</Step>

<Step id="shape-mochi">
  Divide the dough into pieces of equal weight, and roll each into a smooth ball or a flat round.
  One serving is one piece. Dust each piece lightly and set it aside, and eat it while it is still
  soft. This takes <Dur step="shape-mochi"/>.
</Step>

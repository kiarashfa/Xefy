<Step id="mix">
  Put the <Qty ref="flour"/>, the <Qty ref="salt"/> and the <Qty ref="yeast"/> into a large
  bowl and stir them together. Add the <Qty ref="water"/> and the <Qty ref="oil"/>, and mix
  for <Dur step="mix"/>, until no dry flour remains and the dough is shaggy.
</Step>

<Step id="knead">
  Turn the dough onto a lightly floured bench and knead for <Dur step="knead"/>, until it is
  smooth, elastic and springs back when pressed.
</Step>

<Step id="first-rise">
  Put the dough in an oiled bowl, cover it, and leave it to rise for <Dur step="first-rise"/>,
  until it has doubled in size.
</Step>

<Step id="shape">
  Divide the dough into equal pieces and roll each one into a smooth ball. Flatten each ball
  and roll it into a round about <Len cm={18}/> across, dusting the bench as needed. Set the
  rounds on a floured cloth and cover them.
</Step>

<Step id="second-rise">
  Leave the rounds to prove for <Dur step="second-rise"/>, until a fingertip pressed in leaves
  a dent that springs back slowly.
</Step>

<Step id="heat-oven">
  Heat the oven to <Temp c={250}/> with a heavy baking tray or stone on the lowest shelf. Let it
  heat for <Dur step="heat-oven"/> so the tray is fully hot before the first round goes in.
</Step>

<Step id="bake">
  Bake the rounds one at a time, or as many as fit without touching, for <Dur step="bake"/>,
  until they puff and are just beginning to colour. A round that has not puffed within a few
  minutes is not hot enough.
</Step>

<Step id="cool">
  Wrap the breads in a clean cloth as they come out, so they stay soft, and leave them for
  <Dur step="cool"/>.
</Step>

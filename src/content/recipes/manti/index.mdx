<Step id="mix-dough">
  Combine the <Qty ref="flour-dough"/>, <Qty ref="egg"/>, <Qty ref="water"/> and
  <Qty ref="dough-salt"/> in a bowl and bring together into a firm dough, then turn it
  out and knead for <Dur step="mix-dough"/> until smooth.
</Step>

<Step id="rest-dough">
  Cover the dough and rest it for <Dur step="rest-dough"/> at room temperature, until it
  has relaxed and no longer springs back when pressed.
</Step>

<Step id="make-filling">
  Meanwhile, combine the <Qty ref="lamb"/>, <Qty ref="onion"/>,
  <Qty ref="black-pepper"/>, <Qty ref="parsley"/> and <Qty ref="filling-salt"/> in a
  bowl and mix until evenly combined.
</Step>

<Step id="make-sauce">
  Whisk the <Qty ref="yoghurt"/>, <Qty ref="garlic"/> and <Qty ref="sauce-salt"/>
  together and set aside at room temperature.
</Step>

<Step id="shape">
  Roll the rested dough out on a surface dusted with the <Qty ref="flour-bench"/> until
  paper-thin, and cut it into small squares no larger than a fingernail, about
  <Len cm={2}/> across. Place a speck of filling in the centre of each square and pinch
  all four corners up to seal, forming a small closed pouch. Set the shaped mantı close
  together on a floured tray as you go.
</Step>

<Step id="boil">
  Drop the mantı into a large pot of gently boiling water and cook for
  <Dur step="boil"/>, until they float and the dough is cooked through.
</Step>

<Step id="brown-butter">
  Meanwhile, melt the <Qty ref="butter"/> in a small pan and stir in the
  <Qty ref="paprika"/>. Cook for <Dur step="brown-butter"/>, until it foams and smells
  toasted, then take off the heat.
</Step>

<Step id="assemble">
  Spread the yoghurt sauce over warmed plates. Lift the mantı straight from the pot on
  top, drizzle the paprika butter over, and scatter with the <Qty ref="mint"/>.
</Step>

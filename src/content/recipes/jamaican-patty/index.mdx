<Step id="make-pastry">
  Whisk the <Qty ref="flour"/>, <Qty ref="turmeric"/> and
  <Qty ref="salt-pastry"/> together, then rub in the <Qty ref="shortening"/>
  until the mixture looks like coarse crumbs. Add the <Qty ref="cold-water"/>
  and bring together into a firm dough without overworking it. This takes
  about <Dur step="make-pastry"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="chill-pastry">
  Wrap the dough and refrigerate for <Dur step="chill-pastry"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-filling">
  Heat the <Qty ref="oil"/> in a pan over a medium heat and cook the
  <Qty ref="onion"/>, <Qty ref="garlic"/>, <Qty ref="spring-onion"/>,
  <Qty ref="scotch-bonnet"/> and <Qty ref="thyme"/> until softened. Add the
  <Qty ref="beef"/> and cook, breaking it up, until browned. Stir in the
  <Qty ref="allspice"/>, <Qty ref="breadcrumbs"/>, <Qty ref="stock"/>,
  <Qty ref="salt-filling"/> and <Qty ref="black-pepper"/> and simmer until
  the liquid is absorbed. This takes about <Dur step="cook-filling"/> in
  total.
</Step>

<Step id="cool-filling">
  Spread the filling on a tray and leave to cool for
  <Dur step="cool-filling"/>, until close to room temperature.
</Step>

<Step id="shape">
  Roll out the pastry and cut into rounds. Spoon the cooled filling onto one
  half of each round, fold over and crimp the edges firmly to seal. This
  takes about <Dur step="shape"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="bake">
  Bake at <Temp c={200}/> for <Dur step="bake"/>, until the pastry is puffed
  and deep golden.
</Step>

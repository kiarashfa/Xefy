<Step id="whisk-yolks">
  Whisk the <Qty ref="yolk"/> with the <Qty ref="sugar"/> in a bowl until pale, thick and
  falling in a ribbon, for <Dur step="whisk-yolks"/>. The yolks are not cooked, so the
  mixture has to be beaten until it is at its fullest.
</Step>

<Step id="fold-mascarpone">
  Beat the <Qty ref="mascarpone"/> into the yolk mixture in two batches, until the cream is
  smooth and holds its shape on the whisk, for <Dur step="fold-mascarpone"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="coffee-soak">
  Mix the <Qty ref="espresso"/> with the <Qty ref="marsala"/> in a shallow dish, and let it
  cool completely. Dip the <Qty ref="savoiardi"/> one at a time, turning each once, so that
  the dipping takes <Dur step="coffee-soak"/> in all.
</Step>

<Step id="layer">
  Lay a layer of the dipped biscuits in a dish, spread half the cream over them, then repeat
  with the rest of the biscuits and finish with the cream. Dust the surface with the
  <Qty ref="cocoa"/> through a fine sieve just before it is served.
</Step>

<Step id="chill">
  Cover the dish and chill it for <Dur step="chill"/>, until the biscuits have softened right
  through and the cream has set.
</Step>

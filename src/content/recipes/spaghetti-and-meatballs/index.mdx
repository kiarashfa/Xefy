<Step id="soak-panade">
  Stir the <Qty ref="breadcrumbs"/> into the <Qty ref="milk"/> and set aside
  to soften for <Dur step="soak-panade"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="make-meatballs">
  Combine the <Qty ref="beef"/>, the soaked breadcrumbs, the <Qty ref="egg"/>,
  the <Qty ref="parmesan"/>, the <Qty ref="garlic-meatballs"/>, the
  <Qty ref="parsley"/> and the <Qty ref="pepper"/>,
  mixing only until combined. Shape into evenly sized balls.
</Step>

<Step id="brown-meatballs">
  Heat the <Qty ref="oil-brown"/> in a wide pot over a medium-high heat and
  brown the meatballs on all sides for <Dur step="brown-meatballs"/>. They do
  not need to be cooked through yet. Remove and set aside.
</Step>

<Step id="make-sauce">
  Add the <Qty ref="oil-sauce"/> to the same pot and cook the
  <Qty ref="onion"/> and the <Qty ref="garlic-sauce"/> for
  <Dur step="make-sauce"/>, until softened. Add the <Qty ref="tomatoes"/> and
  the <Qty ref="oregano"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="simmer">
  Return the meatballs to the sauce and simmer gently, partly covered, for
  <Dur step="simmer"/>, until the meatballs are cooked through and the sauce
  has thickened.
</Step>

<Step id="combine">
  Divide the drained spaghetti among bowls, top with the meatballs and sauce,
  and finish with the <Qty ref="basil"/>.
</Step>

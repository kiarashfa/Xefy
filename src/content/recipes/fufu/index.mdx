<Step id="boil">
  Put the <Qty ref="cassava"/> and <Qty ref="plantain"/> into a large pot, cover
  generously with water and bring to the boil. Cook for <Dur step="boil"/>, until both
  are completely soft and a knife slides through with no resistance. Drain, keeping
  back a little of the cooking water.
</Step>

<Step id="pound">
  Turn the drained cassava and plantain into a large mortar and pound with a pestle,
  turning and folding the mass between strokes and working in a splash of the reserved
  water now and then. Continue for <Dur step="pound"/>, until the dough is smooth,
  stretchy and free of lumps, and pulls away from the sides of the mortar as one piece.
</Step>

<Step id="shape">
  Wet your hands and divide the dough into portions, shaping each into a smooth,
  rounded mound. This takes <Dur step="shape"/>. Serve warm, alongside a soup or stew.
</Step>

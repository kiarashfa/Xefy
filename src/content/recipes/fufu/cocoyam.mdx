<Step id="boil">
  Peel the cocoyam corms and put them into a large pot along with the
  <Qty ref="cassava"/>, cover generously with water and bring to the boil. Cook for
  <Dur step="boil"/>, until both are completely soft and a knife slides through with
  no resistance, then drain to give the <Qty ref="cocoyam"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="pound">
  Turn the cocoyam and cassava into a large mortar and pound with a pestle, turning
  and folding the mass between strokes. Continue for <Dur step="pound"/>, until the
  dough is smooth, stretchy and free of lumps, and pulls away from the sides of the
  mortar as one piece.
</Step>

<Step id="shape">
  Wet your hands and divide the dough into portions, shaping each into a smooth,
  rounded mound. This takes <Dur step="shape"/>. Serve warm, alongside a soup or stew.
</Step>

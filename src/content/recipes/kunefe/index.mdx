<Step id="make-syrup">
  Combine the <Qty ref="sugar"/>, <Qty ref="water"/> and <Qty ref="lemon-juice"/> in a
  small pan and bring to the boil, stirring until the sugar has dissolved. Simmer for
  <Dur step="make-syrup"/>, then take off the heat and set aside to cool.
</Step>

<Step id="prepare-kadayif">
  Meanwhile, pull the <Qty ref="kadayif"/> apart into short lengths and work the
  <Qty ref="butter-kadayif"/> through it with your fingers until every strand is
  coated. This takes <Dur step="prepare-kadayif"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="assemble">
  Grease a round baking tray with the <Qty ref="butter-tray"/>. Press half the
  buttered kadayıf firmly into the base, lay the sliced <Qty ref="kunefe-cheese"/>
  evenly over it, then cover with the remaining kadayıf, pressing it down so the
  cheese is fully enclosed. This takes <Dur step="assemble"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="bake">
  Bake at <Temp c={200}/> for <Dur step="bake"/>, until the top is deep golden and the
  cheese has melted and is bubbling at the edges.
</Step>

<Step id="finish">
  Pour the cooled syrup evenly over the tray while the künefe is still hot, then
  scatter over the <Qty ref="pistachio"/>. Cut into wedges once the syrup has soaked
  in, after <Dur step="finish"/>.
</Step>

  techniqueRationale:
    - stepId: puree-pumpkin
      text: Boiling the pumpkin separately and blending it before it joins the beef broth is what gives soup joumou its characteristic thick, orange body; added in chunks it would simply fall apart into the broth unevenly instead of thickening it throughout.
  commonPitfalls:
    - stepId: add-pasta
      text: Adding the macaroni too early leaves it badly overcooked and starchy by the time the soup is served; it needs only its own cooking time once everything else is already tender.

<Step id="marinate-beef">
  Rub the <Qty ref="beef"/> with the <Qty ref="lime-juice"/>,
  <Qty ref="garlic"/>, <Qty ref="onion"/>, <Qty ref="spring-onion"/>,
  <Qty ref="thyme"/> and <Qty ref="parsley"/>, and leave to marinate for
  <Dur step="marinate-beef"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="brown-beef">
  Brown the marinated beef in a large pot over a high heat for
  <Dur step="brown-beef"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="simmer-beef">
  Add the <Qty ref="water"/>, <Qty ref="cloves"/>,
  <Qty ref="scotch-bonnet"/> and <Qty ref="tomato-puree"/>. Bring to a
  simmer and cook for <Dur step="simmer-beef"/>, until the beef is tender.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-pumpkin">
  Meanwhile, boil the <Qty ref="pumpkin"/> in a separate pot of water until
  completely soft, about <Dur step="cook-pumpkin"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="puree-pumpkin">
  Drain the pumpkin, reserving a little of its cooking water, and blend to a
  smooth purée. This takes about <Dur step="puree-pumpkin"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="combine-broth">
  Remove the scotch bonnet from the beef broth and stir the pumpkin purée
  into the pot until fully combined. This takes about
  <Dur step="combine-broth"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="add-vegetables">
  Add the <Qty ref="carrot"/>, <Qty ref="white-cabbage"/>,
  <Qty ref="celery"/>, <Qty ref="potato"/>, <Qty ref="plantain"/>,
  <Qty ref="butter"/>, <Qty ref="salt"/> and <Qty ref="black-pepper"/>. This
  takes about <Dur step="add-vegetables"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="simmer-vegetables">
  Simmer for <Dur step="simmer-vegetables"/>, until the vegetables are
  tender.
</Step>

<Step id="add-pasta">
  Stir in the <Qty ref="macaroni"/>. This takes about
  <Dur step="add-pasta"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="simmer-pasta">
  Simmer for a further <Dur step="simmer-pasta"/>, until the pasta is
  tender.
</Step>

<Step id="finish">
  Adjust the seasoning and serve hot. This takes about
  <Dur step="finish"/>.
</Step>

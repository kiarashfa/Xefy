<Step id="trim-and-check">
  Run a finger along the flesh side of the <Qty ref="salmon"/> to find any pin bones and
  draw them out with tweezers, then pat the fillet dry with paper towel.
</Step>

<Step id="mix-cure">
  Stir the <Qty ref="salt"/>, <Qty ref="sugar"/> and <Qty ref="pepper"/> together in a
  small bowl until evenly combined.
</Step>

<Step id="rub-and-wrap">
  Lay the salmon skin-side down on a large sheet of plastic wrap and pack the salt
  mixture evenly over the flesh, working it right to the edges. Scatter the
  <Qty ref="dill-cure"/> over the top in an even layer, then wrap the fillet tightly and
  set it on a tray.
</Step>

<Step id="cure">
  Set another tray on top of the parcel and weight it down evenly, then refrigerate at
  <Temp c={4}/> or below for <Dur step="cure"/>, turning the parcel over once around the
  midpoint so both sides sit in the liquid the cure draws out.
</Step>

<Step id="unwrap-and-dry">
  Unwrap the fillet, scrape away the dill and the spent cure, and discard the liquid left
  in the wrapping. Pat the salmon dry with paper towel rather than rinsing it.
</Step>

<Step id="slice">
  Working from the tail end with a long, thin-bladed knife held almost flat against the
  skin, slice the salmon into thin sheets, leaving the skin behind. Scatter the
  <Qty ref="dill-garnish"/> over the slices before serving.
</Step>

<Step id="melt-chocolate">
  Melt the <Qty ref="chocolate"/> with the <Qty ref="butter"/> in a bowl set over a pan of
  barely simmering water, stirring until smooth. Take it off the heat and leave it to cool
  until it is barely warm. This takes <Dur step="melt-chocolate"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="add-yolks">
  Beat the <Qty ref="yolks"/> into the chocolate one at a time, until the mixture is glossy
  and thick. This takes <Dur step="add-yolks"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="whip-whites">
  Whisk the <Qty ref="whites"/> with the <Qty ref="salt"/> to soft peaks, then add the
  <Qty ref="sugar"/> a spoonful at a time and whisk to firm, glossy peaks. This takes
  <Dur step="whip-whites"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="fold">
  Fold a third of the whites into the chocolate to loosen it, then fold in the rest in two
  more additions, cutting down through the middle and turning the bowl. This takes
  <Dur step="fold"/>. Divide the mousse between pots, one for each serving.
</Step>

<Step id="set">
  Refrigerate the pots for <Dur step="set"/>, until set and firm to the touch.
</Step>

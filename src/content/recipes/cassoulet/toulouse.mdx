<Step id="soak-beans">
  Cover the <Qty ref="beans"/> with plenty of cold water and leave them to soak for
  <Dur step="soak-beans"/>. Drain them before cooking.
</Step>

<Step id="simmer-beans">
  Put the drained beans into a large pot with the <Qty ref="onion"/>, the <Qty ref="carrot"/>,
  the <Qty ref="bay"/> and the <Qty ref="thyme"/>. Cover with water and simmer gently for
  <Dur step="simmer-beans"/>, until the beans are just tender. Drain them, keeping the
  cooking liquor.
</Step>

<Step id="brown-meats">
  Heat the <Qty ref="oil"/> in a casserole and brown the <Qty ref="pork"/> and the
  <Qty ref="mutton"/> in batches over a medium-high heat, for <Dur step="brown-meats"/> in all.
  Add the <Qty ref="sausage"/> and turn it until its skin is lightly coloured.
</Step>

<Step id="crisp-duck">
  Lift the <Qty ref="duck"/> out of its fat and set it skin side down in a dry pan over a medium
  heat for <Dur step="crisp-duck"/>, until the skin is crisp and golden.
</Step>

<Step id="assemble">
  Layer the beans, the pork, the mutton, the sausage and the duck in the casserole, seasoning
  each layer with a little of the <Qty ref="salt"/> and the <Qty ref="pepper"/>. Add the
  <Qty ref="garlic"/> and pour over the <Qty ref="stock"/> and enough of the cooking liquor to
  cover the beans. This takes <Dur step="assemble"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="bake">
  Bake, uncovered, at <Temp c={150}/> for <Dur step="bake"/>, until the beans are creamy and
  the meat is tender when pierced.
</Step>

<Step id="crust">
  Sprinkle the <Qty ref="crumbs"/> over the surface. Each time a crust forms, break it into
  the beans with a spoon, then let a new one form. This takes <Dur step="crust"/> in all.
</Step>

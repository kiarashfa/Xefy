<Step id="prepare-vegetables">
  Peel and cut the <Qty ref="potato"/> into large chunks, cut the <Qty ref="carrot"/> into
  chunks, and cut the <Qty ref="onion"/> into wedges. Keep the potato in cold water until it
  goes into the pan. This takes <Dur step="prepare-vegetables"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="brown-beef">
  Heat the <Qty ref="oil"/> in a heavy, wide pan over a medium-high heat. Lay in the
  <Qty ref="beef"/> in a single layer, in batches if needed, and brown it for
  <Dur step="brown-beef"/>. Lift it out onto a plate.
</Step>

<Step id="simmer-stew">
  Put the onion, the potato and the carrot into the pan. Pour the strained dashi over them
  with the <Qty ref="soy"/>, the <Qty ref="mirin"/> and the <Qty ref="sugar"/>, and bring it
  to the boil. Skim off the scum, then cover, lower the heat and simmer for
  <Dur step="simmer-stew"/>, until the potatoes are tender through.
</Step>

<Step id="add-beef">
  Return the beef to the pan, laying the slices over the top so that they are just covered
  by the sauce. Simmer for <Dur step="add-beef"/> more, only until the beef has cooked
  through, and serve it hot with the vegetables and the sauce spooned over.
</Step>

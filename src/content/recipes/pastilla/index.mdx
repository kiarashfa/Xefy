<Step id="cook-chicken">
  Melt the <Qty ref="cook-butter"/> in a wide pan and cook the <Qty ref="onion"/> over a
  low heat until soft. Add the <Qty ref="chicken"/>, <Qty ref="ginger"/>,
  <Qty ref="filling-cinnamon"/>, <Qty ref="saffron"/>, <Qty ref="salt"/> and
  <Qty ref="pepper"/>, cover and cook gently for <Dur step="cook-chicken"/>, until the
  chicken is tender and surrounded by a reduced, well-flavoured sauce. Lift out the
  chicken, shred it, and set the sauce aside in the pan.
</Step>

<Step id="fry-almonds">
  Meanwhile, fry the <Qty ref="almond"/> in the <Qty ref="almond-butter"/> over a medium
  heat for <Dur step="fry-almonds"/>, until golden throughout, then crush roughly and
  toss with the <Qty ref="almond-icing-sugar"/> and <Qty ref="almond-cinnamon"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="finish-filling">
  Off the heat, stir the <Qty ref="egg"/> into the reserved sauce, then return the pan to
  a very low heat for <Dur step="finish-filling"/>, stirring constantly, until the egg
  sets into a soft curd rather than a liquid. Fold this through the shredded chicken with
  the <Qty ref="parsley"/> and <Qty ref="coriander"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="assemble">
  Brush a round baking tin with a little of the <Qty ref="brushing-butter"/> and line it
  with overlapping sheets of <Qty ref="warqa"/>, brushing each sheet with butter as it
  goes down and leaving enough overhang to fold back over the top. Spread the chicken
  filling evenly across the base, then the almond mixture on top of it. Fold the
  overhanging pastry back over the filling to enclose it completely, brushing the final
  layer with the last of the butter.
</Step>

<Step id="bake">
  Bake for <Dur step="bake"/>, until the pastry is deeply golden and crisp all over.
</Step>

<Step id="dust">
  While still warm, dust the top with the <Qty ref="topping-icing-sugar"/> and mark it
  with the <Qty ref="topping-cinnamon"/> in a crosshatch pattern before serving.
</Step>

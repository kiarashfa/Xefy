<Step id="mix-batter">
  Whisk the <Qty ref="teff-flour"/> and the <Qty ref="ferment-water"/> together in a
  large bowl for <Dur step="mix-batter"/>, until completely smooth and about as thin as
  double cream.
</Step>

<Step id="ferment">
  Cover the bowl loosely and leave it at room temperature to ferment for
  <Dur step="ferment"/>, stirring once a day. The batter is ready when it smells sharply
  sour and a layer of clear liquid has separated on top.
</Step>

<Step id="season-batter">
  Pour off the separated liquid, then stir in the <Qty ref="salt"/> and enough of the
  <Qty ref="thin-water"/> to bring the batter back to a thin, pourable consistency,
  working for <Dur step="season-batter"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="cook">
  Heat a large, flat, lightly seasoned griddle over a medium-high heat. Starting from the
  outside edge, pour the batter in one continuous thin spiral to fill the pan, without
  smoothing or spreading it further. Cover at once and cook for a share of
  <Dur step="cook"/> without turning, until the whole surface is covered in small open
  bubbles, has dried out, and lifts cleanly from the pan at the edge. Repeat with the
  remaining batter.
</Step>

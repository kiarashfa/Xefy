<Step id="assemble-sandwiches">
  Lay out the <Qty ref="bread"/> in pairs and season the inside of each pair lightly with the
  <Qty ref="salt"/> and the <Qty ref="pepper"/>. Layer the <Qty ref="ham"/> and the
  <Qty ref="gruyere-inside"/> on one slice of each pair, then close the sandwiches. Press the grated
  <Qty ref="gruyere-top"/> on top of each sandwich. This takes <Dur step="assemble-sandwiches"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="fry-sandwiches">
  Melt the <Qty ref="butter-sandwiches"/> in a heavy frying pan over a medium heat. Fry the sandwiches
  for <Dur step="fry-sandwiches"/> in all, turning them once, until the bread is deep golden on both
  sides and the cheese has melted. Cover the pan for the last minute or so to melt the cheese on top.
</Step>

<Step id="fry-eggs">
  While the sandwiches fry, melt the <Qty ref="butter-eggs"/> in a second pan over a medium heat and
  fry the <Qty ref="eggs"/> for <Dur step="fry-eggs"/>, until the whites are set and the yolks are
  still soft. Lift one on top of each sandwich, and cut each sandwich in half to serve.
</Step>

  techniqueRationale:
    - stepId: first-prove

  commonPitfalls:
    - stepId: fry

<Step id="make-dough">
  Warm the <Qty ref="milk"/> and stir in the <Qty ref="yeast"/> and a little of the
  <Qty ref="sugar"/>; leave it until foaming. Combine the <Qty ref="flour"/>, the remaining
  sugar and the <Qty ref="salt"/> in a large bowl, then work in the milk mixture, the
  <Qty ref="egg"/> and the <Qty ref="butter"/> to a rough dough. This takes
  <Dur step="make-dough"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="knead">
  Knead the dough for <Dur step="knead"/>, until it is smooth, elastic and no longer sticks
  to the sides of the bowl.
</Step>

<Step id="first-prove">
  Cover the bowl and leave the dough somewhere warm for <Dur step="first-prove"/>, until it
  has roughly doubled.
</Step>

<Step id="shape">
  Turn the dough out and roll it to an even thickness. Cut rounds with a doughnut cutter,
  and cut a hole from the centre of each. Set the rings and holes apart on trays lined with
  paper, using <Dur step="shape"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="second-prove">
  Cover the trays loosely and leave the shaped dough to prove again for
  <Dur step="second-prove"/>, until visibly puffed.
</Step>

<Step id="heat-oil">
  Meanwhile heat the <Qty ref="frying-oil"/> in a deep pan to <Temp c={180}/>, using
  <Dur step="heat-oil"/> to bring it up slowly and settle at temperature.
</Step>

<Step id="fry">
  Fry the doughnuts a few at a time for <Dur step="fry"/> in total, turning once, until
  deep golden on both sides. Lift them onto a rack set over paper to drain.
</Step>

<Step id="glaze">
  Whisk the <Qty ref="glaze-sugar"/> with the <Qty ref="glaze-milk"/> and the
  <Qty ref="glaze-vanilla"/> to a thin, pourable glaze. Dip the warm doughnuts in the glaze
  and set them back on the rack to let it set, over <Dur step="glaze"/>.
</Step>

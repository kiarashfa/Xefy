<Step id="mix-dry">
  Whisk together the <Qty ref="flour"/>, the <Qty ref="baking-powder"/>, the
  <Qty ref="bicarb"/>, the <Qty ref="nutmeg"/> and the <Qty ref="salt"/>. This takes
  <Dur step="mix-dry"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="mix-wet">
  Whisk the <Qty ref="sugar"/> into the <Qty ref="butter"/>, then whisk in the
  <Qty ref="eggs"/>, the <Qty ref="soured-cream"/> and the <Qty ref="vanilla"/>, over
  <Dur step="mix-wet"/>, until smooth.
</Step>

<Step id="combine-dough">
  Fold the dry ingredients into the wet ingredients over <Dur step="combine-dough"/>,
  stopping as soon as a soft, sticky dough comes together. It will be far too soft to cut
  yet.
</Step>

<Step id="chill-dough">
  Cover the bowl and refrigerate the dough for <Dur step="chill-dough"/>, until firm enough
  to roll.
</Step>

<Step id="shape">
  Roll the chilled dough to an even thickness on a floured surface and cut rounds with a
  doughnut cutter, cutting a hole from the centre of each. Gather and re-roll the scraps
  once. This takes <Dur step="shape"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="heat-oil">
  Meanwhile heat the <Qty ref="frying-oil"/> in a deep pan to <Temp c={175}/>, using
  <Dur step="heat-oil"/> to bring it up slowly and settle at temperature — a little cooler
  than for a yeasted dough, since this one is denser and cooks through more slowly.
</Step>

<Step id="fry">
  Fry the doughnuts a few at a time for <Dur step="fry"/> in total, turning once, until deep
  golden and a skewer through the centre of one comes out clean. Lift them onto a rack set
  over paper to drain.
</Step>

<Step id="glaze">
  Whisk the <Qty ref="glaze-sugar"/> with the <Qty ref="glaze-milk"/> and the
  <Qty ref="glaze-vanilla"/> to a thin, pourable glaze. Dip the warm doughnuts in the glaze
  and set them back on the rack to let it set, over <Dur step="glaze"/>.
</Step>

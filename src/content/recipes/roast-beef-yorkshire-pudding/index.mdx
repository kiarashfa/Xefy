<Step id="season-beef">
  Season the <Qty ref="beef"/> all over with the <Qty ref="salt-beef"/> and the
  <Qty ref="pepper"/>, and rub with the <Qty ref="dripping-beef"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="roast-beef-high">
  Roast at <Temp c={230}/> for <Dur step="roast-beef-high"/>, to sear the outside and
  start the crust.
</Step>

<Step id="roast-beef-low">
  Reduce the heat to <Temp c={160}/> and continue roasting for
  <Dur step="roast-beef-low"/>, until a thermometer in the thickest part of the meat
  reads <Temp c={54}/> for medium-rare.
</Step>

<Step id="make-batter">
  Whisk the <Qty ref="batter-flour"/> and the <Qty ref="salt-batter"/> with the
  <Qty ref="batter-egg"/> and the <Qty ref="batter-milk"/> until completely smooth.
</Step>

<Step id="rest-batter">
  Leave the batter to stand at room temperature for <Dur step="rest-batter"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="rest-beef">
  Transfer the beef to a board, tent loosely with foil, and rest for
  <Dur step="rest-beef"/>, reserving the pan and its juices.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-puddings">
  Divide the <Qty ref="dripping-tins"/> between the cups of a pudding tin and heat in
  the oven at <Temp c={230}/> until smoking hot. Pour in the batter and bake for
  <Dur step="cook-puddings"/>, without opening the door, until well risen, deeply
  golden and crisp.
</Step>

<Step id="make-gravy">
  Set the roasting pan over a medium heat, stir the <Qty ref="gravy-flour"/> into the
  juices left behind, and cook briefly before whisking in the <Qty ref="gravy-stock"/>
  and the <Qty ref="salt-gravy"/>. Simmer for <Dur step="make-gravy"/>, until thickened,
  scraping up any browned bits from the base of the pan.
</Step>

<Step id="carve">
  Carve the beef against the grain and serve with the puddings and the gravy.
</Step>

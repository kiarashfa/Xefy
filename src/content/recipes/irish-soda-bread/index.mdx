<Step id="mix">
  Put the <Qty ref="flour"/>, the <Qty ref="bicarbonate"/> and the <Qty ref="salt"/> into a
  large bowl and stir them together. Make a well in the centre, pour in the
  <Qty ref="buttermilk"/>, and mix with a knife or a floured hand for <Dur step="mix"/>,
  until the dough is soft and shaggy and just holds together. Do not knead it.
</Step>

<Step id="heat-oven">
  Heat the oven to <Temp c={200}/> with a shelf in the middle, and line a baking tray with
  baking paper.
</Step>

<Step id="shape">
  Turn the dough onto a floured bench and pat it into a round about <Len cm={20}/> across
  and <Len cm={4}/> high. Cut a deep cross into the top, reaching almost to the edges, and
  set the loaf on the tray.
</Step>

<Step id="bake">
  Bake for <Dur step="bake"/>, until deep golden and hollow when tapped on the base.
</Step>

<Step id="cool">
  Lift the loaf onto a rack and leave it for <Dur step="cool"/> before cutting, so the crumb
  can set.
</Step>

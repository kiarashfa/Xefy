<Step id="sofrito">
  Finely chop the <Qty ref="onion"/>, <Qty ref="green-pepper"/>,
  <Qty ref="garlic"/> and <Qty ref="coriander"/> and combine them in a bowl;
  this is the sofrito.
</Step>

<Step id="render-ham">
  Heat the <Qty ref="olive-oil"/> in a heavy pot over a medium heat and cook
  the <Qty ref="ham"/> for <Dur step="render-ham"/>, until it colours lightly
  at the edges.
</Step>

<Step id="bloom-achiote">
  Stir in the <Qty ref="achiote"/> and cook for <Dur step="bloom-achiote"/>,
  until the oil turns a deep orange-red.
</Step>

<Step id="build-sauce">
  Add the sofrito to the pot and cook for <Dur step="build-sauce"/>, until the
  vegetables soften, then stir in the <Qty ref="tomato-puree"/>,
  <Qty ref="olives"/>, <Qty ref="capers"/>, <Qty ref="oregano"/> and
  <Qty ref="bay-leaf"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="add-rice">
  Add the <Qty ref="pigeon-peas"/>, <Qty ref="rice"/>, <Qty ref="stock"/> and
  <Qty ref="salt"/>, stirring once to distribute the peas evenly, and bring to
  a boil for <Dur step="add-rice"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="simmer">
  Cover, reduce the heat to its lowest setting and cook undisturbed for
  <Dur step="simmer"/>, until the liquid is fully absorbed and the rice is
  tender.
</Step>

<Step id="rest">
  Take the pot off the heat and leave it covered for <Dur step="rest"/> before
  fluffing the rice with a fork.
</Step>

<Step id="whisk-batter">
  Whisk the <Qty ref="flour"/>, <Qty ref="sugar"/> and <Qty ref="salt"/> together in a
  bowl. Make a well in the centre, add the <Qty ref="eggs"/> and about a third of the
  <Qty ref="milk"/>, and work the flour in from the sides to a smooth paste. Whisk in the
  rest of the milk and the <Qty ref="butter-batter"/>, which should be melted and cooled.
  This takes <Dur step="whisk-batter"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="rest-batter">
  Cover the bowl and leave the batter to rest for <Dur step="rest-batter"/>, until it is
  loose and creamy. Whisk it briefly again before the first crêpe goes in.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-crepes">
  Heat a flat, lightly buttered pan over a medium-high heat, using the
  <Qty ref="butter-pan"/> on a piece of kitchen paper. Pour in a ladle of batter and tilt
  the pan at once so that it runs into a thin, even layer. Cook until the underside is
  golden, which takes about a minute, then turn it and cook the other side briefly. Stack
  the crêpes on a plate as they come off. The batch takes <Dur step="cook-crepes"/> in all.
</Step>

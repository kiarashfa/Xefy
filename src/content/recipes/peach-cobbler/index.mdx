<Step id="heat-oven">
  Heat the oven to <Temp c={180}/>, giving it <Dur step="heat-oven"/> to settle, and put a
  deep baking dish inside to warm while the oven comes up.
</Step>

<Step id="make-filling">
  Toss the <Qty ref="peaches"/> with the <Qty ref="filling-sugar"/>, the <Qty ref="cornflour"/>,
  the <Qty ref="lemon-juice"/> and the <Qty ref="cinnamon"/>, and dot with the
  <Qty ref="dotting-butter"/>. This takes <Dur step="make-filling"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="make-batter">
  Whisk together the <Qty ref="flour"/>, the <Qty ref="baking-powder"/>, the
  <Qty ref="batter-salt"/> and the <Qty ref="batter-sugar"/>, then whisk in the
  <Qty ref="milk"/> and the <Qty ref="batter-butter"/> to a smooth, thin batter, over
  <Dur step="make-batter"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="assemble">
  Pour the batter into the warm dish, then spoon the peaches and their juices evenly over
  the top without stirring them in. This takes <Dur step="assemble"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="bake">
  Bake for <Dur step="bake"/>, until the top is deep golden and risen unevenly around the
  fruit, and a skewer pushed into the crust comes out clean.
</Step>

<Step id="rest">
  Leave the cobbler to settle for <Dur step="rest"/> before serving, so the fruit's juices
  finish thickening.
</Step>

<Step id="marinate-beef">
  Combine the <Qty ref="beef"/> with the <Qty ref="garlic"/>, the
  <Qty ref="soy-sauce-dark"/>, <Qty ref="soy-sauce-light"/>,
  <Qty ref="oyster-sauce"/>, <Qty ref="caster-sugar"/> and half of the
  <Qty ref="black-pepper"/>. Mix well. This takes <Dur step="marinate-beef"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="rest-beef">
  Leave the beef to marinate at room temperature for <Dur step="rest-beef"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="prep-salad">
  Arrange the shredded <Qty ref="romaine-lettuce"/>, <Qty ref="tomato"/> and
  <Qty ref="onion"/> over a serving platter. This takes
  <Dur step="prep-salad"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="sear-beef">
  Heat the <Qty ref="vegetable-oil"/> in a wok or heavy pan until almost
  smoking. Add the marinated beef in a single layer and sear hard, tossing
  constantly, for <Dur step="sear-beef"/>, until well browned outside and still
  pink at the centre. Stir in the <Qty ref="butter"/> in the last moments so it
  melts into a light glaze.
</Step>

<Step id="assemble">
  Pile the seared beef over the salad. Stir the remaining
  <Qty ref="black-pepper"/> into the <Qty ref="lime-juice"/> and
  <Qty ref="fine-sea-salt"/> and serve alongside as a dip. This takes
  <Dur step="assemble"/>.
</Step>

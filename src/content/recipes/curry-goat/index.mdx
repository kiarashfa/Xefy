    - stepId: sear
      text: Browning the goat in the same spices it was marinated in, rather than wiping them off first, toasts the curry powder directly in fat and deepens its flavour before any liquid goes in.
  commonPitfalls:
    - stepId: braise

<Step id="season">
  Combine the <Qty ref="turmeric"/>, <Qty ref="cumin"/>,
  <Qty ref="coriander-seed"/>, <Qty ref="fenugreek"/>,
  <Qty ref="allspice"/>, <Qty ref="garlic"/> and <Qty ref="ginger"/> into a
  paste and rub it all over the <Qty ref="goat"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="marinate">
  Cover and refrigerate the goat for <Dur step="marinate"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="sear">
  Heat the <Qty ref="vegetable-oil"/> in a heavy pot over a high heat and
  brown the goat on all sides in batches, for <Dur step="sear"/> in total,
  then return all the meat to the pot with the <Qty ref="onion"/>,
  <Qty ref="spring-onion"/>, <Qty ref="thyme"/>,
  <Qty ref="scotch-bonnet"/>, <Qty ref="salt"/> and
  <Qty ref="black-pepper"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="braise">
  Pour in the <Qty ref="coconut-milk"/> and <Qty ref="water"/>, bring to a
  simmer, then cover and cook at a bare simmer for <Dur step="braise"/>,
  until the meat is tender enough to pull from the bone.
</Step>

<Step id="add-potato">
  Stir in the <Qty ref="potato"/> and continue simmering, uncovered, for
  <Dur step="add-potato"/>, until the potato is tender and the sauce has
  thickened. Discard the whole scotch bonnet before serving.
</Step>

  techniqueRationale:
    - stepId: layer-and-seal
      text: The whole potatoes go in raw with the meat, not parboiled separately. This is a distinctive feature of Dhaka's kacchi against other regional biryanis, where they finish in the same trapped steam as the goat and pick up its spice and fat as they cook.
  commonPitfalls:
    - stepId: parboil-rice

<Step id="fry-onions">
  Heat half of the <Qty ref="ghee"/> in a wide pan and fry the
  <Qty ref="onion-marinade"/> and <Qty ref="onion-layer"/> together over a medium
  heat, stirring often, for <Dur step="fry-onions"/>, until deep golden-brown. Lift
  out and drain, keeping the two portions separate.
</Step>

<Step id="mix-marinade">
  Combine the <Qty ref="goat"/>, <Qty ref="potato"/>, <Qty ref="yoghurt"/>,
  <Qty ref="ginger"/>, <Qty ref="garlic"/>, <Qty ref="turmeric"/>,
  <Qty ref="dried-chilli"/>, <Qty ref="lemon-juice"/>, the
  <Qty ref="marinade-salt"/> and the fried <Qty ref="onion-marinade"/> in a large
  bowl, working the marinade into the meat and potatoes by hand for
  <Dur step="mix-marinade"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="marinate">
  Cover the bowl and refrigerate for <Dur step="marinate"/>, so the yoghurt and
  spice work fully into the meat before it goes anywhere near heat.
</Step>

<Step id="parboil-rice">
  Rinse the <Qty ref="rice"/> until the water runs clear, then bring the
  <Qty ref="boil-water"/> to the boil with the <Qty ref="rice-salt"/>, the
  <Qty ref="cardamom"/>, <Qty ref="cinnamon"/>, <Qty ref="cloves"/> and
  <Qty ref="bay-leaf"/>. Add the rinsed rice and boil for
  <Dur step="parboil-rice"/>, until the grains are still firm at the core, then
  drain.
</Step>

<Step id="layer-and-seal">
  Spread the marinated goat and potatoes in an even layer across the base of a
  heavy, wide pot. Mound the drained rice on top, then scatter over the fried
  <Qty ref="onion-layer"/>. Bloom the <Qty ref="saffron"/> in the warm
  <Qty ref="saffron-water"/>, stir in the <Qty ref="rose-water"/>, and drizzle it
  over the rice along with the remaining <Qty ref="ghee"/>. Seal the lid tightly,
  sealing the rim with a strip of dough or a double layer of foil if it does not fit
  snugly. This takes <Dur step="layer-and-seal"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="dum-cook">
  Set the sealed pot over the lowest possible heat, ideally on top of a heat
  diffuser, and leave it completely undisturbed for <Dur step="dum-cook"/>. The
  meat and potatoes cook through entirely in the steam trapped under the seal.
</Step>

<Step id="rest-and-serve">
  Break the seal away from the heat and let the biryani rest briefly before
  turning it out, working from the bottom up so the rice, goat and potato come
  together in each portion. This takes <Dur step="rest-and-serve"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="slice-tomato">
  Slice the <Qty ref="tomato"/> into rounds about <Len cm={1}/> thick, and lay them out on
  a platter as you go.
</Step>

<Step id="slice-mozzarella">
  Drain the <Qty ref="mozzarella"/> on kitchen paper, then slice it to the same thickness
  as the tomato.
</Step>

<Step id="assemble">
  Overlap the tomato and mozzarella slices along the platter, tuck the <Qty ref="basil"/>
  leaves between them, and season with the <Qty ref="salt"/> and the <Qty ref="pepper"/>.
  Trickle over the <Qty ref="olive-oil"/> and serve within the half hour, while the
  tomato is still at room temperature.
</Step>

<Step id="soak-chickpeas">
  Soak the <Qty ref="chickpeas"/> in plenty of cold water for
  <Dur step="soak-chickpeas"/>, then drain.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-channa">
  Simmer the soaked chickpeas with the <Qty ref="turmeric-channa"/>,
  <Qty ref="cumin"/>, <Qty ref="onion"/>, <Qty ref="garlic"/>,
  <Qty ref="scotch-bonnet"/>, <Qty ref="oil-channa"/> and
  <Qty ref="salt-channa"/> in enough water to cover, for
  <Dur step="cook-channa"/>, until the chickpeas are soft and the liquid has
  reduced to a thick curry. Remove the scotch bonnet before serving.
</Step>

<Step id="make-bara-dough">
  Whisk the <Qty ref="flour"/>, <Qty ref="yeast"/>, <Qty ref="turmeric-bara"/>
  and <Qty ref="salt-bara"/> together, then work in the <Qty ref="water"/> to
  make a soft, wet, elastic batter-like dough. This takes about
  <Dur step="make-bara-dough"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="prove-bara">
  Cover the dough and leave it in a warm place for <Dur step="prove-bara"/>,
  until visibly puffed.
</Step>

<Step id="shape-bara">
  With wet hands, pull off pieces of dough and pat each into a thin, round
  disc directly against the palm. Lay the discs out on a floured tray as they
  are shaped, ready to fry; this takes about <Dur step="shape-bara"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="fry-bara">
  Heat the <Qty ref="frying-oil"/> in a deep pan and fry the discs a few at a
  time for about a minute a side, until puffed, golden and cooked through.
  Drain on paper towels. Frying the whole batch takes about
  <Dur step="fry-bara"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="assemble">
  Spoon the curried channa onto one piece of bara and top with a second piece
  to close the sandwich, in the manner that gives doubles its name. Assembling
  the full batch takes about <Dur step="assemble"/>.
</Step>

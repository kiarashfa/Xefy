<Step id="saute-aromatics">
  Heat the <Qty ref="oil"/> in a wide pan over a medium heat and cook the
  <Qty ref="onion"/>, <Qty ref="garlic"/> and <Qty ref="spring-onion"/> for
  <Dur step="saute-aromatics"/>, until softened.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-down">
  Add the <Qty ref="tomato"/>, <Qty ref="thyme"/>, <Qty ref="scotch-bonnet"/>,
  <Qty ref="salt"/> and <Qty ref="black-pepper"/>, then stir in the
  <Qty ref="amaranth"/> in batches as it wilts down. Cover and cook for
  <Dur step="cook-down"/>, stirring occasionally, until completely soft and
  glossy. Remove the scotch bonnet before serving.
</Step>

<Step id="saute-aromatics">
  Heat the <Qty ref="oil"/> in a large pot over a medium heat and cook the
  <Qty ref="onion"/>, <Qty ref="garlic"/> and <Qty ref="spring-onion"/> for
  <Dur step="saute-aromatics"/>, until softened.
</Step>

<Step id="simmer-soup">
  Add the <Qty ref="dasheen"/>, <Qty ref="okra"/>, <Qty ref="pumpkin"/>,
  <Qty ref="crab"/>, <Qty ref="coconut-milk"/>, <Qty ref="water"/>,
  <Qty ref="thyme"/>, <Qty ref="scotch-bonnet"/> and <Qty ref="salt"/>. Bring
  to a simmer and cook for <Dur step="simmer-soup"/>, until the dasheen and
  pumpkin are completely soft. Remove the scotch bonnet.
</Step>

<Step id="blend">
  Blend the soup until smooth and thick, either in a blender in batches or
  directly in the pot with an immersion blender, then return to a brief
  simmer to heat through. This takes about <Dur step="blend"/>.
</Step>

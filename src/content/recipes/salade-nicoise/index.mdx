<Step id="cook-potatoes">
  Put the <Qty ref="potatoes"/> in a pan of cold salted water, bring it to the boil and simmer
  until a knife slides in without resistance. Drain them and leave them to cool a little, then
  peel and slice them. This takes <Dur step="cook-potatoes"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-eggs">
  Cover the <Qty ref="eggs"/> with cold water in a small pan, bring to the boil, and simmer for
  <Dur step="cook-eggs"/>. Cool them in cold water, shell them, and quarter them.
</Step>

<Step id="blanch-beans">
  Drop the <Qty ref="beans"/> into boiling salted water for <Dur step="blanch-beans"/>, until they
  are just tender and still bright green. Drain them and refresh them in cold water.
</Step>

<Step id="make-vinaigrette">
  Crush the <Qty ref="garlic"/> with the <Qty ref="salt"/>. Whisk in the <Qty ref="vinegar"/> and
  the <Qty ref="mustard"/>, then slowly whisk in the <Qty ref="oil"/> and the <Qty ref="pepper"/>
  until the dressing is emulsified. This takes <Dur step="make-vinaigrette"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="dress-separately">
  Toss the warm <Qty ref="potatoes"/> with a little of the vinaigrette, and the <Qty ref="beans"/>
  with a little more, each in its own bowl. Season the <Qty ref="tomatoes"/> with a pinch of the
  <Qty ref="salt"/> and dress them lightly. This takes <Dur step="dress-separately"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="assemble">
  Line a wide shallow bowl with the <Qty ref="lettuce"/>. Arrange the dressed potatoes, beans and
  tomatoes on it, then scatter over the <Qty ref="tuna"/>, the <Qty ref="anchovy"/>, the
  <Qty ref="eggs"/>, the <Qty ref="olives"/> and the <Qty ref="spring-onions"/>. Spoon over the
  rest of the vinaigrette and scatter the <Qty ref="basil"/>. This takes <Dur step="assemble"/>.
</Step>

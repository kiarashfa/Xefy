<Step id="brown-lamb">
  Melt the <Qty ref="ghee"/> in a heavy pot and brown the <Qty ref="lamb"/> on all
  sides over a high heat. Add the <Qty ref="onion"/> and cook until softened, then
  stir in the <Qty ref="garlic"/>, <Qty ref="cumin"/>, <Qty ref="cardamom"/>,
  <Qty ref="cloves"/>, <Qty ref="cinnamon"/> and <Qty ref="bay-leaf"/>, and cook
  briefly until fragrant. This takes <Dur step="brown-lamb"/> in total.
</Step>

<Step id="simmer-lamb">
  Add the <Qty ref="salt"/> and the <Qty ref="stock-water"/>, bring to a simmer,
  cover, and cook gently for <Dur step="simmer-lamb"/>, until the lamb is
  completely tender. Lift the lamb out and keep it warm; strain and reserve the
  stock.
</Step>

<Step id="caramelise-carrot">
  While the lamb simmers, cook the <Qty ref="carrot"/> in a dry pan over a medium
  heat with the <Qty ref="sugar"/>, tossing often, for <Dur step="caramelise-carrot"/>,
  until glossy and lightly caramelised at the edges but still with some bite. Stir
  through the <Qty ref="raisins"/>, <Qty ref="almonds"/> and <Qty ref="pistachio"/>
  in the last minute and set aside.
</Step>

<Step id="parboil-rice">
  Rinse the <Qty ref="rice"/> until the water runs clear, then boil it in the
  reserved stock for <Dur step="parboil-rice"/>, until the grains are still firm at
  the core. Drain, reserving any stock still left.
</Step>

<Step id="layer-and-steam">
  Return the lamb to the base of the pot. Mound the parboiled rice on top, drizzle
  over any remaining stock, cover tightly, and steam over the lowest heat for
  <Dur step="layer-and-steam"/>, undisturbed, until the rice is fully tender.
</Step>

<Step id="rest-and-serve">
  Spread the rice and lamb onto a platter and scatter the caramelised carrot,
  raisin and nut topping evenly over the top. This takes <Dur step="rest-and-serve"/>.
</Step>

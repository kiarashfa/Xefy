<Step id="rinse-rice">
  Rinse the <Qty ref="rice"/> in several changes of water until it runs mostly
  clear, then drain.
</Step>

<Step id="marinate-pork">
  Combine the <Qty ref="pork"/>, <Qty ref="pork-soy"/> and
  <Qty ref="pork-cornflour"/> in a bowl and set aside.
</Step>

<Step id="simmer">
  Put the rice and the <Qty ref="water"/> in a large, heavy pot and bring to the
  boil, then reduce to the lowest possible simmer. Cook uncovered, stirring
  occasionally to stop the rice catching on the base, for <Dur step="simmer"/>,
  until the grains have broken down into a smooth, thick porridge.
</Step>

<Step id="add-egg">
  Stir in the <Qty ref="century-egg"/>, <Qty ref="salt"/> and
  <Qty ref="white-pepper"/>, and simmer gently for <Dur step="add-egg"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-pork">
  Loosen the marinated pork with your fingers and drop it into the pot in small
  pieces, stirring gently. Simmer for <Dur step="cook-pork"/>, until the pork is
  cooked through and separated into fine strands, then finish with the
  <Qty ref="sesame-oil"/>, <Qty ref="ginger"/> and <Qty ref="spring-onion"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="marinate-pork">
  Combine the <Qty ref="marinade-hoisin"/>, <Qty ref="marinade-soy"/>,
  <Qty ref="shaoxing-wine"/>, <Qty ref="char-sugar"/>, <Qty ref="garlic"/> and
  <Qty ref="five-spice"/> in a bowl and turn the <Qty ref="pork"/> through it.
</Step>

<Step id="rest-marinade">
  Cover and marinate in the refrigerator for <Dur step="rest-marinade"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="bloom-yeast">
  Stir the <Qty ref="yeast"/> into the <Qty ref="milk"/> and set aside for
  <Dur step="bloom-yeast"/>, until foamy.
</Step>

<Step id="mix-dough">
  Combine the <Qty ref="flour-dough"/>, <Qty ref="sugar"/> and <Qty ref="salt"/> in
  a large bowl. Pour in the milk mixture and the <Qty ref="dough-egg"/> and bring
  together into a shaggy dough.
</Step>

<Step id="knead">
  Turn the dough out onto the <Qty ref="flour-bench"/> and knead for
  <Dur step="knead"/>, working in the <Qty ref="butter"/> a little at a time,
  until smooth, glossy and elastic.
</Step>

<Step id="first-prove">
  Return the dough to the bowl, cover and leave to prove somewhere warm for
  <Dur step="first-prove"/>, until roughly doubled in size.
</Step>

<Step id="roast-pork">
  Meanwhile, spread the marinated pork on a lined tray and roast at
  <Temp c={200}/> for <Dur step="roast-pork"/>, turning once, until cooked through
  and lightly charred at the edges. Once cool enough to handle, dice into small
  pieces.
</Step>

<Step id="make-filling">
  Whisk the <Qty ref="cornflour"/> into the <Qty ref="sauce-water"/> until smooth,
  then combine in a pan with the <Qty ref="sauce-hoisin"/>, <Qty ref="sauce-soy"/>
  and <Qty ref="oyster-sauce"/>. Bring to a simmer, stirring, until thickened and
  glossy. Off the heat, stir through the diced pork and the
  <Qty ref="sesame-oil"/>, then leave to cool.
</Step>

<Step id="shape">
  Knock the dough back and roll it into a log, then divide it into even pieces.
  Roll each piece into a round, thicker at the centre than at the rim, spoon
  filling into the middle, and gather the edge up around it, pinching firmly to
  seal completely. Set each bun seam-down on a lined tray.
</Step>

<Step id="second-prove">
  Cover the shaped buns loosely and leave to prove for a further
  <Dur step="second-prove"/>, until visibly puffed.
</Step>

<Step id="bake">
  Brush each bun with the <Qty ref="egg-wash"/> and bake at <Temp c={190}/> for
  <Dur step="bake"/>, until deeply golden and glossy.
</Step>

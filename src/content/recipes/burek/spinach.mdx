<Step id="wilt-spinach">
  Wilt the <Qty ref="spinach"/> with the <Qty ref="onion"/> in a dry pan over a medium
  heat for <Dur step="wilt-spinach"/>, until collapsed and no longer releasing liquid.
  Tip into a sieve and press out as much water as possible, then chop roughly once cool
  enough to handle.
</Step>

<Step id="make-filling">
  Combine the wilted spinach and onion with the <Qty ref="feta"/>, <Qty ref="egg"/>,
  <Qty ref="salt"/> and <Qty ref="pepper"/> in a bowl and mix until evenly combined.
</Step>

<Step id="roll-and-coil">
  Lay out a sheet of <Qty ref="filo"/> and brush it lightly with the <Qty ref="butter"/>.
  Spread a strip of the filling along one long edge and roll the sheet up around it into
  a loose log, leaving the ends open. Coil the log into a spiral in a round, buttered
  baking tin, joining the next log to the end of the last so the spiral grows outward,
  until the tin is filled and all the filling is used.
</Step>

<Step id="bake">
  Brush the top of the coil with the remaining butter and bake for
  <Dur step="bake"/>, until the filo is deeply golden and crisp and the filling has set.
  Rest briefly before cutting into wedges.
</Step>

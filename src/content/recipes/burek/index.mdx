<Step id="make-filling">
  Combine the <Qty ref="beef"/>, <Qty ref="onion"/>, <Qty ref="salt"/> and
  <Qty ref="pepper"/> in a bowl and mix until evenly distributed. Do not overwork it — the
  filling should stay loose rather than pack down like a sausage mix.
</Step>

<Step id="roll-and-coil">
  Lay out a sheet of <Qty ref="filo"/> and brush it lightly with the <Qty ref="butter"/>.
  Spoon a strip of the filling along one long edge and roll the sheet up around it into a
  loose log, leaving the ends open. Coil the log into a spiral in a round, buttered
  baking tin, joining the next log to the end of the last so the spiral grows outward,
  until the tin is filled and all the filling is used.
</Step>

<Step id="bake">
  Brush the top of the coil with the remaining butter and bake for
  <Dur step="bake"/>, until the filo is deeply golden and crisp and the meat inside is
  cooked through. Rest briefly before cutting into wedges.
</Step>

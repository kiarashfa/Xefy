<Step id="make-filling">
  Combine the <Qty ref="feta"/>, <Qty ref="egg"/> and <Qty ref="yoghurt"/> in a bowl and
  mash together into a thick, spreadable curd.
</Step>

<Step id="roll-and-coil">
  Lay out a sheet of <Qty ref="filo"/> and brush it lightly with the <Qty ref="butter"/>.
  Spread a strip of the cheese filling along one long edge and roll the sheet up around
  it into a loose log, leaving the ends open. Coil the log into a spiral in a round,
  buttered baking tin, joining the next log to the end of the last so the spiral grows
  outward, until the tin is filled and all the filling is used.
</Step>

<Step id="bake">
  Brush the top of the coil with the remaining butter and bake for
  <Dur step="bake"/>, until the filo is deeply golden and crisp and the filling has set.
  Rest briefly before cutting into wedges.
</Step>

notes:
  techniqueRationale:
    - stepId: make-tangzhong
      text: The flour and milk are cooked to a thick paste before they go into the dough. Cooking the starch in liquid lets it hold a large amount of water, and that water stays in the crumb as the loaf bakes, which is what keeps the bread soft for days rather than only on the day it is baked.
  commonPitfalls:
    - stepId: knead-dough

<Step id="make-tangzhong">
  Whisk the <Qty ref="flour-tangzhong"/> into the <Qty ref="milk-tangzhong"/> in a small pan. Set it
  over a medium heat and stir constantly until the mixture thickens to a paste that leaves a line
  when the whisk is drawn through it. Scrape it into a bowl. This takes <Dur step="make-tangzhong"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="cool-tangzhong">
  Leave the tangzhong until it is just warm to the touch, and no hotter, for
  <Dur step="cool-tangzhong"/>. A hot starter would kill the yeast when it is added.
</Step>

<Step id="mix-dough">
  Put the <Qty ref="flour-dough"/>, the <Qty ref="yeast"/>, the <Qty ref="sugar"/> and the
  <Qty ref="salt"/> into a large bowl. Add the <Qty ref="milk-dough"/>, the <Qty ref="egg"/> and the
  cooled tangzhong, and mix to a rough, sticky dough. This takes <Dur step="mix-dough"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="knead-dough">
  Knead the dough on a lightly floured surface for <Dur step="knead-dough"/>, until it is smooth and
  springy. Work in the <Qty ref="butter-dough"/> a few pieces at a time, kneading each piece in fully
  before the next goes in, until the dough is glossy and stretches into a thin window without tearing.
</Step>

<Step id="first-prove">
  Put the dough into a clean bowl, cover it, and leave it somewhere warm for <Dur step="first-prove"/>,
  until it has doubled in size.
</Step>

<Step id="shape-loaf">
  Knock the dough back and press it into a rectangle. Fold it in from the sides and roll it up into
  a log. Grease a loaf tin with the <Qty ref="butter-tin"/>, lay the log in seam side down. This takes
  <Dur step="shape-loaf"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="second-prove">
  Cover the tin and leave the dough to rise for <Dur step="second-prove"/>, until it has risen just
  above the rim of the tin.
</Step>

<Step id="bake-loaf">
  Bake the loaf in an oven heated to <Temp c={180}/> for <Dur step="bake-loaf"/>, until it is golden
  on top and sounds hollow when it is tapped underneath.
</Step>

<Step id="cool-loaf">
  Turn the loaf out of the tin onto a wire rack and leave it to cool for <Dur step="cool-loaf"/>
  before it is sliced, so that the crumb sets.
</Step>

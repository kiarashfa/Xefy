<Step id="build-broth">
  Combine the <Qty ref="hock"/> and the <Qty ref="water"/> in a large pot and bring to
  the boil.
</Step>

<Step id="simmer-hock">
  Reduce the heat to a bare simmer and cook, partially covered, for
  <Dur step="simmer-hock"/>, until the meat is tender enough to fall from the bone.
</Step>

<Step id="prep-greens">
  While the hock simmers, strip the stems from the <Qty ref="greens"/> and slice the
  leaves into ribbons. Slice the <Qty ref="onion"/> and mince the <Qty ref="garlic"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-greens">
  Lift out the hock and set it aside. Add the greens, onion and garlic to the pot,
  return to a simmer, and cook partially covered for <Dur step="cook-greens"/>,
  stirring occasionally, until the leaves are tender and silky.
</Step>

<Step id="finish">
  Pick the meat from the hock, discarding the bone, skin and excess fat, and stir the
  meat back into the pot along with the <Qty ref="vinegar"/>, <Qty ref="cayenne"/>,
  <Qty ref="salt"/> and <Qty ref="pepper"/>. Serve the greens in bowls with plenty of
  the broth.
</Step>

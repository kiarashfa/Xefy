<Step id="marinate-chicken">
  Put the <Qty ref="chicken"/> into a bowl with the <Qty ref="soy"/>, the <Qty ref="sake"/>, the
  <Qty ref="ginger"/> and the <Qty ref="garlic"/>. Work the marinade into the meat with your hands
  until every piece is coated. This takes <Dur step="marinate-chicken"/>. One serving is a quarter of
  the batch.
</Step>

<Step id="rest-marinade">
  Cover the bowl and leave the chicken in the fridge for <Dur step="rest-marinade"/>, so that the
  seasoning goes into the meat.
</Step>

<Step id="heat-oil">
  Heat the <Qty ref="oil"/> in a deep, heavy pan to <Temp c={160}/>, checking it with a thermometer.
  It takes <Dur step="heat-oil"/> to come up to temperature, and it happens while the chicken
  marinates.
</Step>

<Step id="coat-chicken">
  Lift the chicken out of the marinade and shake off the excess liquid. Put the
  <Qty ref="potato-starch"/> into a bowl, and toss each piece in it, pressing the starch on so that
  the coating is thin and even. Set the pieces on a tray. This takes <Dur step="coat-chicken"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="fry-karaage">
  Lower the coated pieces into the oil at <Temp c={160}/>, a few at a time so that the temperature
  holds, and fry them for <Dur step="fry-karaage"/> until the coating is pale and set. Lift them out
  onto a rack.
</Step>

<Step id="rest-karaage">
  Leave the pieces on the rack for <Dur step="rest-karaage"/>, while the oil comes up to
  <Temp c={180}/>.
</Step>

<Step id="refry-karaage">
  Fry the pieces again in the oil at <Temp c={180}/> for <Dur step="refry-karaage"/>, until the
  coating is deep gold and crisp. Drain them briefly and serve at once.
</Step>

<Step id="make-syrup">
  Combine the <Qty ref="sugar"/>, <Qty ref="water"/> and <Qty ref="lemon-juice"/> in a
  small pan and bring to the boil, stirring until the sugar has dissolved. Simmer for
  <Dur step="make-syrup"/>, then take off the heat and set aside to cool completely
  while the baklava layers and bakes.
</Step>

<Step id="layer">
  Brush the base of a baking tray with the <Qty ref="butter"/>. Lay in half of the
  <Qty ref="filo"/>, sheet by sheet, brushing each one with butter before adding the
  next. Scatter the <Qty ref="pistachio-filling"/> evenly over the stack, then layer
  the remaining filo on top in the same way, brushing every sheet, finishing with a
  well-buttered top layer. Spend the whole of <Dur step="layer"/> keeping the sheets
  flat and the butter even, since a dry patch anywhere will not soften once baked.
</Step>

<Step id="score">
  Using a sharp knife, cut all the way through the layered pastry into even diamonds,
  working from one edge of the tray to the other. This takes <Dur step="score"/> done
  carefully, and it is the last chance to fix the shape of the pieces before the oven
  sets them.
</Step>

<Step id="bake">
  Bake at <Temp c={160}/> for <Dur step="bake"/>, until the top is deep golden and the
  edges have pulled slightly away from the tray.
</Step>

<Step id="soak">
  Take the tray straight from the oven and immediately pour the cooled syrup evenly
  over the hot pastry, working corner to corner so every piece gets its share. Scatter
  over the <Qty ref="pistachio-garnish"/> and leave the tray to sit undisturbed for
  <Dur step="soak"/> before lifting the pieces out along their cut lines.
</Step>

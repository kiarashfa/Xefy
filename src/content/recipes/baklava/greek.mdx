<Step id="mix-filling">
  Stir the <Qty ref="walnut"/> and <Qty ref="cinnamon"/> together and set aside; this
  takes <Dur step="mix-filling"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="make-syrup">
  Combine the <Qty ref="sugar"/> and <Qty ref="water"/> in a small pan and bring to the
  boil, stirring until the sugar has dissolved. Simmer for <Dur step="make-syrup"/>,
  then take off the heat, stir in the <Qty ref="honey"/> and the <Qty ref="lemon-juice"/>,
  and set aside to cool completely while the baklava layers and bakes.
</Step>

<Step id="layer">
  Brush the base of a baking tray with the <Qty ref="butter"/>. Lay in half of the
  <Qty ref="filo"/>, sheet by sheet, brushing each one with butter before adding the
  next. Scatter the walnut and cinnamon filling evenly over the stack, then layer the
  remaining filo on top in the same way, brushing every sheet, finishing with a
  well-buttered top layer. Spend the whole of <Dur step="layer"/> keeping the sheets
  flat and the butter even, since a dry patch anywhere will not soften once baked.
</Step>

<Step id="score">
  Using a sharp knife, cut all the way through the layered pastry into even diamonds
  or rectangles, working from one edge of the tray to the other. This takes
  <Dur step="score"/> done carefully, and it is the last chance to fix the shape of
  the pieces before the oven sets them.
</Step>

<Step id="bake">
  Bake at <Temp c={160}/> for <Dur step="bake"/>, until the top is deep golden and the
  edges have pulled slightly away from the tray.
</Step>

<Step id="soak">
  Take the tray straight from the oven and immediately pour the cooled honey syrup
  evenly over the hot pastry, working corner to corner so every piece gets its share.
  Leave the tray to sit undisturbed for <Dur step="soak"/> before lifting the pieces
  out along their cut lines.
</Step>

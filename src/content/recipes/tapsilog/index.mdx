<Step id="marinate-beef">
  Combine the <Qty ref="soy-sauce"/>, <Qty ref="vinegar"/>, <Qty ref="garlic-marinade"/>,
  <Qty ref="sugar"/> and <Qty ref="pepper"/> in a bowl and turn the <Qty ref="beef"/>
  through it for <Dur step="marinate-beef"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="rest-marinade">
  Cover and marinate in the refrigerator for <Dur step="rest-marinade"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-rice">
  Meanwhile, rinse the <Qty ref="rice"/>, combine with the <Qty ref="water"/> in a
  covered pot and cook for <Dur step="cook-rice"/>, until tender. Spread out on a tray
  to cool and dry slightly.
</Step>

<Step id="fry-rice">
  Heat the <Qty ref="oil-rice"/> in a wide pan and fry the <Qty ref="garlic-rice"/>
  until golden. Add the cooled rice and fry for <Dur step="fry-rice"/>, tossing, until
  every grain is coated and lightly crisp. Set aside and keep warm.
</Step>

<Step id="sear-beef">
  Heat the <Qty ref="oil-beef"/> in a hot pan and sear the marinated beef, in batches if
  needed, for <Dur step="sear-beef"/>, until browned at the edges and just cooked
  through.
</Step>

<Step id="fry-eggs">
  Heat the <Qty ref="oil-eggs"/> and fry the <Qty ref="eggs"/> for <Dur step="fry-eggs"/>,
  until the whites are set and the yolks still soft. Serve each portion with the beef,
  garlic rice and a fried egg alongside.
</Step>

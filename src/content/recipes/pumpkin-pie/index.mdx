<Step id="make-crust">
  Rub the <Qty ref="butter-crust"/> into the <Qty ref="flour"/> and <Qty ref="salt-crust"/>
  until the mixture looks like coarse crumbs, over <Dur step="make-crust"/>. Add the
  <Qty ref="water-crust"/> and bring the dough together with a few strokes, without
  overworking it.
</Step>

<Step id="chill-crust">
  Flatten the dough into a disc, wrap it, and chill for <Dur step="chill-crust"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="line-tin">
  Roll the chilled dough out and line a pie tin with it, trimming and crimping the
  edge, over <Dur step="line-tin"/>. Prick the base and chill again briefly.
</Step>

<Step id="blind-bake">
  Line the pastry case with baking paper and fill with baking beans. Blind bake at
  <Temp c={190}/> for <Dur step="blind-bake"/>, then remove the paper and beans.
</Step>

<Step id="mix-filling">
  While the crust bakes, whisk the <Qty ref="pumpkin"/> with the <Qty ref="eggs"/>, the
  <Qty ref="brown-sugar"/>, the <Qty ref="cream"/>, the <Qty ref="cinnamon"/>, the
  <Qty ref="ginger"/>, the <Qty ref="nutmeg"/>, the <Qty ref="cloves"/> and the
  <Qty ref="salt-filling"/> until smooth, over <Dur step="mix-filling"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="fill-and-bake">
  Pour the filling into the warm pastry case. Bake at <Temp c={180}/> for
  <Dur step="fill-and-bake"/>, until the outer edges are set and only a small circle at
  the very centre still wobbles.
</Step>

<Step id="cool">
  Leave the pie to cool completely for <Dur step="cool"/>, so the centre finishes
  setting, before slicing.
</Step>

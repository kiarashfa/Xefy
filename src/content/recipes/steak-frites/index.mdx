<Step id="cut-potatoes">
  Cut the <Qty ref="potatoes"/> into batons about <Len cm={1}/> thick. Rinse them, then dry them
  thoroughly in a clean cloth, since water in the oil makes it spit. This takes
  <Dur step="cut-potatoes"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="first-fry">
  Heat the <Qty ref="frying-oil"/> in a deep, heavy pan to <Temp c={150}/>. Fry the potatoes in
  small batches for <Dur step="first-fry"/>, until they are cooked through and pale, but not yet
  coloured. Lift them out and drain them.
</Step>

<Step id="rest-frites">
  Leave the potatoes to rest on kitchen paper for <Dur step="rest-frites"/> while the oil is
  reheated.
</Step>

<Step id="season-steak">
  Season the <Qty ref="beef"/> on both sides with the <Qty ref="salt"/> and the <Qty ref="pepper"/>.
  This takes <Dur step="season-steak"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="sear-steak">
  Melt the <Qty ref="butter"/> in a heavy frying pan over a high heat until it foams. Lay the steaks
  in the pan, in batches if needed, and cook them for <Dur step="sear-steak"/> in all, turning them
  once, until they are browned on both sides and still rare in the centre. Baste them with the
  foaming butter as they cook.
</Step>

<Step id="second-fry">
  Reheat the oil to <Temp c={190}/> and fry the potatoes again in small batches for
  <Dur step="second-fry"/>, until deep golden and crisp. Drain them and season them with a little
  of the salt.
</Step>

<Step id="rest-steak">
  Rest the steaks on a warm plate for <Dur step="rest-steak"/>, so the juices settle before they
  are cut. Serve each steak with a portion of the chips.
</Step>

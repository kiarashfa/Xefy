Steak frites is a steak pan-fried rare and served with deep-fried potatoes. The name is French, but Belgium also claims the dish as its own, and Wikipedia records that some regard it as the national dish of Belgium <Cite ref="wiki-steak"/>, <Cite ref="ne-frites"/>.

The dispute centres on the frites. The New European describes a Belgian story in which poorer people in the towns along the Meuse fried potatoes during winters when the frozen river stopped fishing. The popular Belgian claim dates this to the 1600s, but some historical documents show potatoes did not reach Belgium until the early 1700s <Cite ref="ne-frites"/>.

The same article credits the name "French fries" to American servicemen after the First World War, and describes the argument between Belgians and the French as ongoing <Cite ref="ne-frites"/>.

The New European also suggests that the early twentieth-century "French gastronomic hegemony" may explain the name, and reports that Flemish Belgians campaigned for UNESCO recognition of the snack <Cite ref="ne-frites"/>.

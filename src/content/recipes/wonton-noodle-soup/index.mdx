<Step id="start-broth">
  Put the <Qty ref="chicken"/>, <Qty ref="broth-water"/>, <Qty ref="broth-ginger"/> and
  <Qty ref="dried-shrimp"/> in a large pot and bring to the boil, skimming off any foam
  that rises, for <Dur step="start-broth"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="simmer-broth">
  Lower the heat and simmer gently, uncovered, for <Dur step="simmer-broth"/>, until the
  broth is fragrant and the chicken is cooked through. Lift out the chicken, and once cool
  enough to handle, shred the meat from the bone and set it aside. Strain the broth,
  discarding the solids, and stir in the <Qty ref="broth-spring-onion"/>,
  <Qty ref="broth-soy"/>, <Qty ref="broth-sesame-oil"/>, <Qty ref="broth-pepper"/> and
  <Qty ref="broth-salt"/>. Keep warm.
</Step>

<Step id="make-dough">
  Meanwhile, combine the <Qty ref="flour"/>, <Qty ref="dough-egg"/>,
  <Qty ref="dough-water"/> and <Qty ref="dough-salt"/> in a bowl and bring together into a
  firm dough, then knead for <Dur step="make-dough"/> until smooth.
</Step>

<Step id="rest-dough">
  Cover the dough and rest it for <Dur step="rest-dough"/> at room temperature.
</Step>

<Step id="make-filling">
  Combine the <Qty ref="pork"/>, <Qty ref="shrimp"/>, <Qty ref="filling-spring-onion"/>,
  <Qty ref="filling-ginger"/>, <Qty ref="filling-soy"/>, <Qty ref="filling-sesame-oil"/>,
  <Qty ref="filling-pepper"/>, <Qty ref="filling-salt"/>, <Qty ref="sugar"/> and
  <Qty ref="starch"/> in a bowl and stir hard in one direction until sticky and cohesive.
</Step>

<Step id="roll-and-fill">
  Roll the rested dough out thinly and cut it into small squares, keeping them stacked and
  covered. Spoon a little filling into the centre of each square, gather the corners up
  around it and twist to seal into a small purse.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-and-assemble">
  Blanch the <Qty ref="napa-cabbage"/> in a pot of boiling water until just wilted, then
  lift out and divide between serving bowls. In the same water, cook the
  <Qty ref="noodles"/> until tender and divide between the bowls. Boil the wontons in a
  separate pot of water for <Dur step="cook-and-assemble"/>, until they float and the
  wrappers turn translucent, then lift into the bowls with the shredded chicken. Ladle
  the hot broth over each bowl and scatter with the <Qty ref="garnish-spring-onion"/>.
</Step>

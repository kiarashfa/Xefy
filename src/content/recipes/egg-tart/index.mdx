notes:
  techniqueRationale:
    - stepId: make-custard
      text: Dissolving the sugar in hot water and letting it cool before it meets the egg, rather than beating sugar directly into the egg, keeps the mixture from trapping air; a frothy custard bakes with a pitted, bubbled surface instead of the smooth, glassy top this tart is judged on.
  commonPitfalls:
    - stepId: fill-and-bake
      text: An oven left too hot throughout puffs and cracks the custard's surface. Baking hot to set the pastry and then dropping the temperature is what keeps the custard smooth rather than curdled.

<Step id="mix-dough">
  Combine the <Qty ref="dough-flour"/>, <Qty ref="dough-sugar"/>,
  <Qty ref="dough-egg"/>, <Qty ref="water"/> and <Qty ref="dough-lard"/> into a
  smooth dough, taking <Dur step="mix-dough"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="rest-dough">
  Wrap and rest the dough in the refrigerator for <Dur step="rest-dough"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="laminate">
  Beat the <Qty ref="lamination-flour"/> into the <Qty ref="lamination-lard"/>
  to make a soft paste. Roll the rested dough into a rectangle, spread the
  lard paste over two-thirds of it, and fold as if folding a letter. Roll and
  fold again several times, taking <Dur step="laminate"/> in total, until the
  layers are even.
</Step>

<Step id="rest-laminated">
  Wrap and rest the laminated dough in the refrigerator for
  <Dur step="rest-laminated"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="line-tins">
  Roll the dough out and cut rounds to line a tart tin, pressing each round up
  the sides so the pastry stands slightly proud of the rim, taking
  <Dur step="line-tins"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="make-custard">
  Meanwhile, dissolve the <Qty ref="custard-sugar"/> in the
  <Qty ref="hot-water"/> and leave to cool. Whisk in the
  <Qty ref="custard-egg"/>, <Qty ref="evaporated-milk"/> and
  <Qty ref="vanilla"/>, then strain through a fine sieve, taking
  <Dur step="make-custard"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="fill-and-bake">
  Pour the custard into the lined tart cases, filling almost to the rim.
  Bake at <Temp c={220}/> for the first part of <Dur step="fill-and-bake"/>,
  then lower the heat to <Temp c={160}/> for the remainder, until the pastry
  is golden and the custard is just set with a faint wobble at the centre.
</Step>

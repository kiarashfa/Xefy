notes:
  techniqueRationale:
    - stepId: pull-noodles
      text: Resting the dough under oil relaxes the gluten enough that it can be stretched by hand into long strands rather than snapping back. It is the same principle behind other hand-pulled wheat noodles, and the reason the dough is never kneaded stiff.
  commonPitfalls:
    - stepId: cook-sauce
      text: The peppers and celery go in after the tomato has already broken down, not at the start. Added too early, they overcook and lose the crunch that lagman's sauce is built to keep.

<Step id="make-dough">
  Combine the <Qty ref="flour"/>, <Qty ref="dough-water"/> and
  <Qty ref="dough-salt"/> in a bowl and bring together into a firm dough, kneading
  for <Dur step="make-dough"/> until smooth.
</Step>

<Step id="rest-dough">
  Rub the dough all over with the <Qty ref="dough-oil"/>, cover, and leave it to
  rest for <Dur step="rest-dough"/>, so the gluten relaxes fully.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-lamb">
  Heat the <Qty ref="cook-oil"/> in a wok or wide pan over a high heat and
  stir-fry the <Qty ref="lamb"/> for <Dur step="cook-lamb"/>, until browned. Lift
  out and set aside.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-sauce">
  In the same pan, fry the <Qty ref="onion"/>, <Qty ref="garlic"/> and
  <Qty ref="ginger"/> until fragrant. Stir in the <Qty ref="cumin"/> and
  <Qty ref="dried-chilli"/>, then the <Qty ref="tomato"/> and
  <Qty ref="tomato-puree"/>, and cook until the tomato has broken down. Add the
  <Qty ref="red-pepper"/>, <Qty ref="green-pepper"/>, <Qty ref="carrot"/> and
  <Qty ref="celery"/>, return the browned lamb to the pan, then pour in the
  <Qty ref="stock"/> and the <Qty ref="fine-sea-salt"/>. Simmer for
  <Dur step="cook-sauce"/>, until the vegetables are tender but still have bite.
</Step>

<Step id="pull-noodles">
  Divide the rested dough into pieces and stretch each by hand into a long,
  even strand, doubling and re-stretching as needed until noodle-thin. Boil in
  plenty of salted water until just tender, working for <Dur step="pull-noodles"/>
  in total, then drain.
</Step>

<Step id="assemble">
  Divide the noodles between bowls, ladle the lamb and vegetable sauce over the
  top, and scatter with the <Qty ref="coriander"/> and <Qty ref="spring-onion"/>.
  This takes <Dur step="assemble"/>.
</Step>

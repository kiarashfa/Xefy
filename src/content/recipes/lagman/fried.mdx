<Step id="make-dough">
  Combine the <Qty ref="flour"/>, <Qty ref="dough-water"/> and
  <Qty ref="dough-salt"/> in a bowl and bring together into a firm dough, kneading
  for <Dur step="make-dough"/> until smooth.
</Step>

<Step id="rest-dough">
  Rub the dough all over with the <Qty ref="dough-oil"/>, cover, and leave it to
  rest for <Dur step="rest-dough"/>, so the gluten relaxes fully.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-lamb">
  Heat half of the <Qty ref="cook-oil"/> in a wok over a high heat and stir-fry the
  <Qty ref="lamb"/> for <Dur step="cook-lamb"/>, until well browned and starting to
  crisp at the edges. Lift out and set aside.
</Step>

<Step id="pull-noodles">
  Divide the rested dough into pieces and stretch each by hand into a long,
  even strand, doubling and re-stretching as needed until noodle-thin. Boil in
  plenty of salted water until just tender, working for <Dur step="pull-noodles"/>
  in total, then drain well.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-vegetables">
  Heat the remaining <Qty ref="cook-oil"/> in the same wok and fry the
  <Qty ref="onion"/>, <Qty ref="garlic"/> and <Qty ref="ginger"/> until fragrant.
  Add the <Qty ref="cumin"/> and <Qty ref="dried-chilli"/>, then the
  <Qty ref="tomato"/> and <Qty ref="tomato-puree"/>, and cook until the tomato has
  broken down. Add the <Qty ref="red-pepper"/>, <Qty ref="green-pepper"/>,
  <Qty ref="carrot"/> and <Qty ref="celery"/> and stir-fry over a high heat for
  <Dur step="cook-vegetables"/>, until the vegetables are tender but still crisp.
</Step>

<Step id="toss-noodles">
  Return the lamb to the wok along with the drained noodles, the
  <Qty ref="stock"/> and the <Qty ref="fine-sea-salt"/>. Toss everything together
  over a high heat for <Dur step="toss-noodles"/>, until the noodles are coated
  and lightly caught at the edges. Scatter with the <Qty ref="coriander"/> and
  <Qty ref="spring-onion"/> to serve.
</Step>

    - stepId: assemble

    - stepId: bake

<Step id="mix-dough">
  Dissolve the <Qty ref="yeast"/> in the <Qty ref="water"/>. Combine the
  <Qty ref="flour-dough"/> and <Qty ref="cornmeal"/> in a bowl, pour in the
  yeast mixture and the <Qty ref="oil-dough"/>, and mix until a shaggy dough
  forms. Add the <Qty ref="salt"/> and continue mixing.
</Step>

<Step id="knead">
  Knead for <Dur step="knead"/>, until smooth and only slightly tacky.
</Step>

<Step id="bulk-ferment">
  Cover and leave at room temperature for <Dur step="bulk-ferment"/>, until
  roughly doubled.
</Step>

<Step id="make-sauce">
  While the dough ferments, crush the <Qty ref="tomatoes"/> and combine with
  the <Qty ref="garlic"/> and <Qty ref="oregano"/>. Leave uncooked; the sauce
  finishes in the oven.
</Step>

<Step id="press-pan">
  Coat a deep, straight-sided pan with the <Qty ref="oil-pan"/>. Using the
  <Qty ref="flour-bench"/> to keep the dough from sticking, press it out and
  up the sides of the pan so the rim stands proud of the filling to come.
</Step>

<Step id="assemble">
  Lay the <Qty ref="mozzarella"/> directly over the base of the dough, right
  to the edges, then spoon the tomato sauce evenly over the cheese and finish
  with the <Qty ref="parmesan"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="bake">
  Bake at <Temp c={230}/> for <Dur step="bake"/>, until the crust is deeply
  browned at the rim and the sauce has thickened and started to char in
  patches.
</Step>

<Step id="rest">
  Rest the pizza in the pan for <Dur step="rest"/> before slicing, then
  scatter over the <Qty ref="basil"/>.
</Step>

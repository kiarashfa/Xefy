<Step id="cook-meat">
  Heat the <Qty ref="oil"/> in a wok over a high heat and stir-fry the <Qty ref="pork"/>
  for <Dur step="cook-meat"/> until browned, then add the <Qty ref="shrimp"/> and cook
  until pink. Lift both out and set aside.
</Step>

<Step id="aromatics">
  Add the <Qty ref="garlic"/> and <Qty ref="onion"/> to the same wok and stir-fry for
  <Dur step="aromatics"/>, until softened and fragrant.
</Step>

<Step id="add-vegetables">
  Add the <Qty ref="carrot"/> and <Qty ref="cabbage"/> and stir-fry for
  <Dur step="add-vegetables"/>, until just starting to soften.
</Step>

<Step id="add-liquid">
  Pour in the <Qty ref="stock"/>, <Qty ref="soy-sauce"/> and <Qty ref="oyster-sauce"/>
  and bring to a simmer for <Dur step="add-liquid"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="add-noodles">
  Return the pork and shrimp to the wok, add the <Qty ref="noodles"/> and toss for
  <Dur step="add-noodles"/>, until the noodles have softened and absorbed most of the
  liquid. Scatter with the <Qty ref="spring-onion"/> and serve with the
  <Qty ref="calamansi"/> alongside.
</Step>

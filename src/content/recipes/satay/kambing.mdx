<Step id="cube-meat">
  Trim the <Qty ref="goat"/> of any sinew and cut it into even cubes.
</Step>

<Step id="marinate">
  Whisk the <Qty ref="soy-sauce"/>, <Qty ref="palm-sugar"/>, <Qty ref="garlic"/>,
  <Qty ref="turmeric"/>, <Qty ref="cumin"/>, <Qty ref="oil"/> and <Qty ref="salt"/>
  together, add the goat and turn to coat every piece. Cover and marinate,
  refrigerated, for <Dur step="marinate"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="thread">
  Lift the goat from the marinade, shaking off the excess, and thread it onto
  skewers.
</Step>

<Step id="grill">
  Grill the skewers over direct charcoal heat, turning often, for <Dur step="grill"/>,
  until charred in places and cooked through with no pink remaining.
</Step>

<Step id="serve">
  Serve the skewers with the peanut sauce, <Qty ref="cucumber"/> and <Qty ref="onion"/>.
</Step>

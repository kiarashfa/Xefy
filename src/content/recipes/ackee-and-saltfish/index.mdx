<Step id="soak-saltfish">
  Cover the <Qty ref="saltfish"/> with cold water and leave to soak for
  <Dur step="soak-saltfish"/>, changing the water several times, to draw out the
  curing salt.
</Step>

<Step id="poach-and-flake">
  Drain the saltfish, cover with fresh water, and poach at a bare simmer for
  <Dur step="poach-and-flake"/>, until it flakes easily. Drain, remove any skin
  and bones, and flake into large pieces.
</Step>

<Step id="fry-aromatics">
  Heat the <Qty ref="vegetable-oil"/> in a wide pan and fry the
  <Qty ref="onion"/>, <Qty ref="garlic"/>, <Qty ref="tomato"/>,
  <Qty ref="scotch-bonnet"/> and <Qty ref="thyme"/> for
  <Dur step="fry-aromatics"/>, until the onion has softened and the tomato has
  broken down.
</Step>

<Step id="combine">
  Add the flaked saltfish to the pan and toss through the aromatics. Fold in the
  <Qty ref="ackee"/> gently, along with the <Qty ref="spring-onion"/> and
  <Qty ref="black-pepper"/>, warming everything through for
  <Dur step="combine"/> without breaking up the ackee. Discard the whole
  scotch bonnet before serving.
</Step>

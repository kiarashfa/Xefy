<Step id="thread">
  Thread the <Qty ref="chicken"/> onto skewers, alternating each piece with a length of
  the <Qty ref="spring-onion"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="grill">
  Season the skewers all over with the <Qty ref="salt"/> and grill them over direct
  charcoal heat for <Dur step="grill"/>, turning regularly, until well charred in places
  and the chicken is cooked through.
</Step>

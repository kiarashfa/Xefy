<Step id="thread">
  Thread the <Qty ref="chicken"/> onto skewers, alternating each piece with a length of
  the <Qty ref="spring-onion"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="make-tare">
  Meanwhile, combine the <Qty ref="soy-sauce"/>, <Qty ref="mirin"/>, <Qty ref="sake"/> and
  <Qty ref="sugar"/> in a small saucepan and simmer for <Dur step="make-tare"/>, until
  reduced by roughly a third and syrupy enough to coat the back of a spoon.
</Step>

<Step id="grill">
  Grill the skewers over direct charcoal heat for <Dur step="grill"/>, turning
  regularly and brushing with the tare on each turn, until the chicken is cooked through
  and the glaze has caramelised into a dark, sticky lacquer.
</Step>

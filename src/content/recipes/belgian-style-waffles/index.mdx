<Step id="heat-iron">
  Heat the waffle iron and brush it lightly with butter, allowing <Dur step="heat-iron"/>
  for it to come fully up to temperature.
</Step>

<Step id="mix-dry">
  Whisk the <Qty ref="flour"/>, <Qty ref="baking-powder"/>, <Qty ref="salt"/> and
  <Qty ref="sugar"/> together in a large bowl.
</Step>

<Step id="mix-batter">
  Beat the <Qty ref="egg"/> with the <Qty ref="milk"/>, the <Qty ref="butter"/> and the
  <Qty ref="vanilla"/>, then pour this into the dry ingredients and stir over
  <Dur step="mix-batter"/>, mixing only until the last streaks of flour disappear; a
  few small lumps are no fault.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-waffles">
  Ladle batter into the hot iron and cook until deep golden and crisp, releasing easily
  from the plates. Repeat with the remaining batter, spending <Dur step="cook-waffles"/>
  in total, and serve each waffle as soon as it is done.
</Step>

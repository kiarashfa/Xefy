<Step id="boil-sides">
  Bring a pot of salted water to the boil and cook the <Qty ref="sweet-potato"/> and
  <Qty ref="corn"/> together until the potato is tender to a knife point, about
  <Dur step="boil-sides"/>. Drain and set aside to cool.
</Step>

<Step id="blanch-shrimp">
  Bring a small pan of water to the boil with the <Qty ref="shrimp-salt"/> and drop
  in the <Qty ref="shrimp"/>. Cook for <Dur step="blanch-shrimp"/>, until just pink
  and opaque, then drain and plunge into iced water to stop the cooking. Drain again
  and halve each shrimp lengthways.
</Step>

<Step id="trim-fish">
  Cut the <Qty ref="fish"/> into small, even cubes, discarding any dark bloodline
  or stray bones as you go. This takes <Dur step="trim-fish"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="mix-marinade">
  Stir the <Qty ref="lime-juice"/>, <Qty ref="aji-limo"/>, <Qty ref="garlic"/> and
  <Qty ref="marinade-salt"/> together in a wide, non-reactive bowl. This takes
  <Dur step="mix-marinade"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="cure-fish">
  Add the fish cubes to the marinade and toss briefly to coat every piece, then
  leave for <Dur step="cure-fish"/>, until the flesh has turned opaque and firm
  at the edges but still looks faintly translucent at the centre.
</Step>

<Step id="finish-and-plate">
  Fold the blanched shrimp, <Qty ref="onion"/> and <Qty ref="coriander"/> through
  the cured fish and its marinade. Line each plate with a <Qty ref="lettuce"/>
  leaf, spoon the fish, shrimp and their juices on top, and arrange the drained
  sweet potato and corn alongside. This takes <Dur step="finish-and-plate"/>;
  serve straight away.
</Step>

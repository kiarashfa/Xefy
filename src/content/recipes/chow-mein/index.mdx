<Step id="velvet-pork">
  Combine the <Qty ref="velvet-soy"/> and <Qty ref="velvet-cornflour"/> in a
  bowl and turn the <Qty ref="pork"/> through it.
</Step>

<Step id="rest-pork">
  Leave the pork to marinate for <Dur step="rest-pork"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="boil-noodles">
  Meanwhile, boil the <Qty ref="noodles"/> for <Dur step="boil-noodles"/>,
  until just tender, then drain well and toss with the <Qty ref="noodle-oil"/>
  to stop them sticking together.
</Step>

<Step id="fry-noodles">
  Heat half the <Qty ref="wok-oil"/> in a wide pan over high heat and spread
  the noodles in an even layer. Fry undisturbed for most of
  <Dur step="fry-noodles"/>, then turn and crisp the other side, until browned
  in patches. Lift out and set aside.
</Step>

<Step id="stir-fry">
  Heat the remaining oil in the pan and stir-fry the pork with the
  <Qty ref="garlic"/> for a minute, until coloured. Add the
  <Qty ref="napa-cabbage"/>, <Qty ref="carrot"/> and <Qty ref="bean-sprouts"/>
  and stir-fry for <Dur step="stir-fry"/>, until just softened.
</Step>

<Step id="combine">
  Return the noodles to the pan with the <Qty ref="spring-onion"/>,
  <Qty ref="sauce-soy"/>, <Qty ref="dark-soy"/> and <Qty ref="sugar"/>. Toss
  over high heat for <Dur step="combine"/>, until evenly coated.
</Step>

<Step id="boil-water">
  Bring the <Qty ref="water"/> and <Qty ref="salt"/> to a rolling boil in a heavy pot.
  This takes about <Dur step="boil-water"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="add-meal">
  Reduce the heat slightly and add the <Qty ref="maize-meal"/> in a thin, steady stream,
  stirring constantly with a wooden spoon to stop lumps forming. This takes
  <Dur step="add-meal"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="simmer">
  Cover and simmer over a low heat for <Dur step="simmer"/>, stirring every so often to
  stop the bottom catching, until the porridge has thickened and pulled together.
</Step>

<Step id="work">
  Uncover and beat the porridge vigorously against the side of the pot for
  <Dur step="work"/>, over the heat the whole time, until it is smooth, stiff and
  pulls cleanly away from the sides in one mass. Turn out and serve hot.
</Step>

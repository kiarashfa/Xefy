<Step id="par-fry-chips">
  Heat the <Qty ref="frying-oil"/> to a moderate <Temp c={130}/> and fry the
  <Qty ref="potato"/> in batches for <Dur step="par-fry-chips"/>, until soft through
  but still pale. Drain.
</Step>

<Step id="rest-chips">
  Leave the chips to cool and dry out on a wire rack for <Dur step="rest-chips"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="make-gravy">
  Melt the <Qty ref="gravy-butter"/> in a saucepan, whisk in the <Qty ref="gravy-flour"/>
  and cook briefly, then whisk in the <Qty ref="gravy-stock"/>, the <Qty ref="salt"/> and
  the <Qty ref="pepper"/>. Simmer for <Dur step="make-gravy"/>, until thickened enough to
  coat the back of a spoon.
</Step>

<Step id="crisp-chips">
  Raise the oil to <Temp c={190}/> and fry the chips again for
  <Dur step="crisp-chips"/>, until deep golden and crisp. Drain well.
</Step>

<Step id="assemble">
  Pile the chips into bowls, scatter over the <Qty ref="curds"/> and pour the hot
  gravy over immediately. Serve at once.
</Step>

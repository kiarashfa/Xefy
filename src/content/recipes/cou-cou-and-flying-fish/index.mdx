<Step id="season-fish">
  Rub the <Qty ref="fish"/> with the <Qty ref="lime-juice"/> and
  <Qty ref="salt-fish"/> and set aside while the cou-cou is made.
</Step>

<Step id="boil-okra">
  Bring the <Qty ref="water-okra"/> to a boil with the <Qty ref="okra"/> and
  cook for <Dur step="boil-okra"/>, until the okra is fully tender and the
  water has turned slick.
</Step>

<Step id="work-coucou">
  Reduce the heat to low. Sprinkle in the <Qty ref="cornmeal"/> a little at a
  time, beating constantly with a wooden spoon so no lumps form, then work in
  the <Qty ref="butter"/> and the <Qty ref="salt-coucou"/> and keep beating
  for <Dur step="work-coucou"/>, until the mixture pulls cleanly away from the
  side of the pot.
</Step>

<Step id="gravy-base">
  Meanwhile, heat the <Qty ref="vegetable-oil"/> in a separate pan and cook
  the <Qty ref="onion"/> for <Dur step="gravy-base"/>, until softened, then
  add the <Qty ref="tomato"/>, <Qty ref="scotch-bonnet"/>,
  <Qty ref="thyme"/> and the <Qty ref="water-gravy"/> and bring to a simmer.
</Step>

<Step id="steam-fish">
  Lay the seasoned <Qty ref="fish"/> into the simmering gravy, cover and cook
  for <Dur step="steam-fish"/>, until the fillets are opaque and flake
  easily. Remove the whole scotch bonnet before serving the fish and gravy
  spooned over the cou-cou.
</Step>

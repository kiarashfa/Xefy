  commonPitfalls:

<Step id="cook-filling">
  Cook the <Qty ref="onion"/> and <Qty ref="garlic"/> in a pan over a medium
  heat until soft, then add the <Qty ref="chicken"/>, <Qty ref="potato"/>,
  <Qty ref="peas"/>, <Qty ref="stock"/>, <Qty ref="paprika"/> and
  <Qty ref="cumin"/>. Simmer for <Dur step="cook-filling"/>, until the
  chicken is cooked through and the potato is tender.
</Step>

<Step id="set-filling">
  Bloom the <Qty ref="gelatine"/> in a little of the hot liquid, then stir
  it back into the filling until fully dissolved. This takes about
  <Dur step="set-filling"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="chill-filling">
  Refrigerate the filling for <Dur step="chill-filling"/>, until it has set
  into a firm jelly.
</Step>

<Step id="make-dough">
  Combine the <Qty ref="flour"/>, <Qty ref="sugar"/>, <Qty ref="achiote"/>
  and <Qty ref="salt"/>, then work in the <Qty ref="butter"/> and
  <Qty ref="dough-water"/> to form a smooth, firm dough. This takes about
  <Dur step="make-dough"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="rest-dough">
  Cover the dough and leave it to rest for <Dur step="rest-dough"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="shape-and-fill">
  Roll out the dough and cut into rounds. Place a spoonful of the set
  filling on each round along with a little of the <Qty ref="raisins"/>,
  <Qty ref="olives"/> and <Qty ref="egg"/>, then fold the dough over and
  pinch the edges into a tight, sealed braid. This takes about
  <Dur step="shape-and-fill"/> in total.
</Step>

<Step id="bake">
  Bake at <Temp c={200}/> for <Dur step="bake"/>, until deep golden and the
  filling inside has melted back to a hot, juicy liquid.
</Step>

<Step id="poach-chicken">
  Put the <Qty ref="chicken"/> in a pot with the <Qty ref="water"/>, half the
  <Qty ref="onion"/>, half the <Qty ref="garlic"/> and the
  <Qty ref="salt"/>. Bring to a simmer and cook for
  <Dur step="poach-chicken"/>, until the chicken is cooked through. Lift out
  the chicken, reserving the broth.
</Step>

<Step id="toast-aromatics">
  Meanwhile, toast the <Qty ref="guajillo-chilli"/>, <Qty ref="squash-seeds"/>,
  <Qty ref="sesame-seed"/> and <Qty ref="corn-tortilla"/> separately in a dry
  pan for <Dur step="toast-aromatics"/>, until fragrant, taking care not to
  scorch them.
</Step>

<Step id="blend-sauce">
  Blend the toasted ingredients with the <Qty ref="tomato"/>, the remaining
  <Qty ref="onion"/> and <Qty ref="garlic"/>, the <Qty ref="cinnamon"/>,
  <Qty ref="allspice"/>, <Qty ref="black-pepper"/> and enough of the reserved
  broth to make a smooth, thick sauce. This takes about
  <Dur step="blend-sauce"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="fry-sauce">
  Heat the <Qty ref="oil"/> in a pot over a medium heat and fry the blended
  sauce for <Dur step="fry-sauce"/>, stirring often, until it darkens
  slightly and smells toasted rather than raw.
</Step>

<Step id="simmer-together">
  Stir in the remaining reserved broth, the poached chicken and the
  <Qty ref="potato"/>. Simmer for <Dur step="simmer-together"/>, until the
  potato is tender and the sauce has thickened.
</Step>

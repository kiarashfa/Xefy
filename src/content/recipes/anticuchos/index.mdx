<Step id="trim-cube">
  Trim the <Qty ref="heart"/> of any silverskin, connective tissue and visible fat, then
  cut the meat into even cubes.
</Step>

<Step id="marinate">
  Stir the <Qty ref="aji-panca"/>, <Qty ref="garlic"/>, <Qty ref="cumin"/>,
  <Qty ref="vinegar"/>, <Qty ref="oil-marinade"/>, <Qty ref="salt"/> and
  <Qty ref="pepper"/> together, coat the cubed heart in the mixture, and leave it to
  marinate, covered and refrigerated, for <Dur step="marinate"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="thread">
  Thread the marinated cubes tightly onto skewers, leaving as little gap between pieces
  as the shape of the cubes allows.
</Step>

<Step id="grill">
  Grill the skewers over fierce, direct charcoal heat for <Dur step="grill"/>, turning
  often and brushing with the <Qty ref="oil-basting"/> as they cook, until the outside is
  well charred and the centre is still faintly pink.
</Step>

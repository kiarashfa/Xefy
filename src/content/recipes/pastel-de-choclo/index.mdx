<Step id="cook-pino">
  Heat the <Qty ref="vegetable-oil"/> in a wide pan and fry the
  <Qty ref="onion"/> and <Qty ref="garlic"/> until soft. Add the
  <Qty ref="beef-mince"/>, <Qty ref="cumin"/>, <Qty ref="paprika"/> and half of
  the <Qty ref="fine-sea-salt"/>, and cook, breaking up the meat, for
  <Dur step="cook-pino"/>, until the beef is cooked through. Stir in the
  <Qty ref="raisins"/> and set aside.
</Step>

<Step id="make-corn-topping">
  Blend the <Qty ref="corn"/> with the <Qty ref="milk"/> and <Qty ref="basil"/>
  until fairly smooth. Transfer to a pan with the <Qty ref="butter"/>, the
  <Qty ref="sugar-topping"/> and the remaining <Qty ref="fine-sea-salt"/>, and
  cook over a low heat, stirring constantly, for <Dur step="make-corn-topping"/>,
  until noticeably thickened.
</Step>

<Step id="assemble-pie">
  Spread the beef filling over the base of a baking dish. Scatter over the
  <Qty ref="olives"/> and arrange the <Qty ref="egg"/> on top. Cover evenly with
  the corn topping. This takes <Dur step="assemble-pie"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="bake">
  Dust the top with the <Qty ref="sugar-dusting"/> and bake at
  <Temp c={200}/> for <Dur step="bake"/>, until the top is set and lightly
  golden in patches.
</Step>

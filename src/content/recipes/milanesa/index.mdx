<Step id="season">
  Season the <Qty ref="beef"/> on both sides with the <Qty ref="garlic"/>,
  <Qty ref="parsley"/>, <Qty ref="salt"/> and <Qty ref="pepper"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="crumb">
  Dip each cutlet in the <Qty ref="egg"/>, letting the excess drip off, then
  press it into the <Qty ref="breadcrumbs"/> on both sides until fully
  coated.
</Step>

<Step id="fry">
  Heat the <Qty ref="oil"/> in a wide pan until shimmering. Fry the cutlets
  for <Dur step="fry"/> in total, turning once, until deep golden and crisp
  on both sides. Drain briefly on paper before serving.
</Step>

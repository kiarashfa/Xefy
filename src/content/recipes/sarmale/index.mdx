<Step id="rinse-leaves">
  Rinse the <Qty ref="cabbage-leaves"/> under cold water to remove excess salt, and
  trim away any thick centre rib.
</Step>

<Step id="make-filling">
  Combine the <Qty ref="pork"/>, the <Qty ref="rice"/>, the <Qty ref="onion"/>, the
  <Qty ref="dill"/>, half the <Qty ref="tomato-puree"/>, the <Qty ref="salt"/> and the
  <Qty ref="pepper"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="roll-sarmale">
  Place a small spoonful of filling on each leaf, fold in the sides, and roll into a
  tight, compact parcel.
</Step>

<Step id="layer-pot">
  Scatter half the <Qty ref="bacon"/> over the base of a heavy pot, pack in the
  sarmale in tight layers with the <Qty ref="bay-leaf"/> tucked between them, and
  scatter over the rest of the bacon.
</Step>

<Step id="braise">
  Pour over the <Qty ref="stock"/> mixed with the rest of the <Qty ref="tomato-puree"/>,
  cover, and braise at <Temp c={160}/> for <Dur step="braise"/>, until the leaves are
  completely tender. Serve with the <Qty ref="soured-cream"/>.
</Step>

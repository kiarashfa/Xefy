<Step id="beat-eggs">
  Beat the <Qty ref="eggs"/> hard with the <Qty ref="parmigiano"/>, the <Qty ref="parsley"/>,
  the <Qty ref="salt"/> and the <Qty ref="pepper"/>, for <Dur step="beat-eggs"/>, until the
  mixture is frothy and the cheese has broken into it.
</Step>

<Step id="heat-pan">
  Heat the <Qty ref="olive-oil"/> in a heavy, lidded pan over a low flame until it shimmers
  and runs thin across the base, for <Dur step="heat-pan"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="set-frittata">
  Pour in the beaten egg and let it stand for a moment, then draw the set edges in towards
  the centre with a spatula so that the raw egg runs into the gaps. Cover the pan and cook
  over the lowest heat for <Dur step="set-frittata"/>, until the base is set and the top is
  just firm, with a slight wobble at the centre.
</Step>

<Step id="turn-frittata">
  Slide the frittata onto a plate, invert the plate over the pan, and let it drop back into
  the pan for <Dur step="turn-frittata"/>, to set the second side.
</Step>

<Step id="rest-frittata">
  Slide the frittata onto a board and leave it for <Dur step="rest-frittata"/> before cutting
  it into wedges.
</Step>

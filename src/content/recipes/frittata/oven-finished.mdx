<Step id="beat-eggs">
  Beat the <Qty ref="eggs"/> hard with the <Qty ref="parmigiano"/>, the <Qty ref="parsley"/>,
  the <Qty ref="salt"/> and the <Qty ref="pepper"/>, for <Dur step="beat-eggs"/>, until the
  mixture is frothy and the cheese has broken into it.
</Step>

<Step id="heat-pan">
  Heat the <Qty ref="olive-oil"/> in a heavy, ovenproof pan over a low flame until it
  shimmers and runs thin across the base, for <Dur step="heat-pan"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="set-base">
  Pour in the beaten egg and draw the set edges in towards the centre with a spatula, so the
  raw egg runs into the gaps. Cook over the lowest heat for <Dur step="set-base"/>, until the
  base is set and the top is still soft.
</Step>

<Step id="finish-oven">
  Transfer the pan to an oven at <Temp c={180}/> and cook for <Dur step="finish-oven"/>, until
  the top is set and springs back when touched lightly at the centre.
</Step>

<Step id="rest-frittata">
  Leave the frittata in the pan for <Dur step="rest-frittata"/>, then slide it onto a board
  and cut it into wedges.
</Step>

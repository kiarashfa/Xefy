<Step id="make-broth">
  Combine the <Qty ref="water"/>, <Qty ref="onion"/>, <Qty ref="tomato"/> and the
  strained <Qty ref="tamarind"/> liquid in a large pot and simmer for
  <Dur step="make-broth"/>, until the tomato begins to break down.
</Step>

<Step id="simmer-daikon">
  Add the <Qty ref="daikon"/> and simmer for <Dur step="simmer-daikon"/>, until almost
  tender.
</Step>

<Step id="add-vegetables">
  Add the <Qty ref="long-beans"/>, <Qty ref="aubergine"/> and <Qty ref="chilli"/> and
  simmer for <Dur step="add-vegetables"/>, until the vegetables are just cooked through.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-shrimp">
  Add the <Qty ref="shrimp"/> and simmer for <Dur step="cook-shrimp"/>, until they turn
  pink and curl.
</Step>

<Step id="finish">
  Season with the <Qty ref="fish-sauce"/>, then take the pot off the heat and stir
  through the <Qty ref="water-spinach"/> for <Dur step="finish"/>, just long enough for
  it to wilt. Serve at once.
</Step>

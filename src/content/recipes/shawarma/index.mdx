<Step id="slice-meat">
  Trim the <Qty ref="chicken"/> of any excess fat and slice it thinly.
</Step>

<Step id="marinate">
  Combine the <Qty ref="yoghurt"/>, <Qty ref="oil"/>, <Qty ref="lemon-marinade"/>,
  <Qty ref="garlic-marinade"/>, <Qty ref="cumin"/>, <Qty ref="paprika"/>,
  <Qty ref="turmeric"/>, <Qty ref="cinnamon"/>, <Qty ref="pepper"/> and
  <Qty ref="salt-marinade"/>, add the sliced chicken and turn to coat every piece.
  Cover and marinate, refrigerated, for <Dur step="marinate"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="stack">
  Thread the marinated chicken tightly onto a vertical skewer, pressing each layer
  down onto the last, building an even, dense cylinder with no gaps.
</Step>

<Step id="make-tahini-sauce">
  Whisk the <Qty ref="tahini"/> with the <Qty ref="lemon-tahini"/> and
  <Qty ref="garlic-tahini"/>. It will seize into a stiff, grainy paste before it does
  anything else — keep whisking and add the <Qty ref="water"/> in a thin stream until
  it loosens into a smooth, pourable sauce. Season with the <Qty ref="salt-tahini"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="roast">
  Roast the stacked chicken close to a vertical heat source, turning it steadily, for
  <Dur step="roast"/>, until the outer surface is deeply browned and the centre is
  cooked through with no pink remaining.
</Step>

<Step id="shave">
  Shave the roasted chicken downward off the spit in thin ribbons for
  <Dur step="shave"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="warm-bread">
  Warm the <Qty ref="pita"/> briefly over direct heat until soft and pliable.
</Step>

<Step id="assemble">
  Spread the tahini sauce over the warm pita, fill with the shaved chicken,
  <Qty ref="tomato"/>, <Qty ref="onion"/> and <Qty ref="parsley"/>, and roll it closed.
</Step>

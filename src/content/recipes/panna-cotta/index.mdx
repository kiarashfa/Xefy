<Step id="make-caramel">
  Melt the <Qty ref="sugar-caramel"/> in a small, dry pan over a moderate flame, without
  stirring, until it is a dark gold. Pour it at once into the base of each mould, tilting
  each to coat the bottom, and leave it to harden.
</Step>

<Step id="warm-cream">
  Warm the <Qty ref="cream"/>, the <Qty ref="milk-set"/>, the <Qty ref="sugar-cream"/> and
  the <Qty ref="vanilla"/> in a pan over a low flame, stirring, until the sugar has dissolved
  and the surface begins to shiver. Allow <Dur step="warm-cream"/> for this, then take
  the pan off the heat.
</Step>

<Step id="bloom-gelatine">
  Sprinkle the <Qty ref="gelatine"/> over the <Qty ref="milk-bloom"/> in a small bowl, and
  leave it to swell for <Dur step="bloom-gelatine"/>, until it has taken up all the liquid.
</Step>

<Step id="combine-set">
  Whisk the bloomed gelatine into the warm cream until it has dissolved completely, and
  strain the mixture through a fine sieve into a jug.
</Step>

<Step id="fill-moulds">
  Pour the cream over the set caramel in the moulds, leaving a little space at the top.
  Work for <Dur step="fill-moulds"/>, and cover the moulds as they are filled.
</Step>

<Step id="chill">
  Chill the moulds, uncovered for the first hour and then covered, for <Dur step="chill"/>,
  until they are set firm and wobble only when shaken.
</Step>

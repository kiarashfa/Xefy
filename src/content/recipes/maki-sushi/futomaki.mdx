<Step id="cook-rice">
  Rinse the <Qty ref="rice"/> in several changes of cold water until the water runs almost clear,
  drain it well, and cook it with a measured amount of water in a heavy pan with a tight lid. Leave
  it covered off the heat for the last part of the <Dur step="cook-rice"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="soak-shiitake">
  Cover the <Qty ref="shiitake"/> with warm water and leave it to soften for
  <Dur step="soak-shiitake"/>. Drain it, reserving a little of the soaking liquid, and cut away the
  stems.
</Step>

<Step id="simmer-shiitake">
  Put the shiitake into a small pan with the <Qty ref="soy"/> and the reserved soaking liquid.
  Simmer gently for <Dur step="simmer-shiitake"/>, until
  the liquid is nearly gone, and leave them to cool.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-omelette">
  Beat the <Qty ref="egg"/> with the <Qty ref="sugar-egg"/> and the <Qty ref="salt-egg"/> in a bowl. Cook it in a small oiled pan
  in thin layers, rolling each layer up as it sets, to make a thick omelette. Leave it to cool, then
  cut it into long strips. This takes <Dur step="cook-omelette"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="blanch-spinach">
  Blanch the <Qty ref="spinach"/> in boiling salted water for <Dur step="blanch-spinach"/>, refresh
  it in cold water, and squeeze it dry.
</Step>

<Step id="dress-rice">
  Warm the <Qty ref="vinegar"/> with the <Qty ref="sugar-rice"/> and the <Qty ref="salt-rice"/> until
  the sugar and salt have dissolved. Spread the hot rice in a wide wooden tray, pour the seasoning
  over it, and fold it through with a rice paddle, fanning it as you go, until it is glossy and
  cooled to the touch. This takes <Dur step="dress-rice"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="roll-futomaki">
  Lay a sheet of the <Qty ref="nori"/> on a bamboo mat. Spread the seasoned rice over most of it
  with a thin border at the far edge. Lay the omelette, the shiitake, the <Qty ref="cucumber"/> and
  the spinach along the middle in one straight line, and roll the mat up tightly from the near edge,
  pressing to seal. This takes <Dur step="roll-futomaki"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="slice-rolls">
  Cut each roll into thick rounds with a wet knife, wiping the blade between cuts, and arrange them
  on a plate. This takes <Dur step="slice-rolls"/>.
</Step>

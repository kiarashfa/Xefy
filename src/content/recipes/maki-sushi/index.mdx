<Step id="cook-rice">
  Rinse the <Qty ref="rice"/> in several changes of cold water until the water runs almost clear,
  drain it well, and cook it with a measured amount of water in a heavy pan with a tight lid. Leave
  it covered off the heat for the last part of the <Dur step="cook-rice"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="prepare-fillings">
  Cut the <Qty ref="tuna"/> into strips, and cut the <Qty ref="cucumber"/> into matchsticks. Keep
  the tuna cold on a plate in the fridge until it is rolled. This takes
  <Dur step="prepare-fillings"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="dress-rice">
  Warm the <Qty ref="vinegar"/> with the <Qty ref="sugar"/> and the <Qty ref="salt"/> until the
  sugar and salt have dissolved. Spread the hot rice in a wide wooden tray, pour the seasoning over
  it, and fold it through with a rice paddle, fanning it as you go, until it is glossy and cooled
  to the touch. This takes <Dur step="dress-rice"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="roll-hosomaki">
  Lay a sheet of the <Qty ref="nori"/> on a bamboo mat, with the shiny side down. Spread a thin,
  even layer of the seasoned rice over the lower half, leaving a border at the far edge. Lay a line
  of the <Qty ref="tuna"/> and the <Qty ref="cucumber"/> along the rice, with a thin smear of
  the <Qty ref="wasabi"/> along the rice. Roll the mat up tightly from the near edge, press to seal,
  and set the roll aside. This takes <Dur step="roll-hosomaki"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="slice-rolls">
  Cut each roll into rounds with a wet knife, wiping the blade between cuts. Arrange the rounds on
  a plate with the <Qty ref="pickled-ginger"/>, and serve with the <Qty ref="soy"/> for dipping.
  This takes <Dur step="slice-rolls"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="marinate-beef">
  Combine the <Qty ref="velvet-soy"/>, <Qty ref="velvet-wine"/>,
  <Qty ref="velvet-cornflour"/> and <Qty ref="velvet-oil"/> in a bowl and turn
  the <Qty ref="beef"/> through it.
</Step>

<Step id="rest-beef">
  Leave the beef to marinate for <Dur step="rest-beef"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="sear-beef">
  Heat a wok over the highest possible heat until smoking. Add a little of the
  <Qty ref="wok-oil"/> and sear the beef for <Dur step="sear-beef"/>, until just
  coloured but not fully cooked through, then lift out and set aside.
</Step>

<Step id="fry-noodles">
  Add the remaining <Qty ref="wok-oil"/> to the wok along with the
  <Qty ref="garlic"/>, then add the <Qty ref="noodles"/>, loosening the strands
  gently by hand as they go in. Toss over the highest heat for
  <Dur step="fry-noodles"/>, letting the noodles catch and char lightly in
  places, and add the <Qty ref="dark-soy"/> and <Qty ref="light-soy"/> partway
  through so the colour works evenly through the strands.
</Step>

<Step id="combine">
  Return the beef to the wok with the <Qty ref="bean-sprouts"/>,
  <Qty ref="spring-onion"/> and <Qty ref="sugar"/>. Toss over high heat for
  <Dur step="combine"/>, until the beef is cooked through and everything is
  evenly combined.
</Step>

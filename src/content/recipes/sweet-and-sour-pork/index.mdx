<Step id="marinate-pork">
  Combine the <Qty ref="velvet-soy"/> and <Qty ref="velvet-wine"/> in a bowl
  and turn the <Qty ref="pork"/> through it.
</Step>

<Step id="rest-pork">
  Leave the pork to marinate for <Dur step="rest-pork"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="coat-pork">
  Dip each piece of pork in the <Qty ref="egg"/>, then coat thoroughly in the
  <Qty ref="cornflour"/>, shaking off the excess, over <Dur step="coat-pork"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="fry-pork">
  Heat the <Qty ref="pork-deep-fry-oil"/> until shimmering and fry the pork in batches
  for <Dur step="fry-pork"/>, until pale gold and cooked through. Drain on a
  rack.
</Step>

<Step id="refry-pork">
  Raise the oil to a higher heat and fry the pork again, in batches, for
  <Dur step="refry-pork"/>, until deeply golden and crisp. Drain again.
</Step>

<Step id="make-sauce">
  Heat the <Qty ref="sauce-oil"/> in a wok and stir-fry the
  <Qty ref="green-pepper"/>, <Qty ref="red-pepper"/> and <Qty ref="onion"/> for
  a minute, then add the <Qty ref="pineapple"/>, <Qty ref="ketchup"/>,
  <Qty ref="vinegar"/>, <Qty ref="sugar"/> and <Qty ref="sauce-water"/>. Bring
  to a simmer over <Dur step="make-sauce"/>, then stir in the
  <Qty ref="sauce-cornflour"/> mixed with a little cold water and cook until
  thickened.
</Step>

<Step id="combine">
  Add the fried pork to the wok and toss quickly to coat, over
  <Dur step="combine"/>, until glossy all over.
</Step>

<Step id="cream">
  Beat the <Qty ref="butter"/> with the <Qty ref="brown-sugar"/> for <Dur step="cream"/>,
  until pale and fluffy.
</Step>

<Step id="beat-in-eggs">
  Beat in the <Qty ref="eggs"/> and the <Qty ref="vanilla"/>, taking <Dur step="beat-in-eggs"/>
  and stopping as soon as the mixture looks smooth again.
</Step>

<Step id="mash-in-banana">
  Stir the <Qty ref="bananas"/> through, using <Dur step="mash-in-banana"/>. A few small
  lumps left in the mash are no fault; they turn to soft pockets in the baked loaf.
</Step>

<Step id="fold-dry">
  Sift the <Qty ref="flour"/> together with the <Qty ref="baking-powder"/>, the
  <Qty ref="bicarb"/> and the <Qty ref="salt"/>, and fold them into the banana mixture with
  the <Qty ref="walnuts"/> over <Dur step="fold-dry"/>, stopping the moment no dry flour
  is visible.
</Step>

<Step id="heat-oven">
  Meanwhile heat the oven to <Temp c={170}/>, allowing <Dur step="heat-oven"/> for it to
  settle, and butter and line a loaf tin.
</Step>

<Step id="bake">
  Turn the batter into the tin and bake for <Dur step="bake"/>, until a skewer pushed into
  the centre comes out clean and the top has split along its length.
</Step>

<Step id="cool-in-tin">
  Leave the loaf in its tin for <Dur step="cool-in-tin"/> before turning it out onto a rack,
  where the crumb finishes setting as it cools the rest of the way.
</Step>

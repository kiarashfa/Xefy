<Step id="mix-dough">
  Combine the <Qty ref="flour"/>, <Qty ref="baking-powder"/>,
  <Qty ref="ammonium-bicarbonate"/>, <Qty ref="salt"/> and <Qty ref="sugar"/> in a
  bowl. Add the <Qty ref="water"/> and bring together into a soft, wet dough,
  mixing rather than kneading, until no dry flour remains.
</Step>

<Step id="rest-dough">
  Cover the dough and leave it to rest at room temperature for
  <Dur step="rest-dough"/>, until visibly relaxed and slightly puffed.
</Step>

<Step id="shape">
  Turn the dough out onto an oiled surface without kneading it, and pat it into a
  rectangle. Cut into even strips, stack two strips together and press a
  chopstick along the length of the top strip to seal the pair, then stretch each
  pair gently as you go.
</Step>

<Step id="fry">
  Heat the <Qty ref="frying-oil"/> until shimmering, then lower each pair in
  gently, stretching slightly as it enters the oil. Fry for <Dur step="fry"/>,
  turning constantly, until puffed, hollow and deep golden all over. Lift out and
  drain.
</Step>

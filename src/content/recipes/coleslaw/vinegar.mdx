<Step id="make-dressing">
  Warm the <Qty ref="vinegar"/>, the <Qty ref="oil"/>, the <Qty ref="sugar"/> and the
  <Qty ref="mustard-seed"/> together in a small pan just until the sugar dissolves,
  over <Dur step="make-dressing"/>, then stir in the <Qty ref="salt"/> and the
  <Qty ref="pepper"/> and let it cool.
</Step>

<Step id="combine">
  Toss the <Qty ref="cabbage"/>, the <Qty ref="carrot"/> and the <Qty ref="onion"/>
  with the cooled dressing until evenly coated, over <Dur step="combine"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="rest">
  Leave to rest, covered, for <Dur step="rest"/> before serving, so the cabbage begins
  to soften in the vinegar.
</Step>

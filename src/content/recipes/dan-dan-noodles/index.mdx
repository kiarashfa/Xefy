<Step id="make-chilli-oil">
  Put the <Qty ref="bowl-chilli-oil"/> in a heatproof bowl. Heat the
  <Qty ref="bowl-oil"/> until just beginning to smoke, then pour it over the
  chilli, taking <Dur step="make-chilli-oil"/>, and stir in the
  <Qty ref="sichuan-pepper"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-topping">
  Heat the <Qty ref="cooking-oil"/> in a wok and stir-fry the
  <Qty ref="pork"/>, breaking it up, until browned. Add the
  <Qty ref="preserved-radish"/>, <Qty ref="shaoxing-wine"/> and
  <Qty ref="soy-sauce"/> and cook for <Dur step="cook-topping"/>, until dry and
  fragrant.
</Step>

<Step id="boil-noodles">
  Meanwhile, boil the <Qty ref="noodles"/> for <Dur step="boil-noodles"/>,
  until just tender, then drain well.
</Step>

<Step id="build-bowls">
  Divide the <Qty ref="doubanjiang"/>, <Qty ref="bowl-soy"/>,
  <Qty ref="black-vinegar"/>, <Qty ref="garlic"/> and the chilli oil mixture
  between the serving bowls. Loosen the <Qty ref="sesame-paste"/> with the
  <Qty ref="noodle-water"/> and add it to the bowls, taking
  <Dur step="build-bowls"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="combine">
  Pile the drained noodles into the bowls and top with the pork mixture,
  <Qty ref="spring-onion"/> and <Qty ref="peanut"/>. Toss well at the table
  before eating, over <Dur step="combine"/>.
</Step>

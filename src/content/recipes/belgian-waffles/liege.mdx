<Step id="activate-yeast">
  Stir the <Qty ref="yeast"/> into the warm <Qty ref="milk"/> and leave it for
  <Dur step="activate-yeast"/>, until foamy.
</Step>

<Step id="mix-dough">
  Combine the <Qty ref="flour"/>, <Qty ref="caster-sugar"/> and
  <Qty ref="fine-sea-salt"/> in a large bowl. Add the yeasted milk, the
  <Qty ref="egg"/> and the <Qty ref="vanilla"/>, and bring together into a
  rough, sticky dough. This takes <Dur step="mix-dough"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="knead-dough">
  Knead in the soft <Qty ref="butter"/> a little at a time, working the dough
  for <Dur step="knead-dough"/>, until smooth, glossy and elastic.
</Step>

<Step id="prove-dough">
  Cover the dough and leave it to prove somewhere warm for
  <Dur step="prove-dough"/>, until roughly doubled.
</Step>

<Step id="fold-in-sugar">
  Knock the dough back and fold in the <Qty ref="pearl-sugar"/> by hand,
  distributing it evenly. This takes <Dur step="fold-in-sugar"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="portion-and-rest">
  Divide the dough into equal balls and let them rest, covered, for
  <Dur step="portion-and-rest"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-waffles">
  Heat the waffle iron and brush it lightly with butter. Press a ball of
  dough into the iron and cook until deep golden-brown, with the pearl sugar
  caramelised at the surface. Repeat with the remaining dough, spending
  <Dur step="cook-waffles"/> in total, and serve each waffle warm.
</Step>

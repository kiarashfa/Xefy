<Step id="activate-yeast">
  Stir the <Qty ref="yeast"/> into the warm <Qty ref="milk"/> and leave it for
  <Dur step="activate-yeast"/>, until foamy.
</Step>

<Step id="mix-batter">
  Whisk the <Qty ref="flour"/>, <Qty ref="caster-sugar"/> and
  <Qty ref="fine-sea-salt"/> together in a large bowl. Whisk in the yeasted
  milk, the <Qty ref="egg-yolk"/>, the <Qty ref="butter"/> and the
  <Qty ref="vanilla"/> until smooth. This takes <Dur step="mix-batter"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="whip-whites">
  Whip the <Qty ref="egg-white"/> to stiff peaks, working for
  <Dur step="whip-whites"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="prove-batter">
  Fold the whipped whites gently through the batter, cover, and leave
  somewhere warm to prove for <Dur step="prove-batter"/>, until visibly
  puffed and bubbly.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-waffles">
  Heat the waffle iron and brush it lightly with butter. Ladle in the batter
  and cook until deep golden and crisp, releasing easily from the plates.
  Repeat with the remaining batter, spending <Dur step="cook-waffles"/> in
  total. Dust with the <Qty ref="icing-sugar"/> and serve each waffle as soon
  as it is done.
</Step>

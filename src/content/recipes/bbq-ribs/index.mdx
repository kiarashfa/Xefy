<Step id="season">
  Peel the thin membrane from the underside of the <Qty ref="ribs"/> and pat
  the racks dry. Combine the <Qty ref="salt"/>, <Qty ref="pepper"/>,
  <Qty ref="brown-sugar"/>, <Qty ref="paprika"/>, <Qty ref="mustard-powder"/>
  and <Qty ref="celery-salt"/>, then coat both racks evenly on all sides.
</Step>

<Step id="smoke">
  Smoke the racks bone side down at a steady <Temp c={110}/> for
  <Dur step="smoke"/>, without opening the smoker more than occasionally,
  until the meat has pulled back from the ends of the bones and a rack lifted
  at one end bends and just begins to crack across the surface.
</Step>

<Step id="mop">
  Brush the racks all over with the <Qty ref="vinegar-mop"/> and dust them
  with a last light scattering of any rub left on the board, then set them
  aside off the heat.
</Step>

<Step id="rest">
  Rest the ribs, loosely tented, for <Dur step="rest"/> before cutting.
</Step>

<Step id="serve">
  Cut the racks into individual ribs and serve them dry, with no sauce added
  at the table.
</Step>

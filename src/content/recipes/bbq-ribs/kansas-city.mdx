<Step id="season">
  Peel the thin membrane from the underside of the <Qty ref="ribs"/> and pat
  the racks dry. Combine the <Qty ref="salt"/>, <Qty ref="pepper"/>, the
  <Qty ref="brown-sugar-rub"/>, the <Qty ref="paprika-rub"/> and the
  <Qty ref="mustard-rub"/>, then coat both racks evenly on all sides.
</Step>

<Step id="make-sauce">
  While the ribs smoke, combine the <Qty ref="ketchup"/>, <Qty ref="molasses"/>,
  <Qty ref="vinegar"/>, the <Qty ref="brown-sugar-sauce"/>, the
  <Qty ref="paprika-sauce"/> and the <Qty ref="mustard-sauce"/> in a small pan
  and simmer gently, stirring, until it has thickened enough to coat the back
  of a spoon.
</Step>

<Step id="smoke">
  Smoke the racks bone side down at a steady <Temp c={110}/> for
  <Dur step="smoke"/>, until the meat has pulled back from the ends of the
  bones and is tender enough that a rack lifted at one end bends deeply.
</Step>

<Step id="glaze">
  Brush the racks all over with the sauce and return them to the smoker or a
  hot grill for <Dur step="glaze"/>, just long enough for the glaze to turn
  tacky and set rather than run.
</Step>

<Step id="rest">
  Rest the glazed ribs, loosely tented, for <Dur step="rest"/> before cutting.
</Step>

<Step id="serve">
  Cut the racks into individual ribs and serve with any remaining sauce on
  the side.
</Step>

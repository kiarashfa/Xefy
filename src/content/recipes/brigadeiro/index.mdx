<Step id="cook">
  Combine the <Qty ref="condensed-milk"/>, <Qty ref="cocoa"/> and
  <Qty ref="butter"/> in a heavy-based saucepan over a low heat. Cook for
  <Dur step="cook"/>, stirring constantly, until the mixture thickens enough
  that a spoon dragged across the bottom of the pan briefly leaves it
  visible before the fudge flows back together.
</Step>

<Step id="chill">
  Scrape the fudge onto a plate, smooth it out and chill for
  <Dur step="chill"/>, until firm enough to roll without sticking to your
  hands.
</Step>

<Step id="shape">
  Roll the fudge into small balls between lightly buttered palms, then roll
  each ball in the <Qty ref="sprinkles"/> to coat completely.
</Step>

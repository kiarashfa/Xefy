<Step id="warm-milk">
  Warm the <Qty ref="milk"/> and the <Qty ref="cream"/> with the <Qty ref="sugar-custard"/>
  in a heavy pan over a moderate flame, stirring until the sugar has dissolved and the
  liquid is steaming, for <Dur step="warm-milk"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="beat-yolks">
  Whisk the <Qty ref="yolks"/> with the <Qty ref="sugar-yolks"/> in a bowl until pale and
  thick, for <Dur step="beat-yolks"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="make-custard">
  Pour a ladleful of the hot milk on to the yolks, whisking, then pour the mixture back into
  the pan. Stir over a low flame until it reaches <Temp c={85}/> and coats the back of a
  spoon, for <Dur step="make-custard"/>. Do not let it boil. Strain it into a clean bowl,
  stir in the <Qty ref="vanilla"/>, and let it cool.
</Step>

<Step id="chill-base">
  Cover the base and chill it fully for <Dur step="chill-base"/>, until it is cold right through.
</Step>

<Step id="churn">
  Churn the cold base in an ice cream machine for <Dur step="churn"/>, until it has thickened
  to a soft, dense gelato.
</Step>

<Step id="freeze">
  Transfer the gelato to a container, press a sheet of paper on the surface, and freeze it
  for <Dur step="freeze"/>, until firm enough to scoop.
</Step>

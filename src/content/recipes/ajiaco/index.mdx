  commonPitfalls:

<Step id="poach-chicken">
  Put the <Qty ref="chicken"/> in a large pot with the <Qty ref="water"/>,
  <Qty ref="onion"/>, <Qty ref="garlic"/> and half the <Qty ref="salt"/>.
  Bring to a simmer and cook for <Dur step="poach-chicken"/>, until the
  chicken is fully cooked.
</Step>

<Step id="shred-chicken">
  Lift out the chicken, discard the skin and bones, and shred the meat.
  Return the broth to the pot. This takes about <Dur step="shred-chicken"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-potatoes">
  Add the <Qty ref="potato"/> to the broth and simmer for
  <Dur step="cook-potatoes"/>, until the softer potatoes have broken down
  and thickened the broth while the firmer ones still hold their shape.
</Step>

<Step id="add-corn-and-guascas">
  Stir in the <Qty ref="corn"/>, <Qty ref="spring-onion"/>,
  <Qty ref="guascas"/>, the shredded chicken and the remaining
  <Qty ref="salt"/>. This takes about <Dur step="add-corn-and-guascas"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="simmer-together">
  Simmer for a further <Dur step="simmer-together"/>, until the corn is
  tender and the soup has come together.
</Step>

<Step id="serve">
  Ladle into bowls and serve with the <Qty ref="capers"/>,
  <Qty ref="avocado"/> and <Qty ref="cream"/> alongside, for each diner to
  add as they like. This takes about <Dur step="serve"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="prep-veg">
  Dice the <Qty ref="onion"/>, <Qty ref="celery"/> and <Qty ref="green-pepper"/>, and
  mince the <Qty ref="garlic"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="brown-sausage">
  Heat the <Qty ref="oil"/> in a large, heavy pot over a medium-high heat and brown the
  <Qty ref="sausage"/> for <Dur step="brown-sausage"/>. Lift it out and set aside.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-trinity">
  Add the onion, celery and green pepper to the fat left in the pot and cook for
  <Dur step="cook-trinity"/>, stirring often, until softened. Add the garlic in the
  last minute.
</Step>

<Step id="simmer-beans">
  Return the sausage to the pot along with the <Qty ref="beans"/>, <Qty ref="hock"/>,
  <Qty ref="bay"/> and <Qty ref="water"/>. Bring to the boil, then reduce to a simmer
  and cook, partially covered, for <Dur step="simmer-beans"/>, until the beans are
  tender and the hock meat is falling from the bone.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-rice">
  While the beans simmer, combine the <Qty ref="rice"/> and <Qty ref="rice-water"/> in
  a covered saucepan, bring to the boil, then reduce to the lowest heat and cook for
  <Dur step="cook-rice"/>, until the water is absorbed. Leave covered off the heat
  until serving.
</Step>

<Step id="finish">
  Lift out the hock, discard the bay leaf, and pick the meat from the bone, discarding
  the skin and excess fat; return the meat to the pot. Mash a few spoonfuls of beans
  against the side of the pot to thicken the liquid, then stir in the
  <Qty ref="cayenne"/>, <Qty ref="black-pepper"/> and <Qty ref="salt"/>. Serve ladled
  over the rice.
</Step>

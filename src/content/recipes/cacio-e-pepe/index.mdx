<Step id="toast-pepper">
  While the water heats, tip the <Qty ref="pepper"/> into a wide, dry pan over a
  moderate flame and toast it for <Dur step="toast-pepper"/>, shaking it about, until
  it turns fragrant and resinous rather than dusty. Take the pan off the heat and leave
  the pepper in it.
</Step>

<Step id="make-cream">
  Once the pasta is in the water, put the <Qty ref="pecorino-cream"/> in a bowl and beat
  in a ladleful of the starchy cooking water a little at a time, working it for
  <Dur step="make-cream"/> until it becomes a slack, entirely smooth paste with no grain
  left in it. Leave it to lose its heat while the pasta finishes.
</Step>

<Step id="emulsify">
  Drain the pasta, keeping plenty of the water back, and tip it into the pepper pan with
  a splash of that water, tossing over the pan's residual warmth until every strand is
  slick and coated. Now, off the heat and well below <Temp c={65}/>, add the cheese paste
  and toss hard for <Dur step="emulsify"/>, loosening with more cooking water until the
  sauce turns glossy and moves as one mass. Serve at once under the
  <Qty ref="pecorino-serve"/>.
</Step>

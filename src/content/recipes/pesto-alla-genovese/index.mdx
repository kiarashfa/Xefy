<Step id="pound-base">
  Pound the <Qty ref="garlic"/> with the <Qty ref="salt"/> to a smooth paste in the
  mortar. Add the <Qty ref="pine-nuts"/> and work them to a coarse paste, for
  <Dur step="pound-base"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="add-basil">
  Add the <Qty ref="basil"/> a few leaves at a time, pounding and turning in a circular
  motion until each batch has broken down before the next goes in. Work for
  <Dur step="add-basil"/>, until the mixture is a deep green paste.
</Step>

<Step id="add-cheese">
  Work in the <Qty ref="parmigiano"/> and the <Qty ref="pecorino"/> for
  <Dur step="add-cheese"/>, with a few drops of the oil to help the cheese blend into the
  paste.
</Step>

<Step id="add-oil">
  Trickle in the <Qty ref="olive-oil"/> a little at a time, stirring with the pestle, for
  <Dur step="add-oil"/>, until the sauce is loose and glossy. Serve it at once, with the
  pasta still hot.
</Step>

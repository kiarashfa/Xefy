<Step id="melt-butter">
  Melt the <Qty ref="butter"/> in a heavy pan over a low heat, without letting it colour.
</Step>

<Step id="cook-roux">
  Whisk in the <Qty ref="flour"/> and cook for <Dur step="cook-roux"/>, until the paste
  smells faintly of biscuit rather than raw flour.
</Step>

<Step id="add-milk">
  Add the <Qty ref="milk"/> a little at a time, whisking each addition smooth before the next.
</Step>

<Step id="simmer-sauce">
  Simmer for <Dur step="simmer-sauce"/> at a bare <Temp c={85}/>, stirring often, then season
  with the <Qty ref="salt"/>.
</Step>

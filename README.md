**Xefy** is a recipe encyclopedia with one page per dish. Every number on a page, from ingredient quantities to nutrition and timing, is computed from structured data rather than typed into prose, so change the serving count and the whole page recomputes.

Switch to US customary and temperatures and dimensions change with it, in the method text as well as the ingredient list. Nothing is hand-typed, so nothing can drift out of step. Anything the site remembers about you lives in your own browser.

Under the hood, it is a static site built with Astro and Svelte. Each recipe is structured data, and every quantity, nutrition figure and timing is computed from it, in metric or US customary units, so a page cannot drift out of step with itself. The build runs tests and content checks before anything is published.
